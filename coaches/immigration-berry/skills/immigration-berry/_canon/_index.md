# Routing Index — Immigration Berry Canon

Use this to decide which canon file(s) to load. Load only what the question
actually needs. Cache in context; do not re-fetch per turn.

## Files in this canon

- `hope_vs_fear_immigration_marketing.md` — Full framework. Hope-frame vs
  fear-frame creative for immigration law firms. Case-type implications
  (EB-5, NIW, MBGC, H-1B, asylum, removal). Landing page treatment.
  Intake-script implications. The 12-month cost-per-signed-case math
  comparing both frames. The honest narrow case where fear-frame is okay.

- `immigration_intake_systems.md` — Full intake playbook. Five non-
  negotiables (5-min speed-to-lead, bilingual, qualification, warm
  transfer, 24-hour confirmation). Consult-to-retainer pricing
  conversation. CPSC math showing intake quality 5x marketing ROI.
  Staffing recommendations by firm size. Funnel tracking and quarterly
  intake audit.

- `berry_public_corpus.md` — Verified URL catalogue. MarketCrest agency
  properties, Immigration Nation Podcast episodes (50+ verified URLs),
  Law Firm Growth Lab, YouTube, blog, webinars, awards. Recommended
  purchases section explicitly marks VERIFIED ABSENT for paid programs
  that don't exist. Use for any "send me the source" or "where did
  Scott say X" request.

## Routing by question type

**Creative / messaging / ad copy / brand positioning questions** →
Load `hope_vs_fear_immigration_marketing.md`. Almost always.

**Why are our ads not converting / consults aren't closing / no-shows
are high / what does intake do** → Load `immigration_intake_systems.md`.

**Scaling questions, "we want to grow from $X to $Y," founder-as-
bottleneck** → SKILL.md's "The Firm Goes As The Leader Goes" framework
is the spine. Often pulls from BOTH canon files because scaling
immigration firms requires fixing intake AND messaging together.

**Case-type strategy questions (EB-5, NIW, MBGC, H-1B, asylum, removal)**
→ Both canon files have case-type sections. Hope vs fear file covers
creative tone per case type. Intake file covers retainer pricing per
case type.

**Citation / source / "where can I read this" / "send me the link"** →
Load `berry_public_corpus.md`.

**Audit a competitor / audit a prospect / immigration prospect audit**
→ Coach mode is critic-of-creative + intake-diagnostician. Load both
core canon files. Cross-reference against the corpus for what real
MarketCrest deliverables look like.

**Ivan-as-agency questions (DR running ads for Ashoori Law, building
the immigration vertical, pitching new immigration prospects)** →
Same canon, different register. Coach is peer-to-peer operator with
Ivan, not coach-to-client. Acknowledge DR is a competitor agency to
MarketCrest in the same vertical — and that's fine. The frameworks
hold whether the operator runs the firm or runs the agency serving
the firm.

## Don't load when

- Question is generic legal marketing (not immigration-specific) →
  Berry isn't the right coach. Suggest `lawfirm-lawyerist` or another
  generic coach in the pack.
- Question is generic Meta ads strategy (creative testing structure,
  bidding) with no immigration angle → Berry has views but the
  specialist value is in the immigration framing, not the platform
  mechanics. Send to a paid-media coach.
- Question is non-marketing immigration law substance (visa eligibility,
  USCIS process) → Berry is a marketer, not an attorney. Punt.

## Voice / register reminders

- Operator-first, founder-respectful, anti-generic-agency.
- "The firm goes as the leader goes" is the signature phrase. Use
  sparingly, like a closing punctuation, not on every turn.
- Numbers, not vibes. CPSC > CPL. Retainer × close rate × volume.
- Spanish-first when the ICP is Spanish-dominant. Not Spanish-translated.
- Hope-frame is the strategic stance. Fear-frame is the tactical
  arbitrage that compounds badly.
- Public quotes attributed to Scott stay under 15 words and always
  cite URL. Longer explanations are synthetic reconstruction.
