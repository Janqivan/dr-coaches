# Ad formats — which format for which job
> Scope: which Meta ad formats (founder, partnership, UGC, yapper, mashup, VSL, statics, GIF, carousel, DPA, podcast, AI animation, EGC and more) the practitioners rank, when each works, for whom, and how they shifted into 2026. ### 2.2 Format by funnel stage and awareness (Cottrell)
- When he uses it: planning which format carries which job; "You are one wrong ad format from entirely tanking your meta ads." [quote src: YT:FWWp6NcVo3Q]
- Steps: 1. TOF / problem-unaware → UGC, street interviews, founder ads; aim for "Hey, that's me" [quote src: YT:FWWp6NcVo3Q] familiarity (teen with first acne). 2. MOF / problem-aware → mini VSLs, mashups, podcasts; friction and emotion via UGC demos, testimonials. 3. Solution-aware → us-vs-them statics. 4. Product-aware → trust, remove the blocker. 5. Most-aware / BOF → offer statics, testimonial compilations (customers given free product); first frame of a BOF hook is discount-related; urgency ("limited-time 30% off" [quote src: YT:gP-68LmgAQ4]). Statics "sweeping up the sales" [quote src: YT:y7TFcfgwhP8]; video for TOF.
- Their example: mismatch wastes thousands to millions.
- Sources: [YT:gP-68LmgAQ4, YT:FWWp6NcVo3Q, YT:DPvFnflNEYQ, YT:Mqj05tYuDI0] (2025-11, 2025-11, 2025-07, 2026-09) · DTC
- Counter: Denney — statics aren't only for people who know you; she rejects ugly ads for unaware, polished for most-aware as oversimplified. [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs, YT:2jVcLu3Earg] (2025-01, 2025-05). Savannah 2020 sided with stages: video for prospecting, stills for remarketing.

### 2.3 TEAP — Trigger, Exploration, Evaluation, Purchase (Levinger)
- When she uses it: auditing an account's format mix; from a Google study of digital purchasing, tested across ~150 strategies; people loop through exploration/evaluation plus a post-purchase loop.
- Steps: 1. TRIGGER (doesn't know a solution exists) — earns attention: relatable micro-moments, "this is so me" [quote src: YT:M2ObOuFQSv0], "feel seen, not sold to" [quote src: YT:pV0tM1gSJiU]. 2. EXPLORATION (low commitment, wants novelty) — earns understanding: short-form, ASMR, "gloopy" satisfying content, visual hooks, memes. 3. EVALUATION — earns belief: testimonials, before/afters, education, case studies, comparisons; table-style value ads ("never seen them not work" [quote src: YT:pV0tM1gSJiU] in ~6 years). 4. PURCHASE — earns the sale: long form, founder ads, "it's time to buy" [quote src: YT:pV0tM1gSJiU] offers (50% off, free gift). 5. Count ads per phase — most brands cover basically two; build a Motion library by phase.
- Their example: AutoBrush — Trigger = parent at breaking point ("I just can't do this anymore" [quote src: YT:vBotjaDGNQI]); Exploration = U-shaped brushes remove plaque; Evaluation = why AutoBrush is better. The winner ("my friend recommended this" [quote src: YT:vBotjaDGNQI]) was bottom-exploration/evaluation; opening on just came back from the dentist, first cavity would make it a trigger ad. Ridge rings: TRIGGER "three out of four men misplace their wedding band" [quote src: YT:pV0tM1gSJiU]; EXPLORATION He said I do years ago, but now his ring finally does too; EVALUATION table-style value ad.
- Warnings: purchase creative "completely flop"s on explorers; exploration brains reject too-clever/deep; purchase brains hate over-explaining landers.
- Sources: [YT:pV0tM1gSJiU, YT:vBotjaDGNQI] (2026-06, 2026-07) · NOW · GEN/DTC

### 2.4 Five "money maker" formats + five testing formats (Levinger)
- When she uses it: tested on every new brand; "not trends… not going to disappear in 6 months" [quote src: YT:6ZBccBr5AV4].
- Money makers: 1. Before the problem had a name. 2. Social mirror. 3. Reframe. 4. Specificity. 5. Earned confidence — easiest; for warm fans show "receipts": third-party validation, real customer footage, "proof that doesn't feel like it's produced" [quote src: YT:6ZBccBr5AV4].
- Q3 2026 testing formats: literal POV, multi-series, subtle flex, micro-learning, lo-fi yap (mechanisms: mirror neurons, contrast effect, processing fluency, reciprocity).
- What a money maker must do: match the state + reduce decision friction + give emotional certainty.
- Visual: money makers are square statics with ONE 5–14-word headline, one product, one proof device; testing winners are lo-fi phone video with a small native top-third box and near-wordless statics; POV via the viewer's hand in frame at eyeline height. (VISUAL)
- Sources: [YT:6ZBccBr5AV4, YT:a5ruZB-Urlw] (2026-05, 2026-07) · NOW · DTC/GEN

### 2.5 Size of the ask = complexity of format (Heath)
- When he uses it: choosing image vs video, especially lead gen.
- Steps: 1. Rank the ask: impulse buy, discount, content click, lead magnet, free Zoom = small; in-person meeting (even free), done-for-you service, high-ticket = big. 2. Small ask → image (a 3-minute video loses buyers before the CTA). 3. Big ask → longer video with proof/testimonials. 4. Ladder: image → carousel → slideshow → video. 5. Still test the opposite.
- His examples: his webinar ads are mostly images/carousels; his done-for-you service via image to cold = "no chance"; video-only markets = new products, complex services; image-only = cheap/price-competitive ($12 novelty sunglasses); proof-only video works when the offer is understood (physio). Savannah: $5,000 Outer sofa needs video; When in doubt, video with simple animation.
- If a client says video doesn't work: "maybe your video ads just weren't good enough" [quote src: YT:D6Sxk8Mbkq4] — test UGC, influencer, founder, demo, slideshow, full animation first.
- Sources: [YT:N3X8CuD_VQk, YT:GPp8Y-lKzJw, YT:HzDJpKu7vew, YT:WeQrKeAwZDg, YT:z3LiJ0PbY-0] (2023-02, 2025-02, 2020-07, 2019-07, 2024-10) · LEADGEN/GEN

### 2.6 "The middle ground is where ads go to die" [quote src: YT:OzkRU3Wx_1w] (Cooper)
- When he uses it: deciding how an ad should look in 2026; internal team training.
- Steps: 1. Pick an end: (a) super ugly and organic, indistinguishable from feed content, or (b) obviously an ad but with DR copy so good "that people don't care" [quote src: YT:OzkRU3Wx_1w]. 2. For (b): DR script + AI voiceover + B-roll, great copy, exaggerated visuals — no need to hide it's an ad. 3. For (a): no stock soundtrack, setup, aesthetic or location that signals "I've paid someone to read a script" [quote src: YT:OzkRU3Wx_1w]. 4. Never sit between — "you're in no man's land" [quote src: YT:Ugd7a2sPgZk].
- His example: his agency is simultaneously "super AI forward" [quote src: YT:KSm0pvevV9k] (AI animation) and "super ugly" (real yappers).
- Sources: [YT:OzkRU3Wx_1w, YT:Ugd7a2sPgZk] (2026-03) · NOW · GEN
- Related (Hott): authenticity = tone, delivery, aesthetic, source; they trade off — inauthentic aesthetic can be offset by super-authentic delivery. [YT:6jvIXIjieyA, YT:YuS18wnD_bc] (2025-05, 2023-07)

### 2.7 Creative diversity levels 0 / 1 / 5 (Denney)

### 2.8 Founder ad formats (Denney's six + workarounds)
- Steps: 1. Problem-solution / VSL, 35s–3 min. 2. Objection handler — easiest, top FAQs. 3. Demo. 4. Pure education (listicle/whiteboard) after the first three. 5. Hi-pro shoot. 6. Sale/launch announcements from the founder's POV. Plus founder slideshow; personal history angles.
- Workarounds when the founder won't film: voice note with B-roll over it (Cottrell); voice-only over a mashup (Kolkata Chai); creator green-screened over founder BTS (guest Hamby); interview/prompt style (guest Delmege); Cooper at $10–20K/month: phone out, 5–10 minutes.
- Scale note (Denney): from 7 to mid-8 figures founder content is the highest-leverage activity until 9 figures; Apothekary's founder now mainly for launches.

### 2.9 David and Goliath ads (Denney)
- When: tested on "every single brand that gets in the door" [quote src: YT:ePcEcT2PH8E]; evolved from her controversy ads.
- Steps: 1. Hook calls out an enemy (a specific industry, a massive scam) with mass shock value. 2. Contrast with you — founder directly or brand. 3. Immediately proof: differentiators, why different, customer benefit. Variant that goes educational before the brand also wins. "Americans love that" [quote src: YT:ePcEcT2PH8E] (underdog).

### 2.10 Partnership-ad workflow (Cottrell, Denney, guests)
- Steps: 1. Start from a proven ad — phone-shot, person-led, lo-fi wins; hi-pro from a person's handle flops; so does a partner identity with a different creator in the video. 2. If a UGC ad wins from the brand page, run it as partnership, test 5–7 days for lift, then a 1- or 3-month deal (Cottrell). 3. Demand modular content: three hooks, directions, B-roll — one hero piece "is just a creative" [quote src: YT:9UBSGSS2Yuo] (guest Saliukov). 4. Audit the split: partnership share of spend, rank of best partnership ad, which top hooks aren't yet run as partnership (Denney). 5. Identity ladder (guest Ollie Hudson): founder → new identities → experts → creators → big influencers.
- Definitions: whitelisting = brand runs from creator's page, brand invisible, brand controls budget; partnership = boosted from creator's post, both handles visible ("the best of both worlds" [quote src: YT:q_PsIjZl6e4]).
- Sources: [YT:q_PsIjZl6e4, YT:m8dbcF407H8, YT:ePcEcT2PH8E, YT:-E9sjb2msjo, YT:fls5dwYVY1M] (2025-09, 2026-03, 2026-04, 2026-06, 2025-08)

## 3. Format playbook — when each works and for whom
