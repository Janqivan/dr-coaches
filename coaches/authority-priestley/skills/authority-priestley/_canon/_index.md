## Files

### `key_person_of_influence_frameworks.md`
Cross-book framework structure map (the introductory file). Covers headline frameworks from all four books:
- *Entrepreneur Revolution* (2013/2018) — 5-stage business ladder, entrepreneur seasons, the global small business
- *Key Person of Influence* (revised eds) — the 5 P's (Pitch / Publish / Product / Profile / Partnerships), the lighthouse effect
- *Oversubscribed* (2015/2020) — create the gap, the campaign method, campaign-driven enterprise
- *24 Assets* (2017) — the 24-asset matrix, ascending transaction model, vitruvian entrepreneur, small giant

Each framework: source book + chapter tag, mechanism in own words, failure modes, DR application notes. Load for cross-book diagnostics, the 5 P's, stage-of-business decisions, and the master framework lookup.

---

### `oversubscribed_frameworks.md`
Deep map of *Oversubscribed* (Capstone, 2015 / rev. 2020). Covers demand engineering, lottery vs queue dynamics, why most businesses are underpriced, the six signals of oversubscription, the seven status drivers of premium demand, the four-stage campaign method, the supply cap and why founders fear it, scarcity timing, pre-selling / signal conversion, and the arc from one oversubscribed campaign to a structurally oversubscribed business. Load for demand engineering, campaign design, pricing power, and any "we are chasing customers" diagnosis.

---

### `twenty_four_assets_frameworks.md`
Deep map of *24 Assets* (Rethink Press, 2017). The four quadrants × six assets = 24 leverageable assets architecture. For each asset: what it is, how to build it, how it lifts enterprise value at sale. Quadrants: Intellectual (vision/culture, brand story, methodology/IP, plan, education/training, data/research), Brand (identity, marketing material, publication, digital presence, PR/media, reputation/social proof), System (productized service, technology/platform, operations/SOPs, customer service/delivery, sales process, quality/compliance), and Customer/Community/Cash (CRM/list, community, partnerships, recurring revenue, financial systems, investment/ownership). Load for asset audits, exit-readiness work, founder-dependency diagnoses, and "how do I scale without hiring 50 people" questions.

---

### `entrepreneur_revolution_frameworks.md`
Deep map of *Entrepreneur Revolution* (Capstone, 2013; rev. 2018). Covers the industrial-age vs entrepreneur-age thesis, entrepreneur character vs employee character, the 9-step entrepreneur path (tribe → opportunity → offer → micro-niche brand → KPI → oversubscribed → 24 assets → small giant → scale/sell/repeat), the five filters for spotting a real opportunity, the global small business thesis, the small-giants thesis, the entrepreneur seasons, and the four levels of value creation (labour / service / product / asset). Load for mindset and frame-level diagnoses, "should I" decisions, niching decisions, and questions about long-horizon business shape.
