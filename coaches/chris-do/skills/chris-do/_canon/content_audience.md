## 2. Frameworks (named models and step sequences)

### The 9-1-1 rule (give/ask ratio)
- When he uses it: when engagement dies or a creator keeps pitching ("call 911"); he admits he is "not 100% committed to it yet". [quote src: YT:rOduTNGhW1k]
- Steps: 1. Nine pieces of pure value, no selling ("Don't mix up the waters"). 2. One personal, unusual piece ("turn the camera around"). 3. One full-throated ask, no apology, not dressed up as content: "I have a new program that I'm launching I'd love to have you in it", "thanks for considering it", then back to regular posts. 4. Flex it (7-1-1 or whatever) but "don't flip the equation"; all-education fails too, because people ask "who the hell are you". [quote src: YT:rOduTNGhW1k]
- His example: variants: give 10x value, ask one time, 90/10, his shop's 8-to-1 (credits Daniel Priestley); give-ask-give-ask brands are "culturally bankrupt".
- Sources: [YT:eS9LtexTMFk, YT:rOduTNGhW1k, YT:890eAEHu0NU] (2024–2025)

### Hybrid funnel (content plus permission outreach)
- When he uses it: for people who need clients before content compounds ("people don't have that kind of runway"); content straight to a CTA is "too abrupt of a step". [quote src: YT:CiyNGOIbwcE]
- Steps: 1. Build a real resource from your proof (e.g. top 100 subject lines from 10,000 campaigns). 2. Post three nuggets on LinkedIn and say why they work. 3. Offer the rest free: "comment this word below". 4. Commenters have given "permission to reach out". 5. DM "thank you for downloading it", then "is there a problem that you're trying to solve?" 6. Offer a 25-minute diagnostic call. 7. Propose working together "only if it feels right to you". [quote src: YT:CiyNGOIbwcE]
- His example: the Richard Moore debate: short term "you're going to put all your money on Richard"; long term "My model is more transformational. It's not transactional." The real failure is mixing them: "a thinly veiled disguise and most people can see right through it". [quote src: YT:CiyNGOIbwcE]
- Sources: [YT:CiyNGOIbwcE, YT:b8iwit0yuuI] (2023–2025)
#### Hunter vs farmer (outbound vs inbound)
- When he uses it: outbound vs inbound, or nobody notices your work.
- Steps: 1. Hunter: stalk, wait, ambush; weeks of nothing, then a feast (a $400,000 whale client), then starvation. 2. Farmer: plant, nurture, protect; "not sexy at all but it works", and neglect it and you starve. 3. Most creatives are farmers: till the soil, grow the cash crops people want. 4. If no one notices, check the work, its type, presentation, hashtags, consistent posting and engaging. [quote src: YT:HUfNu9nywuQ]
- Sources: [YT:lcs8zengUxU, YT:HUfNu9nywuQ] (2020, 2023)

### Lead magnets, comment keywords and samples
- When he uses it: building a PDF, checklist or "comment this word" offer.
- Steps: 1. Ask "who are you serving with this PDF?" 2. Make it digital, scalable and aligned with what you sell (a logo designer giving away a sales playbook is "a total misalignment"). 3. Give "15 to 25% of the value" up front: "if you have 10 tricks give them your number one trick". 4. Post one chunk, then "comment some specific keyword and I will send you a link", using a weird word that can't occur in normal conversation ("logo tastic"). 5. Sample funnel (from Hormozi's $100M Leads): magnify the problem, give step one of eight free (the "Costco effect"), DM it to everyone who comments "sample me"; they ask for the next step. [quote src: YT:KXXfXtey9Jc, YT:Bm0nYSJikwo]
- Sources: [YT:KXXfXtey9Jc, YT:Bm0nYSJikwo, YT:guhfsArySxU] (2019–2024)

### Diagnostic-quiz blueprint (content to clients)
- When he uses it: turning content into a buyer-sorting system; most people make content "with no strategy. We just make spray and prey". [quote src: YT:POnPkjjT6Pw]
- Steps: 1. Start from the ICP. 2. Make content that "creates interest or agitates a problem". 3. CTA to a diagnostic quiz (Take this 60-second assessment to see what stage you're in). 4. Route the answers: yes = call or program invite; no (can't afford, can't deliver, wrong timeline) = a scalable resource and a retake in 3, 6, 9 months; maybe = email nurture ("We're not a perfect fit yet"), because The maybes in sales waste all your time. 5. Write it from an empathy worksheet: their stuck moment, what they say aloud vs think privately, the problem they wrongly blame, the real problem, what they wish someone had told them. [quote src: YT:POnPkjjT6Pw]
- His example: the target reaction is "That's me. You're speaking my language." [quote src: YT:POnPkjjT6Pw]
- Sources: [YT:POnPkjjT6Pw, YT:LJVP2wRk7Cc, YT:asE-XOcFMjE] (2024–2026)

### Rewind the tape (awareness stages and lead temperature)
- When he uses it: when creatives only make last-step content (price, timeline).
- Steps: 1. "Rewind the tape. Empathize. Use your imagination." See the unaware buyer "through their eyes and not your own". 2. Map about three phases (the problem, the many possible solutions, then you) and write for each: "we will sell more if we just talk about the problem". 3. Match temperature (Russell Brunson): cold = answer "what is the problem" in their language; warm = "what is the solution", via trusted endorsements; hot (on your list) = personality content and "what is the product". 4. Pre-frame with a content-rich page on your process so you aren't "just another person pitching". [quote src: YT:x6z8-ZAXfHE, YT:75CidDC1qgg, YT:mgNTZRpZmEk]
- His example: tested on his typography course with problem-unaware topics (Are your layouts boring?).
- Sources: [YT:x6z8-ZAXfHE, YT:yevZjd-YwUY, YT:37gKuNp-XLw] (2019–2024)

### Curriculum funnel and authority ladder
- When he uses it: explaining how free content becomes high-ticket fees (Godin's idea; he calls Seth idealistic and works out the practical version).

Until they "give you forty five thousand dollars to design a logo". The mistake is opening with "book me now". [quote src: YT:h-eRwbbpfxo, YT:70un-MNFedA]
- Authority ladder: each episode becomes a blog page (transcript, summary, affiliate links, "book me" CTA) aimed at top-10 ranking; then rank for a problem, speaking ($10,000 plus travel), coaching, a book, courses.

### Instagram from zero (plan, grid, benchmarks)
- When he uses it: starting or reviving an account.
- Steps: 1. Mindset: "this is a long game. It's not a game of instant gratification." 2. Post at least 12 to fill the grid before judging growth; strangers check what else you do. 3. Teach something; the rule is "inform or inspire" (family and dog photos give strangers nothing). 5. About three colors, "one, maybe two fonts at the most"; think in threes (dark/light/dark) and plan three to nine posts ahead; daily life goes in Stories. 6. Adopt new features early (10-slide carousels in 2021); the algorithm skews toward adopters. [quote src: YT:R-EyTBRgZh8, YT:0cyFrU3UaMc]
- Benchmarks (2018): ~10% of followers engaging "isn't horrible, it's not mind-blowing"; ~5% is "not great". [quote src: YT:z8BuocNq1Po]

### Carousel build (AIDA with "Detail", ROT, slide map)
- When he uses it: making or critiquing carousels (Instagram, LinkedIn).
- Steps: 1. Headline = attention + clear promise + timeline; ROT hook (Result, Objection, Time): "learn how to speak Spanish" "in two weeks" "even if you don't know how to speak a foreign language". 2. AIDA, but "I changed desire to detail": deliver the goods or readers are "really angry that I've swiped through seven slides". 3. Slide map: 1 promise; 2 nuance and objections; 8–9 the important thing; 10 summarize and ask a question (comments, dwell time). 4. One idea, "light but satisfying": split a dense slide into ~five; teach the method in the first eight slides rather than withholding it to force a call. 5. One CTA, not "comment below... save this"; a closing question needs guidance ("here are three things"). [quote src: YT:R-EyTBRgZh8]
- Stuck: heads or tails: heads = write 10 headlines for one idea, pick the best; tails = start from the slide 8–9 takeaway and work backwards. If the takeaway isn't "meaningful... insightful... personal... relevant", it's "all garbage".
- Critique: read it aloud, then judge clarity, "are you giving me something or you taking away my time?", typesetting, concept fit, X factor. [quote src: YT:qjZospdjnS0]
- Sources: [YT:qjZospdjnS0, YT:R-EyTBRgZh8, YT:d-g4wIfWt0Y] (2021)
- ➕ The Action slide models direct-mail instructions: go to your mailbox, pull this out, open the envelope, write this, put five dollars in, put a 25-cent stamp on, drop it in the mailbox by Tuesday, then "Is that clear." Step-by-step with a deadline. — [YT:t-7kpMQsRCA] (2020)

### LinkedIn post formulas and the comment loop
- When he uses it: LinkedIn coaching.
