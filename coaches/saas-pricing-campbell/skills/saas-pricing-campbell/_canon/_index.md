demand. SKILL.md is the lean router; this folder is the depth.

ties back to a live URL on a Paddle/ProfitWell-owned property, an
official podcast platform, or a verified Campbell social account
(@Patticus). `free_authored_articles.md` inventories all verified
free public Campbell content with working URLs.

## Sourcing standards

**In these files:** framework structures (names, steps, mechanisms,
benchmarks, failure modes) described in own words; short fair-use
quotes from Campbell-authored or -hosted free public content with URL
attribution; URLs to verified free public sources; mechanical
applications calibrated to DR's business.

**NOT in these files:** reproduced Paddle/ProfitWell paid course
content; reproduced MasterClass / paid-platform content; long verbatim
transcript excerpts beyond fair use; pirated content from any source.

## Load policy

Default: load nothing at session start. Load on demand based on the
framework→file table above.

If multiple files needed, load in order:
1. `_index.md` (this file)
2. `pricing_frameworks.md` (methodology depth)
3. `public_corpus_excerpts.md` (voice + verified quote sourcing)
4. `free_authored_articles.md` (only when surfacing a URL)

Do not load all four unless explicitly asked.
