### 1a. Savannah

1. **The hook is the ad; only the opening changes.** (≈25 mentions, GEN) Her ads follow one formula: novel hook, then a normal UGC sequence (unboxing, features), then CTA. On a 36s ad the last ~33s matter much less than how it starts; the body stays largely the same (creators on value props, product photos, product in use). "the hook is definitely the most important part of the ad"; most people don't reach two seconds — "it's really all about earning seconds of viewership". 2025 focus: "something cool" with editing in the first three seconds; if you film a fun transition, put it at the start (brands bury it 10+ seconds in). — [YT:wrbvSulohNI] (2023-06), [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06), [YT:KALGyNhfrVU] (2025-07), [YT:kFtv5VDkRSg] (2025-10)
2. **UGC is not dead; lazy UGC is.** (≈12 mentions, DTC/GEN) TikTok-style UGC testimonial talking to camera with organic TikTok text overlays was #1 in 2023 and still #1 in Q1 2024 on Meta and TikTok; still the top performer in all her accounts. "UGC doesn't work" usually means one creator, bad lighting, boring rambling. What died: getting away with a simple unboxing/testimonial with no scroll-stopping element as CPMs and competition rose — you can't "half-ass" it. UGC beats high production because it looks native to organic TikTok/Reels. — [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA] (2024-03), [YT:KALGyNhfrVU] (2025-07), [YT:PvjkCV9WelU] (2020-04), [YT:6OmF0BzBOHQ] (2020-05)
3. **Make it look like organic content, not an ad — but keep the DR spine.** (≈12 mentions, GEN) "making TikTok videos, not ads, is the key". UGC works because it reads as a friend/influencer post long enough to earn attention before viewers realize it's an ad. People are increasingly blind to things that look like ads. 2024 client ask: "don't do like three reasons why ads", nothing formulaic. Limit: don't look like an ad taken too far = product dances with no clear CTA; ads still need problem → solution → CTA. — [YT:w1sz_OdVQiA] (2021-03), [YT:wrbvSulohNI] (2023-06), [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA] (2024-03), [YT:PvjkCV9WelU] (2020-04)
4. **Design for TikTok first, run it on Meta.** (≈8 mentions, GEN) Optimize for TikTok first even though most clients spend far more on Meta; TikTok-style organic look works on IG/FB and repurposes to Pinterest/Snapchat; she has seen no Facebook-specific best practices. Reels mirrors TikTok — not really such thing as TikTok specific content anymore. Reverse doesn't hold: Facebook-optimized stills/videos fail on TikTok, and porting IG Stories/Snapchat ads to TikTok was "a complete failure". Meta tolerates more polish (studio shots); TikTok leans raw with native effects/fonts; both keep hook-problem-solution. YouTube is the exception: 16:9, horizontal, more polished. — [YT:l1_xSlj4Z50] (2021-10), [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06), [YT:jAc63K9lRIQ] (2025-02), [YT:wrbvSulohNI] (2023-06), [YT:w1sz_OdVQiA] (2021-03)
5. **Put text on screen in weird, physical ways.** (≈15 mentions, GEN/LEADGEN) Most common hook mistake: no on-screen text — assume sound off on TikTok and Meta. Recurring 2025 principle: find interesting ways to get text on screen instead of a "boring text graphic overlay" — handwritten paper, Post-its, sunglasses reflections, text appearing on a phone, highlighter on a journal, writing on a cake, whiteboard. — [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06), [YT:KALGyNhfrVU] (2025-07), [YT:j9d6j0B3W6U] (2025-02)

Service/website/app businesses must work harder on the first seconds; text-on-paper and message-in-the-clouds hooks work for them. Apps/B2B use the same approach as DTC: transitions, testimonials, green screen. — [YT:3TzdwzAG1X8] (2025-03), [YT:j9d6j0B3W6U] (2025-02), [YT:jAc63K9lRIQ] (2025-02), [YT:KALGyNhfrVU] (2025-07), [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01)

## 2. Frameworks and methods

### Savannah's

**2.1 Novel hook → normal UGC body → CTA (her one formula)** · When: every ad. · Steps: (1) invent an unexpected first frame/prop/device; (2) drop into standard testimonial/B-roll/unboxing/features; (3) end on offer + website CTA. Only the opening is swapped between variants; e.g. heads/tails luggage hook — swapping only the first ~3s gave drastically different results across clients. Visual pattern across ~20 examples: 100% vertical, phone-shot, natural light, real homes/streets; the novelty lives entirely in the first frame, then it falls back to normal UGC. — [YT:KALGyNhfrVU] (2025-07), [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06)

**2.2 "Second zero" / main message first** · When: concepting any video. · Steps: start from the first frame — "what can you say in that first frame visually"; show the product early; clear text overlays; shop-now CTA. TV builds to the message at the end; social flips it — product and problem known in the first 3 seconds; value and a reason to buy within 3–5s. Upload attention-grabbing custom thumbnails; users decide from the thumbnail in under a second. Applies to Facebook and Snapchat too. — [YT:w1sz_OdVQiA] (2021-03), [YT:l1_xSlj4Z50] (2021-10), [YT:PvjkCV9WelU] (2020-04)

**2.3 Cliffhanger first frame** (refinement, 2024) · Don't reveal the product in the first second; introduce it at second two to create intrigue. First frames are visually odd before they are informative (purple teeth, phone-in-phone, double-self FaceTime). — [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06)

**2.4 Great UGC principles (2020)** · Under 15 seconds; open with a question or problem; product as solution; last ~2s is CTA ("swipe up to learn more") — ~10 seconds of substance. — [YT:6OmF0BzBOHQ] (2020-05)

**2.5 Her top-three hooks (2024)** · (1) text-message exchange; (2) TikTok comment question overlay — always in the first month for a new brand; (3) anything extreme in the first second (smashing, exploding tube, Post-its on fire, running over something). — [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06)

**2.6 Text exchange hook** · Steps: friend texts a scripted message; screen-record on iPhone; zoom in; cut to the regular ad (testimonial/B-roll). One of their top formats for Q4 2023, still working evergreen. Sale variant script in §3. — [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06)

**2.7 Writing hook / "interesting writing hook"** · Physically write the main value prop first — phone paint app, chalkboard, whiteboard, sand, food, mirror — then normal problem/solution or testimonial. Customize: phone/kitchen/summer/business must-haves. — [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06), [YT:jAc63K9lRIQ] (2025-02)

**2.8 "Ad Inception" (her name)** · An ad inside an ad: film scrolling through ads on a phone, then film that phone; e.g. "POV you can't escape zeni ads"; ends swiping to the website tab. Loop variant: "after seeing these ads over and over again this brand finally got me", shows one of Loop's own ads, then why she's glad she listened, then purchase. Tested across clients as a strong scroll-stopper. — [YT:q-_wSPr4md8] (2025-02)

**2.9 The self skit** · When: one of the first formats for every new app/service client; her "go-to"; "we see great watch times". Steps: same creator in two outfits talking to herself — FaceTiming herself, or filmed across the room and edited as a conversation, or green-screen with both versions in frame (looking up/down so they converse), or multi-screen (monitor, laptop, in person); app overlay on top. Called "an absolute smash", repeated across nearly all clients; works for nearly any product or service. Example line: "dating app that doesn't suck". — [YT:jAc63K9lRIQ] (2025-02), [YT:3TzdwzAG1X8] (2025-03), [YT:KALGyNhfrVU] (2025-07), [YT:kFtv5VDkRSg] (2025-10)

**2.10 Fake FaceTime** · Talent records twice in two outfits; superimpose second clip on the first; add FaceTime button overlays and text; then regular footage. Uncommon, so it stops the scroll; good for founders starring in their own ads; doable in CapCut (they use After Effects). — [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06)

**2.11 Fake podcast** · In-house talent, $20 Amazon fake mic, laptop in front, holding product, "ON AIR" / podcast graphic superimposed; fits content people already consume. — [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06), [YT:pNECdkJjpCU] (2023-06)

**2.12 Photo flip / mixed formats** · Open with a slideshow of stills, then cut to video; mixing stills + video + text + screen recordings + green screen in one ad helps performance; stills are neglected on TikTok. 2025: photo collage opener before video "to stop the scroll". Split-screen/mixed media gave incremental lift back in 2020. — [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06), [YT:KALGyNhfrVU] (2025-07), [YT:PvjkCV9WelU] (2020-04)

**2.13 No-product toolkit (apps/services/lead gen)** · Formats she runs: self skit; walk-and-talk (handles objections, "I used to not like dating apps…", 15–30s sweet spot, mimics a live/story); green screen over website/app (film creator in front of a white background for easy cut-out); whiteboard ("best $10 investment" — draw a graph, write value props, cross out words; she does a whiteboard concept for every client at some point; reason: a creator only talking has no visuals and viewers get lost); Notes app checklist; Post-its on the screen; man-on-the-street (staged with two of her creators, scripted talking points, "pre-fabricated environment"); success stories (b-roll of the happy end result); before/after; POV with exaggerated reactions + app screenshots; TikTok comment response (best for retargeting objections). End on App Store screenshot/logo + download CTA. — [YT:j9d6j0B3W6U] (2025-02), [YT:jAc63K9lRIQ] (2025-02), [YT:3TzdwzAG1X8] (2025-03), [YT:KALGyNhfrVU] (2025-07)

**2.14 Three-structure test per client** · Each client is tested as three structurally different openers side by side, not hook tweaks of one video (Affordable Health Plans, Alma, Zapier, Jackpocket); caption boxes colored to the brand. — [YT:j9d6j0B3W6U] (2025-02)

**2.15 Visual storytelling with props** · Demonstrate the story with props (rice spread over weeks for payment plans) rather than someone explaining to camera, "which is really boring". — [YT:KALGyNhfrVU] (2025-07)

**2.16 Top 3 transitions** · Drop change (drop phone, pick up in new scene/outfit — clothing old vs new); reverse clip (drop product, reverse so it flies back into hand); magic tap (tap phone screen, product appears — her favorite). Stop motion "consistently works" as a hook (remote-control click through SKUs). — [YT:KALGyNhfrVU] (2025-07), [YT:q-_wSPr4md8] (2025-02)

**2.17 Hook sourcing system (2026)** · Sources: hooks working for other clients (Motion), ad libraries, organic TikTok trends/transitions, her master list of top-performing hooks since 2020 (each linked to the ad), plus a per-client week-by-week log of hooks tested. Statics ideas: Foreplay, filtered to statics, top brands' most-saved ads. Copies formats seen in the wild (jump-out-of-phone, from an ad seen weeks earlier; fake poll from a pharma ad, tested across three clients in one week). Uses ElevenLabs TTS to test hook lines. — [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01), [YT:KALGyNhfrVU] (2025-07), [YT:kFtv5VDkRSg] (2025-10)

**2.18 Edit QC checklist (2026)** · Length (filler/too short); logical sequencing; script alignment; sizes; safe zones; spelling/grammar (copy-paste captions from doc); legible fonts (no white on white); effects on-brand; followable with and without sound; legible on phone-size screen; closed captions correct; CTA present. Audio: music fits vibe, no level jumps, background noise removed (Premiere AI), lip sync, headphone check, VO/music volume. Watch every ad sound-off 1–2 times before sending. Final gut check: "is my ad better than the inspiration?" and would I be proud to post it. — [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01)

**2.19 "Performance boosters" check (2026)** · Engaging hook in imagery and messaging (first 3s); quick pacing — cut pauses, jump cut more, minimum ~3 seconds per frame for legibility; zoom in/out on testimonial to emphasize words; storyline hook → story → CTA (deviate only deliberately, e.g. trend); highlight a human desire; where do eyes go first; add split screens, before/afters, us vs them. — [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01)

**2.20 Native story ads (2020)** · Upload still/video into Instagram Stories, add native text, polls, GIFs, download, run as Stories/Snapchat ad — "slam dunk". — [YT:PvjkCV9WelU] (2020-04)

**2.21 Creator seeding → whitelisting (2021)** · BlendJet: seeded product to food/beverage/cooking creators with branding/feature/content-type guidelines, then ran ads from their accounts — "very successful". — [YT:w1sz_OdVQiA] (2021-03)

**2.22 Top creative tips list (2021)** · Native text; trending effects (green screen, time warp, lenses); tell your story/founder ("people connect with people"); product applications (unboxing, try-on hauls, first impressions, door unboxing, ordering process); design for sound on with captions; build in a reason to comment (quiz, life tip, recipe). — [YT:l1_xSlj4Z50] (2021-10), [YT:w1sz_OdVQiA] (2021-03)
