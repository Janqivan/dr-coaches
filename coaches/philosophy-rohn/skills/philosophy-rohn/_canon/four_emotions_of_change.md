# THE 4 EMOTIONS OF CHANGE — The Day That Turns Your Life Around

Sources: seminar transcript [YT:32KUrAdSrgo] (1981-era recording, pulled Aug 28 2026); Rohn essay "The Four Emotions That Can Lead to Life Change" [GM: getmotivation.com/jimrohn/jr4emotions.html]; "Turning Your Life Around" = *Leading an Inspired Life* Ch. 14 [LAIL via meaningring]; official Girl Scout telling [JR: /wisdom/articles/the-day-that-turns-your-life-around]. "The Day that Turns Your Life Around" is also the title of Ch. 11 of *7 Strategies* [7S].

Change does not come gradually. It comes when one or more of four emotions reaches threshold on a single day.

> "Emotions are the most powerful forces inside us. Under the power of emotions, human beings can perform the most heroic (as well as barbaric) acts." [GM]

> "There are four stages to the day that turns your life around, and the first is disgust." [LAIL]

## 1. DISGUST — "I've had it."

> "Disgust says, 'I have had it.' See, that could be the day. The day you can say, 'I've had it,' may not be the day it ends, but the day it begins." [YT:32KUrAdSrgo]

> "The person who feels disgusted has reached a point of no return. He or she is ready to throw down the gauntlet at life and say, 'I've had it!'" [GM]

> "Disgust is a negative emotion but it can have a very positive, motivating effect." [LAIL]

The stories he tells here [YT:32KUrAdSrgo]:
- **The Girl Scout.** Age 25, broke, she gives "an excellent sales pitch" for $2 cookies; he doesn't have $2; he lies — "no thanks, we've already bought from someone else." When she leaves: "I don't want to live like this any more. I've had it with lying and being broke." [also JR official telling]
- **The two cans of beans.** The man sick inside, knowing his wife will buy the 37-cent can over the 39-cent can — a brand she doesn't even like — "to save two cents." "The guy sick inside finally says, 'I've had it.'"
- **The closet.** "He walks into his closet and rips everything in it to shreds and says, 'I've worn this embarrassing stuff for the last time.' Commit an act that says, 'I've had it.'"

Coaching read: if change keeps failing, disgust usually isn't deep enough yet. Don't soothe it. Let it total up.

## 2. DECISION — "I'm going to do something about it."

> "Decision-making is powerful and it's emotional. That's those knots in the pit of your stomach... We sometimes call it inner civil war." [YT:32KUrAdSrgo]

> "If it's easy, do it easy. If it's hard, do it hard. Just get it done." [YT:32KUrAdSrgo, quoting "a wealthy friend"]

> "If you went home tonight and in the next few days cleaned up a whole list of decisions, that might furnish enough inspiration for the next 10 years." [YT:32KUrAdSrgo; essay version: "If you sat down today and made a list of changes you've decided to make, that list could furnish enough inspiration for the next five or ten years." [LAIL]]

> "Whatever you do, don't camp at the fork in the road. Decide. It's far better to make a wrong decision than to not make one at all." [GM]

> "After you've decided, getting on with it is easier than deciding. Sometimes decision is the toughest part." [YT:32KUrAdSrgo]

## 3. DESIRE — wanting it badly enough

> "Desire. Wanting to bad enough. And I don't know how to tell you to want to. That's something you've got to come up with." [YT:32KUrAdSrgo]

Two things he knows about desire [YT:32KUrAdSrgo + GM]:
1. "It comes from inside, not outside. You don't send off for it."
2. "Desire can be triggered by something... Maybe it's a book, maybe it's a song, maybe it's a sermon, maybe it's a lecture, a seminar, maybe it's the conversation of a friend, a happening, an event." [essay version: "It may be a movie, a seminar, a sermon, a book, a confrontation with an enemy, or a conversation with a friend who finally levels with you." [LAIL]]

> "Welcome every human experience. You never know which one is going to turn it all on. Even the bad experiences. Sometimes from the bitterest experience comes the greatest awakening. So let down the barriers. Take down the walls. The same wall that keeps out disappointment keeps out happiness. Let life touch you. Don't let it kill you, but let it touch you." [YT:32KUrAdSrgo]

## 4. RESOLVE — "I will until."

> "Resolve says, 'I will.' Two of the most powerful words in the language. I will." [YT:32KUrAdSrgo; GM: "These two words are among the most potent in the English language."]

> Disraeli, as Rohn quotes him: "Nothing can resist a human will that will stake even its existence on the extent of its purpose." Shortly put: "I'll do it or die." [YT:32KUrAdSrgo + GM]

> "The world has a strange way of stepping aside when somebody says, 'I'll do it or die.'" [YT:32KUrAdSrgo]

The mountain: "The man says, 'I will climb the mountain. They've told me it's too high, it's too far, it's too rocky, it's too difficult, it's never been done before. But it's my mountain. I will climb it. Pretty soon you'll see me waving from the top or dead on the side cuz I ain't coming back.'" [YT:32KUrAdSrgo; print version in S60 #45]

The schoolgirl's definition (Foster City, CA): "I think it means promising yourself you will never give up." — "That's it. Webster, stand aside." [YT:32KUrAdSrgo; LAIL: "I think resolve means promising yourself you'll never give up."]

The baby test: "How long should a baby try to learn how to walk? How long would you give your average baby before you shut him off? ... Any mother in the world would say, 'You're crazy. My baby's going to keep trying until it learns how to walk.' What a magic formula." [YT:32KUrAdSrgo]

> "How long should you try? Until." [S60/S70]

## THE TRIGGER: ACTION

> "Now, let me show you what triggers all emotions into activity that brings results. And results is the name of the game. Here it is. Action. Finally, you must do something about how you feel." [YT:32KUrAdSrgo]

> "Affirmation without discipline is the beginning of delusion... However, affirmation with discipline can bring the most spectacular results." [YT:32KUrAdSrgo; also 5MP]

> "Make sure you always have a game plan to match your wishes. Otherwise, they will always be wishes." [YT:32KUrAdSrgo]

## The closing questions of the seminar [YT:32KUrAdSrgo]

Why? — Why not? — Why not you? — Why not now?
"Don't postpone your better future any longer... Set some new goals. Get you a new journal... And do it now."

## DIAGNOSTIC (coaching protocol)

Walk the four in order and find the missing one:
1. Disgust — has a line actually been crossed, or is this still tolerable? If tolerable, no change will hold.
2. Decision — is it specific and dated, or still "inner civil war"? Clean up the decision backlog.
3. Desire — is the pull stronger than the pushback? What trigger is being walled out?
4. Resolve — has "I'll try" been replaced with "I will until"? If not, no discipline survives week two.
Then assign the action — the game plan that matches the wish.
