## The frame — five key words [7S, Ch. 1]

FUNDAMENTALS, WEALTH, HAPPINESS, DISCIPLINE, SUCCESS.
> "SUCCESS IS NO MORE THAN THE NATURAL CONSEQUENCE OF CONSISTENTLY APPLYING THE FUNDAMENTALS OF SUCCESS TO LIFE." [7S]
> "Happiness is the skill of reacting to the offerings of life by perception and by enjoyment." [7S]
> "Discipline is the bridge between thought and accomplishment ... the glue that binds inspiration to achievement." [7S]
> "Success is making your life what you want it to be." [7S]

The origin [7S, Introduction]: age 25, six years working, "My weekly paycheck amounted to a grand total of fifty-seven dollars." Then: "Shortly after I turned twenty-five I met a man by the name of Earl Shoaff... For the next five years I learned many of life's lessons from Mr. Shoaff. He treated me like a son, spending hours teaching me his personal philosophy."

Full method in goal_setting_method.md. Anchors: "Reasons come first, answers second." · the four motivators (recognition, feeling of winning, family, benevolence) · "The major reason for setting goals is to compel you to become the person it takes to achieve them." [7S, Chs. 2–4]

## Strategy 2 — SEEK KNOWLEDGE
[7S, Ch. 5 "The Path to Wisdom"]:
> "ALL LEADERS ARE READERS." · "YOU ARE WHAT YOU READ." · "The book unread is the one that can't help you." · "Success leaves clues." · "Repetition is the mother of skill." · "Become self-educated. Standard education will bring you standard results." · "THERE CAN BE A GREAT DEAL OF DIFFERENCE BETWEEN COST AND VALUE."

The three ways to find out how things work, as spoken [YT:OyKIbaSk2s4]:
1. **Read.** "30 minutes a day. Read something positive, something challenging, something inspirational, something instructional... One book might save you five years." "Find out what poor people read and don't read it."
2. **Listen.** "Get around successful people and listen... Poor people ought to take rich people out to dinner." 
3. **Observe.** "Success leaves clues. Watch how the man shakes hands... There's two ways to see. One is called sight... The other one is called insight. See with your eyes, you'll see things. See with your mind, you'll see answers."
Plus the journal: "Don't use your head for a filing cabinet. Put it in your journal... Repetition, repetition, repetition." [YT:OyKIbaSk2s4]
> "Miss a meal if you have to, but don't miss a book." [S60/S70]
> "Successful people have libraries. The rest have big screen TVs." [S60/S70]

## Strategy 3 — LEARN HOW TO CHANGE
> "To have more than you've got, become more than you are." [JR concept page / SU]
> "I used to say, 'I sure hope things will change.' Then, I learned that the only way things are going to change for me is when I change." [S60]
> "Unless you change how you are, you will always have what you've got." [S70]
> "If you don't like how things are, change it! You're not a tree." [S60/S70/JR]
> "We generally change ourselves for one of two reasons: inspiration or desperation." [JR]
> Shoaff's version: "Well, I suggest you change... Everything. If you'll change, everything will change for you." [JR, "Meeting a Mentor"]
The three steps to life change [YT:OyKIbaSk2s4]: find out how things work → go to work ("start with the little disciplines... you can't handle the big challenges unless you take on the little ones"; "self-motivation... it's really the only kind there is") → don't try to beat the system ("find out how it works best and do it that way... don't compromise with right").

## Strategy 4 — CONTROL YOUR FINANCES — the 70/10/10/10
Official article wording [JR: "3 money habits separate rich poor"]:
> "After you pay your fair share of taxes, learn to live on 70 percent of your after-tax income."
> Charity: "Of the 30 percent not spent, one-third should go to charity." 
> Capital: "With the next 10 percent of your after-tax income, you're going to create wealth."
> Savings: "The last 10 percent should be put in savings."
> "Poor people spend their money and save what's left. Rich people save their money and spend what's left."

As spoken [YT:tSn5sEWEv_8] — the child-with-a-dollar teaching:
> "Never spend more than 70 cents." Then: 10¢ charity ("nothing teaches character better than generosity... the best time to start is when the amounts are small"), 10¢ **active capital** ("do something to make a profit... Two bicycles, one to ride and one to rent"; "Wages will make you a living. Profits will make you a fortune."), 10¢ **passive capital** ("compound interest... the borrower is servant to the lender. The lender is the power position.")
> Philosophy first: "Poor people usually spend their money and invest what's left. Rich people invest their money and spend what's left... What's most important is not the amount. What's really important is the philosophy." [YT:tSn5sEWEv_8]
> Financial independence defined: "the ability to live from the income of your own personal resources." [YT:tSn5sEWEv_8]
> Attitude riders: happy bill-payer ("reduce your liabilities and increase your assets"), happy taxpayer ("taxes is how we care and feed the goose that lays the golden eggs... Better a fat goose than no goose."). [YT:tSn5sEWEv_8]
> Shoaff's pay-yourself-first: "From now on, when you get paid, the first person you pay is you... Not if there's money left over. Not when you feel like it. First." [JR, "rich-vs-poor-mindset"]

## Strategy 6 — SURROUND YOURSELF WITH WINNERS (Principle of Association)
> "You are the average of the five people you spend the most time with." [S60 #8; jimrohn.com article variant: "You become the average of the five people you spend the most time with."]
> "Don't join an easy crowd; you won't grow. Go where the expectations and the demands to perform are high." [JR/S60/S70]
> "Spend more time with the right people. The wrong crowd will never take you to the right places." [JR]
> "Get around people who have something of value to share with you. Their impact will continue to have a significant effect on your life long after they have departed." [S70]
The three association moves [SU, "How to Deal With the Negative Influences in Your Life"]: "1. Disassociate." / "2. Limit the association." / "3. Expand your associations." — "your associations should move you forward, not impede your progress."
> "You cannot succeed by yourself. It's hard to find a rich hermit." [S70]

The exercise: list the 5 people you spend most time with; score each on ambition, discipline, standards; the average is your ceiling unless you change the inputs. (Application frame — not a Rohn quote.)

## Strategy 7 — LEARN THE ART OF LIVING WELL
> "The good life comes from a lifestyle that is so fully developed—regardless of your bank account—that it provides you with a constant sense of joy in living." [LAIL]
> "Learn how to be happy with what you have while you pursue all that you want." [S60]
> "Happiness is not something you postpone for the future; it is something you design for the present." [S60/JR-17]
> "Life is not just the passing of time. Life is the collection of experiences and their intensity." [S60]
> "Lifestyle is learning to be wherever you are." / "Wherever you are, be there." [GM; also spoken YT:OyKIbaSk2s4]
> The four "ifs" [LAIL]: "life is worthwhile if you learn... if you try... if you stay... if you care."
