## 1. Core positions (ranked by mentions × specificity; 2026 first)

1. **Creative diversity, not creative volume, is what Meta rewards after Andromeda.** [GEN] (≈22 mentions) Meta asks "which version of this ad should I show to this person", not who to show one ad to; near-identical ads get a hidden similarity score, are grouped as the same asset, and limit reach ("fish from the same pond"). Hook swaps and headline swaps "simply don't count as creative testing in Meta's eyes anymore". Diversity = format diversity + messaging diversity + persona diversity. "Andromeda doesn't reward more ads." It "rewards intentional variety"; "stop testing incremental small changes and start doing big swings". — [YT:CPWoW09WCCo] (2026-02), [YT:_BuCO3Jf-K0] (2026-02), [YT:QoGFOGEqijw] (2026-05), [YT:RSa4wUuw_2k] (2026-07)
2. **Better beats more; volume without intent burns budget.** [GEN] (≈14 mentions) Brands with low win rates launch 40, 50, 100 ads a month with no strategy; the best make fewer ads, each with a known persona, message and format: "better pretty much always beats more". Making more ads when nothing works is "a more expensive version of the same problem". "If volume was the answer, I would be upselling all of my clients." Gurus push volume because they charge per ad. Caveat he grants: the volume play "does work to a degree", and the "perfect mix is creative diversity and creative volume". — [YT:qZhOnITrN0Q] (2026-05), [YT:PuQ2TvXrWg4] (2026-04), [YT:Lkx7nOGIslw] (2026-07), [YT:QoGFOGEqijw] (2026-05)
3. **Know why a winner wins before you copy or replicate it.** [GEN] (≈10 mentions) Most brands have a winner they don't understand and copy it (same script, format, creator), "possibly the most expensive mistake". Separate the variables (hook, angle, format, messaging, persona), read hook rate, hold rate, CTR, CPA and the comments, and look at the "subliminal stuff" (creator look, vibe). "...don't understand why something works, then you're never going to be able to scale it." — [YT:qZhOnITrN0Q] (2026-05), [YT:PuQ2TvXrWg4] (2026-04), [YT:Lkx7nOGIslw] (2026-07), [YT:QKRd7FTZ7vM] (2026-05)
4. **Creative is the targeting.** [GEN] (≈9 mentions) Meta's AI views every video and static (transcript and visuals) before it goes to a test pool; the creative tells it who to reach. "Broad targeting with a very specific creative outcome will always do better than interest stacking." "The hook is your targeting." [quote src: YT:IX-2-uSJDk8] Don't build campaigns per awareness level: "we let Meta decide". "You don't know who your next customer is, but Meta does." [quote src: YT:RSa4wUuw_2k] — [YT:Lkx7nOGIslw] (2026-07), [YT:nUTzR9ilTts] (2026-08), [YT:RExPkxyRB6o] (2026-10), [YT:FWWp6NcVo3Q] (2025-11)
5. **Don't kill ads early; every new ad is a learning first, a winner second.** [GEN] (≈12 mentions) "One bad week does not mean a bad ad." Killing early means "paying for the learning cycle" then discarding the data, and Meta has to relearn who the ad is for. Some ads dip and come "back through the roof". Info-gathering ads look like wasted budget to the untrained eye. $1M brands kill at the first CPM/CPA rise; $10M brands build evergreen ads. Turn off only after months of underperformance. — [YT:Lkx7nOGIslw] (2026-07), [YT:qZhOnITrN0Q] (2026-05), [YT:QdNIajKiC2s] (2026-04), [YT:oZFiSUkJEPE] (2026-03)
6. **CPA is the number one metric; ROAS and hook rate are secondary.** [DTC/GEN] (≈10 mentions) "ROAS is a completely pointless metric"; set a target CPA from LTV/AOV and "Every ad that doesn't make that is a losing ad." Hook and hold rate are "soft metrics"; hook rate is relative to the account average, and "Sometimes a 20% hook can perform better than a 70% hook". Sort by spend, never by thumb-stop. — [YT:Lkx7nOGIslw] (2026-07), [YT:QoGFOGEqijw] (2026-05), [YT:IX-2-uSJDk8] (2026-06), [YT:RSa4wUuw_2k] (2026-07)

## 2. Frameworks and methods

### A. Testing and reading data

**Account audit (first step for every new client)** [DTC] — when: before any research or brief, because most brands have data they don't use; "there's no point doing it if it's already been done". Steps: (1) open a creative-focused tool (Motion; alternatives Atria or Ads Manager custom columns); (2) sort by spend, since spend is "Meta's indicator that this is a winning ad"; ignore high thumb-stop ads with tiny spend (his example: 55% thumb stop on ~$5); (3) watch the top ~5 ads and form a thesis for each; (4) note commonalities (personas, formats, hook rates); (5) check retention, placement and age/gender breakdowns (e.g. drop at ~8 s means something there turns people off); (6) make no media-buying changes: "You're simply observing". His example notes: "most popular format is a UGC testimonial"; high production led to very high CTRs; product shown inside a car "leads to the highest hook rate". — [YT:RSa4wUuw_2k] (2026-07)

**Post-launch reading order** [DTC] — CPA first (profitable?) → CTR (clicking? "if they're not, then they're a loser") → hook rate (grabbing the right people?) → hold rate (entertained through the video?). For statics (no hook/hold rate): CPA, CTR, CPC. — [YT:RSa4wUuw_2k] (2026-07), [YT:HXox1I6QQvQ] (2025-08)

**Dashboard columns he keeps visible** [GEN] — spend ("number one"), purchases, CPA, CPM, ROAS, thumb-stop ratio, Motion hook/watch/convert/click scores, 3-second retention ratio, average play time, outbound CTR, cost per outbound click, landing page views, engagement, engagement rate, hold rate, click-to-purchase ratio. Earlier, shorter list: ROAS, CPA, CTR, frequency (high = fatiguing; low = maybe wrong people), conversion rate. — [YT:RSa4wUuw_2k] (2026-07), [YT:tVXjXJSEjeA] (2025-07)

**Diagnosis rules (metric pattern → next move)** [GEN]
- Low hook rate, strong hold and CTR → relaunch with a new hook; "like a flywheel" feed learnings back. — [YT:RSa4wUuw_2k] (2026-07), [YT:-q2lqfNysUs] (2025-11)
- Terrible CTR → add a better CTA. 50%+ hook rate with poor CTR/click-to-purchase (Black Stuff) = strong education, weak selling → keep hook idea, stronger CTA. — [YT:-q2lqfNysUs] (2025-11), [YT:qZhOnITrN0Q] (2026-05)
- Great hook rate, very low hold rate → hook is irrelevant to the customer. — [YT:KKp3rA0QK9A] (2026-01)
- Podcast ads with great hold but poor hook → check conversion rate to see if hold brings customers. — [YT:RExPkxyRB6o] (2026-10)
- A batch of high-production ads all flop → production style or messaging? Rerun the same messaging as UGC; if it does better, production was the problem. "The testing is essentially like a science experiment". — [YT:-q2lqfNysUs] (2025-11)
- Problem-solution UGC with "three reasons why I love" hook has low hook rate → swap hook; still lagging → move script to another format (e.g. voiceover with no visible creator). — [YT:_FMXAlfgYQo] (2026-01)
- Statics work but video doesn't → usually another top-of-funnel source (Google Ads) is sending warm people who convert on statics; it's a top-of-funnel video problem; "testing formats is now the same as testing angles". — [YT:_BuCO3Jf-K0] (2026-02)
- Affordable CPA + great hook + great hold = the message is the driver → port the message to other formats (Underbrush "history lesson" won "again and again"). — [YT:PuQ2TvXrWg4] (2026-04)

**One-variable variations ("like a science experiment")** [GEN] — every ad gets variations with one thing changed: video = hook; static = headline/copy. He refused an AI-suggested new concept as the A/B because "we're potentially changing too much". If the new-copy version wins, "it was a copy problem and not a format problem." Standard variations: heavily captioned vs raw UGC (no captions); founder ads with several hooks, different location, selfie handheld vs seated "more official", with vs without B-roll; with vs without social proof. — [YT:HXox1I6QQvQ] (2025-08), [YT:jv5ECAtsrHc] (2024-12), [YT:Ry7KQFZaPGA] (2025-12), [YT:2BrGawWj1aI] (2025-06)

**Ad systems "like Lego"** [GEN] — break each ad into trackable, swappable blocks: angle/concept/idea; format; hook, script, pacing, avatar; structure. Test results then tell you which block to change. — [YT:_FMXAlfgYQo] (2026-01)

**Rolling batches / creative engine** [GEN] — launch a batch, prepare the next while it runs, adjust new concepts to the previous batch's data. Phases: import [as captioned] → produce → test → feedback → expansion (where else can the idea go). Monthly: double down on a winning format; if a format fails two months running, stop it but salvage its ideas in another format: "You're reusing ideas. You're not thinking from the ground up every single time." — [YT:_FMXAlfgYQo] (2026-01)

**Five reasons ads don't win** [GEN] — (1) you don't know why your last ad won; (2) you make more instead of better; (3) you kill ads before they can become winners; (4) no diversity in the account; (5) your gut runs the show instead of systems. Fixing them moves a 5% hit rate toward 30%: "it's never talent, it's almost always processes." — [YT:qZhOnITrN0Q] (2026-05)

**Testing vs scaling campaign structure** [GEN] — testing campaign's one job: give new assets enough spend to read. Anything showing signs of winning moves to a scaling campaign where winners share budget. Never launch new ads into a CBO with a legacy ad that has millions in spend; never Flexible ads "because you can't track what's winning and why". Set minimum spend so you learn why even bad ads are bad. — [YT:QoGFOGEqijw] (2026-05), [YT:RSa4wUuw_2k] (2026-07), [YT:RExPkxyRB6o] (2026-10)

**Launch cadence by monthly spend** [DTC] (all [YT:DNI5NI2-lSk] (2026-08), "net new" = new concepts, variations/iterations on top)
| Spend/month | Net new | What else |
|---|---|---|
| 10–20k | 3–4 a month; "one net new every week is probably going to be enough" [quote src: YT:DNI5NI2-lSk] | slower fatigue, focus on finding winning messaging and angle |
| 20–50k | ~2 a week, 5–6 a month, more toward 50k | "The focus here is quality. It is never volume" [quote src: YT:DNI5NI2-lSk]; add educational TOF (VSLs, founder ads) |
| 50–70k | upper end of 10–15 concepts | "a pivotal moment" [quote src: YT:DNI5NI2-lSk]; winners last ~4–6 weeks; need production cadence; mix long, short, carousels, founder |
| 70–100k (Fraggle minimum) | upper end of 20 concepts | "Creative is your main lever here to scale" [quote src: YT:DNI5NI2-lSk]; statics, carousels, founder, problem-solution, UGC, raw UGC, high production; persona and message diversity |
| 100–250k | ~30 as a very minimum | add EGC; internal team + agency; "Volume does actually compound here" [quote src: YT:DNI5NI2-lSk]; "quality matters more than ever" [quote src: YT:DNI5NI2-lSk] |
| 250–500k+ | 50+, minimum high 40s | creative ops systems for briefing/shooting/editing/testing; rapid hook/creator iterations; reuse winning scripts |
Small budgets: ~$50/day → one or two brand-new ads a week; no point loading 50 new ads at $100/day. At every level, overloading means none get spend and you wrongly conclude ideas failed. — [YT:_BuCO3Jf-K0] (2026-02), [YT:tVXjXJSEjeA] (2025-07)

**Static-first idea testing and spreading** [GEN/DTC] — test a message/offer as a static (no building, exporting, QA-ing video). Fails → ask messaging or imagery, test those. Wins → "what other directions can we take this?" (yapper, podcast, more statics). Post-Andromeda static variations: one key message (e.g. the group-chat idea) built into differently designed statics (e.g. a call-out ad), not headline swaps on one static. — [YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ] (2026-09), [YT:_BuCO3Jf-K0] (2026-02)

**Creative diversity as "pots of customers"** [DTC] — a UGC video for one segment, a podcast-style ad to educate before commitment, a founder piece for trust. 2024 version (snack brand): angles "hungry snackers", on-the-go quick snack, protein, each paired with different media types (statics, UGC, GIFs, production). — [YT:QoGFOGEqijw] (2026-05), [YT:VCnx3v_gZQI] (2024-11)

**Single offer focus** [DTC] — "If you have five different entry offers" / "are you really testing anything, or are you just giving spend to everything?" Push the best offer only for a quarter, then judge. — [YT:PuQ2TvXrWg4] (2026-04)

**Winner proxy for competitor research** [GEN] — in Foreplay/ad library, run time = winner signal: ads live 51, 56, 134 days likely winners; one off after 11 days "clearly wasn't a winner". — [YT:NNg9-TDiFDA] (2024-03), [YT:W1PADr1kjGk] (2025-01)
