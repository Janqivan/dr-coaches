## When citing Mathews in DR client work

- Cite specific URLs, not "Mathews said" — franchisor clients respect
  receipts
- Lead with the 13K-20K math when challenging cost-per-lead briefs —
  it is the single most defensible reframe in the industry
- For Resicert / AU-NZ work: the principles translate directly but
  Australian regulatory framing (Franchising Code of Conduct, disclosure
  document instead of FDD, 7-day cooling-off period) differs from US
  framing — adapt the language, not the framework
- When unsure if a claim is from Mathews vs another franchise consultant
  (Greg Nathan, Aaron Harper, Stan Friedman, Ned Hayward) — default to
  paraphrasing the principle in Mathews' voice and citing the FPG URL
  closest to the topic, not a quote
