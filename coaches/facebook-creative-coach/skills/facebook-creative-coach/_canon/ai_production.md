## 2. Frameworks and workflows (named models, step sequences)

### Deep research meta-prompt — let the LLM write the research prompt (Cottrell)
- When: every new client; re-run every couple of months; not the only research.
- Steps: 1. Ask Claude/ChatGPT "write me a prompt for deep research" [quote src: YT:HXox1I6QQvQ] about the client's customers: "Think through every possible reason why someone would or wouldn't buy" [quote src: YT:RSa4wUuw_2k], who to target, pains, "how do they feel after their purchase" [quote src: YT:HXox1I6QQvQ], client name + URL, "we are a creative performance agency creating ads for [brand]" [quote src: YT:HXox1I6QQvQ]. 2. Paste into a new chat with Deep Research on (2026: Gemini — "best when it comes to research" [quote src: YT:RSa4wUuw_2k]); answer qualifying questions (he chose US, DTC only; whole history; TikTok/IG/Reddit). 3. Wait ~15–40 min; download (an 18-page doc). 4. Store in the client's Claude project. 5. Sanity-check: "use your project knowledge to tell me what you know about Leaf Shave" [quote src: YT:EqhboKbkESQ] → should return "a sustainable razor brand" [quote src: YT:EqhboKbkESQ].
- Related: Cooper — "deep research does 50% of a strategist work today" [quote src: YT:c_6XsMe9eXE], cuts 60–70% of research, $200/mo tier worth it. Slagel (guest): GPT-4.5 writes the prompt; ~8 min, 22 sources. Denney does NOT use Claude for first-pass research, by choice.
- Sources: [YT:RSa4wUuw_2k] (2026-07), [YT:EqhboKbkESQ] (2025-05), [YT:HXox1I6QQvQ] (2025-08), [YT:c_6XsMe9eXE] (2025-03)

### Review mining / VOC extraction (Denney, Hott, Cooper, Cottrell, Levinger)
- When: before any scripting; feeds the brand project.
- Steps (merged): 1. Export reviews (Cottrell queried a 20,000-review export in Claude instead of Cmd+F). 2. Denney's four pulls: pain points, transformations, failed attempts ("twist the knife" [quote src: YT:k533N5RxXPY]), "golden nuggets" (the phrase alone finds the best lines); 2026 set adds strengths/weaknesses, complaints/objections, reputation analysis, trigger points to buy. 3. Hott: what are the 5-10 biggest pain points ranked by popularity; find me past tense I statements; positive reviews → content illustrating them, negative → content combating them. 4. Cooper: "look at the failed solutions that our customers had and then rank them by popularity" [quote src: YT:SmNpVtfIRHw] (for us-vs-them); headline pull "should use the customer language" [quote src: YT:c_6XsMe9eXE]; emotive-language pass (ED brand → humiliated, inadequate, broken). 5. Levinger: "tell me what the subtext is" [quote src: YT:LxWwRykBtfc]; "Who's in here? What avatars do you see?" [quote src: YT:pV0tM1gSJiU]; force claims into OBSERVED / INFERRED / UNSUPPORTED (delete unsupported). 6. Build a persona deck, review it as a team (Denney).
- Example: Cottrell — reviews revealed five or six use cases the client "never even knew" [quote src: YT:gP-68LmgAQ4].
- Sources: [YT:pMAxIOL8dtE] (2023-12), [YT:yRgPbqywUJ8] (2026-07), [YT:wfENnO7PA5Q] (2025-02), [YT:in1l2TPFc1c] (2026-09), [YT:A_e_9v_Sy0I] (2025-11)

### Prompt structure (Cooper, Slagel, Heath)
- Cooper: task + parameters + good AND bad output examples, each with why; stack ~10 good performers chosen for different reasons (AI weights good examples more); "shout at the model" [quote src: YT:c_6XsMe9eXE] in caps for critical items; run prompts several times (3 tabs); Text Expander line "tell me if there's any other information that you need to know" [quote src: YT:MAwkpQWEQS8]; stakes hacks ("was a four out of ten. You need a ten" [quote src: YT:ITM4faRVzF8]); add a "confidence check" (pure VOC vs inference-heavy).
- Slagel (guest) checklist: 1. say exactly what you want; 2. examples liked/disliked and why; 3. "context dumping" (paste internal doc or transcript).
- Heath lead-gen prompt: who you are + what you sell and what's included → sales process/next step ("book a call with us using the calendar on our website" [quote src: YT:M2VReAjHiN8]) so CTAs fit → audience (warm+cold, small business owners who know Meta ads but aren't experts) → create a list of 10 meta ad creative ideas that are highly engaging, including multiple hooks for each. [quote src: YT:M2VReAjHiN8] LEADGEN.
- Lucas Hamby (guest): split script-bot context into TOF (unaware/problem aware) and mid–bottom docs plus one per creative type; 50-page docs degrade output.
