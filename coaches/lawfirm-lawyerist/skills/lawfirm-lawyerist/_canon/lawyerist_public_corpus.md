## 7. RELATED CORPUS (frequently cited alongside Lawyerist)

These authors and frameworks come up repeatedly on the podcast and in
Lab coaching. Useful cross-references for the coach to cite:

- **Jack Newton (Clio CEO)** — *The Client-Centered Law Firm: How to
  Succeed in an Experience-Driven World* — https://www.clio.com/resources/client-centered-law-firm/

  Entrepreneur / Manager / Technician frame
- **Gino Wickman** — *Traction* / EOS — many Lab firms run on EOS
- **Greg Crabtree** — *Simple Numbers* — services finance, owner pay,
  labour efficiency ratio

  operations
- **Geoff Smart / Brad Smart** — *Who* / Topgrading — hiring discipline
- **Donald Miller** — *Building a StoryBrand* — many firms use SB7 for
  positioning
