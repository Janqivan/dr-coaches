## 7. RECOMMENDED PURCHASES

In recommended order of value-per-dollar for someone new to Sheridan.

### Tier 1 — Buy the books (~$20-30 each)

1. **Endless Customers: A Proven System to Build Trust, Drive Sales,
   and Become the Market Leader** (2025) — the current, AI-era
   update. Buy this FIRST if you only buy one.
   - Amazon US: https://www.amazon.com/Endless-Customers-Proven-Model-Business/dp/1394282788
   - Order hub: https://www.endlesscustomers.com/
   - Typical pricing: ~$25-30 hardcover, ~$15 Kindle.

2. **They Ask, You Answer: A Revolutionary Approach to Inbound Sales,
   Content Marketing, and Today's Digital Consumer** (2017, revised
   2019) — the original. Still the clearest single book on the
   methodology. Get the revised 2019 edition.
   - Amazon US: https://www.amazon.com/They-Ask-You-Answer-Revolutionary/dp/1119312973
   - Typical pricing: ~$20-25 hardcover, ~$12-15 Kindle.

Note on prices: these fluctuate. Check Amazon directly. The number
above is for orientation, not a quote.

### Tier 2 — IMPACT+ Membership (~$99/mo at typical pricing)

On-demand learning platform with the courses IMPACT uses for paid
client implementations, plus live coaching sessions with Marcus and
the IMPACT coaches. The best choice for an internal content manager
or marketing director who is going to run the program.

- https://www.impactplus.com/what-is-impact-plus
- Current pricing: https://www.impactplus.com/endless-customers-coaching/pricing
  (Tiers and prices change; check the page.)

### Tier 3 — Endless Customers Coaching Program (mid-five-figures+/yr)

For companies past the "we read the book, what now" stage. IMPACT
assigns a coach who works with the leadership team and content
manager to implement Endless Customers / TAYA over 6-12 months.

- https://www.impactplus.com/endless-customers-coaching/pricing
- Speak with IMPACT sales — pricing is custom.

### Tier 4 — Endless Customers Live (the flagship event)

Twice a year. Two days. Marcus keynotes. IMPACT coaching team
delivers workshops. Other practitioner companies share what's
working. The single best venue to meet implementation peers.

- Event info via IMPACT: https://www.impactplus.com/

### Tier 5 — Hire Marcus to Keynote (~$15K-$30K)

Available for corporate keynotes globally. Marcus does roughly 70+
keynotes a year — sales kickoffs, industry conferences, leadership
offsites. Expect to book 4-9 months out.

- Marcus's booking page: https://marcussheridan.com/price-for-hiring-a-speaker/
- Speakers bureau listings (for reference; book direct when possible):
  - All American Speakers: https://www.allamericanspeakers.com/speakers/439863/Marcus-Sheridan
  - APB Speakers: https://www.apbspeakers.com/speaker/marcus-sheridan/
  - GDA Speakers: https://www.gdaspeakers.com/speaker/marcus-sheridan/

Typical fee ranges quoted publicly (subject to change, confirm with
his team):
- Live keynote: $20K-$30K
- Virtual keynote: $10K-$20K
- Workshop / multi-day: custom

### Tier 6 — Custom IMPACT Agency Services

IMPACT also offers full agency implementation (strategy, web, video,
HubSpot ops) for companies that want a partner rather than a coach.
This is the highest-touch and highest-cost option.

- https://marcussheridan.com/agency-services/
- https://www.impactplus.com/
