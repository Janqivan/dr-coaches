# THE LAW OF SOWING AND REAPING

Sources: *The Seasons of Life* [SOL]; official discipline article [JR: "getting-discipline"]; [S70]; seminar material [YT]. Rohn's frame is biblical-agricultural — he calls it the major law to understand.

> "If you sow well, you will reap well. Life is full of laws that both govern and explain behaviors, but the law of sowing and reaping may well be the major law we need to understand." [JR]

## The three principles

**1. You reap what you sow.**
Whatever you plant — time, effort, study, relationships, money — that kind comes back. Different seed, different fruit.
> "It is the promise of spring that as we sow, so shall we also reap." [SOL]
> "If you don't sow, you don't reap. You don't even have a chance." [S70]
> "Everyone has to get good at one of two things: planting in the spring or begging in the fall." [SOL]

**2. You reap MORE than you sow.**
The compound principle. One seed of corn produces a hundred.
> "Faith further provides to us an irrevocable law decreed in heaven which assures that for every disciplined human effort we will receive a multiple reward." [SOL]
> "For every disciplined effort, there is a multiple reward." [S70; official article variant: "For every disciplined effort, there are multiple rewards. That's one of life's great arrangements." [JR]]
The dark side compounds too: "Spend five minutes complaining, and you have wasted five, and you may have begun what's known as economic cancer of the bone." [YT:0sG31mTbHJU] And: "A week of neglect could cost you a year of repair." [YT:0sG31mTbHJU]

**3. You reap in a DIFFERENT season than you sow.**
Spring sowing → fall harvest. Most operators quit between sowing and reaping because the calendar doesn't deliver fast enough.
> "You cannot change your destination overnight, but you can change your direction overnight." [S60/S70/JR]
> "Some people plant in the spring and leave in the summer. If you're signed up for a season, see it through." [S70]
> The mechanism of delayed compounding, as spoken: "If you repeat it, go over it, sure enough, someday, some mysterious day, the idea takes root, starts to grow, and shows up in your bank account and your dress and your personality and your lifestyle." [YT:OyKIbaSk2s4]
> "You need to be making measurable progress in reasonable time." [LAIL] — the check against using "different season" as an excuse for no results forever.

## The input side — guard what gets planted in you

> Shoaff: "Every day stand guard at the door of your mind." [YT:0sG31mTbHJU / l4Sc2QwQ-nc]
> The mental factory: "Whatever you think about all day long pours ingredients into this mental factory. And that's what builds the economic, social, financial fabric of your life... You might as well try making a cake with cement." [YT:0sG31mTbHJU]
> The coffee lesson (Danbury, CT high school): "Life is both sugar and strychnine. You got to be careful... It doesn't matter who hands you the bad stuff... Watch your coffee." [YT:l4Sc2QwQ-nc]

## THE FRIDAY WEEKLY REVIEW (application frame for DR)

Four questions, 10 minutes:
1. **"What did you sow this week?"** — three categories: into the business (pipeline, content, systems), into yourself (reading, skills), into people (team, clients, family).
2. **"What did you reap this week?"** — what came in THIS week from PAST sowing. Name the season-lag explicitly: which past planting paid.
3. **"What's the standard you raised or let slip?"** — one of each, honestly.
4. **"What's the one discipline for next week?"** — one, not five. "Just a few daily disciplines makes a great deal of difference." [SU]

Guard rule for the review: no complaining allowed inside it — "Spend five minutes complaining, and you have wasted five." [YT:0sG31mTbHJU]
