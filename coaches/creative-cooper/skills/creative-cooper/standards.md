## T1. Persona work and persona-gap audit (persona doc, persona table, "why won't this account scale")

- ★ **Personas come from customer voice, not from the strategist's head.** Each persona is backed by quoted evidence (his bar: ~10 supporting reviews/comments/intake notes per persona), not made up "out of like thin air". Count the quotes per persona. [YT:vD8eLrjAxEM] [YT:79doYBhLLFU]
- ★ **Every persona passes the four-question test.** The doc answers, per persona: what they already believe about the problem; what they have tried and what failed; what they fear and secretly want; the exact words they use to themselves. Any one blank = "I do not believe it to be a persona" = FAIL for that persona. [YT:79doYBhLLFU]
- ★ **Gap audit is done in numbers.** A table with each persona's share of customer voice vs share of ads vs share of spend (his example: 30% of voice, 18% of spend; 50% of reviews, 10% of spend). The biggest gap is named and gets the next batch. [YT:79doYBhLLFU]
- **3 to 5 core personas.** Fewer than 3 or more than 5 = FAIL; ranked primary / secondary / expansion. [YT:79doYBhLLFU]
- **Not a usage occasion dressed as a persona.** "Gifter"-type segments that apply across personas are rejected as core personas. [YT:79doYBhLLFU]
- **Ranked on four factors, not on what's spending today.** Score /10 on revenue, LTV, CAC, strategic priority, averaged; the table shows the scores. [YT:79doYBhLLFU]
- **Allocation is explicit.** The next sprint names e.g. three personas and a split like 45 / 40 / 15, with every planned ad tagged by persona. [YT:79doYBhLLFU]
- **Gap is explained, not just flagged.** For the biggest gap the audit asks which of three: too few ads, ads made but failed, or super fans who over-review. [YT:79doYBhLLFU]
- **Awareness level stated per persona** (unaware → most aware), inferred from the VOC. [YT:79doYBhLLFU] [YT:c_6XsMe9eXE]
- **Pre-purchase voice included**, not only post-purchase. For Ivan: ad comments, inquiry forms and consult-call notes from people who didn't buy, not only signed clients. [YT:79doYBhLLFU]
- **Refresh cadence set.** Who re-runs the VOC-vs-spend report, how often (weekly/monthly/quarterly). [YT:79doYBhLLFU]

## T2. Research and voice-of-customer mining (headlines, hooks, trigger events, insider phrases)

- ★ **Nothing invented.** Every candidate line traces to a named source row (review, comment, survey answer, call transcript, Reddit thread). "You don't have to lie. You don't have to make up stories." [YT:9O3-AdYpwyQ] [YT:vD8eLrjAxEM]
- ★ **Longest and most emotive first.** The method shows the sort (by length/emotion) before the pick; short generic praise ("love it", "great service") is not in the output. [YT:9O3-AdYpwyQ] [YT:Ugd7a2sPgZk] [YT:SmNpVtfIRHw]
- ★ **Trigger events are moments, not benefits.** Each one names "the moment a problem stops being tolerable" as a specific scene (who, what happened, where), in the customer's words. A trigger that reads like a feature or outcome = FAIL. [YT:9O3-AdYpwyQ]
- **Insider language captured.** At least some lines use slang/shorthand only the persona would use, the kind a copywriter won't come up with sitting at a desk. [YT:Oj-KGCw7K1g] [YT:vD8eLrjAxEM]
- **Cold-stranger test applied to every headline candidate.** Would it make sense to someone who doesn't know or care about the brand? His bad example: "I'm super picky and these guys hit it out the park" fails; "the only shampoo that ended my dandruff battle" passes. [YT:c_6XsMe9eXE]
- **Lines kept verbatim** (even slightly ungrammatical), not polished into brand voice. [YT:XQwJRuN8p-Q] [YT:c_6XsMe9eXE]
- **Tagged by persona.** Every line is filed under the persona it came from. [YT:79doYBhLLFU]
- **Multiple sources in the stack.** Not reviews alone: ad comments, surveys/intake, support/tickets, Reddit/forums, competitor reviews; with what each tool can and can't reach stated. [YT:79doYBhLLFU] [YT:3wLk3D99JyA]
- **Mined lines are drafts for Ivan's approval**, labeled as such. [canon: SKILL.md, Ivan context] (not found in transcripts)

## T3. Concept batch ("what should we make next")

## T4. Static ads (brief, headline set, AI static batch)

- ★ **"Assume no one knows or cares."** The headline tells a cold stranger what it is, who it's for and why it matters; no brand-name-dependent or warm-only lines ("Love these.", "Always in my car"). Exception only for a stated BOF/remarketing static. [YT:XQwJRuN8p-Q] [YT:SmNpVtfIRHw]
- ★ **One dominant headline; hierarchy is hook → supporting statement → product/offer → social proof.** The headline is the biggest element (his winners: ~25–40% of the frame); social proof is smallest and never first. Name the read order. [YT:XQwJRuN8p-Q]
- ★ **Clarity or curiosity, picked.** The brief states where the selling happens: on the ad (clear TOF static) or after the click (curiosity/native). A half-clear, half-teasing static = "stuck in no man's land" = FAIL. [YT:XQwJRuN8p-Q]
- ★ **Proven format.** One of his 12 (headline + benefits, testimonial, offer, persona-based, collection, comparison, publication, press, native, apology, post-it, meme) or a named swipe with Ad Library staying power; no "too cute or too clever" invented format. [YT:XQwJRuN8p-Q] [YT:SmNpVtfIRHw]
- **Headline uses customer words** (from T2), not "fancy words" ("fancy words don't sell, clarity does"). [YT:SmNpVtfIRHw] [YT:XQwJRuN8p-Q]
- **Image and headline speak to the same persona and claim.** Each headline variant gets an image that matches it; no single image with 4 unrelated headlines. "Congruency is king." [YT:SmNpVtfIRHw]
- **Persona statics call the persona out by name**, Hormozi-style ("If you own a [business] doing $1M+ a year…"), one per persona. [YT:GdYGUirdZYQ] [YT:XQwJRuN8p-Q]
- **Static brief has the four parts:** reference, assets, instruction, and what changes for variations B, C, D. [YT:EOZp34uDIc4]
- **AI statics: every claim and number checked by a human** before launch (his Nano Banana output invented a "7600" figure). [YT:ibzfKRRSHhU]
- **"Make the invisible visible" and atypical-text ideas are offered only in literal form for Ivan** (his literal-visuals rule outranks the canon's metaphor imagery). [YT:XQwJRuN8p-Q] [canon: SKILL.md, Ivan context]
- **TOF and BOF statics both present when the batch is account-level**; offer statics don't crowd out acquisition statics. [YT:XQwJRuN8p-Q] [YT:KSm0pvevV9k]

## T5. Video briefs and scripts (yapper, founder, DR script + VO, wall-of-text, AI animation)

- ★ **The spectrum end is declared and held.** Ugly/yapper: handheld or phone propped, believable location, no soundtrack/SFX/emojis, no word-by-word captions ("A real organic creator is not going to caption every word"). DR/obvious ad: great copy, exaggerated visuals, no pretense of organic. Any tell from the other end = FAIL. [YT:OzkRU3Wx_1w] [YT:Ugd7a2sPgZk]
- ★ **First frame specified.** Who is in it, where, camera position (handheld low/eye-level vs tripod vs phone set on counter), what's in hand; decided "before you open your mouth as a creator". [YT:OzkRU3Wx_1w] [YT:mpj0A4Prxu4]
- ★ **Yapper briefs are not scripts.** Structure = overall instructions, recommended order of talking points, must-get shots, visual notes, key value props, scripted hook only. "Make sure you cover all the talking points, but do not script it for them." [YT:OzkRU3Wx_1w] [YT:GdYGUirdZYQ]
- **Realism test passes.** Would this person really film this here, this way? ("would you set the camera up on a tripod in your kitchen"). Name the answer. [YT:OzkRU3Wx_1w]
- **Direction matches creator quality.** Loose brief only for a proven yapper; bottom 80–90% of creators get more direction/script. The brief names which. [YT:KSm0pvevV9k] [YT:OzkRU3Wx_1w]
- **Full scripts pick the framework by awareness:** unaware/problem-aware → PAS or AIDA (agitate); solution/product-aware → FAB. Stated at the top. [YT:3wLk3D99JyA] (ALLTIME)
- **Built from mined one-liners, not "write me a script".** At least 2 lines in the script are traceable customer phrases. [YT:vD8eLrjAxEM]
- **Doesn't cram.** Covers a subset of value props and pains (his jeans script: 4 of 5 props, 2 of 4 pains). [YT:9ikhnQi2ghE] (ALLTIME)
- **Trigger-event / AI animation scripts follow the five beats:** trigger event → repeat incidents → discovery → mechanism → payoff; trigger taken from real VOC. [YT:9O3-AdYpwyQ]
- **AI production locked in order:** script and stills locked before animation; first image sets "the world" and is reused as reference. [YT:A29szOW7YDE]
- **No first-person claims from an AI avatar or AI character; AI labeled.** "I lost 30 lbs" from an avatar = FAIL. For Ivan's verticals this also covers fake client stories. [YT:_9f0rTKKIOY] [YT:Be9YLcOory4]
- **Lead-gen mandatories:** legal/compliance flags listed (no outcome guarantees for immigration cases unless Ivan supplies approved wording). [canon: SKILL.md, Response mode 3] (not found in transcripts)

## T6. Teardown (own draft, live ad, or competitor ad)

- ★ **First-frame read comes first.** States staged or real, which persona it signals, and whether it makes him think "Yeah, that makes sense". [YT:OzkRU3Wx_1w]
- ★ **Spectrum verdict.** Names which end it is aiming for and every tell that leaks it into the middle (tripod, soundtrack, emoji callouts, captions on every word, a setting nobody would talk in). [YT:OzkRU3Wx_1w] [YT:Ugd7a2sPgZk]
- ★ **No winner call on thin spend; spend is the verdict.** Performance read uses share of spend and cost per result; for competitors, Ad Library impressions rank and staying power (e.g. #1 for days, top 5 for 1.5 weeks), with the proxy caveat stated. [YT:aD7vKl1nkcE] [YT:GdYGUirdZYQ] [YT:Ugd7a2sPgZk]
- **Hook/hold rates used only as diagnostics** ("Stop glorifying hook rates and hold rates"); a hook-swap recommendation explains who the new first frame signals, since a different person makes "a completely different ad". [YT:Be9YLcOory4] (ALLTIME) [YT:_9f0rTKKIOY]
- **Cold-stranger test on the headline/hook.** [YT:XQwJRuN8p-Q]
- **Static hierarchy checked** (hook → supporting → product → proof). [YT:XQwJRuN8p-Q]
- **Congruency checked** ad → landing page (persona, tone, claim). [YT:SmNpVtfIRHw] [YT:XQwJRuN8p-Q]
- **Site checked before blaming the ad** when performance dropped: what changed on the landing page/site in that window. [YT:mpj0A4Prxu4] [YT:Oj-KGCw7K1g] (Hott in conversation; attribute)
- **Ends with one big change to test**, not ten small tweaks. [YT:fawsUmNeCkg] [YT:fls5dwYVY1M]
- **Competitor teardown ends in a transfer, not a copy:** what message/format you could be first to bring to your audience. [YT:Ugd7a2sPgZk]

## T7. Test plan, reading results, iterating winners

- ★ **Primary metric is share of spend + cost per result.** The plan says so explicitly; ROAS/CPA-only rankings get corrected ("I care more about the ads that get the most spend inside the ad account"). [YT:aD7vKl1nkcE] [YT:EOZp34uDIc4]
- ★ **Top spender is not killed on CPA alone.** Any kill rule on the account's top-spending ad requires the account-level effect to be checked first. [YT:mpj0A4Prxu4] [YT:qhVdp32ebWM]
- ★ **Plan is top-down from personas.** Every test row carries persona + hypothesis; results are read by persona over 30/90 days. "What more so matters is that you're tracking ads by persona." [YT:79doYBhLLFU]
- **Acquisition protected from bottom-funnel creep.** The plan keeps TOF concepts funded even when offer/remarketing creative looks cheaper; states why (no incremental reach). [YT:KSm0pvevV9k] [YT:Oj-KGCw7K1g]
- **Big swings over small iterations.** Iterations change something large (new creator, new format, new persona, new trigger); single-headline or single-clip swaps don't count. [YT:fawsUmNeCkg] [YT:fls5dwYVY1M]
- **Squeeze list for each winner:** every plausible reason it won, each with an action (reshoot script with other creators, more from that creator, reuse edit style, port copy into a new format). [YT:Oj-KGCw7K1g] [YT:SmNpVtfIRHw]
- **Fatigue answer is a new persona, not more volume.** More volume without new personas re-hits the same audience. [YT:79doYBhLLFU] [YT:Ugd7a2sPgZk]
- **No hit-rate KPI on strategists** (fine for forecasting only; baseline "90% of the stuff that we do sucks"). [YT:fls5dwYVY1M] [YT:Ugd7a2sPgZk]
- **Diversity read by format, style and angle** over last 90 days, plus net new vs iteration. [YT:EOZp34uDIc4]
- **Days-to-launch tracked** (delivered vs launched). [YT:EOZp34uDIc4]

## Universal checks (every output)

- ★ **Retrieved before writing.** At least 3 searches run; the work uses at least 3 retrieved items or passages, cited as [YT:id].
- ★ **No invented quotes, numbers, stories or dates.** Every quoted line exists verbatim in a retrieved hit; every Cooper number carries its date and "creator-reported" when load-bearing.
- ★ **Did the work.** If Ivan gave a task, the output is the finished thing (persona table, concept batch, brief, script, teardown with fixes, test plan), not a list of questions. Questions only for inputs that are missing and block the work, and only after doing everything possible without them.
- **In voice.** Conversational, contrarian opener then proof ("everyone says X, here's what's spending"), confidence with a number attached, admits mediocre output when it is; signature lines used sparingly and verbatim. Guests (Hott, Delmege, Crespi, Levinger, Batlaw) named, never voiced as Cooper. No praise, no filler.
- **Ends with one move.** One test: which persona it serves, the control, and the kill/scale rule on spend and cost per result.
