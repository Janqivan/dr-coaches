## 1. Core positions

Ranked by mentions × specificity. 2026 positions first where they concern what works today.

1. **"Real strategy lies in the layer before format selection." (NOW, GEN — 9 mentions).** Formats alone no longer grow an account in 2026; every format must be paired with persona understanding plus messaging and angles. The pre-format layer = (1) personas, (2) content pillars/angles (big pain points the customer responds to), (3) platform trends (macro, e.g., employee-generated content, partnership ads). Strategists who skip it default to copying competitors or testing many formats on one angle, and strategy goes "in circles and circles." [YT:yRgPbqywUJ8] (2026-07) · [YT:ePcEcT2PH8E] (2026-04) · [YT:_ZHS6tW3f_Q] (2025-01: "format matters but messaging is more important than ever")

2. **Every strategy needs a written diagnosis (NOW, GEN — 6 mentions).** A 1–2 sentence statement of the "connective tissue" and the why behind everything on the roadmap. "If you don't have a diagnosis, you don't really have a real strategy." Without it you have "aimless week-to-week testing." Most strategists skip it because they don't know to do it. [YT:-E9sjb2msjo] (2026-06) · [YT:4wM-i1cxtAM] (2026-09)

3. **Persona gap = the opportunity (NOW, GEN — 12 mentions).** Build personas two ways: who actually buys (reviews, interviews, ad comments) vs who the live ads target (inferred from 3 months of creative). "It's this gap that's going to be your opportunity as a creative strategist." She almost always finds a gap, so she runs persona analysis before diagnosis. Rank personas by volume AND by emotional intensity; low-volume/high-emotion personas are flagged as opportunities. [YT:-E9sjb2msjo] (2026-06) · [YT:p4yXXdnCgWg] (2026-08) · [YT:4wM-i1cxtAM] (2026-09) · [YT:yRgPbqywUJ8] (2026-07)

4. **Most accounts bleed top of funnel (NOW/EVERGREEN, GEN/DTC — 8 mentions).** Mapping live ads by awareness, she usually finds everything solution-aware or below (testimonials, best-seller, offers). Diagnosis: make much more top-of-funnel content so lower-funnel winners keep working while more people enter the ecosystem. Pushing a winner further up-funnel (more educational, humorous, curiosity) "almost always" extends its lifespan. [YT:-E9sjb2msjo] (2026-06) · [YT:4wM-i1cxtAM] (2026-09) · [YT:yRgPbqywUJ8] (2026-07) · [YT:lWpSmxuwSts] (2024-11)

5. **Optimize for the most scalable message, not the fastest converter (EVERGREEN, DTC/GEN — 8 mentions).** Offers, testimonials and us-vs-them convert "again and again" for small brands but don't scale; you'll be "on the hamster wheel." She reads the user journey simply as scalability (which message attracts the most people), a lens learned from Barry Hott. Test evidence: value/numbers-heavy variants convert first, but the broadest message (10-minute exercise routine, "hate" question, 5 minute routine) wins longest. Since accounts are now mostly broad, "cold audience" = most scalable messaging. [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs] (2025-01) · [YT:CCsty8R0UaA] (2025-01) · [YT:_ZHS6tW3f_Q] (2025-01) · [YT:zDHkSSsjFDw] (2025-02)

6. **Do not start ideation from awareness levels (ALLTIME→EVERGREEN, GEN — 5 mentions).** "I never ever start" a brief from a stage of awareness. People are motivated by desires and problems: start with what they desire, from whom, who they want to be. Use the awareness map only in retrospect to see if creative skews down-funnel. "I really caution people against thinking too deeply about those awareness levels when ideulating creative" Note: she still uses awareness mapping as a diagnostic stone in 2026 (see section 6). ## 2. Frameworks and methods

### 2.1 Brand diagnosis (2026 core method)
- **When:** onboarding a brand, re-evaluating strategy; she re-diagnoses roughly every quarter.
- **Steps:** (1) conduct research (reputation analysis, review mining, competitors); (2) analyze personas both ways (who ads target vs real buyers); (3) map awareness and sophistication levels of current creatives (she has a prompt); (4) determine creative diversity level (her video + Meta's creative diversity cheat sheet), "giving the Andromeda algorithm the juice" [quote src: YT:-E9sjb2msjo]; (5) write the 1–2 sentence diagnosis. She says it's very similar to Sarah Levinger's five-step session.
- **Research is "onion peeling" / "pushing around these stones." [quote src: YT:4wM-i1cxtAM]** First stone = awareness levels across all live ads.
- **Diagnosis examples she gives:** (a) all top content serves one persona → open a brand-new audience; (b) after ~6 months of persona tests the 40+ segment had best performance, video retention and on-site CVR → next quarter solely 40+ creators; (c) top performers almost all promotional/launch → need evergreen; (d) good learnings not doubled down on → more content on winning messages/angles; (e) Bed Threads: everything solution-aware or below → "this business needs more top-of-funnel content." [quote src: YT:-E9sjb2msjo]
- Sources: [YT:-E9sjb2msjo] (2026-06) · [YT:4wM-i1cxtAM] (2026-09)

### 2.2 The three gaps (pre-production gap analysis)
- **Gap 1, persona gaps:** cross the persona-SOP personas against personas the live account targets; look for never-targeted personas, or over-indexed ones when a bigger opportunity exists by volume or emotional intensity.
- **Gap 2, awareness levels:** brands over-index mid/bottom funnel; top performers often super bottom-funnel and offer-led → make more TOF.
- **Gap 3, creative diversity:** a true problem when the account doesn't attack different personas, each persona lacks 2–3 unique angles ("That's not concepts. These are just big pillar ideas" [quote src: YT:yRgPbqywUJ8]), and there's no range of awareness levels per persona. Also check creator diversity: lifestyle types, genders, ages, jobs, "different vibes overall." [quote src: YT:yRgPbqywUJ8]
- For statics the same pre-production research = which awareness levels aren't targeted, which personas missing, which formats not in rotation.
- Sources: [YT:yRgPbqywUJ8] (2026-07) · [YT:5C5VhqW9HCc] (2026-06)

### 2.3 Persona research (two-way persona SOP)
- **Buyer side:** scrape reviews to CSV (Ridge: 47,000 reviews, pulled 3,000 for speed, split by variant) → LLM prompt to extract five named personas (named after trigger/main problem) → rank by frequency AND by emotion → make the AI cite review signals per persona. Get output as a document first (edit in Google Docs; doubles as a Claude project context doc), only then turn into a deck (Canva connector, Gamma, PowerPoint). Also AI synthesis of ad comments.
- **Ad side:** Motion "intended audience" tag as gut check (e.g., top ads split between "daters" and "odor-conscious women" [quote src: YT:-E9sjb2msjo]); deeper: Claude Cowork "can you do an analysis on this brand's ads library?" [quote src: YT:-E9sjb2msjo] + link, asking who they target "from the POV of their ads only" [quote src: YT:-E9sjb2msjo] → snapshot of live video/static/partnership ads + primary/secondary personas. Or review all creative from the last 3 months: who is the intended audience?
- **Extra sources:** Ads Manager demographic breakdowns per test and monthly per account; "Nothing competes to actually just stalking your customers on social media" [quote src: YT:ZydGeio1DGQ]; Ask Amazon AI ("what type of people are buying" [quote src: YT:yRgPbqywUJ8]) for an Amazon persona breakdown; personas from viral organic content ("a little half-baked," [quote src: YT:p4yXXdnCgWg] still in progress, but produced lightbulb moments).
- **Trigger-based segment doc** (body odor client): segment named for the trigger (hormonal / life-stage odor); core story ("body odor suddenly started happening after a life event or a shift" [quote src: YT:-E9sjb2msjo]); demographics; core desires; supporting review signals. She prefers "audience segments" to "personas."
- **No customer data yet:** run the persona SOP on competitors, filtered by where they fail — read the 3-, 2-, 1-star reviews, not the five-star "cheerleader VIP customers." [quote src: YT:-E9sjb2msjo]
- **Quarterly persona sequencing** (from Joanna Wallace): don't test every persona at once; It Just Works held the hot-climate gym-goer persona until Q2.
- Insight: current buyers aren't always the best champion users, and you likely aren't targeting them properly anyway.
- Sources: [YT:-E9sjb2msjo] (2026-06) · [YT:UGiN8aVy2l8] (2026-05) · [YT:p4yXXdnCgWg] (2026-08) · [YT:ZydGeio1DGQ] (2025-12) · [YT:yRgPbqywUJ8] (2026-07)

### 2.4 Reputation analysis / research SOP ("read the room before you write the ad" [quote src: YT:Uh0TE6nZs5A])
- **What it is:** walk the same research journey a customer takes when comparing solutions or vetting the brand before purchase, across sources. Manual version took 3–4 hours and was "often incomplete and incredibly biased to what I thought was the most important" [quote src: YT:Uh0TE6nZs5A]; she has shared the doc with Motion for ~4 years.
- **Agency SOP outputs (four resources):** (1) reputation analysis doc (manual, "very very strategic" [quote src: YT:yRgPbqywUJ8]); (2) customer review mining sheet; (3) AI analysis of the reviews; (4) competitors doc.
- **Step 1 brand/product analysis:** founder and founding story ("the big why" [quote src: YT:yRgPbqywUJ8]); best sellers; merchandising; offers (zoom on paid-social offers). Per bestseller, starting with the lead product: initial promise, claimed transformation, proof, features, benefits, bundling opportunities.
- **Sources to walk:** page one of Google (red flags a first-time buyer would see); press (older demos trust legacy publications; look-alike placement clusters read as bought; scan for brand news like IPO or a nationwide retail launch that changes formats/offers/objectives); Amazon (is the Amazon bestseller different from DTC; rankings; reviews); Reddit (most honest — people "show up and be the most ruthless version of themselves" [quote src: YT:yRgPbqywUJ8]); YouTube reviews and especially their comments (skepticism, objections, FAQs); brand social audit (content strategies, community, storytelling, EGC, behind-the-scenes, transparency, which creators).
- **Must surface:** common objections and the big friction point: lack of trust in efficacy, skepticism it's worth it, cost too high, or value not shown well enough for the price bracket.
- **Review mining:** auto-export all reviews from the review platform; add manual standout lines from Reddit/Amazon; rank products by review count and rating; also take the top 20 ads from the last year and mine all their comments.
- **Recon report sections** (AI version): executive POV + creative implications; sentiment scorecard; strengths, weaknesses, biggest PR risk; reputation by press, reviews, Reddit, Trustpilot, pre-purchase social; product-level reputation; persona profiles with verbatim voice notes; jobs to be done, buying triggers, objections per audience; ad idea teasers.
- **2022 Thesis version** (step 2): study organic IG/TikTok, sign up for emails, sift the website (benefits most represented, how brand answers comments, most common questions, what drives engagement); Google "brand name" / "brand name review" [quote src: YT:rNsOaXtlmRM]; separate bona fide press from review sites.
- Sources: [YT:yRgPbqywUJ8] (2026-07) · [YT:Uh0TE6nZs5A] (2026-06) · [YT:-E9sjb2msjo] (2026-06) · [YT:rNsOaXtlmRM] (2022-04)

### 2.5 Framework 1 — "your next top performing creative already likely exists in your ad account" [quote src: YT:p4yXXdnCgWg]
- **When:** especially at >$100K/month spend or $1M lifetime through the account.
- **Steps:** take top 20 creatives from the last year → extract each one's big idea → pattern-recognize which ideas recur (recurrence = validation) → transfer the core idea into a format with more emotional resonance, often creator content, ideally partnership ads. Get statics live first because they return data fastest.
- Related "internal comparison": is the winner a unicorn? What does it share with other top performers (messaging, journey stage)? Build net-new "in the same vein," [quote src: YT:wRA2VgYIDVs] not cut-and-dry iterations.
- Sources: [YT:p4yXXdnCgWg] (2026-08) · [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs] (2025-01)

### 2.6 Framework 2 — viral content SOP (developed by business partner Miguel)
- **When:** low spend / no core learnings; now run at onboarding and weekly for many brands as TOF push grows; also used on a big data-rich brand to validate a new persona.
- **Steps:** take your big idea (e.g., "parents of picky eaters" [quote src: YT:p4yXXdnCgWg]) → search TikTok/Reels for what's going viral now; the special find is smaller accounts going viral → pattern-recognize recurring phrases, visuals, settings, creator types → the more it repeats, the more confidence to execute.
- Sources: [YT:p4yXXdnCgWg] (2026-08)

### 2.7 Three core principles: big idea · pattern recognition · prioritization
- **Big idea** (Ogilvy: core concept driving a campaign; without it an ad passes "like a ship in the night" [quote src: YT:p4yXXdnCgWg]). Her update: every ad has a big idea or "essence"; she trains strategists to state it succinctly for any ad.
- **Pattern recognition** "mega important": use each creative's essence as "a beacon" to spot what recurs across account, niche, industry (e.g., one persona showing up in multiple winners; "when we've done X, Y, and Z, it hasn't actually performed" [quote src: YT:p4yXXdnCgWg]).
- **Prioritization** axes: (1) easiest/quickest to make (validate a big idea via static, internal creator or founder before a shoot); (2) most likelihood/evidence to perform.
- Sources: [YT:p4yXXdnCgWg] (2026-08)

### 2.8 Evidence ladder / "repetition" evidence
- **Four kinds of repetition** a strategist must bring before testing: (1) repetition of winning elements (messaging, visuals, formats); (2) repeated customer feedback (same objection/pre-purchase concern); (3) repetition across scalable assets in recent months; (4) competitors only for what they keep testing over several months (monthly review of what they keep vs drop).
- **High confidence:** formats, messaging, creators you've pressure-tested (Seed's pooping angle). **Low confidence:** "it's our competitor's longest-running ad." [quote src: YT:-E9sjb2msjo] Weekly filter: "through the lens of what has the most evidence for performance." [quote src: YT:-E9sjb2msjo]
- High conviction is welcome: a strategist who'd "stake like an entire month's salary" [quote src: YT:-E9sjb2msjo] on an ad that flopped = best lesson.
- Sources: [YT:2jVcLu3Earg] (2025-05) · [YT:-E9sjb2msjo] (2026-06)

### 2.9 Roadmapping
- **Golden rules:** (1) all ideas in one central place; (2) rank by highest confidence; (3) prioritize quickest-to-ship so first week or two = quick iterations and low-hanging fruit; also map big swings with massive upside (new personas, production); "leave room for creativity and magic." [quote src: YT:-E9sjb2msjo]
- **Template, four parts:** (1) creators you want to work with; (2) the icebox — every research idea ungraded (leaders enforce it in a Google Sheet, Replit app or any file); (3) quarterly plan: holidays, calendar, launches, which personas fit which time of year; (4) monthly roadmap per persona: 1–2 sentence idea + three messaging options (hooks/variations). Agency duplicates monthly per client; brand side keeps one doc and collapses shipped items.
- **Cadence:** roadmaps monthly; weekly prioritization (Friday or Monday stand-up); daily/bi-daily production stand-ups; monthly creative retro. Anything shippable within a week goes in the weekly sprint even if not on the quarterly roadmap. Don't "churn and burn every single week" [quote src: YT:yRgPbqywUJ8] — quarterly planning prevents only-sprint thinking.
- **Three creative tiers:** tier 1 = iteration edit; tier 2 = post-production; tier 3 = production (new content).
- Roadmapping has no AI replacement; shiny formats she can never get to work come off it. Push back on non-evergreen requests ("your job as a creative strategist is not to make these Mother's Day 25% static" [quote src: YT:-E9sjb2msjo]); build an MVP process for them.
- Sources: [YT:-E9sjb2msjo] (2026-06) · [YT:yRgPbqywUJ8] (2026-07) · [YT:ITM4faRVzF8] (2025-05) · [YT:Y2zEga0aptc] (2024-10)

### 2.10 Messaging buckets and expansion levels
- **Buckets:** human-desire angle (romance, social connection, tranquility, power); demographic angle (age, life role, hobbies, jobs); big strategic angle (celebrity like Skims, negative/taboo, personal history); plus map each message onto the awareness journey. [YT:Lhl4o7yyuDQ] (2025-12) · [YT:3zZiKZUJmms] (2025-12)
- **Angle vs format:** an angle (e.g., celebrity) is cross-platform (OOH, TV) and can go into many formats; a format is how it shows up on one platform.
- **Expansion levels (2025-12):** Level 2 awareness expansion (check which stage most messaging speaks to); Level 3 desire/motivation expansion (count how many desires your account actually markets to; desires also drive visual diversity); Level 4 audience segment and use-case expansion (new groups and use cases can transform a business "literally overnight"; more personas give Andromeda more to target). [YT:ZydGeio1DGQ] (2025-12)
- **What she tells brands (2025-12):** (1) know core audience segments; (2) diverse formats per segment; (3) when formats win, double down with different angles and awareness levels; the bottleneck becomes creative ops speed, not the single best ad. [YT:x1b4h42HTG8] (2025-12)
- **Messaging pillars check (2026-09):** 3–4 core content buckets constantly fed, each carried through to the landing page. [YT:4wM-i1cxtAM] (2026-09)
- **Persona replication:** replicate a top performer across every core persona (David-and-Goliath for 45+ perimenopausal women → men on GLP-1 → new moms). [YT:yRgPbqywUJ8] (2026-07)

### 2.11 Static copy layer (Layer 3, "the most important" [quote src: YT:4g4WgCad_k4])
At minimum a winning static does one of: be specific (demographic, exact number for time/effort/cost: "5 minutes", "under $5", "2 inches in just one use"); call out the audience by name ("Self-selection is absolutely a copy mechanic"); lean into the taboo ("Say what your competitors are too polite to say."); tap a primal desire (status, sex, belonging, safety, approval — "The product is just the mechanism"); open a curiosity loop ("show the setup, but hide the payoff through the click"; pay it off on the LP if it wins); negative marketing naming what the audience fears; show transformation instead of describing the after state. [YT:5C5VhqW9HCc] (2026-06)

### 2.12 Golden nuggets and the Skeptic-to-Superfan SOP
- **Golden nugget** = a testimonial line with a concrete number, timeframe, or vivid specific outcome (see section 3).
