## MISATTRIBUTION WATCH — never attribute these to Rohn as verified

1. **"Motivation is what gets you started. Habit is what keeps you going."** — Absent from all official collections; quote databases credit runner **Jim Ryun**. (Official Rohn Facebook has posted it, so it circulates; do not present as verified Rohn.)
2. **"The 7 Silent Killers of Success"** (indecision, doubt, worry, over-caution, lack of urgency, failing to plan, disconnect from lessons of failure) — apocryphal/AI-era framework; zero trace on jimrohn.com or success.com. The real Rohn frameworks in this space: **Diseases of Attitude** (indifference, indecision, doubt, worry, over-caution, pessimism, complaining — verified in seminar transcript) and the **Five Enemies Within** (indifference, indecision, doubt, worry, over-caution — [LAIL]). SKILL.md framework #9 was corrected to the Diseases of Attitude on Aug 28, 2026.
3. **"If you don't build your dream, someone else will hire you to help them build theirs."** — Not supported as Rohn on any official source.
4. **"For things to change, you have to change; for things to get better, you have to get better."** — Popular couplet; treat as paraphrase of Shoaff-via-Rohn. Official forms: Shoaff's "If you'll change, everything will change for you" [JR]; Robbins renders it "if you want anything to change, you must change..." (tonyrobbins.com).
5. **"…discipline weighs ounces while regret weighs tons."** — Unverified in primary sources; official wording stops at "the pain of discipline or the pain of regret." Use only as credited-with framing.
6. **"Motivation alone is not enough. If you have an idiot and you motivate him, now you have a motivated idiot."** — Only sourcing is the official @OfficialJimRohn X account; acceptable with social-account caveat, not book-verified.
7. **Attribution-direction caution:** "Don't wish it were easier…" and "Work harder on yourself…" are listed as Rohn quotes on jimrohn.com AND printed as Shoaff's counsel in Rohn's own columns. Cite Rohn, with a Shoaff nod when teaching the mentor story.
