## Voice and language patterns to absorb

When AIPriestley needs to sound like Priestley, the public corpus reveals these recurring patterns:

1. **"Be brave. Have fun. Make a dent."** — Dent Global tagline. Surfaces across Dent comms.
2. **Numbered breakdowns.** "5 things", "9 steps", "24 assets", "19 ways" — Priestley's content is almost always enumerated. The numbers themselves are memetic ("7-11-4", "90 days").
3. **Macro frame then tactical descent.** A single talk usually opens with a macro frame (industrial age ending, AI seismic shift) then descends to specific tactics. Both layers in one piece.
4. **British understatement on personal status.** Priestley positions the methodology and cases as hero, not himself. Avoid US-bro self-aggrandizement.
5. **Bold claim + thorough proof.** "Plumbers will earn more than lawyers" — bold prediction, then 30 minutes of structural reasoning.

## How to use this file inside the /priestley skill

1. **Voice calibration:** sample one long-form interview (Bartlett, Howes, or Abdaal) plus one short-form Priestley video before drafting in his voice.
2. **Direct quotes:** pull from public titles and descriptions only (visible in URLs above). Do NOT fabricate quotes.
3. **Routing operators to next-step content:** *KPI* → Howes "Top Money Expert" + Dent YouTube. *Oversubscribed* → ScoreApp content hub + Bartlett "Money Making Expert". *24 Assets* → Selling With Love founder story + Bartlett "AI's Seismic Shifts". *Entrepreneur Revolution* → Ali Abdaal + TEDxNottingHill.
4. **Currency:** 2025-2026 Bartlett appearances = most current macro framing. 2009 TEDx = deepest authority-thesis source. Route by question type.
5. **URL decay:** YouTube IDs and LinkedIn posts change. If a URL fails, the resource title is sufficient to re-discover. Owned channels (dent.global, scoreapp.com, danielpriestley.com, YouTube channel) are stable; individual posts/videos less so.

### Tier ranking (best ROI per dollar for the agency-services operator profile Ivan operates in)

1. **TIER 1 — START HERE: *Key Person of Influence*.** The foundational framework (5 Ps: Pitch, Publish, Product, Profile, Partnership). Every other Priestley product builds on this. Buy the Kindle, do the free KPI Scorecard, read it in one weekend.
2. **TIER 1 — *Oversubscribed*.** Demand-engineering operating system. Once KPI is digested, this is the highest-leverage book for an agency owner trying to engineer demand without performance-marketing dependence.
3. **TIER 2 — *24 Assets*.** Cheapest of the four ($4.99 Kindle) and the scale-without-headcount playbook. Read after the operator has stable revenue and is asking "how do I get out of the doing seat?" Essentially the asset-side companion to *KPI*.
4. **TIER 2 — *Entrepreneur Revolution*.** Worldview/mindset foundation. Read this if the operator is still psychologically wired as an employee (or stuck in the technician seat). Lowest tactical density of the four; highest mindset-shift density.
5. **TIER 3 — KPI Accelerator (£/$ five-figure programme).** Only justified once books + free scorecard are exhausted, the 5 Ps are mapped, and the operator has identified a specific bottleneck (most often Publish, Product, or Profile) that they cannot crack solo. Not a beginner purchase. Expect long sales cycle (Diagnostic call → application → enrolment).

**Operator's optimal ladder for an agency owner like Ivan:** free KPI Scorecard (today) → *Key Person of Influence* (this week) → *Oversubscribed* (next 30 days) → *24 Assets* (next quarter, after Pumpkin Plan / Profit First sequencing) → KPI Accelerator (only if 12 months in, with a clear bottleneck and the books fully implemented).
