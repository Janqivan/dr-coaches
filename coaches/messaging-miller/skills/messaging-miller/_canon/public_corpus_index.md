# Donald Miller / StoryBrand — Public Corpus URL Catalogue

**Purpose:** Authoritative URL list for Miller's spoken and recorded voice — podcasts, YouTube, owned platforms, training programs, and key interview appearances. Use this file when the operator asks "where can I hear Miller in his own words?" or when voice calibration is needed for ghostwritten copy.

**Verification status:** URLs confirmed via WebSearch in this session. Where a URL is platform-redirected (Apple, Spotify), the canonical source link is given.

## Section 1 — Owned Platforms (the canonical voice surfaces)

### Business Made Simple — main platform
**URL:** https://businessmadesimple.com/
**What it is:** Miller's main commercial site. Courses, coaching, certifications, free resources, blog, and the Business Made Simple University login.
**Operator note:** This is the top of the funnel for everything else. The free resources here are the cleanest cross-section of the active framework set.

### Business Made Simple University (paid platform)
**URL:** https://businessmadesimple.com/ (subscribe path)
**Format:** On-demand video courses, taught primarily by Miller, JJ Peterson, and the BMS faculty. Roughly $275/year per user as of recent listings (subject to change).
**Operator note:** Worth the subscription if the operator is going deep on the system. Skip if all that's needed is the framework — the books cover the framework, the platform is the implementation layer.

### Business Made Simple Daily
**URL:** https://businessmadesimple.com/daily/
**What it is:** Short daily emails and content drops from BMS team — usually 3-5 minute reads, framework refreshers, customer questions answered.
**Operator note:** Cheapest way to keep Miller's current thinking in regular rotation.

### Coach Builder — book + program landing
**URL:** https://coachbuilder.com/book-pre-order/ (legacy book landing) | linked through businessmadesimple.com for the program
**What it is:** The Coach Builder book site and adjacent coaching-business program.
**Operator note:** Use this URL when an operator is in the "solo expert to coaching business" transition described in Coach Builder (2024).

### MyStoryBrand — the free BrandScript tool
**URL:** https://mystorybrand.com/
**What it is:** Miller's free online BrandScript builder. Walks the user through the SB7 frame box-by-box and exports a one-page BrandScript.
**Operator note:** Required first step for any client messaging work. Free, fast, low-friction. The /storybrand skill should reference this URL when an operator asks "where do I start?"

### StoryBrand — main site
**URL:** https://storybrand.com/
**What it is:** The StoryBrand brand site. Houses the Guide certification path, marketing services, and the Building a StoryBrand book pages.
**Operator note:** Distinct from businessmadesimple.com — StoryBrand is the messaging-framework brand, Business Made Simple is the broader operating-system brand. Same company, two surfaces.
