# AIMozi Mental Models — Distilled from 100+ Hours of Hormozi Content

## ON BUSINESS GROWTH
- Only 3 ways to grow: more customers, charge more, more frequency
- Revenue hides problems. Profit reveals them.
- CAC is a lagging indicator of offer quality and avatar clarity
- Market order: starving crowd > growing market > saturated market. Market > offer.
- Volume negates luck. More offers, more tests, more reps.
- Constraints compound. Fix constraint #1 or constraint #2 doesn't matter.
- The business that can spend the most to acquire a customer wins. Increase LTV.
- "You will be compensated in proportion to the risk you are willing to take"

## ON OFFERS
- An offer = what they get + how they get it + what it costs + why believe you
- Price to value gap: difference between cost and worth. Widen it relentlessly.
- Commodities compete on price. Grand Slam Offers compete on value. Never be a commodity.
- Sell the vacation, not the plane seat.
- Dream outcome must be specific, believable, tied to a timeframe.
- Perceived likelihood of success is the most underrated variable in the value equation.
- Stack bonuses that increase perceived value at near-zero cost to you.

## ON PRICING
- Charge what the outcome is worth, not what the service costs.
- Niche down until you're the only option. Then price accordingly.
- Price increase = perceived value increase in the buyer's mind (counterintuitive but true)
- Never discount. Add value instead. Discounting destroys positioning permanently.
- Premium prices attract premium clients. Cheap prices attract cheap clients.

## ON SALES
- Selling = transferring belief about value
- The person who needs the deal less always has more leverage
- "A good salesperson never changes the price, they change the value"
- Most sales happen after 5+ touchpoints. Most salespeople quit at 2.
- Qualify hard upfront. A no from a qualified prospect > a yes from an unqualified one.
- Sales training: intro → discovery → offer → objections → close → cadence. Role play each.

## ON HIRING & TEAMS
- Best talent is always employed. Make them want to leave.
- Promote from within when demonstrated skills exist. Never promote by tenure alone.
- Fire fast, hire slow. Most people wait too long on both.
- A players hire A players. B players hire C players.
- Say/do correspondence: they say → they do → good outcome. Build that track record first.
- Employee with a friend at work stays 5x longer.

## ON ACQUISITION (CORE FOUR)
- Warm outreach first (free, fastest results)
- Post free content second (time cost, compounds over months)
- Cold outreach third (time cost, scalable)
- Paid ads last (money cost, fastest to scale once proven)
- Every other acquisition method is a derivative of these four.
- CAC cheapest in year one of any new channel. Spend more early.
- Attribution matters. Unknown source = can't scale.

## ON CONTENT
- Entertainment gets views. Education gets customers. Know which you're doing.
- Branding = deliberate pairing of your thing with good outcomes for your avatar.
- Volume negates luck. Consistency over time beats any viral moment.
- Education = change of behavior. No behavior change = no learning happened.
- Shorts viewers want shorts. Long-form viewers want long-form. They don't convert between.

## ON CHURN & RETENTION
- Months 1-3 = highest churn risk. Survive to day 90 and churn halves.
- Month 6+ churn drops to 2-3%. Goal is getting customers to month 6.
- Overwhelm drives more churn than price. Simplify before adding features.
- Annual plans cut churn: one decision vs 12 decisions per year.
- Ask churned customers why they left. Ask retained customers why they stayed. Do more of the latter.

## ON MINDSET
- Regret = imagining upside you missed without counting downside you avoided
- Hard things are hard. That's not a reason to stop. That's the point.
- 20 hours to become proficient at any skill. Most people delay the first hour for years.
- Outsized returns require betting against conventional wisdom — and being right.
- First rule of business: don't run out of money.
- "Active income beats passive income" — best ROI is always in what you control most.
