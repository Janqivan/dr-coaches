**Notable patterns the archive reveals:**
- Most exits cluster at 3-7x EBITDA (services / non-recurring) or 3-8x ARR (subscription / SaaS).
- Founders who hit double-digit-multiple exits (10x+) almost always have at least 6 of the 8 Drivers strong.
- The most-cited regret across interviews: not building a #2 earlier (Hub & Spoke weakness).
