## T1. Concept batch (what to make next for a DR client or DR's own ads)

- ★ **Every concept names its mental stage.** Each concept is tagged Trigger, Exploration, Evaluation or Purchase, and the brief is phrased as a state ("creative that surfaces a trigger moment for someone who hasn't identified the problem"), not "cold ad / warm ad". Evidence: the tag + brief line per concept. [YT:po85BKDTG-Y]
- ★ **Every concept names a trigger moment (the when), not a pain point.** A specific situation in the day/week/year when the problem gets real ("I was at the dentist"-type first-person moment). "Wants more leads" or "frustrated with cost" = FAIL. [YT:pV0tM1gSJiU] [YT:sdEV9rZ70BY] [YT:in1l2TPFc1c]
- ★ **No two concepts are micro iterations of each other.** Two concepts that differ only by creator, colour, button, single words or hook swap with the same body = FAIL. A different situation/scene/person counts as new. [YT:cJlBabiKX9k] [YT:a5ruZB-Urlw]
- **Batch covers at least three stages, and fills the empty ones first.** If the live account was audited, the batch weights toward the stages with the fewest ads (usually trigger and exploration). [YT:po85BKDTG-Y] [YT:pV0tM1gSJiU]
- **Each concept carries emotion + zone + self.** One exact emotion one ring past the core of the feelings wheel (not "happy", "sad", "frustrated" alone), its valence × intensity zone (1–4), and the self switched on (actual, ideal, ought). [YT:LxWwRykBtfc] [YT:HuI3o3TBI4A] [YT:VtEoAOfQsCU]
- **Format chosen by the state, not the trend.** The concept says why this format fits this stage (trigger = relatable "this is so me" moment; exploration = light, novel; evaluation = proof, comparison; purchase = plain offer/founder). "Format is trending" or "client likes video" as the reason = FAIL. [YT:pV0tM1gSJiU]
- **Trigger concepts are not about the product.** The trigger-stage concept is built on the moment; product absent or a cameo. [YT:po85BKDTG-Y]
- **Each concept pairs two psychological mechanisms** (e.g. specificity + identity, reframe + social mirror) and names them. One mechanism alone gives "very stale" concepts. [YT:vBotjaDGNQI]
- **Era and vertical tagged; DTC plays carry the transfer line.** Every concept marked NOW/EVERGREEN/ALLTIME and GEN/DTC; DTC-sourced plays for law, franchise or vocational say "DTC evidence; here is the transfer, untested." ## T2. Ad teardown, roast, pre-launch QA (Ivan's ad, a client's ad, a competitor's ad)

- ★ **Stage it speaks to vs stage it ran as.** States which mental stage the ad actually speaks to and which campaign/optimization it ran under; a mismatch is named as the first suspect before the idea or offer is blamed. "You didn't build a purchase ad. You built an exploration ad." [YT:RCrvd8pTUzo] [YT:po85BKDTG-Y]
- ★ **Channels agree on one zone.** Words, music/sound and delivery are each placed in a zone; any disagreement is named with the line or second where it happens (an ad "pulling itself apart"). Emotional intensity is checked before the concept is declared dead. [YT:HuI3o3TBI4A] [YT:VtEoAOfQsCU]
- ★ **First 300 ms read frame by frame.** Names what the brain gets by ~300 ms: face mid-emotion, motion against the scroll, format-breaking visual, or precognitive sound. A teardown that judges only the spoken line = FAIL ("hook doesn't mean words"). [YT:DEoj28QXUYE]
- **Ad-or-content test.** Does the first frame read as an ad (polish, studio light, logo, product hero)? Says so; lo-fi video wins over polish she'd price at "probably 40 times more expensive". [YT:DEoj28QXUYE] [YT:a5ruZB-Urlw]
- **Cognitive load counted.** Counts text blocks, fonts, competing focal points; flags an ad that is trigger + exploration + evaluation + purchase in one ("the cognitive load on this ad is so high"). [YT:RCrvd8pTUzo]
- **Static hierarchy check.** The headline reads as the headline (designer didn't bold the CTA over it); the visual sits in the buyer's real setting (her forest-vs-nurse's-office critique). [YT:RCrvd8pTUzo]
- **Shame vs embarrassment.** If the ad uses a negative self-emotion, states which; shame-led framing = FAIL (her own evidence is anecdotal; say so). [YT:LxWwRykBtfc]
- **CTA matches the optimization event.** One consistent ask; the ad asks for the conversion it is optimized for and the destination asks to act, not browse ("a click is not a customer"). [YT:RCrvd8pTUzo] [YT:pV0tM1gSJiU]
- **Casting and set dressing read as targeting.** Names who Meta will send this to from what is on screen ("if anything in your video says mom life, you're going to get moms") and whether that is the buyer DR wants. [YT:sdEV9rZ70BY] [YT:vBotjaDGNQI] [YT:_iMPT2SsjeU]
- **Verdict on the metric agreed before launch**, not CTR alone; ends with one thing to change. [YT:vrBYS4-Gr4o] [canon: SKILL.md Response Mode 2] (one-change ending not found in transcripts)

## T3. Headlines, static copy, objection handling (belief ladder)

- ★ **Anchor belief written first.** States the one belief "right before they're about to purchase" (here: book the consult, apply, submit the form) that blocks the action, in the buyer's words. [YT:RCrvd8pTUzo]
- ★ **Current headline diagnosed by belief zone**, not "good/bad": indifference (sits inside the belief), acceptance (one step), rejection (too far). [YT:RCrvd8pTUzo]
- ★ **Ladder delivered at intensities 1–5, middle shipped.** Five rewrites labeled by rung with predicted reaction; the pick moves them "one step closer… not all 10 steps." [YT:RCrvd8pTUzo]
- **One objection per ad.** Each ad busts exactly one objection; the set spreads many across the account. [YT:RCrvd8pTUzo]
- **4–7 words, 8 max.** Count stated. If the concept runs longer, the rest of the ad is stripped to plain background. [YT:VtEoAOfQsCU]
- **One font, two at most.** Weights count as load. [YT:RCrvd8pTUzo]
- **Specific over vague.** Contains a number, a named situation or a micro-identity ("only for people who…"); every specific claim can be backed (specific claims read as legally earned). [YT:RpRSFn5pznc] [YT:VtEoAOfQsCU]
- **No clever puns, no jargon; customer's native language.** [YT:VtEoAOfQsCU]
- **Proof tone is calm, not stacked.** Evaluation copy gives "permission to choose" ("why people like you choose…") without piling on proof; "Customers can smell unearned ego." [YT:po85BKDTG-Y] [YT:6ZBccBr5AV4]
- **Copy is a DRAFT for Ivan.** All ad copy is marked for his approval. [canon: SKILL.md Context: Ivan] (not found in transcripts)

## T4. Hooks, video scripts, micro movies (incl. Ivan's founder videos)

- ★ **First second specified as three hooks.** Visual + sound (or deliberate silence) + text, each written; the visual uses a 300 ms element. A hook that is only a spoken sentence = FAIL. [YT:DEoj28QXUYE]
- ★ **Emotional job stated from → to, structure picked for it.** (stuck → hopeful = before-after-bridge; urgency = PAS/AIDA; identity shift = hero's journey/Pixar). [YT:8WRS3-y4K98]
- ★ **Product (DR's client's service) enters at the shift, ~40–50% in**, makes its entrance and stays; not in second one, not the whole video. [YT:8WRS3-y4K98] [YT:sdEV9rZ70BY] [YT:sBS1vy-5Be0]
- **Human story written first, product ignored**, ending in emotional resolution; the ending is "for" the viewer. [YT:8WRS3-y4K98] [YT:sBS1vy-5Be0]
- **Open loop: the viewer asks "where is this going?"** Loop type named (shock or alignment) and where it closes. No AIDA march that gives the payoff away at "interest". [YT:sdEV9rZ70BY] [YT:sBS1vy-5Be0]
- **Music matches the self.** Track direction stated per self/zone (ought = somber, ideal = hype, zone 1 = calm). [YT:HuI3o3TBI4A]
- **Captions in 3–4-word chunks**, never one word at a time; ~6 words only on a plain background. [YT:RCrvd8pTUzo] [YT:CwSAZdtqemk]
- **Cuts move.** No single angle held for the opening seconds unless calm is the intended state. [YT:CwSAZdtqemk]
- **Founder/lo-fi videos are one take, phone, off-center, no b-roll.** Scripted polish = FAIL for the yap format. [YT:a5ruZB-Urlw]
- **The odd element is literal, from the buyer's real world** (Ivan's rule outranks her implicit/metaphor examples: no symbolic imagery; she herself brainstorms off-product odd settings such as outer space or a Cinderella story, so this is Ivan's constraint, not hers). [canon: SKILL.md Context: Ivan] [YT:VtEoAOfQsCU] [YT:cJlBabiKX9k]
- **Human at the front.** No AI person presented as a real client, lawyer or franchisee; AI only for back-end or non-human visuals. [YT:qUn6OV_70fQ] [YT:sBS1vy-5Be0]

## T5. Customer research, trigger-moment maps, behavior-based working cards

- ★ **Starts with the decision the research serves.** First line answers "What decision will this research help me make?" No answer = research not run. [YT:pV0tM1gSJiU]
- ★ **Evidence is real customer data, never brand opinion.** Sources are reviews, Reddit/forums, call transcripts, tickets, surveys, search queries; DR's or the client's beliefs about the buyer are excluded. [YT:in1l2TPFc1c]
- ★ **Every claim tagged OBSERVED / INFERRED / UNSUPPORTED; unsupported deleted.** Observed claims carry the exact source snippet. [YT:in1l2TPFc1c]
- **Built around the when, not the person.** Category entry points: when the problem gets noticeable, when they start researching, when it is annoying enough to act. A demographic avatar ("45-year-old franchise buyer") as the output = FAIL. [YT:in1l2TPFc1c] [YT:vrBYS4-Gr4o]
- **Trigger types covered.** Moments are sorted into life event, social comparison, tipping-point frustration, external disruption. [YT:po85BKDTG-Y]
- **Subtext, not pain points.** For each key quote, states what the person is actually communicating (and to which hidden audience). [YT:gRQ1y0fLpWY] [YT:LxWwRykBtfc]
- **"Why now" captured.** What tipped them from objection thinking to action (from surveys, intake forms, call notes). [YT:pV0tM1gSJiU]
- **Finds what personas share, not how they differ.** [YT:vrBYS4-Gr4o] [YT:vXA7E1lcbaU]
- **Mine long problem threads, not praise.** Goes to Reddit, Discord, Facebook groups, TikTok for long threads about the problem; "I love the product" reviews are "not enough context". [YT:Co7Ll-MGPS0]
- **Output is a working card**: trigger/entry point · current vs desired behavior · emotional need and gaps · evidence · what's being tested now. [YT:in1l2TPFc1c]
- **Readiness check passed:** no fictional person invented, behavior over demographics, every claim traceable, creative team knows the next ad to write. [YT:in1l2TPFc1c]

## T6. Test plan, TEAP account audit, winner iteration and fatigue

- ★ **Success metric defined per ad before launch.** Names which metric reads this ad (hook rate, hold rate, CPL, booked-call rate) and why; "a test you can't read is always going to tell you nothing." [YT:vrBYS4-Gr4o]
- ★ **Audit pulls top ads by efficiency, not spend**, then tags each by format, psychological mechanism and mental state; gaps become briefs. [YT:6ZBccBr5AV4]
- ★ **Winner handling follows her line:** scale the winner (Babe Ruth); no micro iterations; when it tires, keep the proven mechanism and change the surface obviously or move the message into a new story/format. Dated: 2026-04 stance. [YT:cJlBabiKX9k] [YT:a5ruZB-Urlw]
- **Ads counted per TEAP stage today**, with the over- and under-indexed stages named. [YT:pV0tM1gSJiU] [YT:po85BKDTG-Y]
- **One optimization goal for the window** (30/60/90 days: cut cost, volume, quality, top of funnel) stated, and the stage weighting follows it. [YT:pV0tM1gSJiU]
- **Hero test per concept:** one video + supporting statics/GIF/carousel carrying the same message. [YT:CwSAZdtqemk]
- **Ads named by zone and self** so results can be read by psychology, not just by creative. ("zone one ideal"). [YT:HuI3o3TBI4A] [YT:OiKpEOXY4zc]
- **Fatigue window dated:** plans for refresh at ~2–3 weeks (2026-07 figure), labeled her number, not DR's. [YT:_iMPT2SsjeU]
- **Decide loop written:** win → build momentum on it; lose → back to research to check the insight, not just a new hook. [YT:vrBYS4-Gr4o]
- **Discounts/offers are not the first fix.** When purchase-stage results slide, upstream stages are checked before the offer is sweetened ("It's almost never the offer"). [YT:po85BKDTG-Y]

## Universal checks (every output)

- ★ **Retrieved before writing.** At least 3 searches run; the work uses at least 3 retrieved items or passages, cited as [YT:id].
- ★ **No invented quotes, numbers, stories or dates.** Every quoted line exists verbatim in a retrieved hit; her numbers carry their month and "creator-reported".
- ★ **Did the work.** If Ivan gave a task, the output is the finished thing (concepts, teardown, ladder, script, working card, test plan), not a list of questions. Questions only for inputs that are missing and block the work, and only after doing everything possible without them.
- **In voice.** Fast, casual, warm-but-blunt; names the science term, then one vivid image, then the contrast; labels opinion vs data and flags thin evidence out loud; diagnoses the system, not the person. No praise padding for Ivan.
- **Ends with one move.** One test: stage and emotion it targets, the control, how it's read, the kill/scale rule, and what would change her mind.
