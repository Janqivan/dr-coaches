## A. Harvard Business Review back catalogue (Dixon byline)

Dixon's HBR archive is the single richest free Dixon corpus on the public internet. Most articles have free public abstracts on hbr.org; the full text is paywalled but the abstracts plus the article structure are publicly viewable. Where a full free version is hosted on the publisher's own site, that's noted.

**Master directory:**
- Dixon search page on HBR: https://hbr.org/search?term=Matt+Dixon
- Alt search (full name): https://hbr.org/search?term=matthew+dixon

### Challenger Sale / Solution Sales / Sales process
- **"The End of Solution Sales"** (Brent Adamson, Matthew Dixon, Nicholas Toman) — *HBR*, July-August 2012 — https://hbr.org/2012/07/the-end-of-solution-sales — Featured in HBR's *10 Must-Reads on Sales*.
- **"Selling Is Not About Relationships"** (Matthew Dixon, Brent Adamson) — *HBR.org*, September 30, 2011 — https://hbr.org/2011/09/selling-is-not-about-relatio — The original 5-profile-finding announcement.
- **"Dismantling the Sales Machine"** (Brent Adamson, Matthew Dixon, Nicholas Toman) — *HBR*, November 2013 — https://hbr.org/2013/11/dismantling-the-sales-machine — Featured in HBR's *10 Must-Reads on Sales*.
- **"The Worst Question a Salesperson Can Ask"** (Matthew Dixon) — *HBR.org*, October 2011 — https://hbr.org/2011/10/the-single-worst-question-a-sa — A short HBR.org piece on the "what keeps you up at night?" trap and what the Challenger asks instead.
- HBR podcast: **"The New Sales Playbook"** — *HBR Podcasts*, August 2012 — https://hbr.org/podcast/2012/08/the-new-sales-playbook — Audio companion to "The End of Solution Sales".

### Effortless Experience / Customer Service / CES
- **"Stop Trying to Delight Your Customers"** (Matthew Dixon, Karen Freeman, Nicholas Toman) — *HBR*, July-August 2010 — https://hbr.org/2010/07/stop-trying-to-delight-your-customers — The original CES / effortless-experience research article.
- HBR podcast: **"Why Delighting Your Customers Is Overrated"** — *HBR Podcasts*, July 2010 — https://hbr.org/podcast/2010/07/why-delighting-your-customers — Audio companion to the article above.
- **"Kick-Ass Customer Service"** (Matthew Dixon, Lara Ponomareff, Scott Turner, Rick DeLisi) — *HBR*, January-February 2017 — https://hbr.org/2017/01/kick-ass-customer-service — The 7-types-of-service-rep research, identifying Controllers as the high performers.
- **"Reinventing Customer Service"** (Matthew Dixon) — *HBR*, November-December 2018 — https://hbr.org/2018/11/reinventing-customer-service — T-Mobile case study; co-located pod operating model.
- HBR webinar: **"How to Fix Customer Service"** — *HBR Webinars*, December 2016 — https://hbr.org/webinar/2016/12/how-to-fix-customer-service — Live walkthrough of the Kick-Ass Customer Service findings.

### JOLT Effect / Customer Indecision
- **"Stop Losing Sales to Customer Indecision"** (Matthew Dixon, Ted McKenna) — *HBR*, June 2022 — https://hbr.org/2022/06/stop-losing-sales-to-customer-indecision — The HBR introduction of the JOLT findings before book publication.

### Activator Advantage / Rainmaker Genome
- **"What Today's Rainmakers Do Differently"** (Matt Dixon, Ted McKenna, Rory Channer, Karen Freeman) — *HBR*, November-December 2023 — https://hbr.org/2023/11/what-todays-rainmakers-do-differently — Pre-book HBR introduction of the Activator finding and the 5 partner profiles.
