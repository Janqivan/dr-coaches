## 1. Core positions (ranked by mentions × specificity; 2026 first where it is about what works today)

### Savannah's own positions

1. **Creative is the scaling lever, not media-buying hacks.** Since 2020 she has said scale comes from weekly new creative tests, not "magic audiences" or buying tricks; top marketers "haven't thought about targeting in six months"; she keeps structure simple and puts "99.99%" of her attention on creative rotation in prospecting. In 2025: "the creative does the work" for targeting — broad audience, platform reads the creative. In 2026 under Andromeda: more persona testing, bigger visual hook swings, more diverse value drivers. GEN/DTC. ~9 mentions. 2. **Meta only counts a variation as different if the first 3–5 seconds look different.** Changing only the text on top isn't seen as a new variation (2026). So iterations must change the opening visual, and the Andromeda adjustment is bigger swings, not small changes. GEN. 2 mentions, NOW. [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01).

3. **Isolate variables; the hook is the variable that matters most.** Hook = first two seconds; keep everything else identical when hook testing. Iterate a winner by swapping only the first ~3 seconds; for every concept, ask what alternate hook you can easily shoot. GEN. ~5 mentions. [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06), [YT:KALGyNhfrVU] (2025-07), [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01).

4. **Volume with discipline: mostly net-new, some iteration.** ~80% new / 20% iterative; "two new hooks on a winning ad per month"; brands at $10K–$50K/mo launch at least two new ads a week; no need for 200 ads/month at small spend. "meta definitely favors net new content." GEN. ~6 mentions. [YT:ZQAjWwxk2C0] (2024-05), [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06), [YT:3TzdwzAG1X8] (2025-03).

## 2. Frameworks and methods

### 2.1 Savannah's frameworks

**A. Iterating a winner — the four levers (2026 version)**
- When: a winner exists and needs more life or more spend.
- Steps: (1) same creator, same value drivers/messaging, new hook (visual or spoken); (2) different creator, exact same script/shot list — isolates creator vs message; (3) reshuffle clip sequence; (4) cut 30s to 20s/15s. Pick one variable and A/B it.
- Older forms of the same method: change first three seconds/hook; refilm exact same script with a different model; add display cards (2023-07). Same script and overlays with a new talent (older, different look) or chop seconds (2024-06).
- Sources: [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01), [YT:FjUkvTGkxWo] (2023-07), [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06). GEN.

**B. "Figure out the ingredients" [quote src: YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA] — iteration from the Motion report**
- When: weekly review of a client's ads.
- Metrics: thumb-stop, CTR, click-to-purchase, hold rate.
- Steps: high thumb-stop ad (e.g. 24%) → keep the first 3s, change the body (talent, script, value props). High hold-rate body with so-so thumb-stop → pair it with the best hook. Combine the best parts.
- Source: [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA] (2024-03). GEN.

**C. The 80/20 production split**
- When: planning a month's ads.
- Rule: ~80% net-new concepts, ~20% iterations; two new hooks on a winning ad per month. Example: 8 ads/month = 6 net-new + 2 new hooks on winners. Brands at $10K–$50K/mo: at least two new ads a week.
- Sources: [YT:ZQAjWwxk2C0] (2024-05), [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06). GEN.

**D. Variation volume by spend tier**
- At least 2 variations per ad (two hooks). ~$50K/mo: 2–4 variations/week. Millions/mo: 10–15 iterations/week. Some clients up to 12 iterations/week from the same base footage (re-cut scenes, hooks, text treatments).
- 2026 smallest package ($30K–$100K/mo): 2 ad inspirations/week × 2 written hook variations × 2 opening visual variations.
- Sources: [YT:jAc63K9lRIQ] (2025-02), [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01). GEN.

**E. Weekly hypothesis loop (2020)**
- Steps: new feed + story ads built around one hypothesis (one motivator or barrier — e.g. Outer's eco-friendly materials) → launch in Dynamic Creative tests with multiple videos/images/headlines → end of week pick the one with most conversions/spend at lowest CPA → scale winners, kill losers → inform next week.
- Source: [YT:PvjkCV9WelU] (2020-04). DTC.

**F. Dynamic Creative Testing (DCT) setup (2020)**
- Steps: toggle Dynamic Creative on the ad set; upload multiple videos, images, primary texts, headlines into one ad; Facebook tests combinations; read via Breakdowns by video/headline/text. Pair with broad targeting (broad/worldwide, 10M+ interests, big lookalikes).
- Why: it optimizes at user level (short vs long video vs image to whoever is likeliest to convert); old way (one ad per variation) was slow and budget-hungry; DCT gets significant results "within even a week" [quote src: YT:Mwla1m64jSg]. She called it "one of the best machine learning features that has been released by facebook" [quote src: YT:Mwla1m64jSg].
- Sources: [YT:Mwla1m64jSg] (2020-07), [YT:6OmF0BzBOHQ] (2020-05). GEN.

**G. Twice-weekly account routine (2023)**
- Steps: launch ≥2 new ads (net-new or iterations of best performers); turn off ads above CPA target; turn back on ads that came in under target after delayed attribution (7-day look-back); check Northbeam + post-purchase survey (Fairing) for incrementality.
- Source: [YT:pNECdkJjpCU] (2023-06). DTC.

**H. Weekly review regimen and cross-account learning**
- ~3 days at start of week reviewing all ~50 clients' prior-week tests in Motion: which hooks won, top spenders, is the hook good but the body needs changing; ask what variable changed and its effect.
- Cross-account: if a format (Post-it note) wins across ~10 accounts, use it for new clients; also log per-client variables (e.g. talent face on camera in first two seconds wins for one client).
- 2026: run one concept across several clients/industries; if it wins broadly, add it to a "winning concept library" [quote src: YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] and roll out further.
- Sources: [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06), [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01). GEN.

**I. Kill rule — spend ≥4x AOV**
- $100 AOV → spend $400 on an ad (ideally within a week) before killing; ~50 conversions/week per ad set for learning.
- Source: [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA] (2024-03). DTC. (Lead-gen translation is not hers; see guest rules in 2.2 for CPA-based versions.)

**J. Testing structure**
- Mostly broad / Advantage+ Shopping; throw ads in and let Meta/TikTok allocate; separate a creative-testing campaign from a proven-winners campaign. Keep 5–7 ads active per ad set. Split broad ad sets by age only so creative matches (older-looking models in 25+/35+, teens in under-24) — targeting serves creative.
- Sources: [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA] (2024-03), [YT:pNECdkJjpCU] (2023-06). DTC/GEN.

**K. Weekly format rotation with the same creators**
- Shoot new content with the same creators each week but rotate format: skits one week, slideshow rating, before/after. Vary length/style to broaden reach: a few-minute tutorial reaches people who watch hour-long makeup tutorials. Standing variable: one creator face vs a mashup of multiple creators — both win depending on client.
- Sources: [YT:3TzdwzAG1X8] (2025-03), [YT:KALGyNhfrVU] (2025-07). GEN/APP.

**L. TikTok default build (2023)**
- Optimize for complete payment, value optimization, no dayparting, exclude 180-day purchasers; 5–10 ad sets: broad split by age, purchaser lookalike, interest+hashtag stack with targeting expansion on, automatic targeting, Smart Performance, combined retargeting. Higher-AOV ($100–200+): optimize for add to cart until ≥50 ATCs/week per ad set. Rule of 1,000: season a new pixel on ATC until ~1,000 ATC events before moving to initiate checkout/purchase. Next move on a winning video: add a display card (discount/CTA sticker).
- Sources: [YT:pNECdkJjpCU] (2023-06), [YT:FjUkvTGkxWo] (2023-07). DTC.

**M. Broad vs narrow by budget (2020)**
- Broad works best at ≥$1,000/day and absent hard constraints (moms-only product, shipping limits); at a couple hundred/day narrow targeting makes sense; broaden at each budget step ($500, $1K, $5K).
- Source: [YT:6OmF0BzBOHQ] (2020-05). DTC.

**N. App measurement**
- Success = cost per download vs account baseline: get average CPD before her ads, then measure % lower CPD after UGC styles. Hook-rate bar is baseline-dependent; her average bar is viewers getting past the first two seconds. Subscription apps can accept a "crazy high" first CPA if LTV data shows retention.
- Source: [YT:3TzdwzAG1X8] (2025-03). APP.

**O. Where AI is allowed in her workflow**
- Research: personas, site reviews, testimonials, Reddit, first stab at value props; ask ChatGPT what shots or lines she's missing on a human-written script.
- Ops: Otter.ai transcribes creator testimonial clips → copy lines into editor briefs; Dropbox AI search ("beach clip") to find clips for mashups.
- Shot lists: give Notion AI/ChatGPT the product website, ask for a UGC shot list (unboxing, using product, testimonial), refine with don't include messaging points, just shots, send to creator.
- Voice: ElevenLabs to clone a creator's voice for ~10 new first-3-second hooks on a winner (voiceover/app-screenshot ads only, not talking-head testimonials); Spanish localization of a winning English ad with native-speaker check; fixing a mispronounced brand name.
- Sources: [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA] (2024-03), [YT:3TzdwzAG1X8] (2025-03), [YT:EHiXurAZPj0] (2025-03), [YT:kFtv5VDkRSg] (2025-10). GEN.

### 2.2 Motion guest frameworks (labeled, not Savannah)

- **Diagnostic order** (guest: Evan Lee, Motion, 2026-06): 1) is Meta spending on it? 2) thumb-stop ratio (3-sec views ÷ impressions) 3) hold rate (exposes slop) 4) outbound CTR 5) conversion (purchase, lead, download, booked appointment). Spend threshold before judging ~$300. Thumb stop doesn't apply to statics. [YT:DqFjak694PQ]. GEN.
- **Metric-to-part hypothesis method** (guest: Mirella Crespi, 2023-02): thumb-stop = hook, hold = post-hook, CTR = resonance, then CVR/ROAS → describe what you see → write three if we change X, metric Y improves hypotheses (e.g. high thumb-stop, drop at sec 5–6: replace boring talking head with visual interest). [YT:4g4WgCad_k4]. DTC.
- **Baseball analogy kill rule** (guest: Jess, 2024-05): each swing = spend equal to CPA; $100 CPA, $300 spent, no purchase = three swings, consider cutting unless hold rate is good ("foul ball"); at 10x CPA definitely cut; give a week for weekday/weekend. [YT:ZQAjWwxk2C0]. GEN.
- **Post-Andromeda scaling levers** (guest: Sophia Beauvoir, ReadySet, 2026-06): low-hanging fruit first — footage refresh with existing library (same proven script, new product/B-roll), same message in new formats, test lengths, reorder messaging; then expansion — new talent, backgrounds, narrative structures, design-heavy formats, new persona/pain point. "Super ad" mashup: ad A's thumb stop + ad B's CTR/CVR. If a same-script/new-talent iteration fails, ask whether script or talent drove the original. [YT:CVWHkhUA5Ms]. GEN.
- **Swing-size color tags** (guest: Viti, Happy Mammoth, 2026-06): green (massive winner) → biggest swings: change creator, product, script (not structure), gender, accent, B-roll; lesser ads → smaller swings. Scaling loop: find winner → deconstruct (hook, angle, format, emotion) → multiply → test fast → scale/ditch → refresh completely at fatigue; the winner is "the blueprint for your next 20 ads". [YT:CVWHkhUA5Ms]. DTC/GEN.
- **"Learned concept" and ad families** (guest: Alicia, Motion, 2025-08): between an iteration and a big swing — pick one component that moved the needle (e.g. a specific pain point) and build a diverse test around it (different creators, formats, products). Example family on "self-care": native text-selfie headline static, UGC comment response, lifestyle product image with social proof. [YT:HIUqsMLp7sc]. GEN/DTC.
- **Tagging that starts small** (guest: Alicia, Motion, 2025-08): four most-cited tags — ad format, hook type, messaging angle, audience; then product, creator, season/offer, visual setting. Bad name "UGC Vid125A" vs good name encoding type, format, angle, persona, product, creator. [YT:HIUqsMLp7sc]. GEN.
- **Success index** (guest: Eric Zorsky, Wispr Flow, 2026-07): 30% thumbstop, 30% hold (past midpoint), 25% CTR, 15% CPM efficiency, scored vs own category average; 50 = average, 75 = standout, >80 best. Tag concept/awareness level/angle/persona pre-launch so a winner yields ten iterations next day. [YT:M-zOOiG5GLw]. APP.
- **Offer value ranking + "fruit fly tests"** (guest: Emily Hickey, Chief Detective, 2026-07): rank all selling points, run cheap fast Meta tests with simple headline ads per benefit, double down when asymmetry appears; only then choose storytelling form (person, testimonial, founder). [YT:ILTXC1L8te8]. GEN.

- **Built Basics flywheel** (guest: Spencer, 2024-12): naming non-negotiables (ad number, LP, product, collection, content type, format, hook, misc test field); "small flip" vs "big flip"; changes made at night in a 30–60 min batch; first check when performance drops is static-vs-video and studio/lifestyle/design ratios.
