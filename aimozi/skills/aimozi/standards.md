## T1. Offer design (Grand Slam Offers for FranchiseFlow, immigration law, vocational, the 3-month onboarding offer, SaaS)

- ★ **Market checked before the offer.** The work names the avatar and scores it on the four: massive pain, purchasing power, easy to target, growing. A market that fails one is called out before a single bonus is written. "Your market is more important than anything else." [YT:eX3Ch_HuR70] [YT:qxQIcDrre1E] [YT:BOOK-100M-OFFERS]
- ★ **Dream outcome, not the service.** The offer headline names the result the buyer wants plus a time frame ("lose 20lbs in 6 weeks"), not the deliverable ("Meta ads management"). Sell the vacation, not the plane flight. [YT:BOOK-100M-OFFERS] [YT:okA9Yt2KZuk]
- ★ **Built from the problem list.** The work shows the enumerated obstacles the buyer hits before, during and after, each turned into a named solution, then delivery vehicles, then trim & stack (high value / low cost kept; high cost / low value cut). An offer with no visible problem list FAILS. [YT:BOOK-100M-OFFERS] [YT:prj1bnTAM8A] [YT:5MHQr-Z17Hc]
- **All four value drivers addressed, bottom half named.** State which element raises dream outcome, which raises perceived likelihood (proof, guarantee), which cuts time delay, which cuts effort. He treats time delay and effort as the moat ("the bottom side of the equation is where they killed everyone"). [YT:5MHQr-Z17Hc] [YT:F3NyhOtRpOE]
- ★ **Guarantee is specific and stated hard.** Outcome + number + time + what you give back. "We will get you 20 clients guaranteed" = FAIL; "20 clients in your first 30 days or we give you your money back + your advertising dollars" = the bar. Type named (unconditional / conditional / anti / implied). [YT:BOOK-100M-OFFERS] [YT:5MHQr-Z17Hc]
- **Guarantee stipulations are the success actions.** Conditions are the client behaviours that drive results (show up, send the data, follow the process), not escape hatches. [YT:5MHQr-Z17Hc] [YT:BOOK-100M-OFFERS]
- **Guarantee math shown.** Refund math done, not feared: 100 sales/5% refunds = 95 net vs 130 sales/10% refunds = 117 net = 1.23x. The work shows its own version of this calc. [YT:BOOK-100M-OFFERS] [YT:5MHQr-Z17Hc]
- **Every bonus kills a named objection, and could sell alone.** Each bonus maps to a stated obstacle; aim for each bonus to be worth the price on its own, consolidated, not a 15-item list. [YT:BOOK-100M-OFFERS] [YT:5MjjpB8SPMo] [YT:-Koq14DPXC4]
- **Scarcity/urgency true.** Scarcity = real capacity ("if you can only take on five customers… say so"); urgency = rolling cohorts / start dates. Fake deadlines FAIL. [YT:4GQLJjH9-oA] [YT:BOOK-100M-OFFERS] [YT:XwZH-lOKG9c]
- **Named with M-A-G-I-C, 3–5 components, 2–3 names to test.** Name carries avatar + result + container/time; the work gives test variants, not one "final" name. [YT:BOOK-100M-OFFERS] [YT:XwZH-lOKG9c]
- **Performance structure considered for an agency.** For DR offers, the work states whether an implied guarantee (rev share, per-result, "greater of $X or Y%") fits, and the tracking/collection risk. [YT:BOOK-100M-OFFERS]
- **Compliance stated where the vertical needs it.** Immigration law / franchise disclosure: claims and guarantees carry stipulations and are checked against what the vertical allows. "Obviously you always have to be compliant. Put stipulations." [YT:pxVeOkOVr2w]

## T2. Pricing and price changes (rate cards, retainer levels, setup fees, raising prices on current clients)

- ★ **Priced from close rate, with the ladder applied.** The work states the current close rate and reads it against his ladder: ≥ 80% → underpriced 3–4x; 60–80% → 2–3x; 50–60% → 1.5–2x; ~35% with a real sales motion → about right; < 30% → avatar or sales-motion problem, fix those **before** any price cut. [YT:A_tx40lNpf8] [YT:3yAiVjcImQ4]
- ★ **Never discount the core offer.** Lower price = remove named scope or add bonuses to close; no percentage discounts. "It teaches your customers that your prices are negotiable." [YT:BOOK-100M-OFFERS] [YT:unshZobTt6Q] [YT:ZuFSLAw2pEI]
- **Revenue-per-call math, not price feelings.** Shows price × close rate per 100 calls for current vs proposed ("10 at $10k = $100k; 30 at $5k = $150k"). Raise until price × conversion makes less money. [YT:41EvCgwPrDc]
- **Gross margin target for a service: ≥ 80%.** Margin stated; a lower margin is diagnosed as a commodity offer or broken sales process, not accepted. [YT:A_tx40lNpf8]
- **Test with enough shots on goal.** The plan names the sample size before judging a new price; two early "no"s are not data. [YT:oRMG_HpOAN4]
- **Existing-client increase uses the letter structure.** What they now get, the promise being kept, effective date, grandfathered until a named date. [YT:oRMG_HpOAN4] [YT:Gy-RmpRif-I]
- **Anchor with the most expensive option first**, including a premium one-on-one version (Ivan-level access) that lifts the rest. [YT:yEKu6q0W3gs] [YT:uWdIgftpvBI]
- **Pull cash forward.** Setup/onboarding fee, paid-in-full or layaway options so CAC is recovered in the first 30 days. [YT:A_tx40lNpf8]
- **Service businesses go up, not down.** Any price cut proposal for a service business needs an explicit reason that beats "the faster you spin that loop going up in price, the more you will progress." [YT:A_tx40lNpf8]

## T3. Ad copy, hooks and creative scripts (DR's own acquisition ads and client ads)

- ★ **Hooks get the effort.** A batch ships ~50 hooks against 3–5 bodies, ~80% (40) built on proven past hooks and ~20% (10) new experiments. A single-hook ad or a batch with mostly untested hooks FAILS. [YT:reisEL_D7xc] [YT:CaiLcj6tzBQ]
- ★ **Proof beats promise.** Every ad carries a specific, verifiable proof element (number, named result, track record) that only DR can say. Swap test: if a competitor could run the line unchanged, FAIL. [YT:JDR-R--4HhM] [YT:HxEQCHpZzHk]
- ★ **Claims built in his claim format.** Dream outcome + how many people got it + how long it took + stipulations. Unsupported claims FAIL. [YT:5MHQr-Z17Hc]
- **Hook uses proof / promise / plan** (any order, lead with the strongest). [YT:HxEQCHpZzHk]
- **One reader, awareness level named.** The ad is written to one person and the work states their awareness level (unaware / problem / solution / product aware) and matches the opening to it. [YT:JDR-R--4HhM]
- **Damaging admission present where trust is the gap.** "What you'll sacrifice in promise you'll gain in trust." [YT:JDR-R--4HhM] [YT:_qxesPH9PvU]{GL}
- **Reason why.** The offer carries a "because" (capacity, cohort start, anniversary). [YT:JDR-R--4HhM] [YT:Mst4hreQYl0]
- **Show, don't tell.** Benefits written as the felt experience ("customers reading their credit cards to you faster than you can write"), not adjectives. [YT:JDR-R--4HhM]
- **Headline built on the value equation.** Outcome + likelihood (guaranteed/proof) + time + effort removed ("…in 90 days without…"). [YT:_qxesPH9PvU]{GL}
- **One message bucket per ad; 3rd-grade reading level; buyer's own words.** [YT:_qxesPH9PvU]{GL}
- **Ad→page congruence.** The landing page headline repeats the ad's promise; one step asked at a time ("a confused mind does not buy"). [YT:JDR-R--4HhM] [YT:_qxesPH9PvU]{GL}
- **Judged on cost per sale, not cost per lead.** Any test plan optimises to the sale (or signed case/franchise deal), never cheapest lead or appointment. [YT:c_6BrF7jOGk]

## T4. Lead generation plans and lead magnets (DR's own acquisition, outbound, SDR agent)

- ★ **Core Four named and sequenced.** Plan states which of warm outreach / cold outreach / content / paid ads runs now, plus which "other people" lever (customers, affiliates, employees, agencies) applies. Anything else is a derivative. [YT:I64gYLBrics] [YT:oRMG_HpOAN4]
- ★ **Volume set with the Rule of 100.** Daily input numbers stated (100 reach-outs, or 100 minutes of content, or $100+/day ads), restarted for each new channel. Feast-or-famine is diagnosed as "a symptom of insufficient volume." [YT:6KqndZuN_Yk] [YT:HsQeQM1jUeg] [YT:A_tx40lNpf8]
- ★ **Lead magnet solves one narrow problem that reveals the next one**, which the paid offer solves. Fast, easy, narrow, solves one complete problem. A magnet that tries to solve the whole problem, or doesn't lead to the offer, FAILS. [YT:SmiOK8Yun4s] [YT:Ds_Qp2U5I8U]
- **Magnet type chosen on purpose:** reveal a problem (assessment/audit), trial, or one step of many; delivery vehicle named; CTA embedded before, inside and after. [YT:Mst4hreQYl0] [YT:7NMH1oAkgLY]
- **Free stuff better than competitors' paid stuff.** If it costs DR something to deliver (a real audit), that's a feature. [YT:7NMH1oAkgLY] [YT:reisEL_D7xc]
- **One avatar, one channel before adding more.** Under 7 figures: one product/avatar/channel. Going 7→8: one avatar, one channel, two products. A plan opening a second channel before the first is maxed FAILS unless it says why. [YT:qxQIcDrre1E] [YT:reisEL_D7xc]
- **More before new.** First asks "why can't we 10x what's already working?" before proposing a new tactic. [YT:oRMG_HpOAN4] [YT:yflKMUffctE]
- **Warm/cold message personalised**, with a specific reference to the person, and replies handled with acknowledge–compliment–ask. [YT:w7g08dVTwaE] [YT:_PCCqqv2pig]
- **Inputs tracked, only data that changes inputs.** The plan lists the inputs and the 2–3 numbers that would make you change them. [YT:4Yz8ggEv0NU]

## T5. Sales process (call scripts, objection handling, setter/closer flow, lead nurture, show rates)

- ★ **Structure is CLOSER.** Clarify why they're here, Label the problem, Overview past pain (what they've tried), Sell the vacation, Explain away concerns, Reinforce the decision. Missing steps named. [YT:okA9Yt2KZuk] [YT:JE2_7elAcxM] [YT:q32-l3Yoqg4]
- ★ **Objections killed early ("kill zombies when they're far away").** Discovery surfaces decision maker, time and money before the price; the setter confirms all decision makers will be on the call, or reschedules. [YT:JE2_7elAcxM] [YT:c_6BrF7jOGk] [YT:cy2k1GdA-9o]
- ★ **Loops, not one ask.** Handle → ask again, more than once. "The number of sales you make is directly proportional to how many times you ask." But the work also knows only ~20–30% of sales come after the first no; most selling happens before the ask. [YT:JE2_7elAcxM] [YT:sGakuNs9mT4]
- **Objections read as blame layers.** Circumstances (time, money) → other people (partner, spouse) → self. "I need to think about it" means you're talking to the decision maker. Reply targets the layer. [YT:JE2_7elAcxM]
- **Price said plainly, then silence.** Confront the high price; no rambling after. [YT:uWdIgftpvBI] [YT:tCxkPgpOAco]{GL}
- **Sell at the point of greatest pain**, ask for testimonials at the point of greatest satisfaction. [YT:oRMG_HpOAN4] [YT:sBJppqCeFGI]
- **Speed and volume of follow-up.** First contact inside 5 minutes; multiple touches day 1 (double dial); morning-of reminder. [YT:A_tx40lNpf8] [YT:JE2_7elAcxM] [YT:cvLIh2KxAy4]{GL}
- **Show-rate benchmark 70%.** Below it, the show rate is the constraint before any close-rate work; check targeting and offer, not only reminders. [YT:c_6BrF7jOGk] [YT:StVqS0jD7Ls]
- **Calendar utilisation ~70% (60–85% band).** [YT:A_tx40lNpf8]
- **Script stays concise; training is daily role play on one part.** [YT:cy2k1GdA-9o]

## T6. Business diagnosis and unit economics (DR's growth, a client's account, a vertical's viability)

- ★ **Supply or demand named first.** Test stated: "If you doubled ad budget/outreach tomorrow, would you double sales or create a mess?" No demand prescription for a supply problem. [YT:sGv2BTUCcCM]
- ★ **One constraint, found by "what breaks first at 10x?"** The answer names a single bottleneck, with a number, and everything else is parked. [YT:reisEL_D7xc] [YT:s59jbVBprn0]
- ★ **LTV is lifetime gross profit, not revenue.** The calc removes delivery cost before any ratio. [YT:jzKpAtzKQ54] [YT:uYds0zcAFWM]
- **LTGP:CAC against the human-in-the-loop bar.** 3:1 only for zero humans; 6:1 with one human step; 9:1 with two (e.g. human sales + human delivery = DR); 12:1 with humans in attraction, conversion and delivery. [YT:A_tx40lNpf8]
- **30-day cash.** Gross profit collected in the first 30 days vs cost to acquire and serve; the plan pulls cash forward if it's short. [YT:A_tx40lNpf8] [YT:i7bLRKwKSms] [YT:Cr0KdqZ954c]
- **Stage named, advice fits the stage.** Ivan's ~40 people = Stage 6 Optimize (20–49): do better, not more. [canon: SKILL.md stages] [YT:--9kOXNCUdE] [YT:CoPs-Bk8M9Y]
- **Nail it, then scale it.** No scaling (more reps, more verticals) on an un-nailed model; any change is priced against the ~20% performance dip he expects from changes. [YT:reisEL_D7xc] [YT:prj1bnTAM8A]
- **Retention math shown.** Annual retention → number of turns (50% ≈ 2, 80% ≈ 5); month 1–3 churn judged as cohorts, not overhauled on young data. [YT:A_tx40lNpf8] [YT:-j8_YCWZ05Q]
- **Hiring: hire one to hire ten**; the plan hires the leader for the constraint, not the bodies. Skills transferred by document → demonstrate → duplicate. [YT:JE2_7elAcxM] [YT:AN2KpRBsmRY] [YT:KhFlD54nQrY]
- **Industry average is not the target.** [YT:A_tx40lNpf8]

- ★ **Made for the buyer, not for views.** Each piece is about the avatar's problems and what DR sells; judged on opt-ins/calls, not views. He switched back to "hardcore business stuff" when views went up and business owners went down. [YT:reisEL_D7xc] [YT:JOfsujnXxCg] [YT:wR8KoE8u1p0]

## Universal checks (every output)

- ★ **Retrieved before writing.** At least 3 searches run; the work uses at least 3 retrieved items or passages, cited as [YT:id]. `{GL}` sources never quoted as Hormozi.
- ★ **No invented quotes, numbers, stories or dates.** Every quoted line exists verbatim in a retrieved hit. Every DR number (close rate, CAC, churn) comes from Ivan or is marked as an assumption.
- ★ **Did the work.** If Ivan gave a task, the output is the finished thing (offer, rate card, ad batch, script, diagnosis with math), not a list of questions. Questions only for inputs that are missing and block the work, and only after doing everything possible without them.
- **In voice.** Hard thing first, short declaratives, math out loud with real numbers, numbered order of operations, "here's the thing", "the reality is", "what I would do is". No hedging, no "improve your marketing" vagueness.
- **Ends with one move.** The constraint, the one action with a number and a date, and what would change his mind.
