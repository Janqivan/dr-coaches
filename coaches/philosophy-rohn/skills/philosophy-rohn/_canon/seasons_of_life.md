# THE SEASONS OF LIFE (1981)

Source: *The Seasons of Life* [SOL] via full-passage reprints (propertyupdate.com.au/the-seasons-of-life-jim-rohn, mypropertyvalue.com.au/the-seasons-of-life-part-2-jim-rohn, meaningring.com/2015/12/09/the-seasons-of-life-by-jim-rohn); official article [JR: "seasons-of-life-recognizing-where-you-are"]; the Ant Philosophy [GM].

The premise: life moves in seasons, in every domain — career, relationships, business, health, learning. You cannot change the seasons; you can change yourself. The strategies differ by season, and most operators misread their season.

> "Life is like the changing seasons—you cannot change the seasons, but you can change yourself." [SU/SOL]
> "You cannot skip a season." / "Work with the season you're in. Not the season you wish you were in." [JR]

## WINTER — hardship, loss, difficulty

> "Let's look at what I consider to be the first major lesson in life to learn, and that is how to handle the winters." [SOL]
> "They come regularly, right after autumn." [SOL]
> "There are economic winters, social winters, and personal winters." [SOL]
> "The big question is what do you do about winters?" [SOL]
> "The winters won't change, but you can." [SOL]
> "You cannot change the circumstances. But you can get stronger, wiser, better." [SOL]

Shoaff's counsel for winter, as Rohn carries it [SOL; Shoaff-attributed in SU]:
> "Don't wish it were easier, wish you were better. Don't wish for fewer problems, wish for more skills. Don't wish for less challenge, wish for more wisdom."

Lesson: prepare for winter during summer; get stronger during winter for spring.

## SPRING — opportunity, planting

> "Fortunately, following the turbulence of winter comes the season of activity and opportunity called springtime. It is the season for entering the fertile fields of life with seed, knowledge, commitment, and a determined effort." [SOL]
> "Everyone has to get good at one of two things: planting in the spring or begging in the fall." [SOL]
> "It is the promise of spring that as we sow, so shall we also reap. Faith further provides to us an irrevocable law decreed in heaven which assures that for every disciplined human effort we will receive a multiple reward." [SOL]
> "Do not pause too long to soak in the aroma of the blossoming flowers, lest you awaken to find springtime gone with your seed still in your sack." [SOL]
> "My suggestion is to choose action, not rest. Choose truth, not fantasy. Choose a smile, not a frown." [SOL]
> "Get busy quickly on your springs, your opportunities. There are just a handful of springs that have been handed to each of us." [SOL]
> "Spring is opportunity. And spring always follows winter." / "Opportunity follows difficulty. Expansion follows recession." [SOL]

Lesson: plant aggressively while you can. Springs are countable.

## SUMMER — defending the crop

> "Learn how to nourish and protect your crops all summer." [SOL]
> "All values must be defended. Social values, political values, friendship values, business values—all must be defended." [SOL]
> "Every garden must be tended all summer. If you don't develop this skill, you'll never wind up with anything of value." [SOL]
> "We must all wage an intense, lifelong battle against the constant downward pull. If we relax, the bugs and weeds of negativity will move into the garden and take away everything of value." [S60/S70]

From the diseases seminar — the war frame [YT:0sG31mTbHJU]: "If democracy sleeps, guess who never sleeps? Tyranny... Weeds are no match for human activity. But if you stand still, how far in will they come? All the way." And: "If you rest too long, the jungle overtakes the village."

Lesson: all good will be attacked. Daily defense is the discipline of summer.

## FALL — harvest and reckoning

> "Learn how to reap in the fall without complaint." [SOL]
> "Learn how to reap in the fall without apology if you have done well and without complaint if you have not." [SOL]
> "Take full responsibility for what happens to you." [SOL]

Lesson: full responsibility for the harvest AND the absence of harvest. No blaming the winter for an empty fall.

## THE ANT PHILOSOPHY — seasons compressed [GM]

> "I think everybody should study ants."
> "Ants never quit... If they're headed somewhere and you try to stop them, they'll look for another way."
> "Ants think winter all summer. That's an important perspective." / "Ants think summer all winter. That is so important."
> "How much will an ant gather during the summer to prepare for the winter? All he possibly can."
> "Never give up, look ahead, stay positive and do all you can."

## Seasons timing wisdom [JR/S70]

> "Learn to use the seasons of life. Decide when to pour it on and when to ease back, when to take advantage and when to let things ride." [JR]
> "Some people plant in the spring and leave in the summer. If you're signed up for a season, see it through." [S70]

## DIAGNOSTIC (coaching protocol)

1. Name the domain, then name the season honestly. Common misreads: running spring playbooks (planting, launching) when it's summer (defend, execute); treating fall's reckoning as someone else's winter.
2. Season-appropriate move: Winter → get stronger, wiser, better; prepare. Spring → plant aggressively, now. Summer → defend daily, tend, kill weeds early. Fall → reap without apology or complaint; take full responsibility; count the harvest honestly.
3. One question that cuts: "Is this a winter that happened TO you, or a fall you planted?"
