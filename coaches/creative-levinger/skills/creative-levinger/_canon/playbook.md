## 1. Working now (2026) — ranked plays

**1. Brief every ad to one mental stage (TEAP: Trigger → Exploration → Evaluation → Purchase), not to a funnel tier.**
What to make: audit live ads by "What mental state am I speaking to?" [quote src: YT:po85BKDTG-Y]; fill the empty stages (usually trigger and exploration). Trigger = name the moment; exploration = talk about the category; evaluation = proof, comparisons, table-style ads; purchase = offer, founder, long form. Why per her: Andromeda cannot predict the stage, but "your creative can speak directly to it"; conversion ads that died were often exploration/evaluation ads in disguise. Who: all. GEN. Sources: [YT:po85BKDTG-Y] (2026-04), [YT:pV0tM1gSJiU] (2026-06), [YT:gRQ1y0fLpWY] (2026-02), [YT:RCrvd8pTUzo] (2026-04), tested across ~150 strategies. → craft §2.1, msg §1.3 §2.6, test §2.2

**2. Write to the WHEN (trigger moment), not the avatar.**
What to make: hooks and scripts built on a hyper-specific situation ("Do you know what her Tuesday at like 2:00 p.m. looks like?"), life events, tipping-point frustrations. Why: her most repeated 2025–26 idea (~16 mentions); Andromeda is "an interest-based algorithm" that matches moments; a client's all-product TOF only worked once rebuilt around the life moment. GEN/DTC. Sources: [YT:in1l2TPFc1c] (2026-09), [YT:pV0tM1gSJiU] (2026-06), [YT:po85BKDTG-Y] (2026-04), [YT:RpRSFn5pznc] (2026-05). → msg §1.2, craft §1.11

**3. Make it behave like content first; blend in as organic, then make one thing weird.**
What to make: creator-looking post (lo-fi, one take, two lines of text, human mistakes) with one broken expectation — object in the wrong place, about to fall, odd motion. Pass her FYP test: fits between two creator videos, stranger feels it in <1 s, opens on a human moment before a claim. Why: Meta runs ads through the same prediction layer as a For You page before the auction; "you're trying to market a 2018 ad in a for you page ecosystem." GEN. Sources: [YT:sdEV9rZ70BY] (2025-12), [YT:CwSAZdtqemk] (2026-02), [YT:a5ruZB-Urlw] (2026-07), [YT:cJlBabiKX9k] (2026-04). → craft §1.4 §2.5 §2.11, test §1.1 §2.1

**4. Lo-fi "yap" video: one take, phone, off-center, no cuts, no b-roll, no lighting.**
Why: the brain's ad filter finds no ad signals (clean cuts, "suspiciously good lighting", read-from-script voice). Lemme sleep-gummies yap beat creative "probably 40 times more expensive." DTC/GEN. Sources: [YT:a5ruZB-Urlw] (2026-07), [YT:ukbQeecMU60] (2026-06). → craft §1.8 §2.3

**5. Micro movie instead of AIDA: setup → tension → emotional payoff; product at the shift (~40–50% in).**
What to make: 15–60 s+ "tiny film", viewer asking "where is this going?"; write the human story first ignoring the product; product makes an entrance and stays, never the whole video. Why: AIDA "flatlines every single time" [quote src: YT:UAvSFd3qF8c]; Norse winner (product at ~49 s, ~3 s selling) outlived the hard-sell version; four-minute supplement confession carried an account while AIDA ads died in days. GEN. Sources: [YT:8WRS3-y4K98] (2025-12), [YT:sdEV9rZ70BY] (2025-12), [YT:HuI3o3TBI4A] (2026-03), [YT:vBotjaDGNQI] (2026-07). → craft §1.6 §2.4, msg §1.9

**6. Build the first second from 300-ms elements, not words.**
What to make: open on (1) a human face mid-emotion, (2) motion that doesn't match the scroll, (3) a visual that breaks format, or (4) a precognitive sound (e.g. a phone call). Spoken hook can land ~3 s. Why: brain decides ad vs content at 100 ms, feel at 300 ms, stay/leave by 1 s; "We are off by about 2.7 seconds." AutoBrush mouth shot: 40% hook rate. GEN. Sources: [YT:DEoj28QXUYE] (2026-06), [YT:vBotjaDGNQI] (2026-07). → craft §1.3 §2.9

**7. The five 2026-07 test formats: literal POV, multi-series, subtle flex, micro-learning carousel, lo-fi yap.**
Literal POV = camera at viewer's eyeline, viewer's hand in frame (answers "Is this me?"); multi-series = same ad type with swapped elements (mere exposure builds by 3rd–4th sighting); subtle flex = one near-wordless iconic image; micro-learning = teach-before-ask carousel, card 1 = icon grid of what you'll learn. Why: each sits on a named mechanism (mirror neurons, mere exposure, contrast/fluency, reciprocity). GEN/DTC. Source: [YT:a5ruZB-Urlw] (2026-07). → craft §2.3

**8. The five "money maker" statics: before-the-problem-had-a-name, social mirror, reframe, specificity, earned confidence.**
What to make: test all five up front per brand; lay winners side by side, tag by format/mechanism, brief the gap. A format pays only when it matches mental state, reduces decision friction and carries emotional certainty. Why: "not trends… not going to disappear in 6 months" [quote src: YT:6ZBccBr5AV4]. DTC. Source: [YT:6ZBccBr5AV4] (2026-05). → craft §2.2, test §2.5

**9. Headlines move a belief one step (Social Judgment Theory ladder).**
What to make: write the anchor belief that blocks purchase; rewrite the headline at intensities 1–5; diagnose each as indifference / acceptance / rejection; ship the middle, push harder only if needed. Why: almost every ad she studied "literally just sits inside the current belief system"; indifference is "where most ads sit." Rise rewrite went from discount-dumping to "I could probably buy off of this ad." GEN. Source: [YT:RCrvd8pTUzo] (2026-04, ~14 mentions). → msg §1.4 §2.5

**10. Aim each hook at one exact emotion, one ring past the core of the feelings wheel; keep words, music and delivery in the same valence/intensity zone.**
Why: blended reactions mean the hook isn't targeting anything; mismatched channels make an ad "pulling itself apart" (Norse loser: zone-4 words, zone-2 hype delivery). Test in the middle of intensity; Zone 4 (negative/high) is very expensive to live there. GEN. Sources: [YT:LxWwRykBtfc] (2026-03), [YT:HuI3o3TBI4A] (2026-03), [YT:RCrvd8pTUzo] (2026-04). → craft §1.9 §2.10 §2.12, msg §1.5 §1.6

**11. Run the 12-combination grid on a working angle (4 zones × actual/ideal/ought self) and name ads by combo.**
What to make: Claude writes all 12; change visual and audio hook per combo; put "zone one ideal" etc. in the ad name to see which combination is crushing. Why: stops crediting format/creator for what was delivery; self is "where I personally believe iteration lives". GEN. Sources: [YT:HuI3o3TBI4A] (2026-03), [YT:VtEoAOfQsCU] (2025-05). → test §2.3, msg §2.2 §2.4

**12. Specific beats vague; clear beats clever (processing fluency).**
What to make: "weirdly almost uncomfortably specific" copy, odd numbers, a specific "when" for each feature, headlines 4–7 words, one font, one simple deal. Why: "Vague claims feel like marketing. Specific claims feel true."; effort "reads to the brain as doubt". Good Chop's single deal beat ButcherBox's five numbers. GEN. Sources: [YT:RpRSFn5pznc] (2026-05), [YT:6ZBccBr5AV4] (2026-05), [YT:a5ruZB-Urlw] (2026-07). → msg §1.8, craft §1.5

**13. Your creative is your targeting — dress the scene for the buyer you want.**
What to make: set dressing, casting, accents, locations chosen deliberately ("if anything in your video says mom life, you're going to get moms"); founder ads told from the side of the audience you want (wife's story to reach women). Feature the user, not the buyer. GEN. Sources: [YT:sdEV9rZ70BY] (2025-12), [YT:pV0tM1gSJiU] (2026-06), [YT:vBotjaDGNQI] (2026-07). → test §1.2, craft §3.3

**14. Hero test launch: one video + two statics + GIF + carousel on the same concept.**
Why: validates the message across content types; statics isolate the message cheaply. When rebuilding a winner, pull reusable visuals and transcribe what it actually communicated. GEN/DTC. Sources: [YT:CwSAZdtqemk] (2026-02), [YT:vBotjaDGNQI] (2026-07). → test §2.6, craft §2.14

**15. Scale winners, don't micro-iterate; fight fatigue with new concepts, not hook/colour/creator swaps.**
What to make: with a winner ask "can I just spend more money on this?"; when it tires, keep the proven mechanism but change the surface obviously (multi-series) or move the message into a genuinely different story/format. Why: micro iterations get dinged by Meta's similarity score; fatigue window now 2–3 weeks (was 6+). GEN. Sources: [YT:cJlBabiKX9k] (2026-04), [YT:OiKpEOXY4zc] (2026-07), [YT:_iMPT2SsjeU] (2026-07). → test §1.4 §2.7 §2.8

**16. Make the product (or a character) a person in a story universe; founder-led for niche.**
Why: with content costs collapsed, good and poor content look the same; Vacation's "HR Associate Neil Smithson" makes people buy because they like Neil. DTC/GEN. Sources: [YT:sdEV9rZ70BY] (2025-12), [YT:ukbQeecMU60] (2026-06). → craft §1.12

**17. Trust tone: be straight, calm, grounded; no competitor trashing, no "bestie" voice, no shame.**
What to make: say the obvious nobody in the category says; show behind the curtain; use embarrassment ("everybody does this"), not shame. Why: her >250-consumer survey — 65% turned off by trashing competitors; consumers say "please stop trying to be my friend"; shame-led ads run higher CPA (anecdotal). GEN. Sources: [YT:qUn6OV_70fQ] (2026-05), [YT:LxWwRykBtfc] (2026-03), [YT:ukbQeecMU60] (2026-06). → msg §1.7 §1.10 §1.11

**18. Move trigger/exploration volume to organic; promote what works into paid.**
Why: "the ad account is meant to convert"; "if you don't have an organic ecosystem, your ads are very expensive." Her own business runs zero ads. GEN. Sources: [YT:ZMXFvjYZoE0] (2026-05), [YT:pV0tM1gSJiU] (2026-06). → test §1.7, craft §1.10

## 2. Converts in general — principles in 2025/older evidence AND repeated in 2026

1. **Emotion first; how you say it beats what you say.** 2024-10 "we are emotional creatures who think not thinking creatures who emote" → 2026-06 strategy is "how do we need to communicate this message". The more specific the emotion, the better. [YT:MAwkpQWEQS8] (2024-10), [YT:VtEoAOfQsCU] (2025-05), [YT:LxWwRykBtfc] (2026-03), [YT:pV0tM1gSJiU] (2026-06). → msg §1.5
2. **Processing fluency / clear over clever.** Headlines 4–7 words (2025-05), one simple deal (2025-02), one font and no puns (2026-04), near-wordless winners (2026-07). Third-grade test. [YT:zDHkSSsjFDw], [YT:VtEoAOfQsCU], [YT:RCrvd8pTUzo], [YT:a5ruZB-Urlw]. → craft §1.5, msg §1.8
3. **Specificity sells.** Identity specificity (2025-02 Dad Gang), specific "whens" (2025-12 bully sticks), uncomfortably specific copy and Porsche-era "very specific when" [quote src: YT:RpRSFn5pznc] (2026-05). Caveat: broad language OK where the audience knows the category. → msg §1.8 §6
4. **Blend in, then one distinct element (von Restorff).** "This is why I love ugly ads" [quote src: YT:VtEoAOfQsCU] (2025-05) → "get weird with it" [quote src: YT:DEoj28QXUYE] and 300-ms broken-format visuals (2026-02/06). "Contrast equals memorability." [quote src: YT:VtEoAOfQsCU] → craft §1.4 §2.11
5. **Image carries the feeling; copy backs it up.** Images ~60,000x faster than text; statics fail when image and copy "fight against each other" [quote src: YT:MAwkpQWEQS8] (2024-10); 2026 subtle-flex and near-wordless statics extend it. → craft §3.2 §2.3
6. **Valence × intensity: match words, music, delivery; avoid living in Zone 4.** Taught 2025-05, diagnostic of the Norse loser 2026-03, belief-ladder intensities 2026-04. → msg §2.1 §1.6
7. **Identity and the three selves.** Identity call-outs "typically run cheaper than everything else in the ad account" [quote src: YT:VtEoAOfQsCU] (2025-05); ads "validate which self is switched on" [quote src: YT:VtEoAOfQsCU]; 12-combo grid built on it in 2026-03. → test §1.12, msg §2.2
8. **Story over pitch.** Participation era (2025-02) → "resonance more than persuasion" [quote src: YT:sdEV9rZ70BY] and micro movies (2025-12) → story is the 2026 skill; AIDA flatlines when overused. → msg §1.9 §6, craft §1.6
9. **Stage-match the message (TEAP).** Working for her 18 months as of 2025-12; 2026-04 replaces TOFU/MOFU/BOFU outright; failed conversion ads are stage mismatches. → msg §2.6, test §1.6
10. **Failed ads are usually emotional-intensity or stage mismatch, not a bad offer.** "It's almost always an emotional intensity issue" [quote src: YT:VtEoAOfQsCU] (2025-05); discounts as "a crutch and it's a very expensive one" [quote src: YT:po85BKDTG-Y] (2026-04). → test §1.6
11. **Loss aversion inside the message, not in a discount.** "hates discounts" (2025-05); "Discounts are not going to close the gap, they're just going to wallpaper over it" [quote src: YT:po85BKDTG-Y] (2026-04). But a true purchase ad must carry an offer. → msg §1.16
12. **Steal from organic creators, not competitor ads.** Weekly organic scroll (2024-10 cadence → 2026-02/06); look-alike brands come from watching competitors' ads; 2026 libraries of 80 and 497 organic formats. → msg §1.13
13. **Statics are the cheap message-validation layer.** statics alone can scale an account (2024-10) → "double down" (2025-12) → 2026-02 hero test still ships two statics + GIF + carousel per video concept. → craft §1.7 §6
14. **Precision before volume; most ads fail.** "me and volume have some beef" [quote src: YT:vrBYS4-Gr4o] (2024-10 → 2026-06); expect ~80% failure, power-law outcomes (2026-07); "test everything" inflates CAC (2026-02). → test §1.3 §1.5 §1.10
15. **Keep a proven message, change its delivery.** Four-year unbeaten core message run as UGC, pro video and founder video (2025-05: "the core message never changed" [quote src: YT:VtEoAOfQsCU]); 2026-07 multi-series keeps the mechanism, changes the surface. → test §4 §2.7
16. **Hooks need open loops (shock or alignment) and a resolution.** Two lines of text with an open-loop top line (2025-12); 0–2 s grab with open-loop text (2026-02); phone-call pauses as loops (2026-06). "Anything you come in with, you need to go out with." [quote src: YT:sBS1vy-5Be0] → craft §2.6 §2.8 §3.3

## 4. Hooks and watch time

**The window (dated).** By 100 ms ad vs content, 300 ms feel or not, ~700 ms understands, 1 s stay/leave; "by the time you hit that 3 second mark, the whole thing is over" (2026-06). One second ≈ 5–6 spoken words. Working reconcile: visual/sonic grab in <1 s, spoken hook can land ~3 s. [YT:DEoj28QXUYE] → craft §5 §6

**Hook types her evidence backs most**
- **Four 300-ms elements (NOW):** face mid-emotion; motion that doesn't match the scroll; visual that breaks format (laptop in a fridge, "Is it real or is it cake?"); precognitive sound (phone conversation). Every hook = visual + sound/silence + text. "No more templates." [YT:DEoj28QXUYE] (2026-06) → craft §2.9
- **Product-free visual open:** AutoBrush inner-mouth shot beat ~15 alternatives; brand only on end card. [YT:vBotjaDGNQI] (2026-07)
- **Odd environment / about to fall / object made odd:** shampoo stuffed in a car, product halfway off the table (catch reflex, comments), square Babybel ("If your baby bell was a square, you would not be able to open it."). [YT:CwSAZdtqemk] (2026-02) → craft §3.1
- **Open-loop text in the first 2 s:** the grab is separate from the payoff people credit (car crash held by text first). Top static line states the topic as a loop ("Here's why I hate menopause") + speech captions below. [YT:CwSAZdtqemk] (2026-02), [YT:sBS1vy-5Be0] (2025-12) → craft §2.8 §3.3
- **Faces and animals:** kittens post with 398,000 likes — faces did the stopping, nobody read the text; "Anytime you put an animal in an ad, it's probably gonna work." [YT:CwSAZdtqemk] → craft §3.1
- **High-potency words on mundane things:** "today's victim", "buckle up", "she accidentally did..." — don't make it political. [YT:sBS1vy-5Be0] (2025-12)
- **Data or "when" hooks for trigger:** "three out of four men misplace their wedding band in their lifetime" (Ridge); "something just happened" storytelling. [YT:pV0tM1gSJiU] (2026-06), [YT:vBotjaDGNQI] (2026-07) → craft §2.1
- **Silent hooks are fine:** "Hooks are only meant to get the brain to stop on something." [YT:CwSAZdtqemk]

**Emotion targeting for hooks.** Pick the emotion and customer type per hook before loading; one ring past the core on the feelings wheel; compare neighbors (curious > inquisitive; pressured > rushed for TOF). Team test: if a room names different emotions, the hook targets none. [YT:LxWwRykBtfc] (2026-03) → craft §2.10

**Hooks to retire.** "I went from X to Y," "why are we still doing X?," "my main complaints about this industry" — the industry recycles "basically four different hooks" and the brain tunes them out (2026-06). Retire "unlike other brands" (2026-05). [YT:DEoj28QXUYE], [YT:qUn6OV_70fQ] → craft §3.1, msg §1.11

**Retention moves**
- Viewer should ask "where is this going?"; loops are "really only just shock or alignment these days"; close the loop personally and exit the way you entered. [YT:sdEV9rZ70BY] (2025-12)
- Don't announce what the video is about (AIDA "killed it when you got to interest" [quote src: YT:sBS1vy-5Be0]); product enters at the emotional shift, ~40–50% in. [YT:8WRS3-y4K98] (2025-12)
- Speed up cuts (one angle "for way too freaking long" is the common UGC mistake); captions 3–4 words per chunk, never one word at a time; keep small human mistakes. [YT:CwSAZdtqemk], [YT:sBS1vy-5Be0] → craft §3.3
- Long rising action then resolution (bread-clip creator held the loop to ~1:26; setup 51 s). Every story needs an emotional resolution. [YT:sBS1vy-5Be0] (2025-12) → craft §2.6 §2.7
- Score music to the dominant self (ought = somber, ideal = hype, zone 1 = calm piano) — though by 2026-07 she finds never changing the track weird. [YT:HuI3o3TBI4A], [YT:a5ruZB-Urlw]
- Review every hook sound on and sound off. [YT:CwSAZdtqemk]
