## 1. Core positions (ranked by mentions × specificity; 2026 first where it concerns what works today)

1. **Creators and partnership ads are now the main selling engine on Meta — get a creator program or lose.** [GEN/DTC · ~9 mentions] Two days at the Meta Performance Summit: Meta sees selling done primarily by creators; selling with anything else (statics or "God forbid, AI animations, which do crush") is "like bringing a butter knife to a gunfight"; expects this to reshape creative strategy "in the coming months, not years" [YT:9UBSGSS2Yuo] (2026-06). AI statics are cool but you'll probably struggle to get performance on Meta without real creators and real content [YT:eFyvp0mu7UQ] (2026-05). To have "a shot in hell" at winning Meta over the next few years, you need a creator program [YT:_VtpfVeB2Kc] (2026-08). Benchmark: 30–50% of all spend on partnership ads; under 25% = trouble, under 5% = "well and truly in trouble" [YT:4wM-i1cxtAM] (2026-09). Seed Health: when performance dips they spend more on partnership-ad content and "performance almost always bounces back" [YT:9UBSGSS2Yuo] (2026-06).

2. **Andromeda era = creative diversity + velocity, built around personas; targeting and account structure matter far less.** [GEN · ~10 mentions] Late 2025 is "the age of creative strategy after Andromeda": the most important things are creative velocity and creative diversity [YT:ZydGeio1DGQ] (2025-12). "Volume is not exactly the answer" — diversity and how fast excellent creative gets into the account [YT:x1b4h42HTG8] (2025-12). Andromeda groups ads into personas, so for every creative ask "who are we targeting with this specific creative" [YT:Lhl4o7yyuDQ][YT:3zZiKZUJmms] (2025-12); "more of a persona-based algorithm" [YT:-E9sjb2msjo] (2026-06). Interests, lookalikes, even prospecting vs retargeting are "pretty much useless"; structure far less important [YT:x1b4h42HTG8] (2025-12).

3. **"Nine times out of 10 you likely have a creative problem."** [GEN · ~6 mentions] Lost results usually = competitors with better creative ops and bigger budgets: "AKA, they probably have more better ads than you do." [YT:x1b4h42HTG8] (2025-12). Creative is "your biggest lever on the platform" [YT:7knQyPYLmfo] (2025-03). Don't quit a format before you find a winner; success hinges on a "testing mindset" [YT:7knQyPYLmfo]. "ruthless creative testing is the only way that you're gonna find success" [YT:KFFcno59vQ8] (2021-09).

4. **Separate creative testing from scaling.** [DTC/GEN · ~8 mentions] Separate testing campaign, one ad set per test, winners graduate to the scaling campaign ("a campaign of winners") [YT:KFFcno59vQ8] (2021-09) [YT:CmkWzdgl6iI] (2024-02) [YT:7knQyPYLmfo] (2025-03). Reasons: protect working ad sets, guarantee spend to new ads, read data cleanly, scale horizontally [YT:CmkWzdgl6iI]. "I've added a creative test to an otherwise performing campaign and I saw performance tank" — "I can't tell you how many times" [YT:7knQyPYLmfo]. 2026 restatement: handed $2M/month she'd still run a dedicated (non-CBO) testing campaign, but is "not romantic about it" [YT:-E9sjb2msjo] (2026-06). Exception: lean budgets (~$5–15K/month) — one ASC that is all testing [YT:7knQyPYLmfo].

5. **Big swings, not one-variable split tests.** [GEN · ~9 mentions] Regimented one-variable A/B creative testing is "totally dead" [YT:ZQAjWwxk2C0] (2024-05). "should we only change one word…?" — "absolutely not": "I am actually taking much bigger swings in my variation testing" [YT:I8tXqqfjIX4] (2026-06). Exception: an experienced brand needing a specific learning [YT:CmkWzdgl6iI]; at $1M+/month variations get finer while creative *types* get bigger [YT:7knQyPYLmfo].

6. **90% net-new, 10% iteration — and "iteration paralysis" is the common failure.** [GEN · ~7 mentions] Pushes teams to ~90/10 [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs] (2025-01) [YT:U7T3_-NnHHg] (2024-11). Iteration paralysis = iterating one look until everything looks the same, can't scale past the "minimum viable customer", faster fatigue [YT:ZQAjWwxk2C0][YT:lWpSmxuwSts][YT:qoOBIPWH_qw] (2024) [YT:pMAxIOL8dtE] (2023-12). But: iterate a winner "until it stops working" [YT:eFyvp0mu7UQ] (2026-05) [YT:p4yXXdnCgWg] (2026-08). See §6 for the tension.

7. **Primary metrics say WHAT works; storytelling metrics say WHY; the real why is in the creative.** [GEN · ~12 mentions] Primary = spend, purchases/results, cost per result, ROAS (leads/CPL for lead gen) — the only ones used to call a winner [YT:CCsty8R0UaA][YT:wRA2VgYIDVs] (2025-01). Storytelling = hook, hold, avg watch time, CTR, CPM [YT:CCsty8R0UaA]. Metrics are "the least important part" of analysis [YT:3zZiKZUJmms][YT:Lhl4o7yyuDQ] (2025-12). CTR is not an indication a creative is working [YT:3zZiKZUJmms]. The why generally isn't in the data; it's in the ad creative itself [YT:CCsty8R0UaA].

8. **Messaging beats format.** [GEN · ~4 mentions] A winner is "generally never" winning just because of format; talent, messaging and imagery move performance most [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs] (2025-01). If one variation wins it's likely the messaging — put it into drastically different formats [YT:lWpSmxuwSts] (2024-11). Format often isn't what converts [YT:Lhl4o7yyuDQ] (2025-12).

New pixel: creative tests before audience tests [YT:KFFcno59vQ8]. Even a new brand starts with a sales campaign optimizing for purchases, never traffic/engagement/ATC [YT:7knQyPYLmfo] (2025-03).

10. **AI = junior strategist/assistant that amplifies thinking, never a replacement; judgment and taste are the skill.** [GEN · ~14 mentions] "I treat AI like it's my junior creative strategist or my marketing assistant"; "the goal isn't to replace your strategic thinking, it's to amplify it" [YT:UGiN8aVy2l8] (2026-05). No fully AI briefs or scripts; "a heavy hand" adapting them [YT:-E9sjb2msjo] (2026-06).

## 2. Frameworks and methods

### 2.1 Three testing setups by budget stage (2025) [DTC]
When: choosing account structure; testing at $3M/month is vastly different from starting out. [YT:7knQyPYLmfo] (2025-03)
- **Lean (~$5K–15K/month or less):** one Advantage+ Shopping (ASC) sales campaign that is all testing. Launch ~10 creatives, add 1–2 a week; only turn ads off if they miss CPA goal while still taking lots of spend. Why: most consolidated (exits learning on low budget, more chances to convert) and easiest to manage — no ad sets, audiences, prospecting/retargeting. Get creative learnings first, then try other structures. Expects past viewers to be "a little mad" [quote src: YT:7knQyPYLmfo].
- **Growth (~$1K/day, $30K–50K/month):** keep the ASC as scaling campaign + a manual creative testing campaign. One ad set per concept, 3–6 creatives (3 on lower budgets, 6–7 with more), $100–$200 per ad set per day. Most brands test mostly video; 4–6 variations with "much bigger swings" [quote src: YT:I8tXqqfjIX4] in messaging, different hooks, different video lengths. Duplicate winners into the ASC. Credits Barry Hott's course for the structure; "widely accepted as a best practice" [quote src: YT:7knQyPYLmfo].
- **Scale (>$1M/month):** same single testing campaign (she's seen a $5M/month brand with just one); 10–20+ tests a week, launched ~$200/day, ~7 days or ~$1,000 spent; nearly all use ABO; many add cost caps so only "the best of the best" [quote src: YT:3zZiKZUJmms] surface; variations get finer, creative *types* get bigger (celebrities, bigger influencers, high-production founder ad after a lo-fi one worked). Agency-specific testing campaigns are "only doing this for reporting reasons" [quote src: YT:7knQyPYLmfo].

### 2.2 Thesis "bare-bones" testing structure (2021) [DTC]
[YT:KFFcno59vQ8] (2021-09)
1. Separate testing campaign (named like `T_prospecting_testing_USA`) + core prospecting and retargeting campaigns holding only top performers.
2. Each test = own ad set, numbered chronologically, named for what it tests. Only ad-unit elements tested (format, net-new asset, thumbnail, primary text, headline, description, CTA) — never bids, budget, audiences. Usually one element per test (2021 stance).
3. Two test categories: **net new** (never-tested asset) and **iterative** (variations on a winner — thumbnail, messaging, copy, CTA).
4. 3–6 ads per test ad set; six seen as optimal.
5. Budget: 50 optimization events/week → at $35 CPA, $1,750/week ≈ $250/day per test (not militant).
6. Broad audiences only.
7. Copy tests get their own ad set (Facebook doesn't report which text option won).
8. Wait ≥3 days of spend. Three scenarios: below-average CPA with 1–2 potential winners / average / bad.
9. Optimize at ad level first; kill ads at ~2x account average CPA; no winners after 5–7 days → turn off ad set.
10. Winner: scale inside the test (~20% every 3 days, sometimes 50%+) 2–3 times, then duplicate into core. Never turn off the good test ad set after duplicating.
11. Testing share of spend: 15–65% across clients; start ~20%.
12. Best for $20K+/month; small brands may do better dripping creatives into core.

### 2.3 2024 structure: ASC + testing campaign [DTC]
[YT:CmkWzdgl6iI] (2024-02)
- One ASC + separate creative testing campaign; one new ad set per test (e.g., Test 1 features point-out image, Test 2 us-vs-them), up to 3 ads, with a test description of how variants differ.
- Start each test at $200/day for brands >$100K/month; $5 ad sets are over; minimum $50–100/day; ~7 days; great after 3–4 days → scale 20% to pressure test; all metrics bad after 3–4 days → off.
- 2–4 variations, much bigger swings; variations bake iteration into net-new concepts.
- ABO vs CBO = media buyer's choice; she uses ABO as an "old school" buyer.

### 2.4 Rapid format testing + format prioritization [GEN]
For new brands/accounts — biggest source of success; "the new hook rate is more like 1 second", so how the ad shows up on the phone drives engagement. Prioritize formats by (1) untested low-hanging fruit that works across the board, (2) expected gain, then reorder by (3) time to execute — a studio founder ad may be a big unlock but an ugly ad can be made tomorrow. [YT:CmkWzdgl6iI] (2024-02). Multi-product brands: a format that wins for one product gets made for all (Arrae). [YT:CmkWzdgl6iI]

### 2.5 Four-step creative analysis: Metrics → Content → Comparison → Feedback [GEN]
[YT:wRA2VgYIDVs] (2025-01) [YT:yNTlVKvuX0o] (2025-03) [YT:3zZiKZUJmms][YT:Lhl4o7yyuDQ] (2025-12) — ~5 mentions.
1. **Metrics** — what the numbers say; least important step; primary KPIs only tell which creatives worked ("get the most amount of spend and results" [quote src: YT:Lhl4o7yyuDQ]).
2. **Content** — most important: format (deeper than image/video: us-vs-them, features point-out, founder, statistic-based), creator/talent, messaging, imagery (production quality, setting), pacing. No one-to-one metric mapping; look at trends over several tests.
3. **Comparison** — vs your top performers, vs overall campaign, vs competitors, industry, platform trends — the step "a lot of people miss out on" [quote src: YT:wRA2VgYIDVs]. No analysis is complete without comparative data [YT:CCsty8R0UaA].
4. **Feedback** — far more than ad comments.
- Analyze first, then tweak; data answers only two questions: what is working, and why [YT:CCsty8R0UaA] (2025-01).
- Full deep analysis only for big swings or "a unicorn banger" [YT:CCsty8R0UaA].
- Categorize first (video type, creator, static format), then compare; keeps a training document of categories across formats, messaging, imagery, talent [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs].

### 2.6 Ads Manager column setup (2025) [GEN]
[YT:CCsty8R0UaA] (2025-01) [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs]
- **Primary (far left):** amount spent, purchases, cost per purchase, ROAS (or leads/CPL). "show you if your campaign is working… everything else is going to tell you why." [quote src: YT:CCsty8R0UaA]
- **Reach:** frequency, reach, impressions, cost per reach, CPM.
- **Storytelling:** unique outbound CTR, cost per link click, hook rate, hold rate, video avg play time, post shares.
- Formulas: hook rate = 3-sec views / impressions; hold rate = ThruPlays / impressions (later: % watching first 15s); conversion rate = purchases / link clicks [YT:rNsOaXtlmRM] (2022-04) [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs].
- First move: sort by spend high→low, cross-check purchases; scale ads with lower cost per result and comparatively higher spend and results [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs]. Anything with a ton of spend that isn't working = first to fix or turn off [YT:CCsty8R0UaA].
- Breakdowns per test: age, gender, placement. Spend pooling at the wrong age (menopause ad delivering to 30–35) = messaging misaligned [YT:pMAxIOL8dtE] (2023-12); creative pulling 50+ and working → make more for them [YT:CCsty8R0UaA].

### 2.7 Storytelling-metric diagnosis [GEN]
[YT:yNTlVKvuX0o] (2025-03) [YT:rNsOaXtlmRM][YT:Yi_1Vb4sfoQ] (2022)
- Low hook + low hold → not resonating, back to drawing board.
- High hook/hold, low CTR → wrong audience or too little target audience for that creative.
- High hook winner → make more hooks similar in visuals, VO, messaging.
- Hook rate = "clickbait potential"; hold rate = follow-up holding power [YT:KFFcno59vQ8].
- Use hold rate to test module order (social proof or competitor comparison right after the problem agitator vs a how-to) [YT:Yi_1Vb4sfoQ] (2022-08).
- CTR on broad is a "false flag" unless tracked over time; declining CTR over an ad's life can signal fatigue [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs]. High CPM = creative costs too much to deliver; low CTR + high CPC = not clicking [YT:CCsty8R0UaA].
- Ads that do well 1–2 weeks then fall off a cliff = product/solution-aware creative that churned through that stage → go up-funnel; unaware-style content brought "major unlocks" [YT:ZydGeio1DGQ] (2025-12).

### 2.8 Iteration rules [GEN]
- **"True iteration" (strict):** (1) swap the hook on a winner; (2) put a winning hook onto a creative that didn't get enough juice; (3) swap the messaging only. Only on "Banger" winners [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs] (2025-01). "iterate that thing until it dies" [YT:U7T3_-NnHHg] (2024-11).
- **Format iteration:** winning variation → likely messaging → put it into drastically different formats (video winner → range of images) [YT:lWpSmxuwSts] (2024-11).
- 9 of 10 times don't iterate losers; exception = clear editing/messaging mistake [YT:lWpSmxuwSts].
- **Iteration analysis loop:** analyze each test (usually 1–2 clear winners) → find shared messaging/imagery/creator/angle/format → extract the bigger learning → spin into quick formats and longer-term creator/TV content → keep pressure-testing [YT:CmkWzdgl6iI] (2024-02).
- A learning counts only after being pressure-tested 5–6 times [YT:ZQAjWwxk2C0]; "I never really have a learning unless I pressure test it" [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs].
- Keep challenging learnings: test a winning message against a new golden-nugget review [YT:CmkWzdgl6iI].
- One learning into many formats builds an ecosystem because "people need to see things more than once"; brands would do better "by doing a lot less" [YT:eFyvp0mu7UQ] (2026-05).
- Andromeda-era rethink: all statics in one ad set 1–2x/month; each video test aims for widest visual range, sometimes mixing images or short clips with creator content [YT:ZydGeio1DGQ] (2025-12).
- 3–6 hook variations in every video test; reformat top performers with all nine hook types as one big test [YT:I8tXqqfjIX4] (2026-06).
- David and Goliath concept: vary the Goliath (enemy) first — biggest performance differentiator [YT:ePcEcT2PH8E] (2026-04).

### 2.9 Million-dollar iteration playbook (onboarding an account) [GEN]
[YT:2jVcLu3Earg] (2025-05)
1. Pull top ~20 ads of the last year; glance at sale/BFCM ads, focus on evergreen winners; jot low-hanging iterations.
2. Test winning messaging in new formats.
3. Pair proven messaging with wildly different visuals. Every idea needs performance evidence.
- Launch the 20–30 (up to 50) low-hanging iterations together in one net-new ASC: proven content, goal is lift, not reading every variant.
- "sometimes your really slight minute changes can actually have a really really big impact" [quote src: YT:2jVcLu3Earg] — rarely done by strategists onboarding.
- Quick win when performance slides: relaunch an ASC with the last year's top performers; no formulaic pause 30 days [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs].

### 2.10 Creative diversity: levels, squint test, content-gap check [GEN/DTC]
- **Three levels (2024):** Beginner — images, statics, carousels, creator partnership ads monthly. Intermediate (most brands, including many who think they're advanced) — sub-formats (us-vs-them, feature callouts, headline images; studio hi-fi, creator, celebrity, mashup video). Advanced — intentionally diverse creator demographics, celebrities, different messaging, core desires and awareness levels, sustained, 50+ tests/month [YT:U7T3_-NnHHg] (2024-11).
- **Levels 0–5 (2025, titled "5 levels"):** L0 statics vs video → L1 format expansion → L2 awareness expansion → L3 desire and motivation expansion → L4 audience segment and use-case expansion (personas) → L5 true creative diversity. Used at PGM to classify each brand and what to focus on [YT:ZydGeio1DGQ] (2025-12).
- **Squint test:** top 10–20 creatives in a deck, sit back and squint — similar colors, visuals, formats, over-reliance on UGC or statics? Use with Meta's diversity cheat sheet/audit template [YT:U7T3_-NnHHg].
- **Content-gap check:** % of monthly spend to images, video, carousel and partnership ads ("partnership ads is really really that big one"), and whether diversity spans the messaging wins [YT:4wM-i1cxtAM] (2026-09).
- **Diversity analyzer (in progress, 2026):** ranks an ad library against her levels; reads primary text + thumbnails because Facebook blocks LLMs from the ad library; she still can't assign a KPI to good vs bad diversity [YT:Uh0TE6nZs5A] (2026-06).

### 2.11 Meta's three updates: Andromeda, GEM, Lattice [GEN]
[YT:x1b4h42HTG8] (2025-12)
- She corrects the myth by stating what it is *not* first: Andromeda is not new (launched December 2024), not a new algorithm, doesn't choose the final ad; it's the retrieval step narrowing millions of ads to a smaller pool each time a user opens the app.
- **GEM** = generative ads recommendation model narrowing further; a Meta employee's "super brain" image — reads a library of books in seconds, may know what customers want next week.
- **Lattice** = library of all Meta's models (GEM lives there); models used to be separate (why interest/lookalike/prospecting splits used to help); Lattice makes the final selection.
- **White t-shirt metaphor:** Andromeda = personal assistant narrowing white t-shirts from many brands by knowing you and your upcoming events; GEM = super brain who knows you prefer cotton vs linen; Lattice = brings it together and picks the final shirts.
- Consequence: competitors with better creative ops win the auction; "Andromeda killed my results" [quote src: YT:x1b4h42HTG8] is "not exactly true" [quote src: YT:x1b4h42HTG8].

### 2.12 Weekly creative operating rhythm [GEN]

### 2.13 Pact account structure (case she presented) [DTC]
[YT:R3Z1VvvgtzU] (2026-04) — Two buckets (BAU + dynamic catalog ads); each ad set = one product category, each collection treated "like an individual business"; value (ROAS-goal) bidding per ad set. Value vs volume: volume = 10 conversions beat 7; value = 7 buyers at $100 beat 10 at $25; at Pact ($15 underwear vs $150 sets) volume bidding would dump spend into underwear. Plus an AI upload agent (see 2.15). She praises team-wide AI only where business impact is big; has often seen AI adoption be a distraction.

### 2.14 AI research and ideation workflows [GEN]
- **Review mining (2023):** paste all reviews into ChatGPT; ask for (1) pain points, (2) transformations, (3) failed attempts (to twist the knife more), (4) "golden nuggets" — the phrase alone finds the best lines [YT:pMAxIOL8dtE] (2023-12).
- **Review analysis question set (2026):** strengths/weaknesses; most common complaints/objections; similar or dissimilar to the reputation analysis; trends across compiled golden nuggets; trigger points that cause purchase [YT:yRgPbqywUJ8] (2026-07).
- **Persona discovery:** review CSV + reputation analysis + internal PDF → their prompt → a deck the team reviews together. Goal: new personas → net-new audiences [YT:yRgPbqywUJ8]. Reputation analysis and review analysis become context docs for any LLM.
- **Messaging expansion:** give AI the top 5 messaging points that worked; ask for more strategies, "add more emotional trigger words", make it more top of funnel — produced top performers. Changes process "nearly monthly" [YT:2jVcLu3Earg] (2025-05). Next level: include why each past winner worked [YT:ITM4faRVzF8].
- **Trigger-word pass before launch:** upload top hooks, have the model inject emotional trigger words into a hook structure already converting — "a really simple step" that lifted many recent creatives [YT:wfENnO7PA5Q] (2025-02) [YT:I8tXqqfjIX4] (2026-06).
