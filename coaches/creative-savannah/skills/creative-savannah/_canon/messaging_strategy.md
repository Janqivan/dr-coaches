## 2. Frameworks and methods

### Savannah's frameworks

**2.1 Motivators and barriers** [GEN] — use it when choosing what to test.
1. List every reason that encourages or prevents buying.
2. Pick the top ones that are distinct enough to be separate tests.
Her example is the intro-message split test (§4, for women by women). — [YT:PvjkCV9WelU] (2020-04)

**2.2 Funnel messaging ladder** [GEN] — use it to decide what each stage says.
- TOF: awareness plus the top value props.
- MOF/BOF: assume they visited and didn't buy, ask why, and answer the objection with testimonials, 5-star reviews, PR, FAQs and real customer stories.
- Retargeting must go deeper and must not repeat the top three value props: reviews, social proof, value/price justification, warranty, shipping, special offer.
- Retargeting answers two questions: is it worth it (price), and is it a scam / will it show up / will it break (uncertainty).
- Category rule: casino and lottery apps have no consideration period, so be experimental with comedy and extreme reactions in the first two seconds. Considered or expensive apps need social proof, PR, guarantees and an offer.
— [YT:3TzdwzAG1X8] (2025-03), [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA] (2024-03), [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06)

**2.3 Skeptic storyline** [DTC] — a storyline she tests "time and time again" [quote src: YT:nAr5wmbnwP8] for cold traffic.
1. Saw it all over TikTok.
2. Didn't know if it was legit (dropshipping? false claims? will it arrive?).
3. "I'll be the test dummy for you" [quote src: YT:wrbvSulohNI].
4. Authentic reaction.
It meets the inherently skeptical cold viewer. — [YT:wrbvSulohNI] (2023-06)

**2.4 Weekly production line** [GEN] — her operating system.
- 2024-03: shot list and scripts to creators by Friday; film weekend to Tuesday; edit Wednesday to Thursday; deliver to clients every Friday. All iPhone. [DTC]
- 2024-05: Mon–Wed research (inspiration, hooks, best performers from other clients and the current client in Motion); Thu–Fri scripts and shot list out; footage back Mon/Tue; edit Wed/Thu; ads to clients Friday. 2–6 ads per client per week.
- 2025-10: shoot every Monday with in-house creators/actors. She personally briefs editors. Every ad ships with partnership (whitelisting) access, and each client gets a custom Motion dashboard.
- 2026-01: shoot Mon–Tue, edit Wed–Thu, deliver 4–12 ads per client on Friday for review (by package size), every week.
- Revisions: client gets a first draft, revisions land in the next few days, then launch.
- Research is the biggest phase (full three days, sometimes not enough). Scripting, footage and editing are "rinse and repeat" [quote src: YT:ZQAjWwxk2C0].
— [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA] (2024-03), [YT:ZQAjWwxk2C0] (2024-05), [YT:kFtv5VDkRSg] (2025-10), [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01), [YT:jAc63K9lRIQ] (2025-02)

**2.5 Three roles, never combined** [GEN] — see §1 #2.
- Strategist: past ad data, research, hooks, scripts, shot lists, footage review, editor briefs, internal and client revisions.
- Creator: raw footage only, executes the brief.
- Editor: text overlays, animations, assembling clips.
Don't leave strategy to creators or editors. Don't make the strategist star in the ads or edit in CapCut. — [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01)

**2.6 Creator brief** [GEN] — 2026 version.
1. Product focus.
2. Inspiration links (transitions, ad examples).
3. Bullet shot list to check off (e.g., swiping up, over-shoulder phone shots, recipe steps, distressed before / happy after).
4. 2+ recorded hook variations (V1/V2) plus script body.
5. Dropbox delivery.
6. Content guide: value props, dos and don'ts, inspiration brands.
In 2024 she wrote the brief as a storyboard: shot 1 try-on in front of mirror, shot 2 unboxing on bed, with clip examples of good try-on/VO. Filming then takes 30–60 min. — [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01), [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA] (2024-03)

**2.7 Script control by creator trust level** [GEN]
- Always give the talent the hook, chosen from data.
- New creators get word-for-word scripts.
- Trusted creators get the hook plus the top value props, or the main message, and say it in their own words. This works especially for products they love emotionally (e.g., kids). She cuts the ramble into a 30-second ad.
- Hooks are read as scripted. Benefits come as talking points in the creator's own words.
- She prefers actors to influencers who sound robotic on script reads.
— [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06), [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01), [YT:jAc63K9lRIQ] (2025-02)
Borrowed technique, reported by guest Trav (Motion host) as borrowed from Savannah: send the script plus 3 hooks, then keep the camera rolling and have the creator ad-lib answers to simple questions. The ad-lib footage often ends up best. — [YT:jAc63K9lRIQ] (2025-02)

**2.8 Raw footage QC checklist** [GEN]
- All required clips and VOs, with enough clip variety, matching the inspiration.
- Lighting.
- Product presented and used correctly.
- Believable acting and authentic testimonials; wardrobe, expressions, energy.
- Appropriate setting (not a messy closet).
- HDR off (it causes saturation issues).
- No shake or lens smudges.
- Audio free of fan/AC/construction noise, or cleaned with AI noise removal.
- VO not monotone or read.
- Correct brand and product pronunciation.
— [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01)

**2.9 Editor brief** [GEN] — a repeatable format.
- Edit inspiration as a Motion report link with comments and metrics.
- VO file link.
- Per line: the VO line and the on-screen caption, kept separate, plus suggested visuals.
- Repeated for each hook version.
Script plus raw footage alone rarely gets what you want. The visual reference carries notes on pacing, lighting and clip selection. Editing is formulaic: TikTok fonts, comment-question overlays, ~30s, cuts every x seconds, the most intriguing shot in the first 3s. — [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01), [YT:KALGyNhfrVU] (2025-07), [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA] (2024-03)

- Sources:

  - 2024: TikTok/IG hashtags and personal connections (sorority, acting clubs), under ~10K followers.
  - 2026: referrals, inbound, TikTok hashtags, the Twitter UGC community, Backstage (actors), and her real-life network.
  - No sourcing platforms.
- Selection: natural energy on camera can't be taught. Trainable traits are lighting and setting (an aesthetic kitchen for a cooking brand).
- Pay: "hidden gems" name their own rate and get raises with performance. She sells them on an organized brief (~1 hour of work, no revisions if they follow the sheet). Mostly per-video contractor pay, but she promises consistent weekly work, bonuses and growth, a multi-year relationship. Long relationships can move to a monthly retainer.

## 6. What changed over time and where she contradicts herself
- **Creator freedom, reversed and then partly restored.**
  - 2021: prefers TikTok creators, gives brand guidelines plus key value points, then lots of creative freedom. [YT:w1sz_OdVQiA]
  - 2024: explicitly against "let creators be creators"; vague briefs damage relationships. [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA]
  - 2026: exact scripts for new creators, own words for long-trusted ones. [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w]
  - Rule now: freedom is earned by trust, and hooks are always scripted.
- **Who edits.** In 2020 creators edited in CapCut. Now creators deliver raw footage only and dedicated Premiere/After Effects editors finish the ads: "let the creators do what they do best". [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01)
- **AI for scripts: a contradiction.**
  - 2024-05: scripting with AI listed with footage and editing as "rinse and repeat". [YT:ZQAjWwxk2C0]
  - 2025-03 onward: human copywriters only, because AI scripts "all sound the same". No ChatGPT for scripts or value props. "don't export your brain to chatgpt". [YT:EHiXurAZPj0], [YT:KALGyNhfrVU], [YT:kFtv5VDkRSg]
  - Treat the later stance as current.
- **Inspiration source shifted.** 2021: TikTok Creative Center and the feed, capped at 1–2 h/week. 2023–24: Facebook Ad Library, TikTok Top Ads, Foreplay. 2026: more organic TikTok than ad libraries. [YT:l1_xSlj4Z50], [YT:wrbvSulohNI], [YT:hS47--F7290], [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w]
- **Trends: a nuance that reads as a contradiction.**
  - 2024: a Foreplay board "Stitch incoming" of TikTok trends working in ads. [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA]
  - 2025-07: stays away from pop-culture trends in ads and uses only "evergreen trends". [YT:KALGyNhfrVU]
  - 2026-01: takes evergreen editing and transition trends from TikTok, not fast in-and-out ones. [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w]
  - The line she draws is format/edit trends (yes) vs topical trends (no).
- **Sourcing widened.** 2020: friends and family, IG/TikTok <5K–10K followers, DMs. 2026: a 40-person roster plus Backstage actors and the Twitter UGC community. Since 2025 she prefers actors to influencers who sound scripted. [YT:6OmF0BzBOHQ], [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w], [YT:jAc63K9lRIQ]
- **Cadence and volume grew.** 2–6 ads/client/week (2024-05) became 4–12 (2026-01). Filming moved from weekend–Tuesday (2024-03) to Monday (2025-10) to Mon–Tue (2026-01). [YT:ZQAjWwxk2C0], [YT:kFtv5VDkRSg], [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w]
- **Ad length.** The ~30s standard edit (2024-03) sits against 10–15 second ads are what's working on TikTok (2024-06). Different contexts, months apart. [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA], [YT:hS47--F7290]
