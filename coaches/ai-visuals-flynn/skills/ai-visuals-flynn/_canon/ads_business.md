## PART 7. Ad creative and selling AI creative work (offers, pricing, clients, creative ops)

### Rules (ranked by how often they say it)
- **Find the friction point before you pick a tool** — Ask "why is that happening" and "Why are you doing that way"; when the answer is "that's the way it's always been done", dig in, find the friction, work backwards to a tool or process that gets A to B quicker. Hunt 10-minute wins; every workflow has a struggling spot (a dry email list is not a copy problem). [YT:EP3WupKVL50, YT:zPIBzQRJVT8, YT:2H0AluIDW50, YT:N-tmQ_Can_o] (n.d., 2024-10, 2026-09) · mentions 12
- **Volume is the product for ads and email** — Media buyers are like day traders (back the winner, kill the rest), so more assets means more data; about 50 creatives a week with maybe 3 working means "no end to the asset game". Supplement photo shoots, do not ban them: "AI just happened to be volume game for us". A hit outside your norm signals an aesthetic that works and can steer brand direction (he would not generate a brand identity in Midjourney). [YT:PJ347ccVJBs, YT:EP3WupKVL50, YT:E41H2WIHMXg, YT:dcrhhIKNaRQ] (n.d.) · mentions 12
- **Stop the scroll: give people something they have not seen today** — Personal test: if you have seen it today, scrap it; a black background with white text "that's been done 10,000 times". Thousand-prompt Midjourney packs make everyone's output alike. The community moved from make it look great to make it weird, different, unique. [YT:dfjMjg_Cy7M, YT:zPIBzQRJVT8, YT:P3w112Qr-2s, YT:o-D4-EYP1Qc] (n.d., 2024-10) · mentions 10
- **Put the brand in the system prompt and run only winners** — Prime the model with company overview, brand guidelines, tone, consumer-communication rules, past winning ads, audience, product focus and copy limits; build only from highest-performing templates. The creative work moves into the system prompt and tool build, then you run at scale and select ("curating versus creating"); about 20% of the importance is the AI, 80% is process and tools, and designers and copywriters are best placed to build the templates. [YT:dcrhhIKNaRQ, YT:AxV8TR3BdqI, YT:jBcL-XrHqzY, YT:o-D4-EYP1Qc] (n.d.) · mentions 9
- **Amplify, never replace** — AI supplements photographers and designers; clients who want to fire the design team get only adequate work. Start very small and slow, "empowering from within" (give early adopters room to play), and make it safe: no repercussions if clients hated the work. [YT:EP3WupKVL50, YT:dcrhhIKNaRQ, YT:BwFzyeHozFk, YT:jBcL-XrHqzY] (n.d.) · mentions 9
- **Measure time, volume, revisions and LTV; do not judge on one month of P&L** — Revisions were the one metric that justified the program (rounds crush designers and cap clients served): isolate where they start (strategy, account managers, copywriters, designers), fix each with tools, and 3.4 rounds fell to 1.7. Payoff showed up indirectly and later; count intangible ROI (faster onboarding, less turnover). [YT:zPIBzQRJVT8, YT:2H0AluIDW50, YT:6w6pKyCCQFg, YT:E41H2WIHMXg] (n.d., 2024-10) · mentions 9
- **Give away more than you think; let public work do the selling** — "you don't have the source code to Google"; reciprocity makes inbound show up unasked, and podcasts, speaking and client work are "99% inbound". Gumroad PDFs are the value ladder: good enough for a stranger, plus a plain-text version with copyable prompts for an email, so people are off LinkedIn in case it bans him. By 2026-09 everything is easily reverse-engineered, so he now has to "cage" some of it. [YT:OJFF_UIgAgc, YT:1V0xODN21tU] (2025-10, 2026-09) · mentions 8
- **Do what you already know; niche down, then expand** — AI makes you "bionic" on an existing skill: a copywriter solves copy, he solved email and paid-media problems. Niche first (images for email), get obsessed with one tool, then widen; build from your own pains (if one person has it, many do). [YT:o-D4-EYP1Qc, YT:zPIBzQRJVT8, YT:OJFF_UIgAgc] (n.d., 2024-10, 2025-10) · mentions 8
- **Email image carries the story, not the product shot** — Email only redirects ("You cannot buy off of email"), so make the image flashy enough to earn the click. Shelf life is about 6-10 hours and 70-90% of opens are mobile, so 1024 px sufficed; pull the active ingredient from the story (salad dressing: avocado oil, no photo existed). [YT:2vHM6owNx6c, YT:EP3WupKVL50, YT:6w6pKyCCQFg, YT:2H0AluIDW50] (n.d.) · mentions 8
- **Treat fully AI-made output as stock, not property** (US, not a lawyer) — Fully AI output cannot be copyrighted, so anyone can reuse it; a human edit (new background in Photoshop) is copyrightable. He does not trust Midjourney's terms, expects tech to outrun the law, and says the upside still outweighs the risk. [YT:E41H2WIHMXg, YT:Mg9oeq5T5Lg, YT:aBgRhbYlF6Q, YT:2vHM6owNx6c] (n.d.; stated Nov 2023 to early 2024) · mentions 8
- **Reuse the composition that works; take AI to 90% and finish by hand** — Pitch existing approved images (set composition or camera angle) as net-new scenes instead of reshoots, a "game changer". Keep the working light and angle, change only what surrounds it; he would rather finish at 90% in Photoshop than build from scratch. [YT:k7UtYDkFGQs, YT:X-Nm3NGvrTE, YT:ZY5GqIXkET4] (2025-10) · mentions 8
- **Localize and version instead of re-shooting** — Rebuild the image aesthetic per market (New York pizza becomes Charleston, Arizona, LA) and localize language to the local demographic; a US suburban home is wrong for a 200 sq ft Paris flat. "you need options and it's volume game which is where I come from". [YT:6w6pKyCCQFg, YT:N-tmQ_Can_o, YT:E41H2WIHMXg, YT:HSZoLRw4e74] (n.d., 2025-10, 2026-09) · mentions 7
- **Show, do not tell; keep demos simple** — Ask the prospect for one asset and say "watch me do this in 20 seconds", live and free. After discovery he sent a small free workflow ("hey, solved it"); never show the "50 group node package", it intimidates. Show reels beat written case studies, which only say what went well. Starter demos: asset resizing (image in, 10 new images at the right specs) and text localization (five languages in about a second). [YT:6w6pKyCCQFg, YT:N-tmQ_Can_o, YT:zPIBzQRJVT8, YT:nROQ4LJ4GcQ] (n.d., 2024-10, 2026-09, 2026-10) · mentions 6
- **Reverse-image-search every commercial image** — Use a duplicate checker (he names Duplichecker); if close, do not use it, redo with changed prompt elements (about 10 more minutes). Midjourney rebuilt the 1980s National Geographic cover from camera details alone; lawyers search all day and he was once sued over a stock-site image. [YT:dcrhhIKNaRQ, YT:aBgRhbYlF6Q, YT:Mg9oeq5T5Lg, YT:2H0AluIDW50] (n.d.) · mentions 6
- **Client work needs precision; expect label bias** — For himself he stays loose; for clients you must nail it, refining one image piece by piece, and that forced problem-solving teaches most. His team showed clients a real and an AI image with the labels crossed; clients picked whichever was labelled real, again after the labels were swapped. [YT:t0dgrFHEcwM, YT:QLbjv3CbfIo, YT:Rzr8TDN_0y8] (2024-10, 2025-10, 2026-06) · mentions 5
- **Not all money is equal; custom beats generic; workflows are not free** — A $5,000 project over weeks is not a one-hour training worth a tenth of it; decide what is not worth entertaining (speaker inferred). Custom builds beat generic SaaS clippers; endorses Blacklight's point that workflows should not be free because learning cost is invisible. [YT:qhxUbwnJ-9g, YT:nROQ4LJ4GcQ, YT:vHeSiuWA6fA] (2025-10, 2026-09, 2026-10) · mentions 5
- **Quote the price mid-call, right after showing the work** — Pauses and negative reactions came when price landed at the end, so he moved it to the middle, then explained exactly how the work gets done. Retold in 2026-08: live audit, drop the price, explain how you work together, end on "any questions". [YT:6w6pKyCCQFg, YT:xLLtYn932qY] (n.d., 2026-08) · mentions 4
- **Disclose AI to clients and protect their data** — It is the first thing he says; set SOPs and do not feed proprietary data into Midjourney or ChatGPT unless on Enterprise or a protected model. Pitch it honestly: AI messes up, but he can run it 5,000 more times a day than designing by hand and one will hit. [YT:aBgRhbYlF6Q, YT:dcrhhIKNaRQ, YT:6w6pKyCCQFg] (n.d.) · mentions 4
- **No artist, film or people names in commercial prompts** — "in the style of Wes Anderson" lets a litigator walk back your prompt. Ask ChatGPT for that artist's composition, colour scheme, lighting and equipment and build your own blocks; never image-prompt with copyrighted images, generate your own in Midjourney first. [YT:dcrhhIKNaRQ, YT:aBgRhbYlF6Q, YT:Mg9oeq5T5Lg] (n.d.) · mentions 4
- **Judge realism by stakes; if you cannot be real, be obviously AI** — Rory: a brand with risk tolerance should go "in the most ridiculous fashion" so everyone knows it is AI; bad AI that tries to look real gets shamed, and once people know it is AI they look for 15 seconds instead of 2. (Fast Hours) AI UGC with a face asking for trust feels like deception; short product B-roll is low stakes. [YT:SbDupYyGXac, YT:qp1JLLd6ut4, YT:Rzr8TDN_0y8] (2025-11, 2026-06, 2026-08) · mentions 4
- **Use the data, then make the creative move it would not suggest** — Whatever the data says he tends to do the opposite, since identical data makes everyone act alike; he loves "ugly ads" (face close to camera, odd angle). Wants data and creative to meet in the middle. [YT:E41H2WIHMXg, YT:xLLtYn932qY, YT:FGJCBhAiQJk, YT:ktSEt_8T9MA] (n.d., 2026-07, 2026-08) · mentions 4
- **AI stops at the literal function; add the human layer** — A pot: AI says it cooks, a person sees family dinners and a space-saving nesting tool. Spend extra time on the step you will run again and again (for hooks say what you are looking for and add constraints). [YT:Dl64zUCJniU, YT:RNF9xZYHOiQ, YT:EaENLPTAt2E] (2025-12, 2026-03, 2026-05) · mentions 3
- **Sell standard packages on performance value, not hours** — Pricing varies by client but rests on standard packages; producing about 5x the assets in the same time is valuable because it can affect performance, so it comes down to the sales and offering tactic. Margin comes from clients staying a year or more; he calls workflow as a service barren because every team's process differs, so customize. [YT:dcrhhIKNaRQ, YT:E41H2WIHMXg, YT:AxV8TR3BdqI] (n.d.) · mentions 3

### Methods (named or ordered)
#### Engagement method (find, build, prove, leave behind)
- When they use it: scoping an "operational AI company" engagement with a brand or creative team.
- Steps: 1. Outside eyes ask why it happens; 2. dig in on "that's the way it's always been done"; 3. find the friction points; 4. work backwards to a process or tool that gets A to B easier, quicker or more creatively; 5. (later model) build a customized workflow, run it many times for client approval, package it as an app and leave it behind. [quote src: YT:EP3WupKVL50]
- Their example: find holes in operations, plug them with AI tools; faster to test in Weavy than n8n or Lovable.
- Sources: [YT:EP3WupKVL50, YT:2H0AluIDW50, YT:PJ347ccVJBs] (n.d.)
#### One prompt equals a thousand ads (static ad engine)
- When they use it: media buyers or e-com brands needing testable static ads and variants of winners.
- Steps: 1. Build highest-performing templates in Figma per aspect ratio; 2. prime ChatGPT with company overview, brand guidelines, tone, consumer-communication rules, past winners, audience, product focus, copy limits; 3. spreadsheet columns: headlines, subheadlines, CTAs, image name, template; 4. images from Midjourney, Stable Diffusion or real photos; 5. zip CSV plus images, sync with the Figma plugin Copy Doc; 6. reverse-image-search outputs.
- Their example: 5 images x 5 copy sets x 5 CTAs x 8 aspect ratios = 1,000; first run under an hour; one winner into 50 variants.
- Sources: [YT:dcrhhIKNaRQ, YT:o-D4-EYP1Qc, YT:aBgRhbYlF6Q] (n.d.)
#### Sales call: audit first, price in the middle
- When they use it: selling a service to a marketing team (email-agency days, retold 2026-08).
- Steps: 1. "pop the hood" and audit live instead of interviewing; 2. state the price mid-call, then explain how the work gets done (who you work with, talk twice a week); 3. end on "any questions"; 4. offer a 24-hour written audit for Klaviyo access, or re-record the audit and send it with no pressure; 5. rebuild their welcome flow in 24 hours with AI.
- Their example: Fireflies sentiment showed tone dropping before the pitch; after the flip sales "jumped insane"; rarely needed a second call.
- Sources: [YT:6w6pKyCCQFg, YT:xLLtYn932qY] (n.d., 2026-08)
#### Friction-point reference tool (BarkBox)
- When they use it: one hero shot must feed many downstream assets.
- Steps: 1. Find the single friction point (composites are slow; late or damaged toys stall the shoot); 2. box plus swappable PNG cut-outs into a compositor node; 3. retexture prompt: add depth, shadow, light and shading so it looks real in a white studio; 4. use that image as "the vector to everything else" for batch engines (studio, social, UGC-style, banners); 5. fix scale misses or keep the funny ones.
- Their example: flat composite looks like Canva, lit version looks real; five sends gave about 200 images.
- Sources: [YT:N-tmQ_Can_o, YT:_az4W2z5Fqw, YT:PJ347ccVJBs] (n.d., 2026-09)
#### Shark expansion ladder (start small, widen)
- When they use it: growing a corporate engagement from one use case.
- Steps: 1. Retouch and re-angle scrapped shoots; 2. localize e-com assets (8-10 shots per product page); 3. old compositions into sketches, rebuild with new products; 4. environment fixes (flatten a bumpy rug); 5. composite, colour change, product swap, keep reusable assets; 6. full campaign style; 7. video: swap one frame to localize, seasonalize, hybrid with 3D/VFX.
- Their example: SharkNinja, hundreds of products, 25 categories, 35 markets; "little pieces that help in the day-to-day". [quote src: YT:N-tmQ_Can_o]
- Sources: [YT:N-tmQ_Can_o, YT:_az4W2z5Fqw] (2026-09, n.d.)
#### Static-ad abstraction database (Notion plus Claude)
- When they use it: turning liked ads into reusable templates.
- Steps: 1. Send any liked static ad to Notion; 2. Claude outputs (a) a visual template as a JSX or SVG file with typography and creative-direction rules, (b) creative direction; 3. each goes in its own database with deep auto-tags (format, categories, tags); 4. feed the template to Nano Banana or ChatGPT with new copy; same move for composition, storyboards, camera angle, look and feel.
- Their example: anything he drops in comes back looking really perfect; the April 2026 version pulled templates in bulk or one by one.
- Sources: [YT:1V0xODN21tU, YT:92I8aNhyxwM] (2026-09, 2026-04)

### Recipe library (exact, reusable)
- **Product-ad batch engine (10 to 1,000 ads)** — Inputs: input image + user prompt + custom instructions (system prompt for formatting: produce 10 prompts that go straight to the image generators) + a visual brand profile (dump ~20 brand images into an LLM, ask it to extract a style guide for each image generator); split and run, scale 10 to 40 to 1,000. For a plain product image the system prompt can say only `preserve the details of the product`. [YT:jBcL-XrHqzY, YT:PJ347ccVJBs] (n.d.)
- **Ad copy limits for ChatGPT** — Hard constraints because it gets verbose: headline under five words, subheadline under seven, CTA two words or less (another talk says under two); "a CTA that's six words isn't going to work". [YT:o-D4-EYP1Qc, YT:dcrhhIKNaRQ] (n.d.)
- **Retexture a flat composite (BarkBox)** — Composite box and cut-out PNGs in a compositor node, flatten, then regenerate with `take this flat image, add the lighting depth and shadow and give it some dimension`: you get a PNG with no background, add depth, shadows, light and shading so it looks real in a white studio. [YT:_az4W2z5Fqw, YT:N-tmQ_Can_o] (n.d., 2026-09)
- **Wireframe-to-ad in ChatGPT image gen** — Gray boxes in Figma (headline, sub-headline, four product images on top, hero slot, icon plus description pairs along the bottom); give the wireframe, four product photos and the product description ("briefed it like a designer"), ask for icons and a hero. Add a V-shaped flow to the focal point and more contrast so dark lines point to the middle; then erase wrong logos by hand and drop real cut-out product photos in (about 5 seconds). [YT:ZY5GqIXkET4] (2025-10; recorded 2025-03)
- **Looping GIF for email and SMS** — Simple base image (a Reve one matched ChatGPT's look); take a brand-site review line as the quote; one prompt per step: scale up about 0.25-0.5x and open the mouth by 50%; vary sunglasses colour, expression or outfit the same way; add text; ask for a fast-paced looping GIF at 0.35 seconds per image. Under 10 minutes. [YT:F6P2EAfAwjw] (2025-10)
- **Competitor review mining** — Screenshot the competitor's Amazon 1-star reviews, feed them to ChatGPT, ask what questions and concerns come up most consistently, build all messaging on that (e.g. sizing is always bad). [YT:E41H2WIHMXg] (n.d.)
- **Sales-call question mining (2023, ChatGPT 3.5)** — About 20 calls a week: strip every question from transcripts into a spreadsheet, reduce each to the real underlying question (price objection, timing, or a good question), put the most asked into the sales script so objections are answered first, then into outbound marketing. [YT:xLLtYn932qY] (2026-08)
- **Product on a person with Midjourney omni ref** — Low `--ow` of 10 to 25 is "the kicker"; paste several items (pants plus shoes) into one image, group and export, use it as the object reference, and add the model photo as an image reference too. [YT:Ye6DOhTgsDc] (2025-10)
- **Interior-design restyle** — Screenshot the client's product into the external editor, swap curtain, weather, plant, rug; retexture to relight and go sketch to lifestyle; keep the lifestyle image and change the style reference code for Cape Cod, modern coastal, farmhouse or masculine versions with everything else identical. - **Fast-fashion e-com chain** — Model builder as character sheet and tester; clothing inputs separately (top, middle, lower half) into virtual try-on; branch into lifestyle or reference shots (a medium-shot reference can carry more detail than full body); then a test-reality branch of new scenes, outfits, characters. - **Direct the photographer and the client with Midjourney** — Show the photographer exact shots (lighting, colours, positioning) instead of a Pinterest board (1-2 weeks trimmed); storyboards in about two hours; at kickoff (about 2 days after signing) show 4-5 email templates mocked up to the client's brand values. ### Fix library (problem → fix → why)
- **Problem:** a client reviewer says an image "looks way too AI" and he cannot see it → **Fix:** take it to a third party or an LLM, repeat the reviewer's complaints, ask how certain they are it is a real photo (it came back real) → **Why:** once people know it is AI they look for 15 seconds instead of 2; a bent finger from a fast shutter becomes "AI". [YT:Rzr8TDN_0y8] (2026-06)
- **Problem:** one input image gives "water bottle floating on the clouds" → **Fix:** have it build a word cloud around the object first (hydration, "hydrating champions", holds water, glass, can be a weapon), then branch ideas off it → **Why:** you need a second level of human connection, not just an advertisement. [YT:RNF9xZYHOiQ] (2026-03)
- **Problem:** (Fast Hours) a product still returns a turntable rotation → **Fix:** describe the action you want (a shoe walking, mushrooms growing) → **Why:** "you want to see what it's going to do first", then adjust. [YT:aqdZAjHVOmQ] (2025-10)
- **Problem:** ChatGPT will not render a full marketing email from a wireframe (not one 1x3 layout, off brand, left-justified, centering re-renders everything, wrong button colour) → **Fix:** feed hero then review sections separately; screenshot the customer review text and give it the template → **Why:** too many variables at once; the review template suits stylized customer feedback. [YT:F6P2EAfAwjw] (2025-10)
- **Problem:** flat composite looks "like you did it in Canva" → **Fix:** run it through the retexture prompt (depth, shadows, light, shading in a white studio) → **Why:** lighting makes it read as real. [YT:N-tmQ_Can_o] (2026-09)
- **Problem:** batch outputs miss (a pug too large against the toys, extreme expressions) → **Fix:** correct the scale or keep the funny ones → **Why:** the goal is "things that have to get done", not Hollywood, and BarkBox has a sense of humor. [YT:N-tmQ_Can_o] (2026-09)
- **Problem:** new e-com clients had few brand assets and reused off-message photos by months 2 to 4 (an avocado-oil client had no avocado image) → **Fix:** from about Feb/March 2023 make on-brand Midjourney imagery (colours, look and feel) → **Why:** re-Photoshopping the same photos could not tell the email's story. [YT:E41H2WIHMXg] (n.d.)
- **Problem:** early Midjourney output too low-res (carousel covers at 1500 x 1200 came out blurry) → **Fix:** aim at small mobile placements first (email, organic, ads) where passable is enough → **Why:** early upscalers were weak. [YT:zPIBzQRJVT8] (2024-10)

### Coaching and critique moves
- Leadership sees a Coca-Cola AI commercial and wants commercials, but he works "in the guts of the creative process": ask what the team does over and over (re-versioning one asset for YouTube, Instagram); "the creativity is not the problem it's the process". [YT:AxV8TR3BdqI]
- Ask "why is that happening" and "Why are you doing that way"; dig when the answer is habit. [YT:EP3WupKVL50]
- Ask "what are you comfortable with?" to size the build: loose output for media buyers, modular control and a shot list for photographers and art directors, VFX-style layers for compositors. [YT:PJ347ccVJBs]
- Scroll test: "have you seen this today?" If yes, scrap it. [YT:dfjMjg_Cy7M]
- Email critique: CTA above the fold; only about 40 or 50 ways to stack an email that converts. [YT:haCwSYl99ks]
- Ask the creator to show the work behind a post (craft, system, narrative), not one prompt. [YT:GtxJdBTXKwE]
- Guest opener: what did you do before AI and what was your inflection moment (Drew calls it Rory's usual question). [YT:haCwSYl99ks]
