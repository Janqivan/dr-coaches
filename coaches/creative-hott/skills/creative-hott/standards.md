## T1. Concept batch (what to make next for a DR account or DR's own acquisition)

- ★ **Guided by what already spends.** The batch names the account's current top spenders (or states that no spend data was given) and each concept says which spender it extends, complements or deliberately breaks from. Random volume = FAIL. "Blind volume is useless. You need guided volume." [YT:Hh4zkUdRVzg] [YT:_lAjkqWSDsA]
- ★ **Every concept states its first frame.** What literally happens in frame one (motion or reveal), and the relevance cue in the top third. A concept described only as an angle or a script line = FAIL. [YT:mpj0A4Prxu4] [YT:fawsUmNeCkg]
- ★ **Setting and cast are written as targeting.** Each concept names the background and the person on camera, and who that setting/cast signals the ad is for (e.g. a franchise-owner prospect's truck vs. a corporate office). "Setting is like the most important thing." [YT:mpj0A4Prxu4] [YT:frk28Y6nOHg]
- ★ **Real diversity, not hook swaps.** No two concepts differ only by the first 3 seconds or the headline; they differ in setting, person, format or story. "Iterating to death" = hook/headline swaps only. [YT:pMAxIOL8dtE] [YT:s4mLWmECoKI]
- **Batch size fits conversion volume.** Number of new ads is checked against leads/day the account produces; with ~1 conversion a day "you can't be running 50 ads." State the number used. [YT:RVRRGf746Bw]
- **Includes at least one cheap Post-it / words test** to find the "words that matter" before production spend. [YT:Oj-KGCw7K1g] [YT:bd9MGQ0-Vqc]
- **Includes at least one long talking/story ad** (founder, principal, or real team member to camera, 1–3 min), with the principal speaking as the person who knows the problem best, not a "why we started" story. [YT:m8dbcF407H8] [YT:EgnN83jIsj8] [YT:qoOBIPWH_qw]
- **Words come from the buyer.** At least half the concepts carry a past-tense I-statement or a mined comment/review line, not marketer copy. [YT:MbqiexQiyGs] [YT:bd9MGQ0-Vqc]
- **At least one ugliness lever changed per concept** (talent, lighting, camera, angle, script, story, setting), and named. [YT:YuS18wnD_bc]
- **Each concept tagged GEN or DTC-transfer and dated** (NOW / EVERGREEN / ALLTIME source). DTC mechanics (unboxing, product-in-hand, DPA, deal feeling) carry the line "DTC evidence; transfer untested." Barry himself says the lead-gen difference is mostly the goal you optimize for, and that the same principles apply to lead gen, so the line is for product mechanics, not for the creative approach. [YT:RVRRGf746Bw] [YT:FTotRZL9vAg] [canon: SKILL.md Rule 3]

## T2. Roast / teardown / pre-launch QA of one ad (video or static, DR's or a competitor's)

- ★ **First-frame read comes first and is concrete.** Names what is literally in frame one, whether anything happens, what feeling it triggers, and the "so what" point (the second where a viewer would say "so what"). In "90–99% of ads" it is the first second. [YT:qoOBIPWH_qw] [YT:mpj0A4Prxu4]
- ★ **Relevance chain checked past the click.** The verdict covers the landing page (headline, image, the person shown) and the homepage hero, not just the ad. Ad judged in isolation = FAIL. [YT:s4mLWmECoKI] [YT:e54fR6s6WSw]
- ★ **Verdict comes with the rewrite.** One-line verdict, then the exact new first frame, overlay text and opening spoken line. "Make it more native" without the replacement = FAIL. [canon: SKILL.md Response mode 2] (not found in transcripts)
- ★ **Spend decides, and the roast says when it can't.** If spend/results were given, the verdict is reconciled with them (a top spender is not called a loser on looks); if spend is too thin, the roast says so. [YT:nUVl_zpeipY] [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs]
- **Ad-signal audit with named items.** Checks for: logo or brand first on cold traffic, styled set or white wall, stock-looking footage, stock music in a talking ad, perfect fades/sweeping zooms, an unknown face filling frame one with no reason to watch. Each present item is listed. [YT:nUVl_zpeipY] [YT:DGTwYyI-81s] [YT:bd9MGQ0-Vqc] [YT:ZyNdRSc9zQc] [YT:mpj0A4Prxu4]
- **Belonging test.** States who would feel "this is for me" and who would think "Nah, I'm good. This isn't for me" from age, speech, language, setting. [YT:s4mLWmECoKI]
- **Overlay discipline.** Hook text at most two lines ("keep it a two"), in the top third, not covering the action; static headline in the top third. [YT:9YKsHMJXiIQ] [YT:e54fR6s6WSw]
- **Clear over clever.** Any pun or insider line that needs context is flagged. [YT:VtEoAOfQsCU] [YT:9YKsHMJXiIQ]
- **Ugly is not a pass on its own.** The roast also judges story and angle; "Ugly ads can be bad ads too." [YT:YuS18wnD_bc]
- **Lead-gen compliance flags.** No second-person accusation of a condition/problem the platform treats as personal (a person saying "I had…" is fine), no AI character giving an "I had an experience" testimonial. For immigration law, read "condition" as status or legal trouble. [YT:bd9MGQ0-Vqc] [YT:4BOtccKnC2Q]
- **Ends with one variable to test**, not a list of ten fixes; he keeps variables to a minimum and knows what he will do with the result before launch. [YT:PSMit4vB8n8] [YT:R0k0rVzv_yQ] [canon: SKILL.md Response mode 2]

## T3. Brief or script (creator, founder/principal talking ad, Post-it or viral-page static)

- ★ **Opens on a first frame, not a greeting.** Script line 1 is a visual action plus the first spoken words; no logo, no "Hi, I'm…" from an unknown face, no company "we". [YT:nUVl_zpeipY] [YT:bd9MGQ0-Vqc] [YT:cpnVXGQAWHg]
- ★ **Setting and cast specified as targeting.** Brief names the location (real, aspir-relatable: about "30% nicer" than the audience's own life) and who is on camera and why they read as relevant. [YT:Oj-KGCw7K1g] [YT:mpj0A4Prxu4]
- ★ **Talking ads are built from questions, not a teleprompter.** Brief lists the off-camera questions to ask; the team does setup (setting, camera, prompts); the founder/principal stays out of the edit. [YT:frk28Y6nOHg] [YT:m8dbcF407H8] [YT:EgnN83jIsj8]
- **Problem beats reach the solution by ~0:45**, not 1:22 (Earth Breeze). [YT:P3IpafQunzU]
- **Long ad carries a mid-roll CTA and an end card**, plus a re-hook ("if you want to hear the rest of the story stick around"). [YT:cpnVXGQAWHg]
- **Front-loaded words.** VO, overlay and first 3 words of primary text each carry the potent part; filler phrasing cut. [YT:fawsUmNeCkg] [YT:e54fR6s6WSw]
- **Shot "how a creator would frame this"**: phone, one take where possible, native audio, no stock music in talking ads. [YT:m8dbcF407H8] [YT:EgnN83jIsj8] [YT:bd9MGQ0-Vqc]
- **Editor note keeps imperfections** (no sweeping zooms, no perfect fades, captions native-style). [YT:DGTwYyI-81s]
- **Do-not list present**: logo up front, stock music, company "we" opener, second-person accusation, AI testimonial, insider pun. [YT:nUVl_zpeipY] [YT:bd9MGQ0-Vqc] [YT:4BOtccKnC2Q] [YT:VtEoAOfQsCU]
- **Post-it/static briefs**: real Post-it on a relevant object in the buyer's world (not photoshopped handwriting, not stuck on a screen showing a photo); the copy is the test variable. [YT:XIn0vQdErsM] [YT:pMAxIOL8dtE]
- **All copy marked DRAFT for Ivan's approval** (Ivan writes and approves all ad copy). [canon: SKILL.md Context: Ivan] (not found in transcripts)

## T4. Spend read, kill/scale call, creative test plan

- ★ **Sorted by spend first.** The read starts from the ads ranked by amount spent; CPA/hook rate/CTR are discussed only after. "Spend is a meritocracy." [YT:RVRRGf746Bw] [YT:nUVl_zpeipY]
- ★ **No kill of a top spender on CPA alone.** Any recommendation to pause the top spender has passed the breakdown-effect check (ad-set level, not per ad) and the kill test "would I want this out there spending more changing more minds". Otherwise FAIL. [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs] [YT:Oj-KGCw7K1g] [YT:mpj0A4Prxu4]
- ★ **Secondary metrics as diagnostics only.** Hook rate is not compared across unrelated ads and never used as a kill switch. [YT:2jVcLu3Earg] [YT:VtEoAOfQsCU] [YT:RVRRGf746Bw]
- **Two-sided empathy stated.** For each top spender: why a consumer pays attention, and why the system would spend on it. [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs]
- **Volume set by conversion volume, not budget.** Number of ads in test is justified by leads/day. [YT:RVRRGf746Bw]
- **Lead-gen goal is hard to fake.** Optimization or judgment event is a downstream / hand-raiser action (booked call, qualified lead), not a click or raw form fill, when the funnel allows. [YT:RVRRGf746Bw] [YT:_lAjkqWSDsA]
- **Comment check on top spenders** (sort by comments too; a top negative comment is "vandalism on your ad" to hide). [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs] [YT:R0k0rVzv_yQ]
- **Post-March-2026 link-click audit** where relevant: ads that won spend on comment bait, fake buttons or tiny text are flagged. [YT:mpj0A4Prxu4]
- **No hit-rate scorecard.** Creative sources judged by spend carried and cost per ad, not % winners. [YT:Hh4zkUdRVzg] [YT:mpj0A4Prxu4]
- **Structure claims dated and tagged.** Testing-campaign/ASC setup cited with its year and marked DTC where it is. [YT:XIn0vQdErsM] [YT:nUVl_zpeipY] [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs]
- **Ivan's spend data (Mirror app) outranks the canon** and is cited where it disagrees. [canon: SKILL.md Rule 2] (not found in transcripts)

- **Cold-audience test ad sets with a high cost cap as a runaway guard.** Test batches of 4–6 versions of one concept, exclude recent visitors and engagers, and if a cap is used set it well above expected CPA (his example: expected 50, cap 100); caps that are too tight choke ads with potential. 2024, DTC; mark as dated. [YT:R0k0rVzv_yQ]

## T5. Performance-swing triage (results dropped or jumped overnight)

- ★ **Site before creative.** Landing page, homepage, form, booking flow, Google results/reviews and any media-buying change are checked before any ad is touched. "It's almost always a website change that just someone didn't know happened." [YT:m8dbcF407H8] [YT:EgnN83jIsj8] [YT:s4mLWmECoKI]
- ★ **The top spender is read as the canary, not the culprit.** States that the top spender looks worst first because it carries the most spend; no pause recommended on that basis. [YT:Oj-KGCw7K1g] [YT:R0k0rVzv_yQ]
- **Timeline of changes with dates** (what changed, when, who), matched against the swing date. [YT:mpj0A4Prxu4]
- **A "what not to touch" list** is in the output: do not turn off the top spender (move it or cap its spend instead), do not duplicate-and-turn-off to "reset" performance. [YT:R0k0rVzv_yQ] [YT:XIn0vQdErsM] [canon: SKILL.md Response mode 6]
- **Daily page screenshots set up going forward** ("if you can't tell me what your site looked like on February 13th"). [YT:mpj0A4Prxu4]
- **Then the top ad itself**: comments, placement breakdown, fatigue. [YT:R0k0rVzv_yQ] [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs]
- **Landing-page changes are tested with dedicated ads, not split traffic.** Duplicate the top relevant ads into identical new ad sets, pair each with the new page, keep targeting and bids unchanged, compare to business as usual (2026). [YT:oFZp553oL2c]
- **Ends with one action.** [canon: SKILL.md Response mode 6] (not found in transcripts)

## T6. Iterating a winner / beating fatigue

- ★ **Why it's dying is asked first** (comment vandalism, site change, trend gone) before any new variant is made. [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs]
- ★ **Iterations change real levers.** At least half the variants change setting, creator/cast, audio, background, or are a full reshoot, not only hook/headline. A batch of hook swaps only = FAIL. [YT:VtEoAOfQsCU] [YT:Oj-KGCw7K1g] [YT:pMAxIOL8dtE]
- **Widens relevance.** Each variant names the new sub-audience it makes the winning angle relevant to (different-looking talent, faster/slower delivery). [YT:pMAxIOL8dtE] [YT:Oj-KGCw7K1g]
- **Static winner → video version** included. [YT:VtEoAOfQsCU]
- **Original stays on.** No variant plan turns off the evergreen winner. [YT:R0k0rVzv_yQ] [YT:Hh4zkUdRVzg]
- **Outrageous moments pulled forward** for story ads (snip the best line to the front). [YT:cpnVXGQAWHg]

## Universal checks (every output)

- ★ **Retrieved before writing.** At least 3 searches run; the work uses at least 3 retrieved items or passages, cited as [YT:id].
- ★ **No invented quotes, numbers, stories or dates.** Every quoted line exists verbatim in a retrieved hit. Never cite the fake-data demo stats or the $1,700 insurance figure.
- ★ **Did the work.** If Ivan gave a task, the output is the finished thing (concept batch, roast with rewrite, script, spend read), not a list of questions. Questions only for inputs that are missing and block the work (usually: what is spending), and only after doing everything possible without them.
- **In voice.** Loud, blunt, short verdicts ("terrible hook", "ugly ads approved") always followed by the concrete fix; argues by analogy (blackjack hands, strike zone, vandalism on your ad); strong opinions loosely held, says when he changed his mind. Era and DTC/lead-gen transfer flagged once per claim. No praise for Ivan, no fluff.
- **Ends with one move.** One test, what it tests, the control, the read on spend, and the kill/scale rule (spend-based, not hook rate).
