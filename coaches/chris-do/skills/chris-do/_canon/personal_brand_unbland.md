> Scope: Chris Do's personal-branding method as taught 2024–2026 (Unbland, shadow work, two-word brand, voice phrase, keyword/enemy/rallying-cry triangle, identity over utility, authenticity vs masking, trust), how he runs an excavation, what he says the field gets wrong, and how his view evolved 2014–2026. ## 1. Core positions (ranked by how often he says it)

### 1a. What he believes
- **Personal branding is inner work, not marketing** — "Your personal brand isn't what you do, it's who you are": an inward journey of "discovering who you are and getting in touch with that," even "healing from trauma that we don't even understand we have." Performance marketing is outward, short-term, "a manufacturing thing"; brand is inward, long-term, "a discovery thing" with no clear ROI — "The more you do this, the less you have this." Need a return? Run ads. Brand is "the accelerant to almost everything that you do." [YT:jd0Ijs0a0ns, YT:1kUCm1JPzxg, YT:DV-yxZpEYvU, YT:abA8qzB_rWc] (2024–2026) · mentions 56 [quote src: YT:PtxhSmYHeuA, YT:DCa1tziujuU, YT:eS9LtexTMFk, YT:tHq6gRdYxqw]
- **Packaging is a tool: change the package, not you** — "Your clothes are not clothes, they're tools": ask "what job are you trying to solve?" and what you are signaling; "make sure the signal and the receiver are in alignment." Black-on-black is "the Sears uniform"; add visual movement (hat, glasses, colour) so shy people can recognize and approach you. "if you don't like the kinds of people you're attracting, change the package" — "don't change you." [YT:PtxhSmYHeuA, YT:eS9LtexTMFk, YT:Zo_SxRuUGjQ] (2021–2026) · mentions 34 [quote src: YT:d7bgouAH3ns, YT:abA8qzB_rWc, YT:Ju4Hz6tZUXg]
- **Remember, don't invent: you were you at 3–9** — Between about 3 and 9 you are who you really are; socialization ("Let's use our inside voice") washes it out because it is "very unsafe to be different," a survival reflex from a "deep rooted fear of not being loved, not being accepted." Like a brand firm retelling the founder's garage story, "it's always like remembering, not inventing." Original = origin: don't chase the new, "return to the source code." [YT:xx5hEGLLvQA, YT:JmiM1SocDgI, YT:1kUCm1JPzxg, YT:hDlWbmH96hw] (2023–2026) · mentions 32 [quote src: YT:efxmE9XX-hE]
- **A personal brand is the voice of one** — Corporate brands are "designed by committee" = "the lowest common denominator versus the one outlier." A person has one name and face and no PR/legal shield. Personal accounts that copy corporate behavior "get the engagement of corporate brands." Write "I," not "we": "If you take the person out, then you're back to being a brand." "People don't fall in love with corporations, they fall in love with personality." [YT:MVz9mm0Z-7g, YT:MTqiV6robGg, YT:EeEsglitUe8] (2018–2025) · mentions 30 [quote src: YT:2dY6MsSedKg]
- **The gift is you: the more you you are, the more you are** — "We think our gift is what we do for a living." "The gift, believe it or not, is you" (Picasso paraphrase). Scrubbing history, ethnicity, accent and look to blend in means "you're becoming less"; on a 0–10 dial, "I want you to be on a 13," "not to a two." "Every negative attribute has a positive attribute": his baldness, oily skin and nearsightedness became hats and big frames. [YT:s9h8bXPLJMg, YT:XdNQC_zDCW0, YT:i10g2BVfWVI] (2024–2026) · mentions 30 [quote src: YT:TE8qfhD4b8I, YT:PZgRQpu61k4, YT:-3B7cbOOis4]
- **Different beats better; normal means average** — "different is better than better." "Can't you just be normal?" really asks for average, yet "nobody tells you, can you just be average? Because that's insulting." Art school's worst critique: "yeah it's kind of average." Most build "a personal bland... There's no seasoning. There's no flavor." The goal is "meaningfully different," not "bad different... unintentional different." [YT:BGwAGtgBMto, YT:_Md-kqXkgaU, YT:efxmE9XX-hE, YT:Zo_SxRuUGjQ] (2023–2026) · mentions 29 [quote src: YT:-hm5u_iiXd0]
- **Authenticity = the smallest gap between who you are and who you show** — "who are you when no one's watching" vs the persona built to be accepted; "The bigger the gap, the less authentic you are." Aim for the smallest gap, not zero: decorum isn't fake, posting a six-figure win while the company is in ruin is. "most of us live in the Persona and we don't even know it," after "a lifetime of these small compromises." [YT:rl69VQbs3gA, YT:x2coJr-C4z4, YT:ZdzDmPon19o, YT:XhxS8c_8-SQ] (2024–2026) · mentions 28 [quote src: YT:NdB1XarAofM, YT:4ZS8dMwPObk]
- **Compel and repel** — Speak from conviction so ideas "both compel and repel"; fear of alienating anyone means "saying nothing to everyone." Audiences split three ways: against you, aligned, and "the flexible middle," so "you're only losing potentially a third of your audience." Fight ideas, not people, and sequence by career stage ("pump the brakes" as a new grad). [YT:d7bgouAH3ns, YT:VYKA7hqZeZU, YT:DV-yxZpEYvU, YT:eluwXdhzJ5k] (2024–2026) · mentions 26 [quote src: YT:IG0mgfkRz1Q]
- **Manufactured shows; curation is a prop** — A team deciding how you show up is "very manufactured... that ain't you"; like an LA set, "it looks good from far but it's far from good." Faked jets and hotels: "eventually a lie will catch up to you"; "human beings are very good at spotting it." 10 people who follow me for me being me beat 100 million following a persona. [YT:Ed9OqOQAfBs, YT:ZmGBqGGshzs, YT:BwrXv5n5eFI] (2015–2025) · mentions 25 [quote src: YT:xx5hEGLLvQA, YT:GXioSuVr8q0, YT:Seb-TYmwO2A]

People hide their top 5% and bottom 5%; "if you want to share your success," "you have to share in equal measures your failures." Strengths-only posting "reeks of inauthenticity." ### 1b. What he says most personal-brand advice gets wrong
- **No person in it.** "99%" of it is funnels, conversion and positioning — "put lipstick on a pig," "it's all hogwash cuz who the hell are you." Of every 100 people talking about it, "three people might understand it the way I understand it." [YT:jd0Ijs0a0ns, YT:1kUCm1JPzxg] (2024–2026) [quote src: YT:DCa1tziujuU, YT:PZgRQpu61k4]
- **Wrong questions, tactics first.** "How do I grow my audience? How do I focus in on target market?" — "Wrong question." What's the first post? comes after "who the heck are you?" [YT:MVz9mm0Z-7g, YT:MqWUPpjMj3o] (2024–2026) [quote src: YT:abA8qzB_rWc]
- **Sold as income.** "the wannabes" sell "full inbox"/"10x your revenue." He unsells it ("No, I unsell it"): "no predictable return on investment." [YT:rl69VQbs3gA, YT:DCa1tziujuU] (2024–2025)
- **Marketing in disguise.** Borrowed corporate playbooks breed a fake persona ("I'm only writing this to get customers"); "If you're writing to grow your business, that's called marketing." [YT:1kUCm1JPzxg, YT:eluwXdhzJ5k] (2025) [quote src: YT:890eAEHu0NU]
- **The sea of same.** Emulation makes you "a poor copy of someone who's doing it better and longer than you"; doing what your space does is "a really horrible way to build a personal brand." [YT:pYxILzVu35A, YT:abA8qzB_rWc] [quote src: YT:Xn5EEDAxCGI]
- **Clever over true.** Lazy inversions of his brand ("I'm a shy extrovert") show zero soul searching; "truth should supersede wit." [YT:1kUCm1JPzxg, YT:MqWUPpjMj3o] (2025)
- **Authenticity with a sales agenda.** Can you do both? "I don't know if you can" — two masters; teaching that teases a pitch feels manipulative. [YT:rl69VQbs3gA, YT:-LB3OX7jLQQ]
- **Unearned expertise.** Gurus resell info they haven't lived: "Where are the receipts?"; headline numbers that are "technically true slightly misleading but not a lie." [YT:eluwXdhzJ5k, YT:ZzLE88D1oIg] (2024–2025) [quote src: YT:GXioSuVr8q0]
- **Outsourced voice.** His ghostwriter and agency year produced "weird false engagement": "you cannot export your thinking to other people or in this case now machines." [YT:RVo22mwJKdk]

## 2. Frameworks (named models and step sequences)

### Unbland (the Unblend workbook, 2025–26)
- When he uses it: his packaged current method; "unbland" is his sub-word ("anti-boring"), distilled from 3 years of workshops.
- Steps: 1. Go back to who you were at 7, before "something happened to that plan." 2. Stop leading with your job ("Why should I care about you?"). 3. Robot test: your script in a robot voice with a blurred face — "would anybody be able to tell it's you?" 4. Find the three: one thing, enemy, rallying cry. 5. Keep it "fast, fun, and easy": "high frequency of exercises, but low intellectual lift," doable "in 30 minutes." [quote src: YT:d7bgouAH3ns]
- His example: deep workshops left "people with a broken head," so he cut from seven parts toward three — a workbook, not a book book.
- Sources: [YT:d7bgouAH3ns, YT:RVo22mwJKdk, YT:U6jNABV8jb4] (2025–2026) · mentions 9
- ➕ 2023 ancestor: personal-style "rule number one, it's called branding, not blending" ("I stole that from Brian Collins"); rule two: "if you want to stand out, don't blend in"; typographic joke "Be bold, be italic, but never regular". — [YT:bc6KY73H8Zo] (2023)

### Keyword / enemy / rallying cry (the triangle)
- When he uses it: to give a brand a stand; missing a leg = "a two-legged table."
- Steps: 1. KEYWORD: what you want to be known for (sneaker → Nike); not too big ("peace" needs an org or Musk/Gates clout). 2. ENEMY: "what are you on a mission to rid the world of?" — an idea, process or system, never a person; find it via "what annoys me what makes me mad because your complaint can become your calling." 3. RALLYING CRY: a 3–5-word call to arms with a strong verb ("don't die") so people say "We believe what you believe." 4. Nail one and the others follow like rock-paper-scissors. 5. Test the enemy: if no one could disagree, "do you stand for anything? Probably not." [quote src: YT:RVo22mwJKdk, YT:MVz9mm0Z-7g, YT:uAsAs8MoW9U]
- His example: "authentic personal brand" / masking ("corporate mask") / "unbland yourself. Put some flavor into it." The Futur: anti-"crippling student debt," not anti-education. [quote src: YT:RVo22mwJKdk]
- Sources: [YT:VYKA7hqZeZU, YT:uAsAs8MoW9U, YT:RVo22mwJKdk, YT:eluwXdhzJ5k] (2024–2026) · mentions 17

### Two-word brand (three routes)
- When he uses it: the core output of an excavation — "the teaser trailer to the rest of your story," a search engine over your life so your "top 25 stories" become talks or a book. [quote src: YT:sYyj--Yci6M]
- Rule: true first, then memorable, then intriguing; mere opposites "might be clever, it might be memorable, but it's not true." [quote src: YT:sYyj--Yci6M]
- Steps: Route 1, shadow + transformer: list shadow words (what you dislike, what trolls or a critical parent say), cross out flat ones, pick one, add a "transformer word" that flips it; don't "wash it of the real meaning." Route 2, origin: the moment that changed your trajectory (age 7/17/47) → a "spine word" → pair with an unflattering opposite word "that is also you." Route 3, nickname: list every nickname, pick personality over job, "the weirder it is the better it is"; test: "known as the ___ in my industry." Then add a mission ("beyond money, why do you exist?") and two stories (back story, transformation). [quote src: YT:MVz9mm0Z-7g, YT:-3B7cbOOis4, YT:rl69VQbs3gA, YT:R2kgIsGnCaY]
- His example: "loud introvert" + "a really big mission to teach a billion people"; other sets: "kind critic," "diligent rebel," "charming razor blade." [quote src: YT:Ns1teHj2bLk]
- Sources: [YT:1kUCm1JPzxg, YT:MVz9mm0Z-7g, YT:rl69VQbs3gA, YT:sYyj--Yci6M] (2024–2026) · mentions 24
- ➕ Apple's "insanely great" campaign as juxtaposition — insane in pursuit of perfection, obsession with detail; packaging like unwrapping a gift, one pull removes the shrink wrap, even unseen insides are designed — [YT:NYRCVhW_hdA] (n.d.)
- ➕ labels like therapist or attorney are understood, and combining two understood terms makes a new, more specific hybrid: The Horse Whisperer trailer line (Robert Redford's character) "I help horses with people problems", from which "accounting whisperer" and "story whisperer" now follow; adds that what you do is not who you are (you may once have pushed paperwork in a cubicle) — [YT:kx7RcSGnO0s] (n.d.)

### Shadow words → transformer (workshop day two)
- When he uses it: day two of a workshop; the Jungian core.
- Steps: 1. "write down all your shadow words" (shame, guilt, anxiety, anger from childhood "infractions"); "observe without judgment," "pour it out," play your own critic; stuck → non-dominant hand. 2. Per word: what did it teach me, what did it let me do, how can I be grateful — "what lesson did you learn from your Shadow word?" 3. Inversion: list positives and their downsides too. 4. Pick the one that "makes you feel most uncomfortable but also feels it's really you." 5. Flip it with a transformer word; 8 Mile rule: say it first — "what can the world do now to hurt you?" [quote src: YT:nRh7i30sKPw, YT:MVz9mm0Z-7g, YT:-3B7cbOOis4, YT:4n3NNsy7UDA]
- Variants: "too much" list ("who needs this? Why is this an advantage? How might this be a gift?"); naysayer route — troll words, "embrace them with the warmest hug" (old/dinosaur → "brandmother"); peel the onion: How do most people see you? + "but what they don't know is that I really struggle with..." [quote src: YT:hDlWbmH96hw, YT:MVz9mm0Z-7g]
- His example: treat it as an obstacle, not a gift, and you're "doomed to repeat it" (Annalie).
- Sources: [YT:nRh7i30sKPw, YT:Zo_SxRuUGjQ, YT:MqWUPpjMj3o, YT:hDlWbmH96hw] (2024–2026) · mentions 13

### Voice phrase ("hard truths gently told")
- When he uses it: to define how you sound — a "North Star."
- Steps: 1. Collect unedited words for how you sound (ask a friend, boss, sibling). 2. Pick 3 that ring true. 3. Write a 2–5-word phrase. 4. Self-audit against it.
- His example: if listeners think "generic nonsense" or "playing it too safe," "I failed my own identity"; if "what an a-hole," he failed "gently told" — bluntness "from a place of generosity," so people feel "that bastard does care." Same info, two voices: "Contrast, contrast, contrast. Did I say contrast?" vs the Bauhaus Gestalt movement talks about contrast. [quote src: YT:5QrdCEH5TXA]
- Sources: [YT:5QrdCEH5TXA, YT:uAsAs8MoW9U, YT:Ns1teHj2bLk, YT:cDTB4auDj2U] · mentions 6

### Authenticity gap diagnostic (+ prism, 90/10)
- When he uses it: to show someone they're performing.
- Steps: 1. Check the signs: drained by or dreading content; procrastinating; second-guessing tone; wrong-fit clients; inconsistent growth despite trends. 2. Probe until they "fall apart like a house of cards" — the "pause face"; "most often they choose to continue the lie." 3. Separate context from fakery: ~90% you, 10% context shell; ask "are you aware that you're doing this? Because most people are not," and "with the right intention?" [quote src: YT:efxmE9XX-hE, YT:DV-yxZpEYvU]
- His example: prism — full white light blinds, so different people get different colors: "It's not that they're not you. They're just parts of you"; a different person per platform is "revealed as a fake, as an impostor." [quote src: YT:RCocxOMQD3M]
- Sources: [YT:rl69VQbs3gA, YT:SABhSGYADUE, YT:890eAEHu0NU, YT:DV-yxZpEYvU] (2024–2025) · mentions 31

### Jung: ego, shadow, persona
- When he uses it: to explain why people mask; inner work is what separates him from "99% of people who use the same term." [quote src: YT:RVo22mwJKdk]
- Steps: 1. The ego mediates between the Shadow (shame, guilt, anxiety — what you fear gets you ostracized) and the Persona (who you show to be accepted), set by ~7–9. 2. "go into the Shadow" — "that's the real you." 3. Make peace with it to be "a whole person, not... a person with holes in it." 4. Reveal in stages: we show 10–15%, and "There's a time and place for each one of us." [quote src: YT:RVo22mwJKdk, YT:6w9F6_NJY8M]
- His example: imposter syndrome = persona vs believed self, "massive cognitive dissonance."
- Sources: [YT:1kUCm1JPzxg, YT:MVz9mm0Z-7g, YT:5_RMSS8Mf30, YT:RVo22mwJKdk] (2024–2026) · mentions 12
- **Show the thing you most fear showing** — Persona is learned codes (how we dress, talk, act to be accepted); show up as you think others want and "no one cares because you're just some poor facsimile of someone else", "two-dimensional paper characters". His answer to "how do I become a key person of influence online": "the thing that you're most afraid to show is the thing you must show". [YT:HUfNu9nywuQ] (2023) · mentions 1

### Know, accept, express
