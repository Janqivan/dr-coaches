   Kindle: https://www.amazon.com/Conversational-Marketing-Companies-Chatbots-Generate-ebook/dp/B07N47FC79
   **Read if** you want the canonical Drift-era category-creation playbook.

   **Buy if** you're a B2B marketer or B2B operator marketing-adjacent
