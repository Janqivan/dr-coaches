# Adam Robinson — Public Corpus URL Catalogue

Curated, navigable index of where Robinson's thinking actually lives —
his own platforms first (LinkedIn, X, RB2B blog/newsletter, his
personal site), then the third-party interviews where he's gone
deepest, then a set of recommended purchases / product entrypoints.

Use this canon when sourcing direct quotes for a coaching answer,
when verifying a claim, or when expanding the skill with new
references later.

## 1. Adam Robinson's own platforms

### 1.1 LinkedIn (primary channel)
**Profile:** <https://www.linkedin.com/in/retentionadam/>

This is the channel. Daily posting, ~17 substantive posts/month,
135K+ followers. The single best place to study Robinson's voice,
cadence, and transparency cap in raw form. Read the most recent
2-4 weeks of posts to calibrate before generating any coaching answer
that involves voice or cadence.

### 1.2 X / Twitter
**Profile:** <https://x.com/RetentionAdam>

Lower volume than LinkedIn. Mostly cross-posts and longer threads on
bootstrap vs VC, capital efficiency, and the case against raising.
Useful for sharper one-liners and contrarian takes than the LinkedIn
feed.

### 1.3 RB2B company site
**URL:** <https://www.rb2b.com>

The product itself — pricing tiers, free tier setup, integrations,
feature differentiation versus 6sense / Clearbit / Demandbase.
Useful when grounding any "freemium-as-distribution" answer in the
actual product UX.

### 1.4 RB2B Newsletter
**URL:** <https://newsletter.rb2b.com/>

Long-form public posts on RB2B's internals: pricing changes,
Baremetrics-style dashboard reveals, churn analyses, internal
disagreements. Higher resolution than LinkedIn for any operational
question.

Notable posts:

  <https://newsletter.rb2b.com/p/6sense-threatened-sue-linkedin-post>

### 1.5 Personal site
**URL:** <https://theadamrobinson.com/>

Hub for speaking, podcasts, longer essays.

### 1.6 YouTube
**Channel:** <https://www.youtube.com/@retentionadam>

Includes "UnF*ck My Startup" weekly workshop format and the
"Billion Dollar Challenge" docuseries on Robinson's journey from
$30M ARR toward $100M+ across his SaaS portfolio. Higher production

### 1.7 Retention.com (historical context)
**URL:** <https://www.retention.com>

His first SaaS — ecommerce identity resolution, ~$22M ARR with 6
people, 87% gross margins. Required reading to understand the
bootstrap origin story and why RB2B was the natural next bet.

## 5. Recommended purchases / product entry points

There is no Adam Robinson book and no flagship paid course at time
of writing. The recommended set is:

### 5.1 RB2B paid tiers
**Best for:** B2B founders / operators who want to live-experience
the freemium-as-distribution model from the buyer side, and who can
actually use person-level visitor ID on their own site.

- Free tier — Slack alerts on ~200 person-level visitors/month, no
  credit card. Real product, not a demo. Sign up at <https://www.rb2b.com>.
- Paid tiers — unlock email enrichment, CRM integrations, higher
  volume. Pricing changes regularly (announced in public on
  LinkedIn / the RB2B newsletter); check the current page.

For DR specifically: running RB2B against the DR website is the
fastest way to see how the model works in practice and to learn the
person-level signal motion from inside.

### 5.2 RB2B Newsletter (free subscription)
<https://newsletter.rb2b.com/>

The single best ongoing source for Robinson's internal operating
decisions, pricing experiments, and Baremetrics-style transparency
posts. Free. Subscribe.

### 5.3 "UnF*ck My Startup" workshop (YouTube)
<https://www.youtube.com/@retentionadam>

Weekly format, free. Closest thing to a paid course Robinson runs.

### 5.4 "Billion Dollar Challenge" docuseries
On Robinson's YouTube channel. Long-form footage of the operating
decisions and the arc from $30M ARR toward $100M.

### 5.5 Communities Robinson is part of (relevant for founder-led
growth peers)

- Founderpath community — bootstrapped SaaS founders, where
  Robinson has spoken multiple times.
  <https://founderpath.com/>
- SaaS Club — same audience profile.
  <https://saasclub.io/>
- The broader B2B SaaS LinkedIn founder cluster — not a formal
  community, but Robinson's reply threads function as one. Engaging
  in the comments on his posts is the path in.

There is no "Adam Robinson certification" or paid mastermind. The
model is: free LinkedIn presence, free newsletter, paid SaaS
product. Studying the model itself by buying the product is the
recommended purchase.
