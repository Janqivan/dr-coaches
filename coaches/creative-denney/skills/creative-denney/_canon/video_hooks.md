## 2. Frameworks and methods

### 2.1 Hook rate and hold rate — the measurement method
- **Hook rate (thumb-stop ratio)**: custom metric, not in Ads Manager by default = 3-second video views ÷ impressions. Shows whether messaging, creative and visuals in the first 3 seconds reach the right people. No hook/hold equivalent for image ads — only CTR. [YT:CdK0jdqxXOs] (2022-07), [YT:CCsty8R0UaA] (2025-01)
- **Hold rate**: % who watch the first 15 seconds (or the whole ad if under 15 s). Cross-check with average play time — long ads (60–90 s vs 30 s) inflate average play time; hold rate is purer because it isolates creative that kept people past 3 s. [YT:CCsty8R0UaA] (2025-01)
- **Account hook analysis (steps)**: (1) add a hook-rate column; (2) compare each ad to the account and campaign average (example DTC: 28% account, 29% campaign); (3) a 52% ad gets a double click — but cross-check spend: low reach = give it time; ~30% with lots of spend and purchases is "really compelling"; (4) open the asset and examine messaging in the first 3 s, imagery/footage in the first 3 s, and the thumbnail. Benchmark per account. [YT:CdK0jdqxXOs] (2022-07)
- **Thumbnail trap**: one ad had outrageously high hook rates because the thumbnail was set on a blacked-out frame — always check thumbnails. [YT:CdK0jdqxXOs] (2022-07)
- **Delayed-sell caution**: she asked whether a delayed sell is "almost like hold rate" (Barry Hott agreed). [YT:M2ObOuFQSv0] (2022-12)

### 2.2 "True hook structure" [quote src: YT:t-Xf12o4jt4] — four layers in the first 3 seconds (2025-08 → 2026)
When: building or diagnosing any video hook. Steps: (1) text-overlay hook; (2) sound hook (spoken line, music, whisper); (3) visual hook (frame zero); (4) the vibe — lighting, overlay, font choices, overall feel. She credits "Callaway" (as captioned) for the idea. Hook talk usually means only (1); she teaches all four. 2026 version: what they read + hear + see in frame zero → overall vibe; shown as an annotated Jones Road frame. ### 2.3 Hook test design
- **Untested footage**: same asset, six variants differing only in the first 3 seconds; copy, headline, description, CTA kept uniform. DTC. [YT:KFFcno59vQ8] (2021-09)
- **Hook mix per test**: one product-oriented, one problem-oriented, one wild card. Newer brands go scattered first, then go deep with 2–4 formulations inside the winning category (e.g. several negative hooks). [YT:k533N5RxXPY] (2024-06)
- **New creator batch**: one green-screen hook, one TikTok-response hook, at least one normal hook. Typical UGC test set (2025-12): one standard video, a green-screen variation, a TikTok response-bubble variation, or a completely new visual. [YT:R1HQW38EcRA] (2024-10), [YT:ZydGeio1DGQ] (2025-12)
- **Reformat test (2026)**: take one top performer and re-cut it with 3–6 different hook types for one big test (her "REFORMAT" board: HOOK 1–5). Big swings, not one-word changes. [YT:I8tXqqfjIX4] (2026-06)
- **Frontload**: "Frontload your problems"; you'd succeed more giving 400 first moments than micromanaging 200 — coach creators that the first moment matters. [YT:qoOBIPWH_qw] (2024-12)

### 2.5 Investment hook (must-test at onboarding)
When: almost every new client — high hit rate. Steps: (1) export all customer reviews to CSV; (2) search for the failed attempts buyers made before finding the product (time or money spent); (3) open on that investment. Why: targets Meta users with the same failed attempts; mirrors their journey; people trust a product more when they see the creator's/brand's investment. Her own title I tested over 1000 ads is one. GEN. [YT:t-Xf12o4jt4] (2025-08)

### 2.6 How-to hooks mapped to funnel stage (2026)
"most brands need more education in their funnel. This is just a fact." How-to signals learning, less selling. (a) **"How to know if"** — problem-aware top of funnel ("do I actually have a problem?"), plays on fears or reassures; (b) **"How do I pick / how do I choose"** — choosing between solutions; top performer for 6 months for a home-decor/apparel client; (c) **"How to start"** — very top of funnel ("How do I start to eat a carnivore diet?") or most-aware getting started with your brand; good for service businesses or deep consideration. GEN/LEADGEN-relevant. [YT:I8tXqqfjIX4] (2026-06)

### 2.7 Multiple-hook "hook reel"
Stack 2–3 hooks at the start; prevents early drop-off and hits several triggers (identity, controversy, demographic). Easiest lift without net-new ads: add an extra hook at the start of an existing winner. Winning combos: demographic + controversy; demographic + anti-traditional. Explosion visuals work slightly better placed after the initial hook as a second hook. GEN. [YT:wfENnO7PA5Q] (2025-02), [YT:t-Xf12o4jt4] (2025-08)

### 2.8 UGC script modules (2022 → 2025)
- **Thesis UGC outline** (some teams use AIDA instead): hook (imagery + messaging, first 3 s) → problem agitator → product intro as the solution → optional product how-to (in-depth explanation or a quiz) → benefits/testimonials ("better life realized") → catchy CTA. [YT:rNsOaXtlmRM] (2022-04)
- **"Perfect UGC ad" recipe**: hook → problem agitator → product as solution → specific benefits/features → authentic testimonial → catchy CTA. Add-on modules: social proof, competitor comparison, how-to/tutorial, shipping details (esp. fast shipping). [YT:Yi_1Vb4sfoQ] (2022-08). On-screen 2023 version: Hook, Problem statement, Product introduction, Features/benefits, Close with a strong CTA. [YT:MiurhGwirfY] (2023-03)
- **Rule**: don't jump to the solution right after the hook — agitate the problem first so the viewer knows "you're in the right place, this is not clickbait". [YT:Yi_1Vb4sfoQ] (2022-08)
- **Modular content (2025)**: break creator scripts into swappable modules (hook, problem agitator, solution, features & benefits, social proof, CTA) reusable across creators and compilations. [YT:yNTlVKvuX0o] (2025-03)

### 2.9 Founder ad formula (from "hundreds" of top founder ads, 2025-06)
(1) Personal intro — "my name is … and I'm the founder of"; (2) the personal problem/friction that made you create it; (3) turning point — why you were the one to build it; (4) solution — "go ham" on product, features, benefits that beat everything else; (5) proof points — how many bought/love it, lives changed, guarantee or risk-free offer; (6) soft, simple, genuine CTA. Production: any good modern phone; most of her founders use no external mic — turn fans off, shoot somewhere quiet. Add on-hand or stock B-roll of deep human emotion (pain, fear, love, connection). Settings: office, home, where the product is used; warehouse/manufacturing BTS; cars as wild card (Jones Road has Bobbi Brown do at least one car ad per launch). Hi-fi method: don't script — ask 10–20 questions to draw out emotional storytelling (credits Barry Hott). GEN. [YT:NsHk8o3jVvM] (2025-06), [YT:ToTQBWHm38I] (2025-06), [YT:lWpSmxuwSts] (2024-11)
- **Founder hooks to test every round**: (1) why you built the company — usually shows failed attempts that mirror the customer's journey; (2) the founder introducing themselves as founder — gurus say "no one cares", but it often tops because it feels organic and "we really champion entrepreneurs" in the US. [YT:t-Xf12o4jt4] (2025-08)
- **Prescriptive founder ad** (her recommended starting point): lo-fi founder video answering a TikTok comment-reply bubble that raises a common objection (often price, a recurring FAQ, pre-purchase anxiety); founder answers honestly. Oats Overnight's cost-breakdown founder ad ran for years and was "iterated on it to death". [YT:_ZHS6tW3f_Q] (2025-01), [YT:ToTQBWHm38I] (2025-06)

### 2.11 Creator selection and diagnosis
- **"UGC doesn't work for us" [quote src: YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA] — diagnose the creators (2026-09)**: (1) creator profiles don't match personas — not strict one-to-one; it's who the customer feels aspirational toward; (2) not aspirational enough — "bottom of the barrel" creators "just in it for the money", "nothing going on behind the eyes"; (3) budget pooled only in big or only in small creators; (4) weak delivery; (5) low volume; (6) not enough personal storytelling. Her team uses Modash AI to find creators already living a story (moving house, pregnant). DTC. [YT:4wM-i1cxtAM] (2026-09)
- **Criteria**: "magnetism" (eye contact, presence that pulls you in); vocal variety (speeding up and slowing down); energy — the bar has risen; not reading flat from a script. She predicts viewers will reject flat "boom boom boom" script-readers. Same creator can be great or flat on a given day → work with multiple creators, consider longer partnerships. [YT:_VtpfVeB2Kc] (2026-08)
- **Prompts not scripts**: most UGC is bad — too scripted or not given enough; give creators/founders questions, because people answer questions better than they read lines. Authentic-style prompt: imagine FaceTiming a friend to show the product, or an Instagram Story POV. [YT:qoOBIPWH_qw] (2024-12), [YT:pMAxIOL8dtE] (2023-12)
- **Education products**: real customers beat paid creators who don't understand why they'd need it; don't hand creators "best bidet" lines or unboxings when viewers don't yet know why they need one. [YT:qoOBIPWH_qw] (2024-12)
- **Look-alike luck isn't a mechanism**: a Gwyneth Paltrow look-alike got comments but didn't scale — it goes back to storytelling. [YT:qoOBIPWH_qw] (2024-12)

### 2.12 Creator shooting brief (2022 tips + specs)
Tips: (1) film multiple, really different hooks; (2) shoot in golden hour (she Googles the times; or sit by a window) — lighting/aesthetic is a top reason she doesn't rehire a creator; (3) clean backgrounds — shoot a few clips then check; (4) kill background noise (AC, fans, washer); record testimonial/VO separately with ~$20 wired earbuds with mic, not AirPods; (5) hair, makeup, an outfit you like — her best-feeling content outperformed; (6) deliver a folder of transitions (camera up close to product then pulling away); (7) invest in aesthetic — backgrounds, props, plants. Bonus: iPhone 4K, HDR off. Specs to state explicitly: good lighting, film everything vertical, add 1–3 s at start and end of all raw clips, ACs off, no filters, no logos on clothing, clean aesthetic background. Example fitness brief: sitting/stretching b-roll with laptop in view; at least five workout clips with full body in frame; closer lifestyle clips (water, snack); creator texted test shots first. DTC. [YT:ZrNh5MQuL3Y] (2022-08), [YT:Ss7bdFf0bsk] (2022-10)

### 2.13 Editing rules for paid social
- **Pacing**: much faster than any other editing — Claudia Walsh example, every clip ~0.5 s or less on mundane footage (made a team lesson of it). [YT:R1HQW38EcRA] (2024-10)
- **J-cuts and L-cuts** raise engagement/watch time; rarely discussed in ads. **Zooms**: hard-cut punch-in or slow zoom both lift engagement. [YT:R1HQW38EcRA] (2024-10)
- **Native fonts only** in UGC-style ads — brand fonts "scream ad"; TikTok = Proxima Nova Semibold, Stories = San Francisco; stock footage + native fonts (Stories, TikTok, CapCut) removes the stock feel. [YT:R1HQW38EcRA] (2024-10), [YT:NsHk8o3jVvM] (2025-06)
- **B-roll is "liquid gold"**: request far, close, too close, selfie, top-down, low angle; fill gaps with stock (e.g. aging agitation for skincare; age diversity for 50+). For 55+ audiences, stock footage tends to beat iPhone footage behind text-on-video. Testing "a little bit of subliminal messaging": stock shots evoking the feeling (menopause, relief from itchy skin). [YT:R1HQW38EcRA] (2024-10), [YT:yNTlVKvuX0o] (2025-03), [YT:ePcEcT2PH8E] (2026-04), [YT:eFyvp0mu7UQ] (2026-05) — several of these said inside Storyblocks sponsor segments.
- **Overlays/stickers**: TikTok-comment response overlay has the most potential; also a picture of another ad, press, native stickers — sticker tests tended to beat no stickers. [YT:R1HQW38EcRA] (2024-10)
- **Captions**: A/B tests inconclusive; using fewer captions performs just as well. [YT:lWpSmxuwSts] (2024-11)
- **Sound**: design for sound on; add sound effects for emphasis without overdoing. [YT:R1HQW38EcRA] (2024-10)
- **Double-person effect** (same creator twice in frame), from Jarrod's "Video Editing Rodeo" course — works in the hook, rarely used, creator must film for it. [YT:R1HQW38EcRA] (2024-10)

### 2.14 Video format tier list (2026-08, NOW)
- **S**: founder content (hi- or lo-fi); partnership ads (also request lo-fi statics from contracted creators); VSLs (health/wellness, fitness, anything needing upfront education).
- **A**: yappers (hard to nail creator + script + setting; scale nicely once right); green-screen commentary (esp. apparel + education); David & Goliath (Goliath visualized with stock, cut with creator/founder); amateur investigation (e.g. creator investigating car dealerships).
- **B**: conversation ads (hard to execute); objection handling (often top 15); TikTok love letter / supporting characters (easy); EGC; challenging-your-beliefs (needs great b-roll and known limiting beliefs).

- **Sound off → sound on.** 2020: design for sound off with subtitles. 2024: design for sound on (70% stat); captions A/B inconclusive, fewer captions fine. 2025–26: listen to the creator; whisper VO converted most across clients; vocal variety a selection criterion.
