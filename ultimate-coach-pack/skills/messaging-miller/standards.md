# Donald Miller — Standards Bank

Corpus status: 174 transcripts (1031002 words) in the raw layer plus canon items. Checks re-sourced on 2026-10-20; tags without YT ids were not found in transcripts.

This file is the bar. Every page, script, email or asset the coach produces or judges is graded against the checks for its task type. A check is PASS only with evidence pointed at the work itself (a quoted line, a count, a position on the page). "Reads clean" is not evidence. State the evidence type next to each verdict.

Scoring: count PASS / total for the task type. Deliver only at >= 85% with zero FAIL on checks marked ★. Otherwise fix and regrade (max 2 loops), then deliver with the remaining FAILs listed honestly.

Canon shorthand: SB7 = storybrand_sb7_frameworks.md, MMS = marketing_made_simple_frameworks.md, BMS = business_made_simple_frameworks.md, CB = coach_builder_grow_small_business_frameworks.md, PCI = public_corpus_index.md, SKILL = SKILL.md.

Canon tensions to respect when grading (state which version the output uses; do not silently pick):
1. One-liner template. SKILL and SB7 Framework 10: "We help [character] who want [desire] but face [problem]. We do this by [plan] so they can [success]." MMS Framework 1: "You know how [problem]? Well, we [solution], so that [result]." SKILL says never accept a one-liner that doesn't fit its template, so the SB7 form is the default; the MMS form is allowed for spoken/ad-lib use and must be labelled.
2. Flight Plan contents. SKILL and SB7 Framework 12: mission, key characters, goals (three), themes, three economic objectives, income streams. CB Framework 7: mission, key characters, three economic priorities, cultural themes, income streams, scored plane diagram. A Flight Plan PASSES with either, if it says which.
3. Canon examples carry unverified claims. The "gold standard" DR one-liner in SKILL ("sign 5-10x more cases without losing $30K to ad waste") and "$5.19M / 5.91x ROAS" are examples inside the canon, not verified facts for any page. Any outcome number on a published page must come from Ivan's or the client's data, or be marked "to verify". Advertising-rules compliance for law firms and franchisors is outside Miller's canon and is not judged here.

## T5. Email sequences: nurture and sales

Evidence type: each email, its subject line, word count and its job.

- ★ **Nurture emails carry no hard pitch.** Tip, notification, or soft-pitch reminder only; each tip email is one tip, around 300 words. A pitch in every email = FAIL. [canon: MMS Framework 6] (not found in transcripts)
- ★ **Sales sequence is the five-email arc in order.** (1) deliver the asset, (2) the problem (external plus internal), (3) a customer transformation told as a mini-story/testimonial, (4) overcome the biggest objection, (5) direct sales email with a specific offer and a deadline. Only email 5 has an unambiguous buy-now. [canon: MMS Framework 8; SKILL Core Framework 6] (not found in transcripts)
- ★ **Stage-matched.** The sales sequence is not sent before the nurture sequence has run; no pitch on the lead-gen page. Curiosity first, enlightenment second, commitment last. [YT:p6FvTKrpu4U] [YT:5OJT1ph-yL4] [canon: MMS Framework 5 failure mode]
- **Subject lines are one of the three types:** curiosity gap, specific number/list, or direct promise. Branded subject lines ("DR Weekly Roundup #47") = FAIL. [canon: MMS Framework 7] (not found in transcripts)
- **Sequence length scales with price.** Canon floor: 6 or more nurture emails, then weekly, same day and time. For higher-priced offers Miller says at least 16 emails, more like 32 or 52, because buyers arrive 1 to 2 years into nurture. [YT:WearNKXvAIs] [canon: MMS Framework 6]
- **One topic per email.** A newsletter of five topics = FAIL. [canon: MMS Framework 6 failure mode] (not found in transcripts)
- **Benchmarks stated for the audit:** open rate above 25%; if below, the subject line is the lever. [canon: MMS Frameworks 7, 10] (not found in transcripts)
- **Email 3 uses a real client story** with permission; no invented testimonials. [canon: MMS Framework 8; SKILL Rule 4] (not found in transcripts)
- **Objection in email 4 is the one that actually stalls DR deals** (price, time, risk, fit), named from Ivan's pipeline or marked as hypothesis. [canon: MMS Framework 8] (not found in transcripts)
- **Customer-first pronouns and customer-words problem statement** in every email. [YT:rnwvrvWZ9CY] [canon: SKILL Behavior Rules; SB7 Framework 2]

## T6. Sales conversations as story (call scripts, discovery questions, pitch opening)

Miller's sales canon is the SB7 arc applied to a call. Negotiation and price pushback belong to the negotiation coach; hand off those.

Evidence type: the script, beat by beat.

- ★ **Opens with the customer's desire and the "drama in their story" question,** not with the pitch. The question is in the form "Right now, what's the biggest source of frustration in [area]?" or equivalent specific; generic goals questions ("what are your goals this year?") = FAIL. [canon: BMS Framework 9; BMS Framework 8 beat 1] (not found in transcripts)
- ★ **Tells their story back before presenting anything.** The script has a step where the three problem layers, the stakes and the success vision are reflected in the prospect's own words. Miller's follow-up opens with the prospect's problem ("I heard you when you said you were challenged by..."), before small talk. [YT:0v2kEMi-c4I] [canon: CB Framework 3 step 2; BMS Framework 8]
- ★ **Seven beats in order:** desire, three problem layers, guide positioning (empathy first), three-step plan, calls to action (direct plus transitional), success, stakes. [canon: BMS Framework 8] (not found in transcripts)
- **Sales process is four steps:** qualify, tell their story back, present the plan, direct ask. [canon: CB Framework 3] (not found in transcripts)
- **Direct ask is unambiguous,** no waffle. [canon: CB Framework 3 step 4] (not found in transcripts)
- **Guide posture.** Empathy line before authority proof. [YT:C9CBjaAOuNE] [YT:e9w8B0H0Fls] [canon: SB7 Framework 4]
- **Handoff.** Price pushback or discount requests in the script are marked "negotiation coach". [canon: SKILL Escalation] (not found in transcripts)

## Universal checks (every output)

- ★ **Retrieved before writing.** At least 3 searches run; the work uses at least 3 retrieved canon passages, cited as [YT:id] where a transcript hit exists, else [canon: file].
- ★ **No invented quotes, numbers, stories or dates.** Every quoted Miller line exists verbatim in a retrieved hit.
