## T2. Creator briefs, editor briefs, shot lists, scripts

- ★ **Reference ad attached.** The brief links at least one visual reference (ad or organic TikTok) per concept. "Here's my product, good luck" / "here's our app, record a video" = FAIL. A linked reference gets the result "nine out of 10 times". [YT:3TzdwzAG1X8]
- ★ **Checkbox shot list.** Shots are a bulleted list a creator can tick off, each one literal (angle, action, location, lighting, time of day where it matters). "B-roll of happy client" without the action = FAIL. [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA]
- ★ **2+ recorded hooks per concept** (V1/V2), visually different, plus one script body. [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w]
- ★ **Script control matches creator trust.** New creator: word-for-word script. Trusted creator: hook scripted, body as main message/talking points. The hook is scripted in both cases. For regulated clients (immigration law): claims are word-for-word from an approved list, no ad-libbing, refilm if a claim slips (regulated rule: items layer only [YT:jAc63K9lRIQ]; she does work with an FTC-compliance lawyer [YT:CcaG5RYNcSI]). [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] [YT:hS47--F7290]
- **Content guide present.** Value props, dos/don'ts, inspiration brands, delivery instructions (folder, file naming). [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w]
- **Human-written script.** No pasted AI script; lines read as spoken, not as copy. "it's going to sound very robotic". [YT:hS47--F7290] [YT:EHiXurAZPj0]
- **Editor brief is a separate document.** Reference link (Motion report or top ad with notes on pacing, lighting, clip choice), VO file link, and per line: VO line and on-screen caption kept separate, plus suggested visual; repeated per hook version. [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w]
- **Captions are not the VO transcript.** On-screen captions carry ~3-word key points, not every spoken word. [YT:hS47--F7290]
- **Production specifics for her devices.** Green screen: creator filmed against plain white/neutral wall. Text exchange: contact name/photo changed, chat cleared, screen-recorded. Transition named from a library with a linked example. [YT:hS47--F7290] [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] [YT:jAc63K9lRIQ]
- **Usage rights stated.** Brief or contract terms give full, perpetual usage rights to raw footage and whitelisting access ("I need full rights ... in the contract from day one"). [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] [YT:CcaG5RYNcSI]

## T3. Teardowns and pre-launch QC (own ad, client ad, competitor ad)

- ★ **Read by part, with the metric for each part.** Verdict is split: opening (thumb-stop / first 2–3s), body (hold rate), click (CTR / click-to-lead). Not one overall grade. [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA] [YT:3TzdwzAG1X8]
- ★ **No verdict on tiny spend.** If spend is under a meaningful threshold the teardown says "not readable yet" instead of winner/loser ("doesn't mean anything" on $10). Her own rule: spend at least 4x AOV on an ad before deciding to kill it, aiming to get there within a week (DTC); for lead gen the coach names whose rule it uses. [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA]; "doesn't mean anything" on $10 is items layer only [YT:ZQAjWwxk2C0]
- ★ **Good hook + good CTR + no leads = not a creative verdict.** The teardown routes to landing page, form, speed, offer before proposing creative changes. [YT:3TzdwzAG1X8]
- ★ **Sound-off watch done.** States what a muted viewer understands at 3s and at the end; spelling checked with sound off. [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] [YT:PvjkCV9WelU]
- **"Why did I stop" answered.** Names the stopping element and whether it is new on the feed, plus talent energy and delivery. [YT:hS47--F7290]
- **Edit QC items checked individually:** length/filler, sequencing, script alignment, safe zones (TikTok captions and native elements cover the edges; keep key elements centered), legible fonts on phone, captions correct, CTA present, audio levels/noise. [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] [YT:l1_xSlj4Z50]
- **Performance boosters checked:** cuts on pauses (but ~3s minimum hold for legibility), zoom on key testimonial words, a named human desire, where the eye goes first, split screen / before-after / us-vs-them used where it fits. [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w]
- **Energy verdict.** Talent "rambling off product benefits" with flat voice/face = FAIL on talent, named separately from script. [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA]
- **Ingredients verdict.** Ends with keep-hook/change-body or keep-body/graft-best-hook, not "make a new ad". [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA]
- **"Would you be proud of this" gut check** stated against the inspiration ad: better or worse, and why. [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w]
- **Platform spend is a signal.** Do not turn off an ad that is soaking up spend just because its ROAS is lower than another ad's (AppLovin, ecom; transfer to Meta lead gen untested). [YT:I0SPikVQdYs] (2026)
