# Facebook Creative Coach — Standards Bank

Corpus status: 1213 transcripts (4430258 words) in the raw layer plus canon items. Checks re-sourced on 2026-10-20; tags without YT ids were not found in transcripts.

This file is the bar. Every piece of work the coach produces or judges is graded against the checks for its task type. A check is PASS only with evidence pointed at the work itself (a frame, a line, a number, a row in a table). "Feels right" is not evidence. Each check states what it is judged **on**.

No persona. Every check names whose standard it is. Where the seven split, the check is written as a labeled split ("Hott: … / Heath: …"): the work PASSES if it picks a side, names it, and says why that side fits the case. Averaging the two positions, or silently using one, is a FAIL. Consensus means 3+ of the seven stated it independently. Motion guests are named as guests and never count toward consensus.

Each check carries its source. Pull full context with `scripts/search.py "<check words>" --layer items` when a check is borderline or Ivan disputes it. Era: NOW = 2026, EVERGREEN = 2025, ALLTIME = before 2025 (possibly dated).

Ivan's house rules (all copy is DRAFT for his approval; literal visuals from the buyer's world, no metaphor imagery) outrank the canon. They are tagged [Ivan] and are not practitioner standards.

Scoring: count PASS / total for the task type. Deliver only at ≥ 85% with zero FAIL on checks marked ★. Otherwise fix and regrade (max 2 fix loops), then deliver with the remaining FAILs listed honestly.

## T1. Hooks: hook sets, first frames, hook-rate / hold-rate diagnosis (/fbc-hooks)

- ★ **Relevance in frame zero, not just attention.** A cold viewer knows who the ad is for from the first frame and the top of the frame before a word is read. Hott (consensus with Savannah, Cottrell, Cooper): "so many ads just die in the first frame"; setting at the top signals relevance before play. *On: frame-0 description + top-third text.* [YT:mpj0A4Prxu4] [YT:s4mLWmECoKI] [YT:fawsUmNeCkg]
- ★ **All layers filled per hook.** Each video hook specifies visual, spoken/sound, on-screen text, and vibe. Denney: four parts (text, sound, visual, vibe). Cottrell: three (visual, audible, text), "all of those combined". A hook that is only a line of copy FAILS. *On: each hook row.* [YT:t-Xf12o4jt4] [YT:Mqj05tYuDI0]
- **The visual carries second one.** Spoken line in the first second ≤ 5–6 words; the frame does the stopping. Levinger: "hook doesn't mean words". Denney: changing the visual hook moves performance more than the text or verbal hook. *On: word count of the spoken opener + frame description.* [YT:DEoj28QXUYE] [YT:t-Xf12o4jt4]
- **Passes Levinger's 300 ms check.** Frame zero contains at least one of: face mid-emotion, motion against the scroll, a literal break in the scene, a familiar sound. *On: frame 0.* [YT:DEoj28QXUYE]
- **Hook sets up the body.** Each hook names what the next 3–15 s must pay off. Cottrell (2026-09): less "glue this hook onto the front", more "use the hook to set up the rest of the video". *On: "sets up body" field.* [YT:Mqj05tYuDI0]
- ★ **Every hook looks different, not reworded.** No two hooks share setting + person + device; none is an overlay-only swap. Heath: re-record with a different background, person, location or angle to pass the "I've seen this before filter" (outfit and location changes make a different visual hook on the same body [YT:oMhsPXBTqtg]). Heath dissent (2026-10): Meta itself now recommends swapping the first 3–5 s of a winner, so he treats a hook swap as acceptable Meta-side; state which side you took. Cottrell (2026-02): headline/hook swaps "don't count as creative testing in Meta's eyes anymore". Savannah: Meta reads creatives as distinct only when the visual differs in the first 3–5 s. *On: diversity matrix.* [YT:IRyR9PzSnM8] [YT:_BuCO3Jf-K0] [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] [YT:RTrRKB0iXwQ]
- **Mix before depth.** A new hook set contains one offer/product-oriented, one problem-oriented and one wild-card hook before going deep in a family. Denney's mix. *On: family column.* [YT:k533N5RxXPY]
- **No logo or brand handle first on cold hooks.** Hott: logo up front "only telegraphs to existing fans". Cottrell: removing the brand handle "can massively help your hook rate". *On: frame 0–3 s.* [YT:nUVl_zpeipY] [YT:q_PsIjZl6e4]
- **Trigger written as a scene.** Persona × trigger is a moment, not a demographic. Cooper: "a trigger event is the moment a problem stops being tolerable". Levinger: life event / social comparison / frustration tipping point / external disruption. *On: trigger line.* [YT:9O3-AdYpwyQ] [YT:po85BKDTG-Y]
- **Emotion split, declared.** Levinger: embarrassment, not shame ("I would so rather you go after embarrassment than shame") / Hott: ugly ads work off "their problems their insecurities and their interests"; his rule is "make people feel something" within the first frames. A shame- or insecurity-led hook names which side it took. Legal verticals: compliance is Ivan's call. *On: emotion line.* [YT:LxWwRykBtfc] [YT:YuS18wnD_bc] [YT:bd9MGQ0-Vqc]
- ★ **Diagnosis judged on cost per result, hook/hold as the why.** Cooper: "Stop glorifying hook rates and hold rates". Cottrell: hook rate is relative to the account (some clients profitable at ~12%, others ~70%). Any diagnosis that calls a winner on hook rate alone FAILS. *On: diagnosis block.* [YT:Be9YLcOory4] [YT:Lkx7nOGIslw] [YT:opWKMc6iavo]
- **Benchmark is the account, not a universal number.** Any cited benchmark carries speaker + date (Denney ~24% Thesis average, 2022-07, ALLTIME; Cottrell 12–70%, 2026-07) and is set against the account average. *On: numbers in the output.* [YT:CdK0jdqxXOs] [YT:Lkx7nOGIslw]
- **Hold-first vs hook-first split, declared.** Guest Lavin: fix the body (3–15 s) first, then iterate hooks / Heath: keep the proven body, Frankenstein a top hook onto it. Heath also reads hook rate against the account average and finds ads with a low hook rate but good cost per result. Both protect the body; a fix that rewrites a converting body FAILS. *On: fix recommended.* [YT:M-zOOiG5GLw] [YT:bAo6SICFyIE] [YT:mycqb92wJqk]
- **Statics judged on CTR, not hook rate.** Cottrell (2025-08): a static has no hook rate or hold rate, so read click-through rate and cost per click for why it won or lost. *On: metric named for static hooks.* [YT:HXox1I6QQvQ] [canon: hooks_retention.md §5, Denney attribution not found in transcripts]

## T2. Video scripts (UGC, founder, yapper, problem-solution, villain-hero, mini VSL, lead-gen service script) (/fbc-video-script)

- ★ **Offer + next step named before the script.** The script states the service and the real next step (form, booked call, consult). Heath: "free consultation" alone fails; name the service. *On: header + CTA beat.* [YT:1EQrp8MJRek] [YT:KgO_J6pwv9I]
- ★ **Format sized to the ask.** Big ask (in-person consult, done-for-you) cold = longer proof video; small ask = short. Heath: for a huge trust decision a simple ad cold has "no chance"; his done-for-you ads are "more complex longer videos". *On: format + length vs offer.* [YT:GPp8Y-lKzJw] [YT:N3X8CuD_VQk]
- ★ **Product/offer enters at the emotional shift, not second 2 (cold).** Levinger: shift point ~40–50% through (12–15 s of 30 s; ~30 s of 60 s); too early "collapses into 'that's an ad'". Remarketing exempt (offer right after the hook, Cottrell's stage rule). *On: timed beat table.* [YT:8WRS3-y4K98] [canon: hooks_retention.md §2 for the remarketing exemption (not found in transcripts)]
- **Loop held to the payoff.** Levinger's drop-off rule: if viewers bounce ~50% in, "you told people about the video way too soon". The script does not resolve the open loop before the last third. *On: where the hook's question is answered.* [YT:sBS1vy-5Be0]
- **Payoff worth the setup (villain-hero, problem-solution).** Cottrell: the most common villain-hero mistake is "the payoff just isn't worth it"; the hero beat lists specific USPs, not "go buy". *On: solution beat.* [YT:4o46eGI6XwQ]
- **Structure is a named canon structure, timed.** Cottrell problem-solution (hook → problem → agitate → solution+benefit → proof → CTA); Denney founder formula (intro → personal problem → turning point → solution → proof → soft CTA); Heath lead-gen timings (hook 0–3 s, problem 3–8, solution 8–15, proof 15–18, CTA 18–20). *On: beat table labels.* [YT:RExPkxyRB6o] [YT:NsHk8o3jVvM] [YT:TpZrPY6jTE8]
- **A retention device on every beat.** Each beat names its device: open loop (Levinger), outfit/location change or prop (Cottrell), punch-in/J-L cut (Denney), rehook every 5–10 s (guest Lucas Hamby). Cottrell: 2–3 location changes in a ~30 s video. *On: retention column.* [YT:aTL4CVsA3mo] [YT:mVb5HKHsC3w] [canon: video_ugc.md §3 (open loop, punch-in not found in transcripts)]
- ★ **Ad signals stripped.** No logo open, perfect fades, sweeping zooms (Hott); no "suspiciously good lighting" or read-script voice (Levinger); passes Cooper's realism test (phone propped where a real person would put it). Levinger (2026-07): the brain asks "is this an ad?" and reads really clean cuts and very quick movement as ad. *On: edit spec + shot list.* [YT:DGTwYyI-81s] [YT:Z82ehPFBY54] [YT:a5ruZB-Urlw]
- **Delivery split, declared.** Denney/Cooper: give questions and talking points, not a read script ("nine times out of 10" it's the creator) / Cottrell: "Every single good ad comes from a good script" (guests Mikac/Markovic: strict specific scripts won). The creator-direction block picks one and says why (strong vs weak talent). *On: creator direction.* [YT:qoOBIPWH_qw] [YT:GdYGUirdZYQ] [YT:RExPkxyRB6o]
- **No traditional read-and-edit UGC as default.** Cooper (2026-03): scripted read UGC "does not work on paid social anymore"; Denney rates run-of-the-mill UGC C tier. Hott (2026-03): the default of finding a creator and handing over a script "is not 2026". Prefer yapper, founder, team member. *On: format choice.* [YT:OzkRU3Wx_1w] [YT:m8dbcF407H8] [canon: video_ugc.md §1; Denney C-tier rating not found in transcripts]
- **Caption lane chosen, split named.** Cottrell/Denney: single-line native captions / Levinger: two lines (static open-loop top line + speech captions) / Cooper (2026-03): hook overlay then few or none. *On: on-screen text spec.* [YT:ahwbstiCVpQ] [YT:sBS1vy-5Be0] [YT:OzkRU3Wx_1w]
- **Works sound-off.** Savannah: most common hook mistake is no on-screen text; assume sound off. *On: text spec vs VO.* [YT:hS47--F7290]
- **Service ads have a human on camera.** Savannah: the biggest service/app mistake is no humans (screen recordings, VO slideshows). Hott: a team member who actually does the work is "really hard to beat". *On: who is on camera.* [YT:3TzdwzAG1X8] [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs]
- **Length variants cut from one shoot.** 15–20 s / 30 s / 60–90 s, each with beats kept. Heath's physio compilation best ~90 s (LEADGEN, 2024-10). *On: length-variants block.* [YT:z3LiJ0PbY-0]

## T3. Static briefs (/fbc-static-brief)

- ★ **One goal, one persona per static.** Denney: "Don't try to do two jobs in one ad." Cooper: one persona per ad, separate ads for others. Consensus (Denney, Cooper, Levinger). *On: "one goal" + persona fields per variant.* [YT:5C5VhqW9HCc] [YT:GdYGUirdZYQ]
- ★ **Stranger / one-second test.** Someone who has never heard of the client knows what is offered and to whom in about a second. Cooper: "Assume no one knows or cares." Denney: if a stranger can't tell, kill it; "clarity is always going to out-beat creativity". Warm-only headlines FAIL cold. *On: headline + image read cold.* [YT:XQwJRuN8p-Q] [YT:5C5VhqW9HCc]
- ★ **Format fits the ask.** Heath: a simple image for a big ask cold has no chance; brief the static for warm, send the cold job to video. Small ask (named free consult, webinar) may run as image. *On: ask-size line + temperature.* [YT:GPp8Y-lKzJw] [YT:N3X8CuD_VQk]
- **Hierarchy specified.** Headline in the top third, never over the busiest part of the photo (Hott); headline → key visual → support (Denney); ≤ ~3 callouts or one oversized number. *On: reading-order + layout fields.* [YT:e54fR6s6WSw] [YT:5C5VhqW9HCc] [canon: statics.md §2 (callout cap not found in transcripts)]

- **Proof device present, golden-nugget grade.** Cottrell: "Social proof is at the heart of any good static". Denney (2026-08): testimonial statics "E. F" unless "golden nugget" quotes. Generic praise FAILS. *On: proof field.* [YT:TouPE2n7mzo] [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8]
- **Look picks an end.** Cooper: "The middle ground is where ads go to die" (super ugly/organic or obviously-an-ad with great DR copy). Generic "them", never a named competitor without Ivan's call. *On: us-vs-them variants only.* [YT:NsHk8o3jVvM] [YT:qUn6OV_70fQ]
- ★ **Variants are distinct entities.** No two variants share persona + message + format; ≥ 2 formats and ≥ 2 looks. Cottrell (2026): "completely different statics instead of one static with 50 different headlines". Denney: "Statics tend to become more stale more quickly." *On: diversity matrix.* [YT:_BuCO3Jf-K0] [YT:eFyvp0mu7UQ]
