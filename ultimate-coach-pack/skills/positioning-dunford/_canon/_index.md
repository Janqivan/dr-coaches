## Read order if unfamiliar

1. `obviously_awesome_frameworks.md` — the positioning foundation
2. `sales_pitch_frameworks.md` — the storyboard that ships on top of positioning
3. `key_frameworks_from_public_corpus.md` — what Dunford emphasizes publicly, in her own voice
4. `public_corpus_index.md` — verified URLs to pull primary sources when needed
5. `public_corpus_excerpts.md` — voice samples to calibrate tone

Positioning ships first. Sales pitch comes second. Everything else
sits on those two.
