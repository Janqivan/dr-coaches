Company credentials go last. [YT:7jgj-Mvu_FQ] [YT:bSfE8zZUoMc] [YT:RF-kXeOCAEw] (rewritten: the canon's five-step list delivered the unique strength first; Dixon says the story leads to it and credentials come at the end)
- ★ **The data point is real and carries its denominator.** From DR's own accounts with the sample size stated, or marked "to verify". An invented stat = FAIL. [YT:RF-kXeOCAEw] (a provocative claim must be backed up with data) [canon: SKILL rule 4; excerpts D] (denominator rule is canon only)

## T3. JOLT script (email, call talk track, or reply to an indecisive buyer)

- ★ **Diagnosed source matches the move.** Valuation problem calls for limiting, lack of information for a recommendation, outcome uncertainty for taking risk off. The script names the source it is addressing: choice overload (the valuation problem), information overload, or expectations overload (outcome uncertainty). [YT:fVUgDE1beSA] [YT:a-wNs2oF94U] [YT:_IJKmiYRPAg] [canon: jolt three sources]
- ★ **A concrete safety net.** Dixon: it is typically not an opt-out clause. The core moves are managing expectations (set the customer up for upside, do not promise the once-in-a-lifetime case study result to the CFO) and concrete assurance of success (the worst case, the result you expect to beat, how success is guaranteed). A guarantee, phased pilot or pause clause may be added with the actual terms. Uses terms Ivan has already approved; any new guarantee is flagged "needs Ivan's sign-off". "We're flexible" = FAIL. [YT:ly8ri7pRkbE] [YT:_IJKmiYRPAg] [YT:FdHmx3Ye6ww] [Ivan: no invented terms] (rewritten: the canon led with money-back and cancel-at-will terms; Dixon puts expectation-setting first)
- ★ **No pressure.** No "pricing changes Friday", no "you will lose the slot". The script lowers the stakes. Sequence: dial up the fear of missing out only to win the change, then dial down the fear of messing up once they have agreed. [YT:cUzEgRetmC4] [YT:GB48DV8as0E] [YT:S1sJJKH6Z0Q]
- **Judge question worded correctly.** "What's making this hard to decide?" and not "what would help you move forward?". [canon: jolt J] (exact wording not found in transcripts; the judging technique is pings and echoes, see T2)
- **Offers a recommendation with reasoning.** "Based on what you've told me, I'd start with X because A, B, C." One position, no hedge, no neutral menu. Dixon: stop diagnosing, diagnosing, diagnosing and make the recommendation; it shifts the burden of a bad choice from the decider to the recommender. [YT:Rls1e5I6kZY] [YT:ly8ri7pRkbE] [YT:EekBITPX-S0]
- **Limits the exploration.** Closes at least one branch explicitly ("you don't need another case study; here's what's relevant and what isn't"). Builds trust, shows expertise, and proactively raises objections instead of leaving them to fester. [YT:ggbW6Dgbzao] [YT:UJSz_ron9r0]
- **Does not dump new information.** No additional case studies or decks sent to an indecisive buyer. [YT:UJSz_ron9r0] [YT:S1sJJKH6Z0Q]
- **Anchors on the realistic outcome,** not the spectacular case study. [YT:_IJKmiYRPAg]
- **Fewer options, not more.** [YT:Rls1e5I6kZY] [YT:a-wNs2oF94U] (too many choices make "choose none" the easiest choice)
- **Radical candor on the repeat request.** When the buyer asks again for another demo, reference call or report, ask why, and say honestly that it will not address the worry they are showing, then offer the better route. "With all due respect, I don't think that's going to address the concerns you're still struggling with." [YT:ggbW6Dgbzao] [YT:kgMnjmWYiKs]

## Universal checks (every output)

- ★ **Retrieved before writing.** At least 3 searches run; the work uses at least 3 retrieved canon passages, cited as [YT:id] where a transcript holds the point, otherwise [canon: file §n].
- ★ **No invented quotes, numbers, stories or dates.** Every statistic matches the canon and carries its denominator and source (6,000 reps [YT:uxFZC5y_ZtA]; 2.5 million calls [YT:cUzEgRetmC4]; 3,000 partners across 41 firms [YT:DhN8-NA-qLI]; the "83 companies" figure was not found in transcripts). Quotes are short, attributed, and limited to the canon. DR facts come from Ivan or current memory, not from the v1 SKILL's DR block. Unverifiable claims are replaced with "I'd want to verify that before quoting it".
- ★ **Did the work.** If Ivan gave a task, the output is the finished thing (the insight, the script, the map, the asset), not a list of questions. The SKILL's "one clarifying question first" is met by stating the assumed sale type and the one open question inside the deliverable, never instead of it.
- **In voice.** Data point first, implication second. Named profiles (Mobilizer, Talker, Blocker, Challenger). Calm, formal, no hype words, no motivational language. "The data does not support that" is the closest thing to a flourish. Counterintuitive findings stated as findings. DR vocabulary: "signed case", "cost per signed case", "intake". No em dashes.
- **Ends with one move.** The specific move, why in one line, and what would change the call.
