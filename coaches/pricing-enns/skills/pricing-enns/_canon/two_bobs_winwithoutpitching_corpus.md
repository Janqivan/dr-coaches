## 1. 2Bobs Podcast — Episodes Featuring Blair Enns

The 2Bobs podcast is the long-form Enns canon. He co-hosts every
episode with David C. Baker. The episodes below are the ones most
load-bearing for pricing and selling expertise.

- **2Bobs podcast home (all episodes):**
  https://2bobs.com/podcast — full episode index, ~230+ episodes,
  biweekly cadence. Apple Podcasts mirror at
  https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/2bobs-with-david-c-baker-and-blair-enns/id1219490591

- **The Four Conversations: A New Model for Selling Expertise:**
  https://2bobs.com/podcast/the-four-conversations-a-new-model-for-selling-expertise
  — Blair walks through the full four-stage architecture: probative,
  qualifying, value, closing. Anchor episode for the Four
  Conversations framework.

- **Pricing Creativity:**
  https://2bobs.com/podcast/pricing-creativity — David and Blair on
  the launch of the Pricing Creativity book and the three core
  rules creative firms should apply.

- **Mastering the Value Conversation:**
  https://2bobs.com/podcast/mastering-the-value-conversation —
  Blair: "the value conversation is where value pricing theory
  goes to die." Required listening before any value pricing attempt.

- **The Power of Options:**
  https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-power-of-options/id1219490591?i=1000468097709
  — Blair on offering proposal options and anchoring.

- **Different Pricing Models:**
  https://www.iheart.com/podcast/256-2bobs-with-david-c-baker-a-30937638/episode/different-pricing-models-45726929/
  — Blair on the range of pricing models and where billable hours fail.

- **Five Levels of Pricing Success:**
  https://podtail.com/en/podcast/2bobs-with-david-c-baker-and-blair-enns/five-levels-of-pricing-success/
  — Blair's stairstep model of pricing maturity.

- **Selling to Different Buyer Types:**
  https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/selling-to-different-buyer-types/id1219490591?i=1000557467566
  — Convenience, Relationship, Price, Value, Poker Player. How to
  build proposals each type cannot game.

## Recommended Purchases — In Order

If Ivan is going to buy anything, this is the sequence. Each builds
on the previous.

1. **The Win Without Pitching Manifesto** (~$10-20 paperback,
   ~$15 audiobook). Stance. Read first.
   https://www.amazon.com/Win-Without-Pitching-Manifesto/dp/1999523504

2. **The Four Conversations** (Oct 2024, ~$20-25 paperback,
   ~$15 Kindle, audiobook available). The architecture book.
   Replaces older sales-process content in the Manifesto with the
   full four-conversation playbook.
   https://www.amazon.com/Four-Conversations-Model-Selling-Expertise/dp/B0DCCZL2D7

3. **Pricing Creativity** (sold only through winwithoutpitching.com,
   three format tiers — paperback, deluxe binder, and full
   workshop-style kit. Prices range from ~$40 to several hundred
   dollars depending on format).
   https://www.winwithoutpitching.com/book/pricing-creativity/

4. **2Bobs podcast** — free. Subscribe and listen episodes
   chronologically from the Pricing Creativity episode forward.
   https://2bobs.com/podcast

5. **Win Without Pitching workshop** (priced by engagement, not
   published) — only worth pursuing once books 1-3 are internalised
   and Ivan has tried the frameworks on two or three live
   engagements. The workshop is most useful for teams, not solo
   founders.
   https://www.winwithoutpitching.com/workshops-wwp/

Total cost to get the full Enns canon, books only: roughly
$70-100 USD plus shipping. Cheapest high-leverage purchase in
agency owner reading.

## A Note On Quotes

Short fair-use quotes from the public sources above (under 15 words,
with attribution and URL) are appropriate when AIEnns is being
asked "where did Blair actually say that." Examples that meet the
fair-use bar:

- "The value conversation is where value pricing theory goes to
  die." (Blair Enns, 2Bobs episode "Mastering the Value
  Conversation," https://2bobs.com/podcast/mastering-the-value-conversation)

- "Those who make money talk about money early and often." (Blair
  Enns, paraphrased across multiple public interviews; see
  winwithoutpitching.com articles on the Value Conversation.)

- "Diagnose before you prescribe." (Blair Enns, The Win Without
  Pitching Manifesto; restated in nearly every guest interview.)

Anything longer than 15 words, anything from the books beyond
chapter titles, or anything that reproduces structural lists from
the books verbatim — do not include. Paraphrase, attribute, link.
