# ROHN'S GOAL-SETTING METHOD — the signature framework

Sources: *7 Strategies* Chs. 2–4 [7S]; seminar transcripts [YT:tSn5sEWEv_8] and [YT:siw7Jqlc3ak] (pulled Aug 28 2026); "How to Have Your Best Year Ever" Ch. 3 structure (full transcript index: spreadgreatideas.org); SUCCESS columns [SU]; Twelve Pillars [12P].

## The origin story — Shoaff's bank-balance line

> "When I first met Earl Shoaff... he asked me, 'Let me see your current list of goals and let's go over them and talk about them.' ... And I said to him, 'I don't have a list.' He said, 'Well, if you don't have a list of your goals, I can guess your bank balance within a few hundred dollars.' Which he did. ... Then I said to him, 'You mean if I had a list of goals, it would change my bank balance?' He said, 'Drastically.'" [YT:siw7Jqlc3ak; book version: "If you don't have a list of your goals, I can guess your bank balance within a few hundred dollars." [7S, Introduction]]

Shoaff also taught him "not to trust my memory, but to write it down." [SU, "It Only Takes 6 Steps to Plan Your Success"]

## The core doctrine: the goal's value is the person

> "The major reason for setting goals is to compel you to become the person it takes to achieve them." [7S, Ch. 4]
> "The major reason for setting a goal is for what it makes of you to accomplish it. What it makes of you will always be the far greater value than what you get." [S60; Twelve Pillars carries the same line [12P]]
> "When someone becomes a millionaire, the least important thing is what they have. The most important thing is what they have become." [7S, Ch. 4]
> "What kind of person will you have to become to get all you want?" [7S, Ch. 4]
> "INCOME RARELY EXCEEDS PERSONAL DEVELOPMENT." [7S, Ch. 4]

The millionaire story, as spoken [YT:tSn5sEWEv_8]:
> "He said, 'Jim, why don't you set a goal to become a millionaire?' ... 'Here's why. For what it will make of you to achieve it.' I'm telling you that statement changed my life. ... By the time I was 31, I was a millionaire. By the time I was 33, I was broke. ... When I lost all my money, guess what? I found out Mr. Shoaff was right. What was valuable was not the money. What was valuable was what I became to earn the money."
> "It's not what you get that makes you valuable. It's what you become." [YT:tSn5sEWEv_8; same line spoken in YT:siw7Jqlc3ak and OyKIbaSk2s4: "true happiness is not contained in what you get. Happiness is contained in what you become."]

## THE EXERCISE (run it exactly like this)

Step 0 — Participant, not spectator: "Get out a notebook or a diary. I want you to transform yourself from spectator (reader) into participant (writer)." [7S, Ch. 3]

**A — Decide what you want and brain-dump 50+ goals.** As spoken: "Number one, decide what you want... skills you want, income, places you'd like to go, habits you'd like to acquire... Number two, write it down." [YT:tSn5sEWEv_8] Across all categories: career, financial, health, relationships, learning, spiritual, adventure, contribution. No filtering. ("The key is to put everything on your list... take it out of your head and put it on paper." [YT:siw7Jqlc3ak])

**B — Categorize by timeframe.** Mark each goal 1, 3, 5, or 10 years (Best Year Ever Ch. 3 structure; lifetime list optional).

**C — Pick your top four one-year goals and write the reasons.** "Why are those four goals important to you?" — a paragraph of why for each. [YT:siw7Jqlc3ak] Five-plus reasons per major goal. With strong enough reasons, methods reveal themselves.

**D — The two questions per major goal:**
1. "What are the reasons?" (C above)
2. "What kind of person must I become to achieve all I want?" — "And I'll give you time to write that down now." [YT:siw7Jqlc3ak]
   > "What you become helps you to achieve, and what you achieve helps you to become. And the more you become, the more you can achieve." [YT:siw7Jqlc3ak]

**E — Motivators and de-motivators.** "What's got you turned on?... Now, here's the next question. What's got you turned off?" — make both lists. "Once I got that settled... my life has never been the same. It was like a revolution." [YT:siw7Jqlc3ak]

**F — Housekeeping rules for the list** [YT:tSn5sEWEv_8 + siw7Jqlc3ak]:
- "Number three, keep the old lists." ("I look back now 10, 20 years ago at goals I set... I've grown. I've changed.")
- "When you get something that's on one of these lists, check it off. Part of the fun of having a list is checking it off. And then if you can add some drama to checking it off, see that's what really helps." (He waited for the wheels to touch the runway in Madrid to check off Spain.)
- "If something's not that important to you, take it off your list. You don't have to accomplish this whole list... If you had a hundred items on your list and you accomplished eighty, who cares about the other twenty?"
- Carry the list; review weekly: "carry them with you at all times. Review them once a week." [7S, Ch. 3]

**G — Promise and price.** "For every promise, there's a price to pay... If the promise is clear and powerful, the price is easy to pay." [YT:tSn5sEWEv_8] Convert each top-four goal into its price: the daily discipline and the weekly review.

## Guardrails

> "Don't set them too low so that you don't have to grow... And then don't sell out. Don't go for something that's going to cost you your virtue or cost you your values." [YT:tSn5sEWEv_8]
> "Beware of what you become in pursuit of what you want." [YT:siw7Jqlc3ak]
> "We cannot move casually into a better future... A goal that is casually pursued is not a goal; at best it is a wish, and wishes are little more than self-delusion." [S70]
> "Goals are like a magnet. They pull, and the stronger they are... the stronger they pull." [S70]
> "If you go to work on your goals, your goals will go to work on you... Whatever good things we build end up building us." [S60/S70]
> The Law of Diminishing Intent: intentions weaken when action is delayed. [SU, "It Only Takes 6 Steps to Plan Your Success"]
> "Where are you going? Ten years from now you will surely arrive. The question is where?" [SU, "Where Do You See Yourself in 10 Years?" — page carries "Ten years from now you will surely arrive."]

## Templates for DR use

**Annual (Dec/Jan, 90 min):** full exercise A–G. Output: top 4 (one per timeframe), reasons paragraphs, become-statement per goal, one daily discipline each, review cadence.
**Quarterly (Rocks alignment, 30 min):** re-read annual list; check off with drama; kill stale items; re-answer "what is this quarter making of me?"; set the quarter's disciplines.
**Weekly (Friday, 10 min):** sowing/reaping review (see sowing_and_reaping.md) + list touch: anything to check off, add, delete; one discipline for next week.
