## Files in this folder

### `fanatical_prospecting_blount.md`
Jeb Blount's spine framework map — top-of-funnel prospecting discipline.
Drawn from *Fanatical Prospecting* (2015) with cross-references across his other books.

**Load this file for:**
- Prospecting blocks / calendar discipline
- The 30-Day Rule + Law of Replacement
- Cold call scripts (5-Step Phone Framework)
- Cold email scripts (4-Step Email Framework)
- The five emotional sales killers
- Anything about emotional avoidance of the phone

### `sales_eq_blount.md`
Jeb Blount's emotional intelligence layer. Drawn from *Sales EQ* (2017).

**Load this file for:**
- Mid-deal emotional friction (stalled champions, unexplained ghosting)
- Four Pillars of Sales-Specific EQ (empathy, self-awareness, self-control, social skills)
- Three-buyer model (rational / emotional / tribal)
- Pattern interrupt, emotional contagion, decision equation
- Acknowledge-Connect-Pivot, OPP filter, Influence Triangle, Ultra High Performer traits

### `objections_blount.md`
Jeb Blount's objection-handling map. Drawn from *Objections* (2018).

**Load this file for:**
- The Four Objection Types (RBOs, Prospecting, Buying Commitment, Buying)
- The Ledge, Disrupt-and-Pivot, Surface-Validate-Reframe-Advance
- Diagnostic posture at the close
- Rejection Response Protocol
- Avoidance behaviors / silent deal death
- The compounding math of objection-handling skill

### `virtual_selling_inked_blount.md`
Jeb Blount's modern closing layer. Combined map of *Virtual Selling* (2020) + *INKED* (2020).

**Load this file for:**
- Video presence and virtual engagement (camera, lighting, frame, audio)
- Asynchronous deal momentum (Loom, calendar discipline, document-as-conversation)
- Pre-suasion in virtual environments
- Negotiation discipline at the close (the ABC replacement)
- Concession strategy (never give without getting)
- Closing signals, trial closes, walking-away power

### `sales_acceleration_formula_roberge.md`
Mark Roberge's spine framework map. Drawn from *The Sales Acceleration Formula* (2015).

**Load this file for:**
- Sales hiring (A-Method, scorecard, the promote-vs-external decision)
- Predictable rep profile (coachability > experience)
- Sales training & ramp curves
- Metric-driven coaching (leading vs lagging)
- Comp plan design (HBR article core)
- Inbound vs outbound channel allocation
- Buyer journey process mapping

### `roberge_public_corpus.md`
Mark Roberge's post-2015 thinking. Stage 2 Capital, HBS, Lenny's, SaaStr, podcasts.

**Load this file for:**
- Buying journey > selling journey reframe
- Rep archetypes (Educator / Closer / Hunter / Farmer)
- PMF before sales hiring (founder-led-to-rep-led sequence)
- The 5 Quotas Test for Head of Sales candidates
- Stage 2 Capital GTM Diagnostic (5-layer)
- Comp as strategy signal (HBR extension)
- Coachability test design inside the interview loop
- URL catalogue: Stage 2 Capital, HBS, HBR, Lenny's Newsletter, SaaStr
