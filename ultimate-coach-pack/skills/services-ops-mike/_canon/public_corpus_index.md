## Recommended Purchases

All 8 Mike Michalowicz books, tier-ranked for a **services-firm operator** (specifically: a digital agency owner running a $1M–$10M team, which is the relevant lens for this skill pack). Prices vary; check the listings linked in the ASIN table above before recommending to a client.

### Tier S — Buy first, in order, full read (services-ops operators)

1. **Profit First** *(2017 revised edition — Kindle ASIN B01HCGYTH4)*
   The financial operating system. Non-optional for any services-firm operator. Cash allocation discipline is the lever that unlocks every other Mike concept. Buy Kindle for re-reads + Audible for car commutes.
2. **Clockwork, Revised & Expanded** *(2022 — Kindle ASIN B09HFW3NG8)*
   The operations operating system. Queen Bee Role, 4D Mix, the four-week vacation test. Together with Profit First, this is the two-book core for getting a services firm to run without the owner. Revised edition has Wickman / EOS-adjacent material the original lacked.
3. **Fix This Next** *(2020 — Kindle ASIN B07SZQMH7Z)*
   The diagnostic operating system. Tells the owner which fire to fight first using the Business Hierarchy of Needs. Highest-leverage when an operator is overwhelmed and doesn't know where to point the team. The free fixthisnext.com diagnostic is the gateway; the book is the depth.

### Tier A — Buy next, role-dependent

4. **The Pumpkin Plan** *(2012 — Kindle ASIN B0064VPS24)*
   The positioning / niching operating system. Especially relevant for any services firm sitting on a portfolio of mixed clients and refusing to fire the bottom-30%. Older book; Mike's writing voice is rougher than the post-2016 titles, but the framework is durable.
5. **All In** *(2023 — print ISBN 9780593544501, Kindle ASIN verify on Amazon)*
   The team-building operating system. Buy once the firm crosses ~15 employees and the hiring/retention problem starts dominating ops bandwidth. Newer than the others — Mike's most refined writing.

### Tier B — Read only if the specific problem applies

6. **Get Different** *(2021 — Kindle ASIN B08SBWTS1K)*
   Marketing differentiation via the DAD method. Useful if the services firm's lead-gen is undifferentiated.
