**1. Build the account on diversity: persona × message × hook × format, 4–5 net-new ads a week, big swings not tweaks.** Why per him: post-Andromeda Meta groups near-identical ads under a similarity score and treats them as one asset; "Andromeda doesn't reward more ads." [quote src: YT:CPWoW09WCCo]; "factory-printed" UGC accounts are the ones saying creative stopped working. Who: any account, every spend level. GEN/DTC. **2. Make founder ads — the format he trusts most (S tier).** Founder story as problem-solution: they had a problem, couldn't find a fix, made one. On camera, studio, or a voice memo over archive B-roll; cut customer testimonial clips in. Why: instant trust, nobody can copy your story ("that is your moat"), explains price, feels native; "constantly eating up spend", $1M+ spend profitably. Who: higher-consideration, expensive or unusual products; he says matching landing page matters "even more" for B2B/agencies driving appointments. DTC/GEN. **3. Treat statics as S-tier bottom-funnel workhorses and feed them new angles, not variations.** One quick message (why us, or an offer) to catch the sale video created. Brands now ask for "loads more new static angles"; a message that works goes into a completely new static, not a headline swap ("That just doesn't work anymore."). ## 2. Converts in general — principles in 2025/older evidence and repeated in 2026

1. **Research the customer before format, cast or copy.** Audience one sheet at every onboarding (2024); research source order Reddit → reviews → own ad comments → competitor ads → AI (2025); "You are just guessing and that is where paid media gets really expensive." [quote src: YT:RSa4wUuw_2k] (2026-07). The $200K Black Stuff founder montage came from weeks of research, not an edit trick. GEN. [YT:NNg9-TDiFDA] (2024-03) · [YT:gP-68LmgAQ4] (2025-11) · [YT:zpUsN6xQSGQ] (2026-05) → M §1.1, §2.1
2. **Customers write the copy: reviews, Reddit, comments.** Lift review quotes as headlines; look for specific outcomes, use cases, and comparisons customers already made; keep negatives and answer them. "The strongest ad copy you can ever write will always be guided by your customer." [quote src: YT:RExPkxyRB6o]. GEN. [YT:NNg9-TDiFDA] (2024-03) · [YT:tVXjXJSEjeA] (2025-07) · [YT:RpNcnKS2WYc] (2025-08) · [YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ] (2026-09) → M §1.2, T §1.12
3. **Format and hook must match funnel stage and awareness.** 2024 funnel map → 2025 "The format should always match the intent, not just the trend." [YT:gP-68LmgAQ4] → 2026 stage-specific hooks. TOF = UGC, street interview, founder; MOF = mini VSL, mashup, podcast; BOF = statics, offer ads, testimonial compilations. Spec details belong lower in the funnel. GEN/DTC. [YT:qpJqASEvYuc] (2024-02) · [YT:FWWp6NcVo3Q] (2025-11) · [YT:93gJ8_qvOQg] (2026-03) → F §1.5, §2.3, M §2.3, V §1.3
4. **Native, raw, organic-looking beats polished.** Found footage, TikTok-native captions, creator handle over brand handle, start mid-movement (2024–25); 2026 the trend is "Less branded, more organic looking" [quote src: YT:FWWp6NcVo3Q] and native statics do UGC's old job. "a lot of the time, the less polished the ad, the better." [YT:RExPkxyRB6o]. Resolution of his design-vs-ugly flips: winners break the account's default look. DTC/GEN. [YT:VCnx3v_gZQI] (2024-11) · [YT:opWKMc6iavo] (2025-10) · [YT:KlhBwk1kmzI] (2026-04) → V §1.7, F §1.3, §6
5. **Simple and clear wins.** "the best Statics are the simple ones" [YT:rztrOsDpci0] (2024-04); skimmable in about a second (2025-06); "the best ads are obvious ones" [YT:GCugREdurJk] (2026-01); "The best hooks are the simplest." [quote src: YT:Mqj05tYuDI0] (2026-09). Longest-running statics shown (339 days, 1,000+ days) are single-image, single-message. GEN. → F §1.7, V §1.13

[quote src: YT:oVpT0GnGY8Q] (2025-05); 2026 Black Stuff body ties every product mention to a pain (sore muscles after standing all day). Body copy written like a product brief kills hold rate. DTC/GEN. [YT:W1PADr1kjGk] (2025-01) · [YT:zpUsN6xQSGQ] (2026-05) · [YT:IX-2-uSJDk8] (2026-06) → M §1.4, V §1.6

## 4. Hooks and watch time

**How he builds a hook**
- Three layers every time: visual (what happens on screen) + audible (line + SFX) + text (caption or a different supporting line). Write the line first, then find a visual people don't often see. Most top ads tie visual and audio together. GEN. [YT:opWKMc6iavo] (2025-10) · [YT:IX-2-uSJDk8] (2026-06) · [YT:0K7WXqYPFCE] (2024-11) → V §1.2, §2.1
- Grab the right attention, not the most: "As well as grabbing attention, it's all about grabbing the right attention." [YT:opWKMc6iavo]. Call-outs lower CPM even if hook rate drops. → V §1.1
- First frame = a crazy visual or a face, never blurry or mid-transition; start mid-movement (posing "instantly feels like an ad"); viewers attend to ~2 s, eye-path set in ~5 s; remove visual chaos, one focus point. [YT:Lkx7nOGIslw] (2026-07) · [YT:GCugREdurJk] (2026-01) · [YT:zpUsN6xQSGQ] (2026-05) → V §1.7, §2.11, §3

**Hook types his evidence backs most**
- Product hook (product doing something attention-grabbing): "the easiest way to improve your hook rates" [YT:opWKMc6iavo]. Black Stuff satisfying soap cut + "This $9 Irish soap has outlasted my last four drugstore bars". → V §2.2, §4
- Expose: "I'm reposting this because I had to share with you what she's just said" + red bubble "SHE FINALLY EXPOSED IT!" → Humantra 45–50% hook, top ad. [YT:opWKMc6iavo] (2025-10)

**Hook rate: how to read it**
- No universal benchmark: profitable accounts run ~12% to ~70%; beat your own average (~15% avg → 30% "really good"; 25% avg → 30% a winner). "Sometimes a 20% hook can perform better than a 70% hook" [quote src: YT:IX-2-uSJDk8]. [YT:Lkx7nOGIslw] (2026-07) · [YT:opWKMc6iavo] (2025-10) · [YT:QoGFOGEqijw] (2026-05) → V §5, T §5
- Diagnosis: low hook + strong hold/CTR → new hook; great hook + low hold → hook irrelevant to customer; 50%+ hook + weak CTR (Black Stuff towel objection) → strong education, weak selling, fix CTA. → T §2A

**Watch-time / retention moves**
- 2026: hold rate and watch time matter more than hook rate; the hook "sets up" the video rather than being glued on; "we need to start making ads that people actually want to consume" [YT:Mqj05tYuDI0]. [YT:RExPkxyRB6o] (2026-10) → V §1.4
- Longer needs a reason to be longer: outfit and location changes, funny prop (sleep mask on "turning a blind eye"), other people mixed in, handwritten/physical text, large centred read-along captions, J and L cuts, mid-ad re-pitch, late re-sell ("If you're still here watching, wondering if it's worth trying"). [YT:mVb5HKHsC3w] (2025-07) · [YT:zpUsN6xQSGQ] (2026-05) → V §2.16
- Delay the product in long-form (Leaf villain ad at 40 s; VSL near the end) but put it first as reason #1 in short listicle/story UGC. → V §1.15, §6
- Body as a journey: open loops, objection resolved, curious → wanting → craving; "well-written body copy is not just a list. It's a journey." [quote src: YT:IX-2-uSJDk8]. → V §1.6
- Captions: TikTok-native single line inside safe zone; alternatives large centred, Snapchat-style bar, karaoke; his rule: find your style and test. → V §3, §6
