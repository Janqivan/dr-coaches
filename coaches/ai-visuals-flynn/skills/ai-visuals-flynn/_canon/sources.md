## Accuracy notes
- Transcripts are YouTube auto-captions without speaker labels. In Fast Hours, attribution to Rory was made only when clearly him.
- Every quoted fragment of 8 or more characters was machine-checked against the transcripts and found verbatim. Quotes are capped at 15 words.
- Tool and model behavior changes month to month. Give the date with every tool claim.
