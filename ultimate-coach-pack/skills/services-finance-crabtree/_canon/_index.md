## What's NOT in this folder (and why)

- **Verbatim book text.** None of Crabtree's prose is reproduced. Frameworks are described mechanically for synthetic-coach application. For Crabtree's own voice and the case studies that make the frameworks stick, buy the books.
- **Country-specific tax / payroll tables.** Crabtree's frameworks are US-default. AU / Serbia / Canada specifics belong with a country accountant, not in this canon. The LER math is universal; the tax math is not.
- **Public corpus excerpts.** The Simple Numbers podcast and YouTube channel, EO chapter talks, and simplenumbers.me articles are the live voice samples — they're not transcribed here. Listen to them directly when calibrating voice.
