## 1. Working now (2026) — ranked plays

**1. Make every variation look different in the first 3–5 seconds, not just read different.** NOW · GEN
- What to make: per concept, 2 written hooks × 2 opening visuals (her smallest 2026 package: 2 inspirations/week × 2 × 2). Change the opening shot, setting or device, not only the text overlay.
- Why per her: Meta only counts a variation as different if the first 3–5 seconds look different; text-only changes are not seen as new. Her Andromeda adjustment is bigger visual hook swings, more persona testing, more diverse value drivers.
- Corroborated (guests, 2026): Andromeda clusters near-identical ads into one retrieval ticket; iterations need a different person, room and framing (Sophia Beauvoir; Viti; Eric Zorsky citing Meta GEM docs).
- Sources: [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01); guests [YT:CVWHkhUA5Ms] (2026-06), [YT:M-zOOiG5GLw] (2026-07). → test §1.2, §2.1D, §6; craft §1b

**2. Novel first frame → normal UGC body → offer + website CTA.** NOW/EVERGREEN · GEN
- What to make: one unexpected opening (prop, device, odd angle, transition) dropped onto a standard testimonial/B-roll/demo body, ending on offer + website. Only the opening swaps between variants.
- Why per her: "the hook is definitely the most important part of the ad" [quote src: YT:wrbvSulohNI]; most people don't reach two seconds — "it's really all about earning seconds of viewership" [quote src: YT:hS47--F7290]. Swapping only the first ~3s of the luggage ad (heads/tails vs rock-paper-scissors) gave drastically different results across clients. Her 2026 performance-boosters check still leads with an engaging hook in imagery and messaging, storyline hook → story → CTA.
- Sources: [YT:KALGyNhfrVU] (2025-07), [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06), [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01). → craft §1a.1, §2.1, §2.19

**3. No-product businesses: real humans on camera + the website/app/form as the "product shot".** NOW · LEADGEN/APP
- What to make: creator talking to camera, green-screened over the site or the lead form; walk-and-talk; self skit; whiteboard; Notes-app checklist; Post-its on screen. End on site/app screenshot + CTA.
- Why per her: the biggest app/service mistake is no humans — only screen recordings, text overlays, photos, VO slideshows. Services must work harder on the first seconds. Apps/B2B use the same approach as DTC.
- Sources: [YT:3TzdwzAG1X8] (2025-03), [YT:j9d6j0B3W6U] (2025-02), [YT:jAc63K9lRIQ] (2025-02), [YT:KALGyNhfrVU] (2025-07), [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01). → craft §1a.6, §2.13

**4. Partnership/whitelisting access on every ad.** NOW · GEN
- What to make: run ads from creator handles as well as the brand page; get partnership access as part of every creator deal.
- Why per her: 2025 — she includes it with every ad because it has been a huge performance driver for brands; 2026 — she has seen creator whitelisting work incredibly well, clients run from both pages. Back to 2021 (TikTok codes beat brand page; BlendJet seeding → whitelisting "very successful").
- Guest (LEADGEN, 2026-07, Josh Lavin): at least 4 of 10 impressions to partnership ads; partnership ads with experts build trust fastest (Monks).
- Sources: [YT:kFtv5VDkRSg] (2025-10), [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01), [YT:w1sz_OdVQiA] (2021-03); guests [YT:M-zOOiG5GLw]. → craft §1a.8, §2.21; msg §2.4

**5. Real people, real voices, human-written scripts in the finished ad.** NOW · GEN
- What to make: real creators/actors; no AI avatars, AI voices or AI scripts in the final ad. AI only behind the scenes (research, transcripts, shot lists, localization).
- Why per her: clients' #1 ask is ads that don't look AI ("counter AI movement" [quote src: YT:EHiXurAZPj0]); ~90% of her ads use real creators with real voices; AI scripts all sound the same; she hasn't seen a US account where AI-avatar-only videos top. "One good ad is worth a thousand bad AI ads" — [YT:kFtv5VDkRSg]. Her stated switch condition: "if the motion data showed me that AI content worked better" — [YT:kFtv5VDkRSg].
- Counter-evidence (guests): AI UGC/statics as top spenders (Mirella Crespi, John/Airpost, Will Sartorius); AI first-two-second hooks work but full AI ads mostly don't (Oren John). See §5.
- Sources: [YT:EHiXurAZPj0] (2025-03), [YT:kFtv5VDkRSg] (2025-10), [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01). → test §1.6–8, §2.1O; craft §1a.9; msg §1.11

**6. Pair a broad opener against a persona/benefit call-out opener on every hook.** NOW · DTC (transferable)
- What to make: for each hook, version A universal (e.g. saving money, talking to someone behind camera), version B calling out a persona or functional benefit.
- Why per her: persona specificity is part of her 2026 Andromeda play; she maps value drivers as pain, identity, outcome, gifting, portability, connection (Tobios Kit) and persona lists (Wandering Bear: coffee snobs, deal seekers, novelty seekers).
- Sources: [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01). → msg §1.13, §3, §4

**7. Steal evergreen edit/transition trends from organic TikTok, not topical trends.** NOW · GEN
- What to make: replicate transitions and editing effects that non-TikTok users understand (products flying into a cart, jump out of the phone, fake poll lifted from a pharma ad). Avoid pop-culture moments.
- Why per her: over the last ~6 months she pulled more from organic TikTok than ad libraries; trends appear there before ads; organic creators are "masters of using different transitions" — [YT:kFtv5VDkRSg]. Ads need evergreen, broad appeal; trend-heavy ads go stale.
- Sources: [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01), [YT:kFtv5VDkRSg] (2025-10), [YT:KALGyNhfrVU] (2025-07). → msg §1.4, §1.12; craft §2.17

**8. Iterate a winner with the 2026 four levers — one at a time.** NOW · GEN
- What to make: (1) same creator, same value drivers, new hook (visual or spoken); (2) different creator, exact same script/shot list; (3) reshuffle clip sequence; (4) cut 30s → 20s/15s. A/B one variable.
- Why per her: isolates creator vs message; from Motion reports, keep a high thumb-stop opening and change the body, or pair a high-hold body with the best hook.
- Sources: [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01), [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA] (2024-03), [YT:FjUkvTGkxWo] (2023-07). → test §2.1A–B

- Why per her: "if it's working for 20 brands, it's probably going to work for the 21st" — [YT:kFtv5VDkRSg]. The Post-it format won across ~10 accounts and became default for new clients.
- Sources: [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01), [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06). → test §2.1H; msg §1.3, §2.12

## 2. Converts in general — principles in 2025/older evidence and repeated in 2026

1. **Earn the first seconds or nothing else matters.** Hook = first two seconds (2024), first three seconds (2021 "second zero", 2025), and her 2026 boosters list still opens with the hook. → craft §1a.1, §2.2, §2.19 · [YT:w1sz_OdVQiA] (2021-03), [YT:hS47--F7290] (2024-06), [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026-01)
2. **Creative is the scaling lever; targeting and buying tricks are not.** 2020 "It's not about hacking your way to success" — [YT:6OmF0BzBOHQ]; 2023 media buying "nothing proprietary"; 2025 the creative does the targeting; 2026 Andromeda answer is creative swings and persona testing. → test §1.1; msg §1.15
3. **Look like organic content, keep the direct-response spine.** "making TikTok videos, not ads, is the key" [quote src: YT:w1sz_OdVQiA] (2021) — but problem → solution → CTA stays; 2026 storyline rule: hook → story → CTA, deviate only deliberately. → craft §1a.3, §2.19
4. **Sound off is the default.** 80%+ of FB/IG video viewed sound-off (2020); no on-screen text is the top hook mistake (2024–2025); 2026 QC: followable with and without sound, watch sound-off before sending. → craft §1a.5, §2.18, §5
5. **Talent and on-camera energy decide performance; keep a trained long-term roster.** Under-10K-follower scouting since 2020, "followers definitely does not equal higher" — [YT:l1_xSlj4Z50] (2021); 40 long-term creators matched to each client's market (2026). → msg §1.5; craft §1a.7
6. **Research and adapt beats inventing.** ~80% of her ads start from an existing ad or viral organic video, ~20% net-new weird (2024–2025); 2026 sourcing system still runs on Motion reports, ad libraries, organic TikTok and a master hook list since 2020. → msg §1.3; craft §2.17
7. **The brief is the work.** Storyboard briefs (2024) → checkbox shot list + 2 hooks + reference ad (2026). A linked reference ad gets what she wants "nine out of 10 times" [quote src: YT:DW6iH4tVFmA]. → msg §1.1, §2.6
8. **You can't call winners by taste — test.** "no one knows what's going to work until you test it" — [YT:ZQAjWwxk2C0] (2024): the "so boring" 5-second product shot beat the team's picks; 2026: the BlendJet 2020 ad was never matched by countless iterations — "a little bit of algorithm magic" — [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w]. → test §1.13, §4
9. **Iterate by changing one variable.** Change hook / refilm same script with new model (2023); swap only first ~3s (2024–2025); 2026 four levers, one A/B variable — now with visibly different openings. → test §2.1A, §6
10. **Volume and speed beat polish.** "I'm all about volume and speed" — [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA] (2024); polished on-brand ads take months then sit at $50/day; 2026 weekly cadence: shoot Mon–Tue, edit Wed–Thu, 4–12 ads per client Friday. → msg §1.6, §2.4
11. **Mostly net-new, some iteration.** ~80% net-new / 20% iterations, two new hooks on a winner per month (2024); "meta definitely favors net new content" — [YT:3TzdwzAG1X8] (2025); 2026 bigger swings under Andromeda. → test §1.4, §2.1C
12. **Humans over AI in what the viewer sees.** AI ads "really cheesy… I just cringe" — [YT:KJ3Q5bha6WA] (2024); counter-AI movement (2025); no AI scripts, AI scripts "they sound fake" — [YT:MT2MzrTGC5w] (2026). → test §1.6–7, §6
13. **Creator whitelisting compounds.** 2021 creator codes beat brand page → 2025 partnership access with every ad → 2026 works incredibly well. → craft §1a.8
14. **Evergreen over topical.** 2021 favor evergreen TikTok-style content over dance/song trend ads; 2025 only evergreen trends; 2026 evergreen edit/transition trends, not fast in-and-out ones. Top performers often run months or a year+. → msg §1.12, §6; test §1.10
15. **Read results weekly across accounts.** ~3 days each week reviewing all clients' prior-week tests in Motion (2024); 2026 ~50 clients reviewed weekly, winning concept library. → test §1.12, §2.1H
