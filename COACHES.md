# DR Coaches — Playbook

31 synthetic operator-coaches across 4 packs. Each is a slash command and also fires on plain
language ("what would Voss do", "audit my offer"). This doc tells your team **which coach to reach
for, when** — and which neighbor to use instead when two overlap. Read the Router first; the cards
are reference.

- **Install:** see `INSTALL.md` (Cowork → Customize → Plugins → add the GitHub repo).
- **Don't memorize 31 commands.** Find your role below, learn your 3–4, ignore the rest until you need them.

## Router 3 — the overlap calls (this is the part that saves arguments)

- **Enns vs Campbell** — Enns prices a *service/agency deal or proposal* (value conversation, no hourly). Campbell prices a *SaaS/subscription product* (willingness-to-pay, tiers, churn).
- **Baker vs Dunford** — Baker positions *the agency/firm itself* (verticalization, economics). Dunford positions *a product/service* against its competitive alternatives.
- **Wiebe vs Kennedy** — Wiebe = conversion copy from voice-of-customer (landing/sales pages, modern web). Kennedy/Georgi = direct-response sales letters & VSLs (long-form, offer-and-close).
- **Dixon vs Voss vs Outbound** — Dixon moves a *stalled* complex deal (indecision). Voss handles the *negotiation/close* (price, pushback). Outbound *builds the pipeline* in the first place.
- **Gardner vs Laja vs Wolf** — Gardner = page layout & attention ratio. Laja = conversion research & what to test. Wolf = the *emotional* reason it converts.
- **Gerhardt vs Robinson vs Welsh vs Priestley** — Gerhardt = B2B founder brand/voice. Robinson = build-in-public, founder-led growth engine. Welsh = one-person/solo operating system. Priestley = authority + becoming "oversubscribed."
- **Mike vs Crabtree** — Michalowicz = profit system + owner-independent ops. Crabtree = the actual financial diagnosis (labour efficiency, owner salary).
- **Martin vs Baker** — Martin = strategy cascade (where-to-play / how-to-win) for any business. Baker = the agency-specific positioning + economics.

# The 31 cards

Format: **purpose · use when · use a neighbor instead when · example prompt.**

## Pack 1 — agency-builder-pack (8): agency mechanics + B2B GTM

### `/agency-baker` — David C. Baker
- **Purpose:** Agency positioning, verticalization, and the economics that prove it (AGI/FTE, effective hourly rate, client concentration).
- **Use when:** the agency feels generalist, undifferentiated, or underpriced; deciding a vertical/horizontal focus.
- **Instead:** product positioning → `/positioning-dunford`; pricing a specific proposal → `/pricing-enns`.
- **Try:** "Channel Baker: we're a generalist performance agency at ~20 people. Diagnose our positioning and the economics that should change."

### `/pricing-enns` — Blair Enns
- **Purpose:** Value-based pricing and the sales conversation that supports it (Win Without Pitching, four conversations, three-option proposals).
- **Use when:** writing a proposal, defending price, killing hourly billing, facing a discount ask.
- **Instead:** SaaS tier pricing → `/saas-pricing-campbell`; live price pushback on a call → `/negotiation-voss`.
- **Try:** "Channel Enns: structure a 3-option proposal for a $12k/mo retainer and how I open the value conversation."

### `/challenger-sales-dixon` — Matt Dixon
- **Purpose:** Move complex B2B deals through *indecision* (Challenger, JOLT effect, Mobilizer/Talker/Blocker).
- **Use when:** a deal is stalled, ghosting, or "we'll think about it" despite fit.
- **Instead:** building pipeline → `/outbound-sales`; price/close mechanics → `/negotiation-voss`.
- **Try:** "Channel Dixon: this $30k deal has stalled after a great demo. Diagnose indecision vs competition and give me the next move."

### `/saas-pricing-campbell` — Patrick Campbell
- **Purpose:** SaaS/subscription monetization (willingness-to-pay, Van Westendorp, tiers, packaging, churn/expansion).
- **Use when:** a client sells software/subscriptions and the question is what to charge or how to tier.
- **Instead:** services/agency pricing → `/pricing-enns`.
- **Try:** "Channel Campbell: tear down this SaaS pricing page and propose a 3-tier structure based on willingness-to-pay."

### `/demand-walker` — Chris Walker
- **Purpose:** Demand creation vs capture; fix the *source* of pipeline (dark social, self-reported attribution, brand → demand → expand).
- **Use when:** leads look fine but pipeline/revenue doesn't follow; over-reliance on MQLs/gated content.
- **Instead:** the ad mechanics themselves → `/paid-media-marshall` / `/linkedin-wilcox`.
- **Try:** "Channel Walker: our MQLs are up but sales says they're junk. Where's our demand actually being created vs captured?"

### `/inbound-sheridan` — Marcus Sheridan
- **Purpose:** Educational content engine that wins trust (They Ask You Answer, the Big 5, the Selling 7, assignment selling).
- **Use when:** building a content/SEO/video engine, or sales needs content that closes.
- **Instead:** founder-brand voice → `/brand-gerhardt`; demand strategy → `/demand-walker`.
- **Try:** "Channel Sheridan: give me the Big 5 articles for an immigration-law client and the first one outlined."

### `/brand-gerhardt` — Dave Gerhardt
- **Purpose:** B2B founder brand and modern, personality-led marketing ("B2B should act like B2C").
- **Use when:** a founder/exec wants to build presence; B2B marketing sounds corporate and dead.
- **Instead:** authority/productized status → `/authority-priestley`; build-in-public mechanics → `/founder-robinson`.
- **Try:** "Channel Gerhardt: turn our founder's raw take on agency pricing into a week of LinkedIn posts in his voice."

### `/linkedin-wilcox` — AJ Wilcox
- **Purpose:** LinkedIn Ads specifically (AMO: audience/message/offer, format matrix, CPC discipline, lead-gen forms vs LPs).
- **Use when:** planning, auditing, or fixing LinkedIn ad campaigns.
- **Instead:** broad budget/80-20 allocation → `/paid-media-marshall`.
- **Try:** "Channel Wilcox: audit this LinkedIn campaign with the AMO framework and tell me what to cut."

## Pack 2 — craft-builder-pack (9): conversion craft + verticals + solo

## How to actually roll this out (don't skip)

1. **Don't announce 31 coaches.** Announce the Router. Tell each person their 3–4.
2. **One test install first** (you), confirm the slash command shows up, then share the repo URL with the team.
3. **Pin this doc** where the team works (Drive + the repo). When a pack changes, regenerate from the repo's `COACHES.md` — don't hand-edit the Drive copy.
4. **Trigger habit:** these fire on natural language too. "What would Voss do with this objection?" works as well as `/negotiation-voss`.
