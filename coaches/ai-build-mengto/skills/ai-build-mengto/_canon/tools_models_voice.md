### Methods (named or ordered)
#### Aura landing flow (building a landing page in his own tool)
- Steps: 1. design system (one design.md; import from URL, template or the library of 621 and growing) 2. sections (hero, footer, features, testimonials, story, pricing, contact form) 3. animation (rich interactions, smooth scroll) 4. polish (Awwwards, Dribbble, Apple or Framer-style reference, SVG logo, responsive, rich images, icon set such as Solar Linear) 5. dictate the product description 6. add to prompt bundles design system, HTML, sections, interactions, description 7. copy the whole prompt and run it on several design systems at once to compare.
- Sources: [YT:T1WTGQesbrE] (2026-06)
#### Newform remix modes (reusing a community design; his tool, disclosed)
- Steps: 1. "remix with prompt" molds a design (HTML plus design.md) to your product 2. iterate keeps as much as possible, remix explores different designs, quick remix steers toward another design.md 3. favourite what you like, hide the rest; a design is not yours until you remix it 4. tokens are spent once, so download the design.md and reuse it in Stitch or Claude Design.
- Sources: [YT:LfgMBy0auL8] (2026-05)
#### AI-era product strategy (his on-screen list)
- Steps: 1. no more subscription 2. no underselling, go niche not mass 3. "no slop", specialize 4. lifetime option on every product 5. open source, give for free 6. convince first, then sell 7. chase feedback and let AI process it.
- Sources: [YT:UqKXQR5zcVo] (2026-09)
#### Comment-to-DM lead funnel
- Steps: 1. post says comment a keyword 2. ManyChat auto-DMs the commenter (a lead) with the workflow 3. the bundle, built with Codex: article, research, key takeaways, template file, his free design.md and a PDF, uploaded to Google Drive by the agent.
