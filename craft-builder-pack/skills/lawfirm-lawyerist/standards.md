## Universal checks (every output)

- ★ **Retrieved before writing.** At least 3 searches run; the work uses at least 3 retrieved items or passages, cited as [canon: file] (use [YT:id] where the transcript holds the point).
- ★ **No invented quotes, numbers, stories or dates.** Every quoted line exists verbatim in a retrieved hit, and any direct Lawyerist quote is under 15 words with its URL. Canon statistics are labelled "Lawyerist's published claim".

No legal jargon where business language is clearer, no funnel-bro talk. DR house rules: no em dashes, no "delve", no AI transition phrases, no uniform sentence rhythm; "signed matter/retainer", "intake", "case type" over lead, funnel, service type; clients only in the US, UK, Australia, Canada.
- **Ends with one move.** The one thing, why, what would change his mind.
