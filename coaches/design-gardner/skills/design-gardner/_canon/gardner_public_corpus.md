# Oli Gardner — Public Corpus URL Catalogue

This is the source material the synthetic coach is built on. Use these
URLs when you want citations, when you want to send a client to the
original work, or when you want to read Oli's actual words instead of
the coach's paraphrase.

Gardner's public quote bank is short on purpose — most of his work is
the frameworks, not aphorisms. The phrases that travel as quotes (e.g.
"every campaign needs a dedicated landing page", "attention ratio 1:1",
"message match", "the headline/subhead flip") are framework names. The
canon files in this skill paraphrase the framework content; the URLs
below are where to verify and go deeper.

## RECOMMENDED PURCHASES

Most of Oli's body of work is free. The paid surface area is small but
real.

- **Unbounce paid plans** — https://unbounce.com/plans-and-pricing/
  The product itself. If you actually want to build conversion-centered
  pages with the tooling Oli's frameworks were originally validated
  against, the Build, Experiment, and Optimize tiers are where the
  testing features live. For DR, Unbounce competes with Instapage and
  with Webflow + a CRO stack. Pick on integration footprint and team
  workflow, not on price.

- **CTAConf tickets** — historically priced at ~CAD $1,500 + travel
  when the conference ran. The conference has not run since 2019.
  Treat the back-catalogue of recordings as the asset; do not budget
  for a future ticket unless Unbounce announces a return.

- **Stukent course access** —
  https://www.stukent.com/expert-sessions/the-7-principles-of-conversion-centered-design
  Stukent is a textbook/education platform. Their Expert Session with
  Oli is paywalled inside the higher-ed bundle. Only worth it if you
  already have a Stukent subscription.

- **Books in Oli's lineage (not by him)** — for adjacent reading:
  - "Don't Make Me Think" by Steve Krug — usability foundation Oli
    cites repeatedly.
  - "Influence" by Robert Cialdini — the persuasion layer underneath
    Credibility and Closing.
  - "Made to Stick" by Chip & Dan Heath — the clarity argument
    extended.
  - "Predictably Irrational" by Dan Ariely — friction and
    decision-architecture underpinnings.
  These are not Oli's books, but they are the bibliography behind
  CCD. Read them if you want to understand the foundations.

## CITATION PROTOCOL

When citing Oli publicly:
- Quote sparingly. His public corpus is mostly framework, not
  aphorism. Paraphrase with attribution.
- If you must use a verbatim phrase, keep it under 15 words and link
  the source.
- The safest direct quotes are framework names: "Attention Ratio",
  "Message Match", "Conversion Coupling", "The 7 Principles of
  Conversion-Centered Design", "Attention-Driven Design". These are
  publicly attributable to Oli without controversy.
- Anything else: paraphrase. Use the URL above as the source.
