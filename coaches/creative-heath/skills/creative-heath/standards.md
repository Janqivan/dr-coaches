This file is the bar. Every piece of work the coach produces or judges is graded against the checks for its task type. A check is PASS only with evidence pointed at the work itself (a line, a number, a frame, a column in Ads Manager). "Feels right" is not evidence.

Scoring: count PASS / total for the task type. Deliver only at ≥ 85% with zero FAIL on checks marked ★. Otherwise fix and regrade (max 2 fix loops), then deliver with the remaining FAILs listed honestly.

## T1. Offer and funnel check (the entry offer, the ask, what the ad has to carry)

His strongest mode for lead gen. Run it before any creative work on a new client or a campaign that isn't producing leads.

- ★ **Offer judged before creative.** The output names the entry offer exactly as written and gives a verdict "offer problem or creative problem" before any creative advice. No leads at any price = offer first; "nearly always an offer problem". Evidence: the verdict line precedes the first creative recommendation. [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M] [YT:WeQrKeAwZDg]
- ★ **The free thing is named.** "Free consultation" / "free session" / "free call" alone = FAIL ("free consultation for what?"). Must read like "free interior design consultation", "free personal training session". Evidence: quote the offer line. [YT:1EQrp8MJRek] [YT:KgO_J6pwv9I]
- ★ **Ask size stated and format matched to it.** Ask graded small / medium / big on time, money and trust (in-person meeting at premises = big, free Zoom call = smaller), and the format recommendation follows: big ask to cold → longer video with proof; small ask → image/carousel can carry it. "the complexity of the ad format should match the size of the ask". [YT:GPp8Y-lKzJw] [YT:N3X8CuD_VQk]
- **Low-risk entry step exists.** Service offers a free quote/consultation/strategy session or deep-discounted first session (his physio client: ~80–85% off a discovery session). If the client says it can't afford the time, the answer given is "raise your price", not "drop the free step". [YT:WeQrKeAwZDg] [YT:z3LiJ0PbY-0]
- **Free step demonstrates the service.** In a me-too market the offer itself is the differentiator; the check passes only if the entry step shows what working with them is like (designer visits the property, trainer runs a session). [YT:WeQrKeAwZDg]
- **Offer = the whole package, not just the price.** Guarantees (results or money back, keep working free until the result), genuine scarcity, guaranteed timings are listed as levers to test at the offer level. [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M] [YT:E0lbvr71wR8]
- **Offer in the headline, plainly, readable in "a micro second".** No curiosity headline; curiosity belongs in email subjects and YouTube titles. [YT:KgO_J6pwv9I]
- **Optimised result named.** States what the campaign optimises for (lead, schedule/booked call) and flags if Meta's 7-day window will undercount a long-consideration service (his Hyros examples, sponsor segments: £15K vs £3K seen; £58K of £96K unreported). [YT:D6Sxk8Mbkq4] [YT:onFwSud9C2Y]
- **Phone number asked, lead called fast.** Lead form asks for name, email and phone; email-only follow-up gets "a very low response rate". The plan names who calls and how soon (his test: three or four times as many contacts within 5 minutes; keep calling twice a day for a week). Missing phone or no follow-up owner = FAIL. [YT:599Nfhn6ovQ] [YT:pkijK2f1cO4] [YT:MS_C64RE9tM] [YT:VGtNbtqKdCk]
- **Form type matched to lead quality.** Instant form = more leads, lower cost per lead; website = fewer, higher quality; "the more hoops" the higher the quality (Messenger easiest, lowest). If volume is fine but nobody picks up: higher-intent form with review screen, or website. If quality is the complaint, cost per lead is not the verdict. [YT:04JIo_lmux0] [YT:qNq9Rt0W-z0] [YT:OmZAcatTkuY] [YT:Cz8Bi6_rkwM]
- **Qualified lead is the optimised event.** Qualifying question (budget, location) in the form; only qualifiers hit the lead conversion event or end page, so Meta "learns" qualified leads, not all leads. Manual filtering after the fact = Meta can't see it. Sample volume needed before it works. [YT:oTWfDI_91Fs] [YT:6TRy9eUAPVg] [YT:4z65TiLYut4]
- **Transfer flagged.** His offer evidence is interior design, gyms, physio and his own agency; the output says so when applied to immigration, franchise or vocational. His evidence base is interior design and personal training (also gyms, physio, his own agency). [YT:yrWUeXeOvsQ] [YT:599Nfhn6ovQ] [canon: playbook.md §7f]

## T2. Concept batch and angle test (what to make next)

- ★ **One angle per ad.** Each concept carries exactly one reason-to-buy. Two angles in one ad = FAIL ("don't mix multiple angles in one ad"). The angle he expected to win often isn't the one that does. [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M] [YT:inJWYXen3Xc]
- ★ **Angles come from a written reasons-why list.** The full list of reasons people buy is shown first, including status/social reasons (his agency list: ROAS, scale, time, anxiety, status). Angles not traceable to the list = FAIL. [YT:KgO_J6pwv9I] [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M] [YT:E0lbvr71wR8] [YT:inJWYXen3Xc]
- ★ **Visibly different, not variations.** Concepts differ by angle or creative style (UGC, founder-led, demonstration, testimonial, influencer, animation), not by background colour or headline. Post-Andromeda near-duplicates get merged and "won't get a fair crack". [YT:onFwSud9C2Y] [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M]
- **At least one proof-led concept.** Proof in virtually every ad is his default (25% to 80–90% of runtime). [YT:z3LiJ0PbY-0]
- **Formats mixed and sized to the ask.** Statics, carousels and video in the batch; non-video wins ~40% of the time even in his video-heavy agency. [YT:GPp8Y-lKzJw] [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M]
- **Built for one ad set.** Angles launch side by side in one ad set, named by angle ("Ad 1.1 - Results Angle"…), so the winner reads cleanly. [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M]
- **Launch size is realistic.** About 4 very different creatives plus a few variations, not 20+. If the plan argues for volume, it cites the 2026-07 Arcads-sponsored position and names the conflict. [YT:onFwSud9C2Y] [YT:TpZrPY6jTE8]
- **Winner becomes the line.** The plan says what happens when an angle wins: all marketing goes that way (his ROAS angle → ROAS testimonials, "result of the day" posts). [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M]
- **Ad Library modelled, not copied.** Any borrowed concept comes from an ad active 6+ months, copies format/length/hook angle/structure, never the words or footage, and checks the 2025 pitfalls (seen hook, brand voice, shared audience notices). [YT:4mZ3F77dNdI] [YT:M2VReAjHiN8]
- **Every concept tagged** era and LEADGEN / GEN / DTC. [canon: SKILL.md Rule 3] (not found in transcripts)

## T3. Video brief or script (founder, lawyer, creator, testimonial compilation)

- ★ **Hook specified as opening scene + opening line.** Every hook states both the visual and the words for the first ~3 seconds, "by far the most important part"; 90–95%+ drop before it. A hook given as a line only = FAIL. [YT:IRyR9PzSnM8] [YT:GPp8Y-lKzJw]
- ★ **About 10 hooks per body, 1–2 bodies.** Brief asks for 2 × 30–60s bodies and ~10 visual hooks (≥ 4–5 minimum). [YT:IRyR9PzSnM8] [YT:crId74rHjuA] [YT:onFwSud9C2Y]
- ★ **Hooks pass the "I've seen this before filter".** Each hook changes location, clothing, background, camera (selfie vs camera), lighting or colour vs B&W. A new line on the same frame does not count as a hook in 2026 (he counted "one camera angle is one hook" in 2025-10; Andromeda changed it in 2026-02). [YT:IRyR9PzSnM8] [YT:onFwSud9C2Y] [YT:M2VReAjHiN8] [YT:TOII24BtKAo] [YT:oMhsPXBTqtg]
- **Guardrails, not a word-for-word script.** Creator briefs say "nothing too scripted" and keep the creator's own voice; talking points and proof beats, not a teleprompter. [YT:IRyR9PzSnM8]
- **Length sized to the ask.** Big-trust ask → longer video that does the selling (proof, testimonials, upside); people lost early were unlikely to buy a big ask. [YT:GPp8Y-lKzJw]
- **Service structure present.** Hook to camera → problem agitation → revelation with proof → specific pain moment → the offer. [YT:M2VReAjHiN8]
- **Proof beats on screen, real.** Results filmed on a real screen in sceptical categories (not screenshots), testimonials, before/afters, reactions. [YT:z3LiJ0PbY-0] [YT:M2VReAjHiN8]
- **Proof-only compilation used only where the service is understood.** Physio reaction compilation (ROAS "more than doubled", ~5s clips, deliberately diverse clients); if the service needs explaining, offer info is added and copy/headline carry it. [YT:z3LiJ0PbY-0]
- **Ends on the named ask.** Last beat states the entry offer and the action (book a call, claim the free consultation). [YT:M2VReAjHiN8] [YT:KgO_J6pwv9I]
- **One video spun into statics.** Brief lists the stills/headlines to cut from the shoot (his whiteboard video → four navy statics). [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M]
- **Shoot plan: record the body once, the hooks many times.** Same ~45s body, opening 3 seconds recorded in ~10 locations/outfits/camera setups; ad fatigue is "hook fatigue", so a fresh opening resets a 6-month ad. Creators asked for 2–4 per test round, 2 ads plus hook variations each. [YT:oMhsPXBTqtg] [YT:kuSq-pmNfnM] [YT:gbcQq19jbjs]
- **Copy marked draft.** All spoken and on-screen lines labelled draft input for Ivan's approval. [canon: SKILL.md Ivan context] (not found in transcripts)

## T4. Static ads and ad copy (image, carousel, primary text, headline, CTA)

Most of this is ALLTIME (2019–2023). Say so when a check is load-bearing.

- ★ **Headline states the offer plainly.** Descriptive of the entry offer ("Free interior design consultation") plus one or two benefits if they fit; clever or curiosity headline = FAIL. [YT:KgO_J6pwv9I]
- ★ **Offer in the first ~3 lines of primary text,** above "See more". Offer buried below = FAIL. [YT:1EQrp8MJRek]
- ★ **Built headline-first.** Headline (states offer) → primary text (supports it) → creative last, even though people see them in reverse. Evidence: the creative brief references the headline it supports. [YT:KgO_J6pwv9I]
- **Call-out opener.** Primary text opens on a question naming the situation ("Moving house? Redecorating?") or a location ("New Yorkers…") so the right scroller thinks "that's me". [YT:WeQrKeAwZDg] [YT:KgO_J6pwv9I]
- **Length matches the ask.** Low-risk intro offer = little copy ("sign up, no obligation"); bigger ask = more reasons why, never wordy. [YT:WeQrKeAwZDg] [YT:KgO_J6pwv9I]
- **No testimonials in primary text, no fake urgency.** Testimonials go in the creative (video/image), not the copy; scarcity only if genuine. [YT:1EQrp8MJRek] [YT:WeQrKeAwZDg]
- **CTA at the end of primary text; button names the ask.** Sign Up / Learn More for a consultation, not Shop Now; description echoes the CTA; for lead ads "sign up" where it fits. [YT:KgO_J6pwv9I] [YT:T8Lyj9O6_ss]
- **Portfolio image for visual services.** Best work in the image ("it's why people hire you"); proof statics = Trustpilot review, result screenshot, "want results like this" carousel. [YT:WeQrKeAwZDg] [YT:z3LiJ0PbY-0]
- **Carousel/flexible cards each stand alone.** No card that carries only part of the message. [YT:M36tVQD26TY]
- **Tone matches the price point.** Serious for high-end services; playful emoji copy only for cheap fun products. [YT:WeQrKeAwZDg]

## T5. Teardown and hook-rate diagnosis (own ad, client ad or competitor ad)

- ★ **Hook rate read against cost per result, never alone.** Hook rate = 3-second plays ÷ impressions, placed next to results. Good hook + poor cost = body fails; low hook + great cost = strong body. A verdict from hook rate or watch time alone = FAIL ("who cares about average watch time" when cost per result is better). [YT:bAo6SICFyIE] [YT:wb9ELEGXXZ8] [YT:z3LiJ0PbY-0]
- ★ **Judged against the account's own average.** His numbers (best just under 27% at ~£30/schedule over 64 schedules; worst 7–9%; Frankenstein body 15.55% at £11.02/booked call over 33) are cited as one account's, not as a benchmark for Ivan's verticals. [YT:bAo6SICFyIE]
- ★ **Enough conversions to read.** States the conversion count behind each verdict; "It's not time, it's conversion volume". 2 conversions in 2 hours is noise. [YT:D6Sxk8Mbkq4] [YT:E0lbvr71wR8]
- **Frankenstein option named.** Which hook onto which body, only where the pairing makes sense ("not every hook fits every body"). [YT:bAo6SICFyIE]
- **Lead quality is not a creative verdict.** Cheap leads that don't pick up point to form type, missing phone number or slow follow-up before the ad is judged; an ad with fewer, dearer leads can be the better ad if more of them convert. [YT:pkijK2f1cO4] [YT:rZkhSHveQek] [YT:Cz8Bi6_rkwM]
- **Offer clarity checked.** Can the deal be understood "in a micro second" from the headline and first lines? Main failure reasons he names: offer unclear or not appealing. [YT:KgO_J6pwv9I]
- **One angle? Proof share? Format vs ask?** Each answered with evidence from the ad (seconds of proof / total runtime; ask size → format). [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M] [YT:z3LiJ0PbY-0] [YT:GPp8Y-lKzJw]
- **Competitor ads: run time first.** Active 6+ months = assume it works (1–2 years "golden"); weeks-old ads are no evidence. [YT:4mZ3F77dNdI]
- **Anti-panic close.** Ends on one of three: turn it off, give it more time, or one specific change. [YT:E0lbvr71wR8]
- **Next test sits at the right level of the testing order.** [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M]

## T6. Test plan, account structure and reading results

- ★ **Testing order respected.** Offer → angle → creative style → hooks → small variables (primary text, headline, CTA, background colour), which are often never reached. A plan that tests headlines or background colour while the offer or angle is unproven = FAIL ("I don't think it matters"). [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M] [YT:inJWYXen3Xc] [YT:fV6P9MF7FqU]
- ★ **Kill/scale on conversion volume and cost per result, not days.** Rule written as conversions needed, with cost per lead/booked call as the decider. A fixed "kill after 3 days" = FAIL. [YT:D6Sxk8Mbkq4] [YT:dwUxmlgWfxQ] [YT:bR-UqLS1FFM]
- ★ **Testing campaign + scaling campaign, one broad ad set each.** Separate ad sets only for location; reason stated (one campaign starves new ads). [YT:IRyR9PzSnM8] [YT:D6Sxk8Mbkq4] [YT:v_u9qcbW5hY]
- **Budget split tied to results.** Good results → up to ~80% to scaling; unprofitable → most budget to testing. [YT:IRyR9PzSnM8]
- **Big swings without the tool, fine detail with it.** Image vs video, UGC vs founder-led tested in the ad set; hooks and text overlays through Meta's creative testing tool (2–5 versions, metric changed to cost per lead, extended if Meta warns of low confidence). [YT:onFwSud9C2Y]
- **Formats together in one ad set** (statics, carousels, video), not separate funnel campaigns (2026). Format kept unless 40–50% worse CPA (2020 rule, flag ALLTIME). [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M] [YT:HzDJpKu7vew]
- **Venture-capital framing.** Plan expects most tests to lose and the control's ROAS/CPA to dip while testing; doesn't kill tests before learning. [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M]
- **Attribution caveat for services.** Notes where Meta's window undercounts long-consideration revenue (sponsor data). [YT:D6Sxk8Mbkq4] [YT:onFwSud9C2Y]
- **Meta-rep advice tested, not adopted.** Platform recommendations treated as "a bit too early" until tested. [YT:sxQwH4kjGW4]
- **Position changes dated.** Structure went from multi-step funnels (2020) to 1 campaign × 4 ad sets (2021) to testing + scaling (2025) to all formats in one ad set (2026); the plan uses the current one and dates it. [YT:cqNsMJPoM7c] [YT:13s-G9Uj51A] [YT:BO-CLO9lhr8] [canon: canon.md §7]

## T7. AI ad production, ideation and enhancements (dated; most of it sponsored)

- ★ **AI actors speak as a third party, never as a customer.** "I went through the program and got great results" from an AI actor = fake testimonial = FAIL. Industry hype and truthful claims only. [YT:_MIWfyD91yc]
- ★ **Human is the filter.** Every AI output (script, actor image, Meta's image variations) has a stated review step; reject variants that swap the person or distort the founder. "You always need to be the filter". [YT:M2VReAjHiN8] [YT:mMKLLRTk-7c]
- ★ **Sponsor and date named.** Arcads, Poppy, InVideo, Hyros claims labelled sponsored, with month; the Claude + Arcads loop is DTC-tested (sales objective, $30/day × 5 days). [YT:TpZrPY6jTE8] [YT:_MIWfyD91yc]
- **Ideation prompt carries context.** Who you are, what's included, the sales next step ("book a call with us using the calendar on our website"), the audience, past winners and competitor ads; bare "10 ad ideas for X" = FAIL (everyone gets the same ideas). [YT:M2VReAjHiN8]
- **Actor matched to the prospect.** Age, gender, look, setting match the buyer; multiple versions per sub-segment. [YT:_MIWfyD91yc]
- **Enhancements set by asset, not all on/off.** Overlays off if the image already has text; animation off for text-heavy images; music checked (default was "super corporate"); flex media on. [YT:mMKLLRTk-7c]
- **Short UGC structure where used.** Hook 0–3s, problem 3–8s, solution 8–15s, proof 15–18s, CTA 18–20s, one pain-point angle per script (DTC evidence; transfer flagged). [YT:TpZrPY6jTE8]

## Universal checks (every output)

- ★ **Retrieved before writing.** At least 3 searches run; the work uses at least 3 retrieved items or passages, cited as [YT:id].
- ★ **No invented quotes, numbers, stories or dates.** Every quoted line exists verbatim in a retrieved hit; every number carries its account and date.
- ★ **Did the work.** If Ivan gave a task, the output is the finished thing (offer verdict, concept batch, brief with hooks, teardown with a Frankenstein option, test plan), not a list of questions. Questions only for inputs that are missing and block the work (spend, results, optimised event), and only after doing everything possible without them.
- **In voice.** Calm, measured UK English; hedge, then commit; anti-panic; tells Ivan to run it through his own filter and that his own data decides. No hype, no "game-changer". Signature lines used sparingly and verbatim.
- **Ends with one move.** One test, at the right level of the testing order, with its control, the result it is judged on (cost per lead or booked call), and the kill/scale rule in conversion volume.
