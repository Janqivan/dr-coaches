## 2. Frameworks and methods

### 2.1 Two doors (Andromeda / FYP delivery model) — 2025-12
- **When:** explaining why "good ads" stopped working.
- **Steps:** Door 1 = the content prediction layer (same as FYP: will this hit this person at this moment?). Door 2 = only if yes, the media-buyer auction against competitors. Old system: "find the right audience for this ad." [quote src: YT:sdEV9rZ70BY] New system: "find the right moment for this predicted reaction." [quote src: YT:sdEV9rZ70BY]
- **Signals the FYP reads ("the big four" [quote src: YT:sdEV9rZ70BY]):** hover time, swipe speed, rewatch, hesitation; plus day and hour (her feed shows holiday-cookie content only on Saturday mornings). It rewards content that "feels inevitable" and lets people recycle their own beliefs.
- **Origin read:** after a week of research she concludes Andromeda is "more of a content shift" [quote src: YT:sdEV9rZ70BY] caused by TikTok's FYP; Meta, YouTube, Pinterest, Reddit all followed. "Meta basically took TikTok's organic ecosystem and slapped it on top of their paid one." [quote src: YT:cJlBabiKX9k]
- Sources: [YT:sdEV9rZ70BY] (2025-12) · [YT:cJlBabiKX9k] (2026-04)

### 2.2 TEAP phases and account structure — 2026-02 to 2026-06
- **Phases:** Trigger, Exploration, Evaluation, Purchase (taught in her program "Tether"; captioned variously "deep"/"T").
- **Structure (budget-dependent):** one campaign, four ad sets, one per phase, so "I need it organized so that I can understand the data." [quote src: YT:gRQ1y0fLpWY] Trigger optimizes for link click or traffic; everything else for conversion.
- **Avatars:** ideally one campaign per avatar with four phase ad sets so Meta doesn't get "freaking confused"; at medium budget put all personas' exploration ads into one ad set per phase.
- **Products:** keep in categories (a training treat and a bully stick are both treats); find the core best seller, "feed that monster." [quote src: YT:gRQ1y0fLpWY]
- **Monthly decision:** fund top of funnel (eyeballs), purchase (conversions) or mid-funnel efficiency.
- **One-CBO brands:** get purchase-level people but "You're going to struggle with top of funnel... You just are." [quote src: YT:gRQ1y0fLpWY] Unless each phase is its own campaign.
- **Goal-weighted mixes (2026-06):**
  - Eyeballs: weight to trigger; link-click/traffic optimization; short-form video, pattern interrupts, novelty; rest spread evenly.
  - Sales this month: weight to evaluation/purchase; depth, proof, specificity, trust; "not really a place for short hooks." [quote src: YT:pV0tM1gSJiU]
  - Cost reduction: formats your team can make at very high quality (UGC or quick-and-dirty is fine); more effort in the middle of TEAP. "Quality is a cost reducer." [quote src: YT:pV0tM1gSJiU]
- **Open question she admits:** whether valence zones map to TEAP phases; untested. [YT:HuI3o3TBI4A] (2026-03)

### 2.3 The 12-combination system (valence/intensity × self) — 2025-05 to 2026-07
- **Pieces:** 4 valence/intensity zones × 3 selves (actual, ideal, ought) = "12 combinations of any concept ever." [quote src: YT:HuI3o3TBI4A] "If you hear that word should, it's ought." [quote src: YT:HuI3o3TBI4A]
- **Steps:** (1) take a working angle/concept; (2) have Claude write all 12 combinations (take that core idea, write me combinations of every valence, intensity, and self zone); (3) change visual hook and audio hook; (4) launch the 12 — "that becomes your structure of strategy" [quote src: YT:HuI3o3TBI4A]; (5) put the combination in the ad name ("zone one ideal" [quote src: YT:HuI3o3TBI4A]) so you can see "every single ad we run towards zone one ideal is like crushing right now." [quote src: YT:HuI3o3TBI4A]
- **Rules:** test as many variations as possible even when a brand clearly sits in one zone; but "You don't have to test all four zones" [quote src: YT:HuI3o3TBI4A] if a category loves one (pet food lives in zone 2). Lean toward the outlier reaction: from a zone 3 winner, dial up slightly or ship a zone 3 and a zone 4 version. A tiny zone 4 audience can be lovely in "a month where nothing is working." [quote src: YT:HuI3o3TBI4A]
- **Why naming matters:** stops you crediting format ("because it typically is not" [quote src: YT:HuI3o3TBI4A]), creator, a digital factor or offer; "how we're delivering the information is really key." [quote src: YT:HuI3o3TBI4A]
- **Targeting angle:** instead of a persona she targets "communication sensitivity" (people who respond to zone 3/4 arcing to zone 1, ought → actual → ideal): "I like to go after communication styles because it opens up the market." [quote src: YT:HuI3o3TBI4A]

### 2.4 Pick the lever by the metric — 2025-05

- Higher CTR → framing effect; offer generation is very very important.
- More engagement/awareness → better story ("not a psychology hack… just good marketing" [quote src: YT:VtEoAOfQsCU]): do clips connect, better actors, change environment and lines; mind cognitive load in video.

### 2.5 Three-step efficiency audit (new client) — 2026-05
1. Pull top 10 ads of the last 12 months **by efficiency** (CPA, CPM, ads that "punched above their weight" [quote src: YT:6ZBccBr5AV4]), not by spend. "This is not the same list." [quote src: YT:6ZBccBr5AV4]
2. For each: which of the five formats is it closest to; which psychological mechanism; what mental state was the customer in.
3. Find gaps (missing formats, unspoken psychological stages) → next briefs (be more specific; "show them that there's only one step for them to do, not 50" [quote src: YT:6ZBccBr5AV4]; reframe; be more confident).
- Finding: "the gap is usually pretty obvious within about 20 minutes" [quote src: YT:6ZBccBr5AV4]; brands typically have "about four ads that all do the same psychological thing." [quote src: YT:6ZBccBr5AV4] "This is not a content problem, it's a systems problem." [quote src: YT:6ZBccBr5AV4]
