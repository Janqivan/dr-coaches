## 2. Converts in general — principles in 2025/older evidence AND repeated in 2026

1. **Uglier, not ugliest — native beats polished.** Any step away from the most perfect branded ad; content must still be useful. Hott 2022–2026 (30+ mentions), Denney, Cooper, Cottrell, Savannah; 2026: "The bar to make pretty ads is basically zero" [quote src: YT:4BOtccKnC2Q]. Caveat: "Ugly ads can be bad ads too" [quote src: YT:YuS18wnD_bc] (Hott 2023). CONSENSUS. [YT:PSMit4vB8n8 (2022-12), YT:mpj0A4Prxu4 (2026-04)] → formats §1, §6
2. **Creative is the targeting — go broad.** Meta reads transcript, visuals, setting and creator to seed delivery; "if anything in your video says mom life, you're going to get moms" [quote src: YT:sdEV9rZ70BY] (Levinger). Hott: a women-only product run fully broad sent 99% of spend to women. CONSENSUS (7 speakers), 2023–2026. [YT:RVRRGf746Bw (2025-04), YT:_iMPT2SsjeU (2026-07)] → testing_diversity §1
3. **Research is where the money is made.** Most ad problems are research problems; research is ongoing, not onboarding. "if it's a messaging problem then it's a research problem" [quote src: YT:Oj-KGCw7K1g] (Cooper). CONSENSUS (~70 mentions). [YT:3wLk3D99JyA (2024-03), YT:RSa4wUuw_2k (2026-07)] → strategy_research §1
4. **Clear over clever.** Puns need decoding time the scroll doesn't give. Savannah: "Clear always beats clever" [quote src: YT:PvjkCV9WelU]; Levinger third-grade test; Heath: no curiosity headlines in feed ads; Cooper: pick clarity or curiosity, never the middle. CONSENSUS, 2023–2026. → copy_angles §1, statics §1
5. **Specific beats vague.** "Vague claims feel like marketing. Specific claims feel true" [quote src: YT:6ZBccBr5AV4] (Levinger); odd numbers feel measured; Denney: a number for time/effort/cost. 5-minute beat 2-minute "again and again" (Denney 2025-01). [YT:RpRSFn5pznc (2026-05), YT:wRA2VgYIDVs (2025-01)] → copy_angles §1
6. **Spend is the winner signal; don't turn off the top spender.** Meta optimizes at budget level (breakdown effect); pausing worst-CPA ads is "a race to the bottom" [quote src: YT:Oj-KGCw7K1g] (Hott). Denney's team killed a top performer and the account "fell over". CONSENSUS (Hott, Denney, Cooper, Cottrell), 2025–2026. [YT:RVRRGf746Bw (2025-04), YT:mpj0A4Prxu4 (2026-04)] → testing_diversity §1
7. **Hook, hold, CTR are storytelling metrics; spend and CPA call the winner.** Cooper: "Stop glorifying hook rates and hold rates." [quote src: YT:Be9YLcOory4]; Cottrell: a 20% hook can beat a 70% hook. Java Sock: 22% thumb-stop, 49% hold, 0.3 ROAS. Dissent: guest Sophia Beauvoir (2026-06) says hook/hold matter more now. CONSENSUS. → testing_diversity §1, §4
8. **Test cold; warm data flatters bad ads.** "a great ad will work for both of those audiences" [quote src: YT:RVRRGf746Bw] (Hott); Cooper's fired-then-rehired clients had nothing bringing new people in. 2024–2026. → testing_diversity §1
9. **Let ads cook.** Cottrell: "One bad week does not mean a bad ad" [quote src: YT:qZhOnITrN0Q]; Heath: "It's not time, it's conversion volume" [quote src: YT:D6Sxk8Mbkq4]. CONSENSUS (Cottrell, Heath, Hott, Savannah, Denney). → testing_diversity §1
10. **The why of a winner is in the creative, not the data.** Cottrell: "you almost certainly don't know why your winning ad is actually winning" [quote src: YT:Lkx7nOGIslw]; Denney trusts a learning only after 5–6 pressure tests. CONSENSUS (Cottrell, Denney, Levinger, Hott). → testing_diversity §1
11. **Offer first and instantly clear.** No leads/sales is "nearly always an offer problem" [quote src: YT:8dJxOaZrK1M] (Heath 2019–2026); name the service ("free interior design consultation" [quote src: YT:1EQrp8MJRek], not "free consultation"). Cottrell 2025–26: statics and offers "always go hand in hand" [quote src: YT:7c0aEpo8-bg]. → copy_angles §1, leadgen_transfer §2a
12. **Front-load and pass the "so what" test.** Hott: 90–99% of losses happen in the first second; chopping the first 3–5 s of an underperformer often wins. Denney: "tell me sooner, tell me way sooner" [quote src: YT:kYSxNySgmKc]. 2023–2026. → hooks_retention §1
13. **Emotion, desire and identity over features.** Cottrell: "People buy power, relief, belonging. They don't buy features." [quote src: YT:oVpT0GnGY8Q]; Denney: "The product is just the mechanism" [quote src: YT:5C5VhqW9HCc]; Levinger: embarrassment, never shame. 2024–2026. → copy_angles §1
14. **Live in the problem; agitate; open curiosity loops.** Hott: "live in multiple problems" [quote src: YT:pMAxIOL8dtE] (2023); Levinger 2026: agitation means "what? why?", not making people feel bad; Cottrell: open loops people want to close. → copy_angles §1, hooks_retention §1
15. **Show, don't tell; prove every claim on screen.** Visual for every line; receipts, tests, real accounts on a laptop (Heath). Sound-on era: Cottrell — sound-off optimisation ~3 years outdated. CONSENSUS. → video_ugc §1
16. **Raw footage is an asset library.** Contract for raws, recut into mashups and new hooks; "there is money sitting in your Dropbox" [quote src: YT:FWWp6NcVo3Q] (Cottrell). 2024–2026. → strategy_research §1
17. **AI amplifies the operator; it does not replace strategy.** Good AI ads come only from people who make good ads without AI; AI "averaging the averages always" [quote src: YT:CwSAZdtqemk] (Levinger); feed it real customer data. CONSENSUS (41 mentions), 2025–2026. → ai_production §1

## 4. Hooks and watch time

**What the evidence backs most**
- **A hook is visual + sound + on-screen text + vibe**, not the first line (Denney, crediting Callaway; Cottrell writes visual, audible and text hooks for every hook). The visual device is the biggest lever inside it (35 mentions). → hooks_retention §1
- **Win the right attention, not maximum attention.** Cottrell: "the hook is your targeting" [quote src: YT:IX-2-uSJDk8]; skincare exploding-melon hook hit ~70% and was one of the brand's worst ads (wrong customer). Guest Mikac: curiosity without relevance = high hook, terrible hold. CONSENSUS. [YT:opWKMc6iavo] → hooks_retention §1, §4
- **Hook families with the most backing:** (1) identity / "If you" / situational call-outs (top family per Denney 2025–26); (2) negative, regret, "ruining my life", reasons not to buy (Denney: negative "almost always" beats positive on hook rate; "ruining my life" [quote src: YT:I8tXqqfjIX4] hooks >45%); (3) physical/handwritten text — Post-it, paper flip, whiteboard (Savannah, 9 mentions); (4) native-UI devices — comment-reply bubble, text thread, Notes, search bar (Savannah, Denney; must look genuinely native per Hott); (5) curiosity/open loops (Cottrell, Hott, Levinger, Savannah); (6) face mid-emotion or a single high-drama object in frame one (Levinger's 300-ms elements; Bellabeat pregnancy test). → hooks_retention §1, §3
- **Lines that worked (verbatim in canon):** "You can't wear makeup because of flakes around your nose? This might be why." [quote src: YT:-E9sjb2msjo] (Denney A/B winner, 2026-06); "This $9 Irish soap has outlasted my last four drugstore bars" [quote src: YT:opWKMc6iavo] (Cottrell); "Everyone's been asking when this tiny device is coming back" [quote src: YT:nUTzR9ilTts] (35% hook); "My husband's gas was horrifying." [quote src: YT:eFyvp0mu7UQ] (Dr. Squatch pattern interrupt); grandma/80s-model curiosity + nostalgia beat a viral-Witcher-perfume line (Cottrell 2026-10). → hooks_retention §4
- **First frame and top of frame decide it.** Blurry or mid-transition first frames switch people off; start mid-movement (Cottrell); setting signals relevance before a word (Hott); upload custom thumbnails (Savannah). Don't lead with the logo for cold audiences — brand in the first 3 s makes Meta deliver to people who already know you (guest Mikac); removing the brand handle "can massively help your hook rate" [quote src: YT:q_PsIjZl6e4]. → hooks_retention §1
- **Hook rate = 3-sec plays ÷ impressions; benchmark against your own account.** Profitable clients range ~12% to ~70% by niche (Cottrell 2026-07); Denney's Thesis average ~24%, best 40–50%. Hold beats hook: Wispr Flow "false winner" 39.5% hook, 14% reached 25%, 3.5% completed. → hooks_retention §5, testing_diversity §4

**Retention past the hook**
- Delay the product and live in the problem: shift point ~40–50% through (Levinger); product should "make an entrance... and then just stay" [quote src: YT:sBS1vy-5Be0]; don't over-stack agitation (Hott: Earth Breeze 1:22, get there ~0:45). → video_ugc §1
- Pace hard: mundane clips ~0.5 s (Denney), scene change ~every second (Savannah), "slash and burn" [quote src: YT:cpnVXGQAWHg] (Hott); same angle too long is the common UGC mistake (Levinger). Yappers are the exception. → video_ugc §1
- Sound carries it: "You can salvage a bad video with good audio" [quote src: YT:PRKp0sSUb2c] (Cottrell); stock music makes you sound like an ad (Hott). → video_ugc §1
- Iterating hooks: Heath's Frankenstein splice (best-converting body + best hook) still reported "so many times"; Cooper warns swapped hooks can signal a different segment ("a completely different ad" [quote src: YT:_9f0rTKKIOY]). → hooks_retention §6, leadgen_transfer §2a
