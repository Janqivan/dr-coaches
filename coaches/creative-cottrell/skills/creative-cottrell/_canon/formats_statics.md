## 1. Core positions (ranked by mentions × specificity; 2026 first where it is about what works today)

1. **Creative diversity across formats beats volume of one format (NOW, GEN/DTC, ~14 mentions).** Wall-to-wall UGC testimonial accounts are the ones saying creative "isn't performing anymore": people are bored. "Factory-printed" accounts (10 variations of the same UGC ad, same structure, same creator type) stop performing because there is nothing new for the audience to see or for the algorithm to push. Winners run UGC, founder, podcast, static, mashup and AI ads across personas, angles, hooks and formats at once. "this is not a static or UGC debate": it is a sequencing and scaling question, and "format distribution unlocks diversity within your ad account". Mela's sunglasses account (every static is a selfie plus product inset) is "guaranteed" to be flagged as similar, telling Meta you have one ad; The Black Stuff's library shows different thumbnails, giving Meta signals for new audiences. Diversity does not mean more polish: "a lot of the time, the less polished the ad, the better." 2. **Statics are underrated workhorses that "mop up" bottom-funnel sales (NOW, DTC, ~20 mentions).** S tier, a "quiet performer" nobody credits. Of his ~15,000 career ads a large majority were statics and many were account top performers. In many accounts at least the top one or two ads are statics; Happy Mammoth's top three ads are all statics. Brands do the hard work with TOF/MOF video and have nothing at BOF to catch the sale; statics deliver one quick message (why us, or an offer) and remind people you exist. "Most brands are treating static ads as filler." [YT:y7TFcfgwhP8] (2026-06) · [YT:KlhBwk1kmzI] (2026-04) · [YT:Lkx7nOGIslw] (2026-07) · [YT:RExPkxyRB6o] (2026-10) · [YT:HXox1I6QQvQ] (2025-08)

3. **Native-looking beats ad-looking, now in statics too (NOW, DTC/GEN, ~12 mentions).** "Native looking statics truly kill on Facebook ads right now" because most ads look too much like ads. The trend is "Less branded, more organic looking": statics now play the role early UGC did. Instagram-story statics, Reddit/tweet screenshots and native storytelling statics are his current go-tos. Balance: keep enough brand familiarity (colors, fonts) but the ad must not "scream ad"; if it does, "it needs to be a pretty good ad to really hold people". 4. **Founder ads are the format he trusts most (NOW/EVERGREEN, DTC/GEN, ~13 mentions).** "After building over 10,000 ads", founder ads are the one format he trusts more than any other; S tier, "constantly eating up spend"; seen "a million plus dollars in spend profitably". Reasons: instant trust, story pulls people in, nobody can copy your story ("that is your moat"), feels native. Can be on camera or a voice note with B-roll: "It's pretty much free. ## 2. Frameworks and methods

### 2.1 Format tier list, 2026 version (4 criteria)
- **When:** deciding which formats to invest in "based on real data of what is working right now" [quote src: YT:y7TFcfgwhP8].
- **Criteria:** (1) ease of production, (2) conversion power, (3) scalability (how much budget it takes), (4) longevity (still around in 6 months or a trend).
- **Final board:** S = Static Ads, The Founder Ad, Mashup · A = UGC Testimonials, Mini VSL, TikTok Shop yapper video · B = Before & After, AI Ads, High-production videos · C = Street Interview · D = empty · E = Podcast Style.
- **His reasoning per format:** Static = cheap, easy, scalable, not a flash in the pan. Founder = person-to-person; one of two brands selling the same product wins on it. Mashup = anything reusing existing footage (b-roll + VO, b-roll + text); "an easy win" [quote src: YT:wfENnO7PA5Q]. UGC testimonial = still great, but must now be made as yapper-style. Mini VSL = holds the most winners entirely that we have ever made at Fraggle but only A tier because scripts take "a million iterations" [quote src: YT:y7TFcfgwhP8] and editing is craft. Yapper = performs "like absolute crazy" [quote src: YT:y7TFcfgwhP8]; scroll TikTok Shop where sales per video are visible. Before/after = video version scales but is cheesy/hard; split static rarely gets past Meta. High production = converts only when it visually demonstrates every claim, not "a load of random pretty shots" [quote src: YT:y7TFcfgwhP8]. Street interview = worked in one account of many. Podcast = "I have grown" [quote src: YT:y7TFcfgwhP8]; seen ~two convert to top of account.
- Sources: [YT:y7TFcfgwhP8] (2026-06, NOW)

### 2.2 Format tier list, 2025 version (named tiers)
- **Criteria:** ease of production, funnel-stage flexibility, production cost, scalability (will it become evergreen).
- **Tiers:** "Scroll Stopping Royalty" [quote src: YT:DPvFnflNEYQ] > "CPA Slayers" > "Was Better in the Script" [quote src: YT:DPvFnflNEYQ] > "Post and Pray" [quote src: YT:DPvFnflNEYQ] > "Your Mom Loves It" [quote src: YT:DPvFnflNEYQ] > "Creative Sin Bin" [quote src: YT:DPvFnflNEYQ].
- **Final board:** Royalty = Founder Story, Product Demo, Listicle Style · CPA Slayers = Mashup/Compilation, VSL · Was Better in the Script = UGC Testimonial, Skit/TikTok Native, Us vs Them · Post and Pray = Educational, Static Offer Ad, Street Interviews, EGC, One-take UGC · Your Mom Loves It = High Production Product Video, Benefit Callout Static · Sin Bin = Public Interaction.
- **Notes per format:** founder story very easy (AI VO or founder VO/to-camera), low cost, TOF if story is compelling. Product demo can be free, introduces and converts, needs a visual product. Listicle: find lists that naturally integrate the product; can be mashups. Mashup needs a great archive + editor; go hard but then quickly sink; moved up during the video. VSL works across the funnel, seen scale to millions; pay a specialist scriptwriter. UGC testimonial ~a couple hundred dollars if self-edited, mid-to-bottom, not for first introduction. Skits TOF only: "Nobody is going to be converting through like a skit video" [quote src: YT:DPvFnflNEYQ]. Educational needs a very strong script. Street interviews: release forms, hours for two usable clips. EGC: for things only an employee can credibly say. One-take UGC $200–300. High-production product video (15–30s, glamorous) his favorite to make but never seen it work TOF. Public interaction (staged, two actors) the only Sin Bin entry; his own Foldi example.
- **Visual pattern:** top tiers all show the product doing something in real environments with small native captions; bottom tiers hold the most designed assets: production polish does not track his ranking.
- Sources: [YT:DPvFnflNEYQ] (2025-07, EVERGREEN)

### 2.3 Funnel/awareness → format map
- TOF (never heard of you, often problem-unaware): easy-to-consume education: UGC, street interviews, founder ads.
- MOF (problem-aware, not product-aware): heavy education and desire: mini VSLs, mashups, podcast ads.
- BOF (ready to buy): statics ("the easiest to pump out and normally give you the best results" [quote src: YT:gP-68LmgAQ4]), offer ads with urgency ("limited-time 30% off" [quote src: YT:gP-68LmgAQ4]), testimonial compilations ("like UGC, but real UGC" [quote src: YT:gP-68LmgAQ4]).
- Awareness version: problem unaware → make the situation familiar ("Hey, that's me" [quote src: YT:FWWp6NcVo3Q]; teen with first acne); problem aware → friction, inconvenience, emotion via UGC demos and testimonials; solution aware → us-vs-them statics with yours on top; product aware → build trust, remove the blocker; most aware → offer statics.
- Earlier rules: statics usually retargeting, video TOF; I wouldn't put a high production on TikTok but I would put a UGC on TikTok; GIFs elevate a static to video for lower CPMs and TOF education because Meta usually ends up pushing statics to BOF.
- Sources: [YT:gP-68LmgAQ4] (2025-11) · [YT:FWWp6NcVo3Q] (2025-11) · [YT:_BuCO3Jf-K0] (2026-02) · [YT:qpJqASEvYuc] (2024-02) · [YT:cUHJkQzisDQ] (2024-04)

### 2.4 Persona × messaging × hook × format (distinct ad unit)
- Steps: fix persona (e.g. Humantra athletes) + message (peak performance on long runs/marathons) → pick format (podcast: expert interview with a marathon runner, or two runners swapping tips for a 4-hour run) → if it fails, switch format (UGC testimonial documenting a run, making and drinking Humantra mid-run) → write the hook for the format (podcast: lift a dramatic moment to the start; UGC: visual hook like pouring a sachet or water over their face).
- "Same persona, same messaging, different format" [quote src: YT:CPWoW09WCCo]; for account-wide diversity change all three.
- Same idea at message level: The Black Stuff shower cloth (better and cleaner than your dirty washcloth) run as both podcast and UGC to see which format customers connect with.
- Sources: [YT:CPWoW09WCCo] (2026-02, NOW) · [YT:gP-68LmgAQ4] (2025-11)

### 2.5 Static anatomy (three versions over time)
- 2024 checklist: scroll-stopping image + core benefits customised to what the customer cares about + headline that describes the image and encapsulates the ad + social proof (guarantee helps) — "ticks every single box". [YT:a_hMenl-qes] (2024-07)
- 2025 three things every static needs: (1) easily skimmable (understood in about a second); (2) handle objections (price → break down to a daily figure; trust → social proof); (3) social proof in every static (review, star rating, Trustpilot icon). [YT:2BrGawWj1aI] (2025-06)
- 2026 "three pieces that go into every single static ad": headline, image, CTA: "catch people's eye with the image", "give them more information with the headline", "tell them what to do with the CTA". [YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ] (2026-09)
- Rule tying them: "your image supports the headline"; link copy and image so the image shows the main benefit instead of a wall of text. [YT:TouPE2n7mzo] (2026-02) · [YT:a_hMenl-qes] (2024-07)

### 2.6 Static creation process
- Recap (2025-06): (1) research the customer deeply; (2) start from a popular objection (it "can normally spiral a couple of ideas"); (3) build in social proof wherever possible; (4) design for the scroll: "Is this eye-catching enough? Can I skim read this?" ("Think before you design"); (5) "Test fast, test messy". [YT:2BrGawWj1aI]
- Agency system for 7–9-figure DTC (2025-08): research the brand → insights → "a really sharp angle" with AI's help → great copy → design free in Canva (or drop copy into a Creative OS template, "an ad up in minutes") → launch with single-variable variations. [YT:HXox1I6QQvQ]
- Performance-minded design (2026-02): "the ads that actually held spend are the ones that had performance in mind": plan by scrolling Foreplay, checking competitors, Pinterest for design. [YT:TouPE2n7mzo]
- When ideation stalls: browse templates by type (comparison ads in Creative OS), then prompt "I want to do a us versus them static ad. Could you help write it?" plus audience and tone ("target women... a little cheeky"). That produced the breakup letter. [YT:HXox1I6QQvQ] (2025-08)

### 2.8 Native storytelling static
- **What:** looks "like someone made it by accident" [quote src: YT:5R2grcV0WzY]: low-fi image, enormous block of text in the primary text, basically no branding; "one of the best performing static ads in certain accounts" [quote src: YT:5R2grcV0WzY]; fast becoming an incredibly popular format across our clients. An alternative to spending on standard UGC.
- **Flip:** normally the image does the work; here the image is "a supporting piece for the copy" [quote src: YT:5R2grcV0WzY]. Mimics a Reddit post where someone spills their heart beside a semi-related image, or a boomer's Facebook tirade.
- **Steps:** (1) write the story first ("unless you have a really good story, then the ad just isn't going to work" [quote src: YT:5R2grcV0WzY]; spend the time on copy, then find an image that supports it); (2) image: raw, shocking or bizarre enough that sufferers recognise it instantly; usually the problem, not the product ("It feels too much like an ad" [quote src: YT:5R2grcV0WzY]), unless the product itself looks strange (Perifit); (3) image cheat code: Google Images for the type you want, use as reference in Nano Banana, change it enough to be new while keeping "the same shocking factor" [quote src: YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ]; (4) first above-the-fold line acts as the headline (e.g. "A customer shared this with us the other day" [quote src: YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ]); (5) story beats (from The Black Stuff): family origin, relatable onset, mundane specifics with numbers, failed solutions with prices, a mate's recommendation with a vivid reason, skepticism, casual product arrival, week-by-week timeline, honest disclaimer, closing for people like him.
- **Fit:** problem-solution products; brands that "don't really care too much about their brand guidelines and just want performance" [quote src: YT:5R2grcV0WzY]. Risk: "fairly ugly looking" [quote src: YT:5R2grcV0WzY], may get "Oh god, I don't want to read all this" [quote src: YT:5R2grcV0WzY] comments; "I think the performance often enough is worth it" [quote src: YT:5R2grcV0WzY]. Might be a trend; dropshippers used it "way back when" [quote src: YT:qZhOnITrN0Q].
- Sources: [YT:5R2grcV0WzY] (2026-09) · [YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ] (2026-09)

### 2.9 Instagram-story "native image" static
- **What:** photo of the target customer's hand/selfie with the product, as if posted to their story; Instagram-story fonts and text-box styling; copy split the way Instagram does it. "wins time and time and time again" [quote src: YT:TouPE2n7mzo]; "fades perfectly into the feed" [quote src: YT:TouPE2n7mzo].
- **Steps:** get the photo (producer's hand in office kitchen, or AI POV/mirror selfie) → text in Figma replicating story styling (serif Georgia Regular boxes in one version) → write copy as a real story post → brand as an underlined @-tag (people try to tap it, lifts CTR) → optional urgency line → swap image/persona for variations → pair with whitelisting/partnership ("and boy, you are on to a winner" [quote src: YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ]).
- **Why:** in Stories you stop on something unrecognised, consume it, then realise it's an ad; he watched his wife do it ("Oh, it's an ad" [quote src: YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ]).
- **Time:** 5–15 minutes. Results: Humantra six figures spend, CTR "like 3.5% or something" [quote src: YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ].
- Sources: [YT:TouPE2n7mzo] (2026-02) · [YT:KlhBwk1kmzI] (2026-04) · [YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ] (2026-09) · [YT:FWWp6NcVo3Q] (2025-11)

### 2.10 AI static workflows
- **Image-only AI (preferred):** use AI for the image (the hardest part) and build all text in Figma/Canva so copy and colour stay editable; fully-AI statics bake text in, so any change means regenerating (costly on credits/time); small text in one-shot outputs was not fully accurate. [YT:KlhBwk1kmzI] (2026-04)
- **Product-shot remake:** find a reference (Grind Coffee) → ask Claude for a Nano Banana prompt placing your product in a similar position with none of the reference's text → set size (1080x1080) → fix errors by asking for correct colour, more headroom for text, and explicit use of the provided product image → headline in Figma. Changing just the font made it "a million times better". [YT:KlhBwk1kmzI] (2026-04)
- **One-shot remake:** Foreplay reference (Space Goods) + packaging images → Nano Banana Pro: "Create an ad like the example above for [brand] using the packaging images" / "as well as the context to write the copy." [YT:wXe0B2XZTGI] (2026-03)
- **Clone a competitor winner:** a winning static has two replicable parts: messaging (paste into an LLM that knows your brand and ask for a rewrite) and imagery. Custom GPT → JSON prompt → Nano Banana Pro preserved layout, type hierarchy and badge position while swapping product, copy and mascot. But the original winner was "not a one-shot thing" (research, design iterations, client feedback); one-shot replication skips most of what makes ads win. Before copying, ask Angle? / Persona? / Copywriter? [YT:QKRd7FTZ7vM] (2026-05)
- **Claude Q&A build:** answer Claude's questions (9:16, "dark and dramatic", headline, which review); Claude pulled CTA colour from the wrapper; feed brand references to fix "amateur" fonts; then Canva Magic Layers swaps headline + review for four ads "in a couple of minutes" [quote src: YT:xIpMOTNFvkY]. [YT:SJi5todbhJg] (2026-06)
- **Template workflow (2024):** generate product scene → choose a template whose product slot fits (or regenerate to fit) → always recolour to your brand (don't copy-paste someone else's work) → swap copy. ~5 minutes per ad. [YT:KH7FZfCUFn4] (2024-12)
- **ChatGPT + Creative OS:** generate images in ChatGPT, drop into a Creative OS template (Canva/Figma), "within seconds" a batch of statics; no designer or photography. (Creative OS is a service he co-founded.) [YT:2BrGawWj1aI] (2025-06)
- **Product imagery:** invest in real product photography if possible ("will pay dividend", reusable); with no budget give Claude your product plus many reference images: "Generate me some prompts for Nano Banana to recreate these images for my brand"; "just trial and error". AI is best for unique setups that would cost a lot to shoot. [YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ] (2026-09)
- **Clean AI scenes for templates:** one product, centred, seamless coloured background, a few floating ingredient props, soft studio light; not busy lifestyle scenes. [YT:KH7FZfCUFn4] (2024-12)

### 2.11 Partnership and whitelisting
- Whitelisting: brand runs an ad from the creator's page; creator handle on top, brand invisible, brand controls strategy and budget ("borrowing their brand, their face, their voice" [quote src: YT:q_PsIjZl6e4]). Partnership: boosted from the creator's own post, both names visible. Both give "the trust of a creator's voice paired with the skill of paid media" [quote src: YT:q_PsIjZl6e4].
- Add-on process: UGC ad wins from the brand page → negotiate partnership with that creator → test 5–7 days for incremental lift → if lift, sign a one- or three-month deal.
- Agency tactics (his words: they and other brands do this): Instagram/Facebook pages for made-up people to run partnership ads; a blog-style "Nice Finds" review page every client can whitelist from; many dropshippers run almost exclusively from whitelisting pages.
- Fails when content doesn't fit: high-production ads from a person look weird; partner identity + a different creator in the video "just gets messy" [quote src: YT:q_PsIjZl6e4]. Winners are usually phone-shot, person-led, lo-fi, unedited UGC.
- History: whitelisting was a "black hat technique" [quote src: YT:q_PsIjZl6e4] 5–6 years ago; Meta then launched partnership ads, "the best of both worlds" [quote src: YT:q_PsIjZl6e4] and transparent.
- Sources: [YT:q_PsIjZl6e4] (2025-09) · [YT:RExPkxyRB6o] (2026-10)

### 2.12 Video→variants and GIF tactics
- Run the plain talking version (creator + captions) as-is, then duplicate and cut AI B-roll in at matching lines (bottle shot on "That's why I switched to...") for two launchable ads from one generation set. [YT:ahwbstiCVpQ] (2026-03)
- "Secret sauce" GIF: animate a designed static (background motion, elements popping on, changing text), usually under 10 seconds, for video's lower CPMs (Chomps). 2026: animated statics were "incredibly popular", stopped, now "a bit of a resurgence"; any clean static already has "all the ingredients". [YT:qpJqASEvYuc] (2024-02) · [YT:xIpMOTNFvkY] (2026-08)
- Mashup: archive raw UGC without captions, cut with AI voiceover, test messages and hooks fast; "Every brand should be making them." [YT:gP-68LmgAQ4] (2025-11)
