# /storybrand canon — Table of Contents

The _canon folder holds operator-grade framework maps for Donald Miller's
body of work. These files are loaded on demand by SKILL.md — do not load
all at once.

---

## Files

### `storybrand_sb7_frameworks.md`
Chapter-mapped framework descriptions from three Miller books:
- **Building a StoryBrand** (HarperCollins Leadership, 2017) and the
  **2.0 revised edition** (2025) — the SB7 spine, the BrandScript, the
  Three Problem Layers, the Guide position, Plan, Call to Action,
  Success/Failure, the Grunt Test, the One-Liner.
- **Marketing Made Simple** (Miller + J.J. Peterson, 2020) — the 5-Part
  Marketing Funnel + the canonical StoryBrand wireframe section order.
- **How to Grow Your Small Business** (Miller, 2023) — the 6-Part
  Flight Plan for clarifying the underlying business operating layer.

**Load for:** framework deep-dives, BrandScript fills, Grunt Test
diagnostics, full landing-page wireframes, funnel chain builds, Flight
Plan work, DR agency-site and NO BS Ads positioning fixes.

---

### `marketing_made_simple_frameworks.md`
Deep map of **Marketing Made Simple** (Miller + J.J. Peterson, 2020) —
the 6-part marketing rhythm: one-liner, wireframe, lead generator,
nurture email sequence, sales email sequence, marketing rhythm. 10
frameworks covering the One-Liner template, the 9-Section StoryBrand
Wireframe, the Header Test, the PDF lead-generator structure, the
Three Stages of Customer Relationship (curiosity / enlightenment /
commitment), the nurture-email rhythm and subject-line principles, the
5-email sales sequence, the weekly + quarterly marketing rhythm, and
the 10-point Marketing Department Audit.

**Load for:** full-funnel builds, lead-gen design, nurture sequence
writing, sales email arc construction, conversion troubleshooting,
audit of an existing client funnel, "we have SB7 but it's not
converting" diagnosis.

---

### `business_made_simple_frameworks.md`
Deep map of **Business Made Simple** (Miller, 2021) — the 60-day
operator curriculum on character, leadership, productivity, strategy,
sales, and execution. 10 frameworks covering the 10 Character Traits
of a Value-Driven Professional, the Self-as-Product mindset, the
Mission / Three Priorities / Key Players doc, the Five Characteristics
of a Leader, the Five Productivity Practices, the Eisenhower Box
applied for small-business operators, the Five-Part Strategic
Framework, the Sales-as-Story conversation arc, the "drama in their
story" discovery question, and the Execution Operating System (the
four-meeting rhythm).

**Load for:** team / leadership work, hiring or firing scorecards,
operator productivity diagnosis, sales-team training arcs,
execution-rhythm design, the "messaging is fine but execution is a
mess" problem.

---

### `coach_builder_grow_small_business_frameworks.md`
Combined map of **How to Grow Your Small Business** (Miller, 2023) and
**Coach Builder** (Miller, March 2024). 11 frameworks covering the
Airplane Metaphor (six functional parts of any business), the Three
Economic Priorities (Cockpit / leadership), the Sales Process as
Storied Conversation (Left Engine), the Product Ladder (Wings), the
Operations Discipline (Body), the Five-Account Cash Flow System (Fuel
Tanks), the Small Business Flight Plan (integrating one-pager), the
8-Step Coach Builder Plan, the Niche Equation for solo experts, the
3-tier Coaching Service Menu, and the Coach's weekly Marketing Rhythm.

**Load for:** whole-business diagnostics, airplane-part scoring,
Flight Plan builds for DR and NO BS Ads, solo-expert / coaching
business positioning (any operator going from corporate to solo or
consultant to coach), cash-flow discipline interventions, product
ladder architecture.

## Pairing note

`/storybrand` is the messaging-clarity coach. Positioning belongs to
`/dunford`. Always check whether the problem is positioning (which
category, which best-fit customer) before writing copy. Confused
positioning produces confused copy regardless of how clean the
StoryBrand wireframe is.
