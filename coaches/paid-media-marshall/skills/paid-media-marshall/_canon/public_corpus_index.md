## 8. The book bibliography (confirmed)

In approximate chronological order:

1. *Ultimate Guide to Google AdWords* — first edition early 2000s. Six editions total. Co-authored later editions with Bryan Todd and Mike Rhodes. Most recent edition titled *Ultimate Guide to Google Ads*. Entrepreneur Press.
2. *Ultimate Guide to Facebook Advertising* — first edition 2011. Four editions total. Co-authored with Bob Regnerus and Thomas Meloche. Entrepreneur Press.
3. *80/20 Sales and Marketing: The Definitive Guide to Working Less and Making More* — 2013. Foreword by Richard Koch. Entrepreneur Press.
4. *Evolution 2.0: Breaking the Deadlock Between Darwin and Design* — 2015. Benbella Books.
5. *Ultimate Guide to Local Business Marketing* — 2016. With Talor Zamir. Entrepreneur Press.
6. *Memos from the Head Office* — 2022. Marshall's distilled essays on operating principles.

For the Star Principle work specifically, the original source is Richard Koch's *The Star Principle: How It Can Make You Rich* (2010). Marshall extended this into the Star Principle Seminar materials.

### Tier rank — recommended ladder for Ivan

Ordered by price-to-density, from cheapest first-touch to highest-density commitment. This is the ladder to spend on if you want progressive depth on Marshall's frameworks without overcommitting.

1. **Tier 0 — Free.** Free email Marketing Letter (`perrymarshall.com` homepage opt-in) + the 80/20 free chapters + the Memos free chapters + the Free Tools (80/20 Scorecard). Zero dollars. Sets the voice and the core moves.
2. **Tier 1 — Books (~$100 total for all 6 in paperback).** Start with *80/20 Sales and Marketing*, then *Detox Declutter Dominate*, then the platform book closest to the current job (Google Ads or Facebook Ads), then *Memos*, then *Evolution 2.0* if the mechanism-of-life angle matters to the Star Principle work.
3. **Tier 2 — LOPS ($197).** Cheapest paid product. Email-copy training, but it's the easiest single-payment way to feel Marshall's actual hand on a campaign asset.
4. **Tier 3 — New Renaissance ($199/month).** The recurring membership. Best ROI if Ivan wants ongoing Marshall calibration (newsletter, forum, monthly calls). Cancel-anytime. 12 months ≈ $2,400.
5. **Tier 4 — 80/20 Productivity Express ($299 or $499).** Time-bounded program. Useful when the bottleneck is operator productivity / Pumpkin-Plan-style 80/20-of-80/20 work, not new tactical knowledge.
6. **Tier 5 — Star Principle Seminar 2.0 with Koch (verify price, typically multi-thousand).** Highest-density treatment of the Star work — co-taught with the original Star Principle author. Worth it if Star Principle is the active strategic frame.
7. **Tier 6 — AMN / Roundtable (verify, high-ticket / application-gated).** For when Ivan wants peer-group depth, not more content. Roundtable historically includes top-tier marketing operators (Keap founders, FanDuel team, etc.).
