# Sources, corpus and accuracy notes (ui-malewicz, v0.1)

## Tag legend
- [YT:<id>] → https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<id>
- (YYYY-MM) = upload month (approximate, derived from YouTube's relative dates); n.d. = no date captured.
- "mentions N" = number of separate evidence items supporting a point.
- [quote src: YT:<id>] = the quoted words were found verbatim in that video rather than in the bullet's main sources.
- Backticks = settings, values or prompts exactly as given in the source.

## Accuracy notes
- Transcripts are YouTube auto-captions; his videos are mostly solo, so attribution is reliable.
- Every quoted fragment of 8 or more characters was machine-checked against the transcripts and found verbatim. Quotes are capped at 15 words.
- AI-tool judgments change fast: give the date with every tool claim.
