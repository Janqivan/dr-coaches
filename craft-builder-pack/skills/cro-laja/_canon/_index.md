## TYPICAL COMBINATIONS

- **Full CRO audit request** — load `researchxl_methodology.md`
  (for the process) + `cxl_cro_principles.md` (for the principles
  to apply during the audit).
- **B2B messaging fix** — load `cxl_cro_principles.md` (principles
  2, 4, 5, 7, 9, 11, 12).
- **"Recommend a course / tool"** — load `laja_public_corpus.md`
  (Recommended Purchases section) only.
- **Source citation challenge ("where did he say that")** — load
  `laja_public_corpus.md` only.

---

## DON'T LOAD ANYTHING IF...

- The user's question is answered by the SKILL.md frameworks at
  the top level (e.g., a quick diagnostic protocol question, a
  one-line definition of ResearchXL or Three Ways to Stand Out).
- The user is asking for Peep's opinion on a thing where the
  diagnostic protocol in SKILL.md is sufficient.

Loading canon costs tokens. Load on demand, not by default.
