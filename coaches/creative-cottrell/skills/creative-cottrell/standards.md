# Fraser Cottrell — Standards Bank

This file is the bar. Every piece of work the coach produces or judges is graded against the checks for its task type. A check is PASS only with evidence pointed at the work itself (a line, a frame, a number, a row in the plan). "Feels right" is not evidence.

Each check carries the source it comes from. Pull the full context with `scripts/search.py "<check words>"` when a check is borderline or Ivan disputes it.

Scoring: count PASS / total for the task type. Deliver only at ≥ 85% with zero FAIL on checks marked ★. Otherwise fix and regrade (max 2 loops), then deliver with the remaining FAILs listed honestly.

Corpus status: 150 transcripts (349,775 words) in the raw layer plus canon items. Checks re-sourced on 2026-10-20; tags without YT ids were not found in transcripts.

Transfer note: the canon is DTC-heavy (12 LEADGEN items of 1,557). Wherever a check below cites a DTC result, the check still applies to DR's lead-gen work as a rule of craft; the DTC number never applies as proof.

## T1. Concept batches (what to make next, new angles, refreshing a fatigued account)

- ★ **Every concept is a full ad unit.** Each row names angle/message, persona, format and hook. A row missing any of the four is a FAIL for that row. [YT:RExPkxyRB6o] [YT:CPWoW09WCCo]
- ★ **Diversity is real, not cosmetic.** No two concepts differ only by hook or headline; each pair differs in persona, message or format. Hook/headline swaps "simply don't count as creative testing in Meta's eyes anymore" (2026-02). [YT:_BuCO3Jf-K0] [YT:CPWoW09WCCo] [YT:nUTzR9ilTts]
- ★ **Built on the account first.** The batch states what is carrying spend now (top ~5 ads by spend, a thesis for each) or explicitly flags that this audit is missing. [YT:RSa4wUuw_2k] [YT:qZhOnITrN0Q]
- **Format spread.** At least 4 formats across the batch; not wall-to-wall UGC. One-format accounts are the ones saying "our creative just isn't performing anymore". [YT:PuQ2TvXrWg4] [YT:RSa4wUuw_2k]
- **At least one hidden sub-persona.** One concept targets a persona the account likely hasn't touched (his dirt-bikers-for-soap move), framed as core buyer + sub-persona, not a replacement. [YT:nUTzR9ilTts]
- **Winning message spread, not cloned.** If a winner exists, at least one concept takes its message into a new format instead of remaking the same ad. "Don't let just one idea be one idea." [YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ] [YT:CPWoW09WCCo]
- **Funnel stage tagged per concept** (TOF never heard of you / MOF already shopping / BOF needs a nudge), and the format fits the stage (statics usually mid/bottom). [YT:Mqj05tYuDI0] [YT:y7TFcfgwhP8]
- **Each concept has a reason to exist.** One line on why this persona × message × format should work, from research or account data, not taste. [YT:qZhOnITrN0Q]
- **Era tag on every format claim.** NOW / EVERGREEN / ALLTIME with month; flips flagged (traditional talking-head + B-roll UGC "doesn't really work anymore", 2026-10). [YT:RExPkxyRB6o] [canon: playbook.md §5, month tagging convention] (era-tag convention not found in transcripts)
- **Count fits the spend band** (see T6). [YT:DNI5NI2-lSk]

## T2. Video scripts (UGC/yapper, founder, mini VSL, villain-vs-hero, problem-solution, mashup)

- ★ **Hook → body → close, and the close says what to do, where, and what they get.** A CTA that is a logo + URL, or a vague "check us out", FAILs. "people won't do something unless you tell them what to do" [YT:tVXjXJSEjeA]. [YT:IX-2-uSJDk8] [YT:KKp3rA0QK9A] [YT:PRKp0sSUb2c]
- ★ **A literal visual for every spoken line.** The shot list pairs each line with something shown; no "random pretty shots" or B-roll dumped over VO. Count lines without a visual: any > 0 = FAIL. [YT:RExPkxyRB6o] [YT:y7TFcfgwhP8]
- ★ **Every claim is proven on screen.** Each claim has a demo, test, study or matched social proof in the next beat ("flatters every body" needs different bodies). [YT:KKp3rA0QK9A] [YT:PRKp0sSUb2c]
- **Framework named and used loosely.** Problem-solution as an umbrella (proof, social proof, objection handling woven through, not "done the problem bit, now the solution"), villain-vs-hero (villain = competitor, industry, habit; twist the knife), founder (problem → couldn't find a solution → made one), or mini VSL (problem, aggravate, educate, product late). [YT:505rIrOKhWI] [YT:4o46eGI6XwQ] [YT:y7TFcfgwhP8] [YT:oZFiSUkJEPE]
- **Body resolves at least one named objection** from research, not an invented one. [YT:IX-2-uSJDk8] [YT:nUTzR9ilTts]
- **Customer's words, not brand copy.** At least one line traceable to a review, Reddit post or ad comment; stories are real reviewers' stories, never made up. [YT:svW3EyoYAiw] [YT:RExPkxyRB6o]
- **Not over-sold up front.** Product is woven into a story or problem talk; delaying the product reveal is allowed and often best. A script that is product-first from line one in a TOF slot FAILs. [YT:RExPkxyRB6o] [YT:4o46eGI6XwQ]
- **Social proof matched to persona and angle**, not generic praise. [YT:505rIrOKhWI]
- **Vehicle chosen before writing** (founder: to camera, studio, or VO; UGC: yapper while doing something, not talking head + B-roll cut-ins). [YT:Ry7KQFZaPGA] [YT:RExPkxyRB6o]
- **Casting specified in the buyer's demographic**; scripted UGC cast with actors, not "UGC creators" without examples. [YT:2cT3ccIzLTU] [YT:xB4jykCt_yU] [YT:jv5ECAtsrHc]
- **3–4 hook variants supplied** (core video + three hook variations per angle), each a different hook, not rewordings. He makes "at least three or four different variations of hooks per video"; "one core video" split into "three other variations" = four per angle. [YT:djUIjF-I2hQ] [YT:E_iKdQEtUi0] [YT:Ry7KQFZaPGA]
- **Founder ad → matched landing page named.** For agency/B2B appointment funnels he calls this "even more" important. [YT:Ry7KQFZaPGA]

## T3. Hooks (hook sets, fixing low hook rate, first 3 seconds)

- ★ **Every hook has three layers written out:** visual (what happens on screen), audible (line + SFX), text (on-screen). A hook given as a line only = FAIL. [YT:Mqj05tYuDI0] [YT:opWKMc6iavo]
- ★ **The hook targets.** It names a situation, category or persona the buyer recognises so non-buyers scroll on; "the hook is your targeting". Attention for its own sake (the ~70% hook-rate exploding melon that was one of the worst ads) = FAIL. [YT:93gJ8_qvOQg] [YT:IX-2-uSJDk8] [YT:Lkx7nOGIslw] [YT:F_fdLYnF-hs]
- ★ **Stage-matched.** Each hook is tagged TOF (educate and qualify), MOF (already shopping, e.g. "This is not your regular X"), or BOF (product + offer nudge), and the copy fits that stage. [YT:Mqj05tYuDI0] [YT:93gJ8_qvOQg]
- **Situational self-targeting, not demographic call-outs.** "Hey, you a woman that's 30 to 60" style is the dated version; 2026 version describes a situation some will find very familiar. [YT:nUTzR9ilTts]
- **Visual people don't often see.** Write the line first, then find an unusual visual; faces and product-in-hand alone are weak. [YT:0K7WXqYPFCE] [YT:opWKMc6iavo]
- **Familiar anchor for swap hooks.** "I swapped this for this" must open on something everyone recognises, ideally a comparison customers already made in reviews. [YT:93gJ8_qvOQg] [YT:Mqj05tYuDI0]
- **Opens a loop the body closes.** [YT:RExPkxyRB6o] [YT:gP-68LmgAQ4]
- **Hook rate read vs the account average and the platform.** Time of year and platform shift costs and rates; compare to the account's own average. [YT:ORojkjcIE_c]
- **Hook-rate target is relative to the account average**, never a universal number (he cites profitable clients at ~12% and ~70%). Any fixed benchmark in the work = FAIL. [YT:Lkx7nOGIslw] [YT:QoGFOGEqijw]
- **Low hold rate diagnosed by cause.** Pacing, structure or creator relatability (creator doesn't relate to the customer; very traditional UGC) before blaming the hook. [YT:E_iKdQEtUi0]
- **Hook iteration only where the data says so.** New hooks recommended for low hook rate + strong hold/CTR; not as the account's diversity plan. [YT:RSa4wUuw_2k] [YT:CPWoW09WCCo]

## T4. Static briefs (native storytelling, Instagram-story, headline, review, call-out, us-vs-them, offer)

- ★ **Static type chosen for the job, and the right element does the work.** Standard static: image catches the eye, headline gives information, CTA says what to do. Native storytelling: copy carries it and the image is "a supporting piece for the copy". The brief states which and builds accordingly. [YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ] [YT:5R2grcV0WzY]
- ★ **Copy sounds like the customer.** Native/personal statics use a real review or customer phrasing; copy that reads as brand voice on a "personal" static = FAIL. [YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ]
- ★ **Variants are differently designed statics, not headline swaps.** One message → call-out, story, headline, review layouts. A variant set of one layout with N headlines = FAIL (post-2026-02). [YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ] [YT:_BuCO3Jf-K0]
- **Scroll-stopping image that also targets.** Image chosen so only people with the problem stop ("the more bizarre, the more scroll-stopping the image is, the better"); the brief says why it stops the right person. [YT:5R2grcV0WzY]
- **Native lane looks native.** IG-story static: in-hand/POV photo, story font and text boxes, no logo/button, brand as @-tag; pair with partnership/whitelisting. [YT:TouPE2n7mzo] [YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ] [YT:FWWp6NcVo3Q] [YT:tVXjXJSEjeA] ("no logo/button" detail not found in transcripts; "almost no branding" is said of native storytelling, [YT:5R2grcV0WzY])
- **Offer statics show the offer.** If the static is about an offer, the offer is on the image. (For DR lead gen: the offer = the consult/guarantee/free audit; say so.) [YT:7c0aEpo8-bg]
- **AI route: image by AI, text built in Figma/Canva**, not baked-in AI text. [YT:KlhBwk1kmzI]
- **No realistic AI people.** People-free AI images or real photos only (2026 position: "the moment that they clock what they're watching isn't real, a lot of people just instantly turn off"). [YT:n_EyIK5eSWY] [YT:A_e_9v_Sy0I]
- **Static judged on CPA, CTR, CPC** (no hook/hold rate) in the read-out plan. [YT:HXox1I6QQvQ]
- **Static-first role stated.** The brief says whether this static is testing a message that will be spread into video if it wins. [YT:_BuCO3Jf-K0] [YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ]

## T5. Teardowns and account audits (why did this win/lose, critique a draft, audit an account)

- ★ **No verdict without spend.** A winner/loser call names spend and CPA; with no meaningful spend the verdict is "untested", never "loser". "One bad week does not mean a bad ad." [YT:qZhOnITrN0Q] [YT:Lkx7nOGIslw] [canon: SKILL.md mode 4]
- ★ **Variables separated.** The teardown says whether it won on hook, angle, message, persona or format, and proposes the test that would prove it (same message, different format). [YT:PuQ2TvXrWg4] [YT:qZhOnITrN0Q]
- ★ **Metric-pattern diagnosis in his order:** CPA → CTR → hook rate → hold rate, with his patterns: great hook + low hold = irrelevant hook; low hook + strong hold/CTR = new hook; high hook + weak CTR = selling/CTA problem. Hook and hold are "soft metrics"; very high hook + low hold and CTR = "attracting people... not necessarily the right people"; high thumbstop with few clicks = hooking people but something else is needed to get the click. [YT:RSa4wUuw_2k] [YT:KKp3rA0QK9A] [YT:QoGFOGEqijw] [YT:ORojkjcIE_c] [YT:E_iKdQEtUi0] [YT:p8isefl5j1o] ("irrelevant hook" label is canon wording, not found verbatim)
- **Audit observes, doesn't touch.** No budget or on/off changes recommended during the audit read. [YT:RSa4wUuw_2k]
- **Thesis per top ad + commonalities** (personas, formats, hook rates shared across the top ~5). [YT:RSa4wUuw_2k]
- **First 3 seconds checked for "what is being sold".** Pretty footage with no product read and a logo-URL end card = named failure. [YT:PRKp0sSUb2c]
- **Claims-vs-proof check run** on every on-screen claim. [YT:PRKp0sSUb2c] [YT:KKp3rA0QK9A]
- **Landing-page match checked.** The creative and the page feel like one journey; mismatch flagged as a CPA cause. [YT:PuQ2TvXrWg4] [YT:Lkx7nOGIslw]
- **Comments read** as part of explaining a winner. [YT:qZhOnITrN0Q]
- **Physical, specific critique.** Each issue points to a timestamp, frame or line; ends with one variable to change next. [canon: SKILL.md Voice, mode 4] (not found in transcripts)

## T6. Test plans, launch volume, reading data

- ★ **Testing and scaling separated.** New ads get forced spend in a testing campaign; never launched into a CBO beside a legacy winner; no Flexible ads where results must be read. [YT:QoGFOGEqijw] [YT:RSa4wUuw_2k] [YT:RExPkxyRB6o]
- ★ **Net new counted by his spend bands (2026-08), labelled DTC:** 10–20k/mo = 3–4 net new; 50–70k = 10–15; 70–100k = up to 20; 100–250k = ~30; 250–500k+ = 50+. Net new ≠ variation ≠ iteration, and the plan uses those words as he defines them. [YT:DNI5NI2-lSk]
- ★ **No overload.** Ad count fits the budget so each ad can exit learning; ~$50/day = one or two new ads a week. [YT:DNI5NI2-lSk] [YT:_BuCO3Jf-K0] [YT:tVXjXJSEjeA]
- **Kill/scale rule on CPA, with patience.** Primary metric is CPA (CPL/cost per booked call for DR); no kill after one bad week; turn off only after sustained underperformance. [YT:qZhOnITrN0Q] [YT:Lkx7nOGIslw] [YT:tVXjXJSEjeA]
- **Diversity audit first.** Plan shows current persona × message × format coverage and the gaps it fills. [YT:RSa4wUuw_2k] [YT:tVXjXJSEjeA]
- **Messages tested as statics first, winners spread** into yapper, VSL, founder, mashup. [YT:_BuCO3Jf-K0] [YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ]
- **Weekly flywheel cadence** (hooks → briefs → launch → data → repeat), not a one-off project. [YT:_FMXAlfgYQo] [YT:RSa4wUuw_2k]
- **Better over more stated.** If volume rises, it rises in diversity, not clones; "better pretty much always beats more". [YT:qZhOnITrN0Q]
- **Losers mined.** Plan says what each loser teaches (where the retention graph drops, why the hook failed). [YT:qZhOnITrN0Q] [YT:ofZxTu1SkQ0]

## T7. Customer research → personas, angles, messaging map

- ★ **Sources in his order and named:** Reddit, reviews, own ad comments, competitors' ads, then AI. AI-only research = FAIL. [YT:gP-68LmgAQ4] [YT:RpNcnKS2WYc]
- ★ **Customer language captured verbatim** with source, and each line tagged as "messaging angle to build out" or "direct quote for a static". [YT:1FpqxqbHxVQ] [YT:RExPkxyRB6o]
- ★ **Generic praise discarded; negatives kept.** "very tasty"-type lines dropped; objections and complaints kept and turned into objection-handling beats. [YT:RpNcnKS2WYc] [canon: messaging_strategy.md §2.1] (discarding generic praise not found in transcripts; he does reuse a plain "really tasty" line as a selling point in [YT:-bxjCMB1qWA], so treat as: keep specific lines, drop empty ones. Reddit as the place for buyer objections: [YT:RpNcnKS2WYc])
- **Personas = core buyer + hidden sub-personas**, each with a message pair. [YT:nUTzR9ilTts] [YT:CPWoW09WCCo]

## Universal checks (every output)

- ★ **Retrieved before writing.** At least 3 searches run; the work uses at least 3 retrieved items or passages, cited as [YT:id] with month.
- ★ **No invented quotes, numbers, stories or dates.** Every quoted line exists verbatim in a retrieved hit; every DTC figure is labelled DTC and never presented as proof for immigration law, franchise recruitment or vocational schools.
- ★ **Did the work.** If Ivan gave a task, the output is the finished thing (batch, script, hook set, brief, teardown, test plan), not a list of questions. Questions only for inputs that are missing and block the work, and only after doing everything possible without them.
- **In voice.** British, casual, blunt; opens against the popular take and names the incentive behind it; numbered lessons; hedges on media buying then gives a firm view; signature lines used sparingly and verbatim. No praise for Ivan, no fluff.
- **Ends with one move.** One test: what it tests, the control, the spend it needs to be read, the kill/scale rule on CPA (CPL for DR).
