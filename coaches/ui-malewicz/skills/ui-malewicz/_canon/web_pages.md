## PART 1. Web pages: structure, layout, typography and color

### Rules (ranked by how often they say it)
- **Build on an 8-based grid and reuse about four spacing values** — Drag squares of 4/8/16/24/32/48 between elements; state the grid first or "just kind of call it a day, it's not going to be good"; left margin equals right margin is the first check; web-app heights need not divide by 8. [YT:ThmHV38Ecqk, YT:w2mNSOWiZbU, YT:N-34Q9mhwvs, YT:Kqg3vN1ms2E] (2025-10–2026-09) · mentions 14
- **Gaps between groups are bigger than gaps inside groups** — "the closer things are, the more they are perceived as a group": label-to-field 8, field groups 24, margins 32; a CTA is its own action group 32 below; tiles in one list 24 apart; "practically the most important one to remember". [YT:ThmHV38Ecqk, YT:w2mNSOWiZbU, YT:Kqg3vN1ms2E] (2025-10–2026-09) · mentions 13
- **Write headline and description first; the visual only amplifies the text** — A hero needs no visual ("Let that sink in"): a visual adds time to convince and most are vanity; the key visual must connect headline and description; "most designers do it completely the opposite way". [YT:Ni34Y2uSIF4, YT:9JnNtm4imqA, YT:lpqTva43Bxo, YT:GzDBkU0irpA] (2025-10–2026-09) · mentions 12
- **Mobile is its own design, made last but best** — Stacked desktop is "irresponsible, not responsive"; bigger touch targets and graphs, rewritten copy, hero visual skipped or shrunk, no hero animation, no sticky bars, max two form fields; design desktop first even at 95% mobile traffic; set breakpoints by hand. [YT:q8yUIbRiNRc, YT:cS1J77CLqmA, YT:-xKgBgGOAqs, YT:Hzr3Q1CJPbI] (2025-10–2026-03) · mentions 12
- **Show value, then clear doubts; no landing-page blueprint exists** — Hero does about 90% of the selling, the doubt section the last 10%; its order comes from research (text plus video testimonials, case studies with the main action repeated, press and logos, use cases, comparison); he says blueprint sellers are lying. [YT:cS1J77CLqmA, YT:9JnNtm4imqA, YT:nWbBZPjev_0, YT:lpqTva43Bxo] (2025-10–2026-07) · mentions 10
- **Guide the eye: text lines get shorter toward the button, the only colorful element** — Headline > description > button row sends the gaze diagonally to the CTA; photo gaze, edges and curves point at it; a description longer than the headline lets the eye run away, so some never click; bend the rule only without raising cognitive load. [YT:wrj4GDcLqJY, YT:Ni34Y2uSIF4, YT:nWbBZPjev_0] (2025-10–2026-09) · mentions 10
- **Gradients: neighboring hues, diagonal, darker end at the bottom** — Hue gap 20–30 on blue (under 20 may vanish), 10–20 on orange and purple; brightness gap 10–20; far-apart hues look grotesque; "the best looking gradients are the ones that barely look like a gradient." [YT:FBDVzr0peO4, YT:w2mNSOWiZbU, YT:ySbVqIMV5Ng] (2021-10–2026-09) · mentions 10
- **Shadows and glows take the background's hue** — Copy the background hue into headline, card shadow and blurred shapes; redo on every new background; shadow brightness −10 to −20, glow keeps brightness and drops saturation, hue shift only 3–5; blur at least 2x Y, Y never negative, one light source. [YT:FBDVzr0peO4, YT:ThmHV38Ecqk, YT:_PJLefMZwko] (2025-10–2026-03) · mentions 10
- **Accent colour only for important actions; first palette is three colours** — One accent, white, very dark gray with a hint of the accent; second accent only in the next app; don't paint everything in the vibrant primary; no red with green "unless it's emojis"; safe zone is the middle 70–80% of the spectrum. [YT:FBDVzr0peO4, YT:ThmHV38Ecqk] (2025-10) · mentions 9
- **Subtlety is the guard-rail: "Don't go over the top."** — Shadow opacity 50% or less, background shapes at about 10% (20% too strong), one big shadow and one heavy-effect object per screen, max about three heavy images in a header; "The best mockups, the best visuals are subtle". [YT:FBDVzr0peO4, YT:_PJLefMZwko, YT:Kqg3vN1ms2E] (2025-10–2026-08) · mentions 9
- **Keep photos consistent and on-palette** — Match saturation to the least saturated photo, equal face size, plain mostly black or white clothing, people looking at the camera; recolor off-palette objects (orange folder, blue-gray shirts); one style for place photos. [YT:w2mNSOWiZbU, YT:nWbBZPjev_0, YT:N-34Q9mhwvs] (2025-10–2026-09) · mentions 9
- **Animation is subtle and tied to the message** — One small header animation that steers the eye (vertical motion pulls attention off the left-right path to the button); no slide-in on every section; loop a GIF in every other section as a focus point; heavy 3D/scroll effects only for portfolios and showpieces. [YT:Toonu-cTE60, YT:Qb7FVefKH3I, YT:PFQsC-8z8X4, YT:DMUZEMEI_Xc, YT:tP5KHuevhSI] (2024-10–2025-11) · mentions 9
- **Portfolio hygiene: remove friction and decoration** — No splash page (10–15% have one), no Figma link as a portfolio (account wall, leaks viewer email, 20–30% do it), no stock photos, no wall of text on screen one, no hamburger or surfboard decoration; thumbnails are mockup eye candy, never logos ("Nobody cares about the logos"). [YT:Kqg3vN1ms2E, YT:N-34Q9mhwvs] (2025-11–2026-08) · mentions 9
- **Portfolio: the work comes first, sold by thumbnails** — Order: work, short who-you-are, resume link, menu, contact; at least 1/3 of thumbnails above the ~700px fold; 2–3 large thumbnails beat five small; show the best hi-fi version, never the "before"; "Even a different color in the background of one thumbnail can make or break" the page. [YT:Kqg3vN1ms2E, YT:N-34Q9mhwvs] (2025-11–2026-08) · mentions 8
- **Dark text is tinted, never pure black** — Dark gray with a hint of the main or background hue (demo: saturation 20, lightness 40), lower-left of the picker; pure black stands out when you zoom out; on colorful screens drop colour from text; data text takes the header green at lower brightness and saturation. [YT:ThmHV38Ecqk, YT:FBDVzr0peO4, YT:_PJLefMZwko] (2025-10–2026-03) · mentions 8
- **Put a person in the hero, matched to the audience, gazing at the CTA or copy** — Right for 99% of businesses, a random image is wrong; recolor one clothing item to the CTA hue via a vector mask; first photo looks at the camera, later ones at the key text; a rating or "best reviewed" can replace the kicker. [YT:nWbBZPjev_0, YT:aGTqFp7GAKE, YT:1sC_KA3kTTo, YT:_PJLefMZwko] (2024-10–2026-03) · mentions 8
- **The key visual must be relevant; abstract is fine if relevant** — "Abstraction is not your enemy. It can work just fine if it's relevant." Most pages he analysed sit in the irrelevant half; a big app screenshot or dashboard in the hero usually hurts because visitors don't know the app and can't decode it. [YT:lpqTva43Bxo, YT:nWbBZPjev_0, YT:Ni34Y2uSIF4] (2025-10–2026-09) · mentions 8
- **White space is not waste, but not too much** — "a little bit of white space never hurt anybody"; big login white space is normal, "you shouldn't fear it"; too much means move content down and enlarge the visual; empty space goes right of a left-aligned form (F-pattern); body copy ragged, centered text not for long paragraphs. [YT:w2mNSOWiZbU, YT:FBDVzr0peO4, YT:ThmHV38Ecqk] (2025-10–2026-09) · mentions 8
- **One typeface, two or three sizes, safe free sans** — Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, Satoshi (Poppins in the 2021-10 and 2025-10 lessons); no font pairing in the first year or two; titles may be fancier, reading text stays simple; bold only for what matters; left-align inside cards. [YT:ThmHV38Ecqk, YT:Kqg3vN1ms2E, YT:w2mNSOWiZbU, YT:FBDVzr0peO4] (2025-10–2026-09) · mentions 7
- **Header = framing + selling point + what you do, in plain words** — His: 25 years + high converting + websites and apps; no "seamless", "empowering", "leverage"; name startups if clients ask whether you take only big brands; simplified logo with white space on its left pushes the eye right; menu Projects, Testimonials, Client list, Pricing, CTA. [YT:QaJyYl0SvyY, YT:DEs40FcLWUI] (2025-10–2026-03) · mentions 7
- **Every proof element is real and justified** — Client logos tied to real projects with short blurbs; FAQ holds only questions clients actually asked; text plus video testimonials; realistic stats, no 10x claims; "not afraid to actually show our work". [YT:DEs40FcLWUI, YT:QaJyYl0SvyY, YT:9JnNtm4imqA] (2025-10–2026-03) · mentions 7
- **Usable UI passes AA; only decoration may be low contrast** — Text, fields, buttons, switches and nav must pass the checker; test: if switching an object off loses no function or readability, low contrast is fine (cards, shapes, background decoration); aim for AA, not AAA. [YT:FBDVzr0peO4, YT:ThmHV38Ecqk] (2025-10) · mentions 6
- **Real content only** — Never Lorem Ipsum (lazy design); name the screen after the product; descriptions match the photo (no ingredient that isn't visible); vary names, counts, prices; spellcheck, because typos make viewers more critical. [YT:FBDVzr0peO4, YT:w2mNSOWiZbU, YT:ThmHV38Ecqk] (2025-10–2026-09) · mentions 6
- **Give the CTA a colour used nowhere else; keep a repeated header CTA quieter** — A button colour that exists only on the button, never in the header, makes eyes lock on it; a repeated header CTA must not copy the main one's colour or style and can light up after the hero scrolls past. [YT:Toonu-cTE60, YT:9JnNtm4imqA, YT:3BvDDjmzYY8] (2024-10–2026-07) · mentions 6
- **Convert first; don't just apply the newest trend** — Linear was simplified because it's what converts not what looks good on design blogs; reskinning an old site with glass panels and dark gradients is "not how we do it"; brutalism "had a good run but it didn't really stick". [YT:u01ZGoiLJ70, YT:Qb7FVefKH3I, YT:tP5KHuevhSI] (2025-10, n.d.) · mentions 6
- **Align to baselines and the grid; optical vs bounding box depends on audience** — Text aligns to baselines not boxes; spacing comes from the grid (29 becomes 24, 32 or 40); bounding box for developer handoff, optical for portfolio pieces; icons rarely center perfectly, close is good enough. [YT:w2mNSOWiZbU, YT:ThmHV38Ecqk, YT:ySbVqIMV5Ng] (2021-10–2026-09) · mentions 6
- **Type floors: no thin weights early, body 14–16, never under 12, line height about 1.3–1.4** — 10 only for tab-bar labels; sizes 12–40, 40 only for onboarding titles or critical info; regular body, heavier titles; button label about 1/3 of button height. [YT:ThmHV38Ecqk, YT:FBDVzr0peO4] (2025-10) · mentions 5
- **Lighter is closer** — White cards on a page about 95% lightness (F5, F1 for more separation), never a white page with darker cards; floating windows pure white; a black bar can sit on top because black is far from the white/grey family. [YT:Kqg3vN1ms2E, YT:w2mNSOWiZbU] (2026-08–2026-09) · mentions 4

### Methods (named or ordered)
#### Red square method (his own alignment technique, built for mobile, extended to web)
- When they use it: any layout, to set spacing, grouping and alignment instead of eyeballing.
- Steps: 1. Build squares 8/16/24/32/40/48 (to 64) and park them off-canvas. 2. Define the grid first: web = 12 columns, align Center, gutter 32, column 64 (1120 wide), grid coloured blue; mobile = 32 margins. 3. Treat every object, even text, as a rectangle; drag squares between elements, big squares between sections, small inside groups. 4. Check left and right margins and baselines. 5. "Soft grid" for card views: 32 outer margins and 32 between cards, return to 12 columns for lists and full screens.
- Their example: login rhythm header to subtext 24, to first field 48, between fields 40, 40 to button, group centered vertically.
- Sources: [YT:w2mNSOWiZbU, YT:ThmHV38Ecqk, YT:_PJLefMZwko] (2025-10, 2026-03, 2026-09)

#### Block framing
- When they use it: before styling, to prove structure is not random.
- Steps: 1. Coloured rectangles stand in for text, images and controls (not wireframes). 2. Set font size equal to block height (title 24, subtitle 16), then shrink for realism. 3. Vary widths, keep heights identical. 4. Heights divisible by 8; the button is its own group.
- Their example: iPhone 12 card margin 32, inner padding 24, title to lines 16, text to button 32, button to card bottom 24.
- Sources: [YT:ThmHV38Ecqk] (2025-10)

#### "64 by 32 by 12" web build (Blueprint grid)
- When they use it: AI-assisted web builds; "not vibe coding" because the prompts are precise.
- Steps: 1. Define the grid (64 column, 32 gutter, 12 columns) first. 2. Main wrapper, nav, top bar, H1, description, CTA. 3. Reuse code snippets from earlier sites. 4. Decide mobile breakpoints, font sizes and line breaks by hand (a BR tag shown only on mobile or desktop). 5. Never let AI define breakpoints.
- Their example: paper sketch, copy structure in Obsidian exported as MD, Claude builds, manual Sublime edits, Git push to a Mac mini Apache preview on real phones.
- Sources: [YT:-xKgBgGOAqs] (2026-03)

#### Hero build order (anatomy of a hero)
- When they use it: every landing page hero; copy before key visual.
- Steps: 1. Kicker: small social proof (traction, a non-round proof number), not a "pre-headline". 2. Headline: problem plus solution in very simple words, benefit or curiosity. 3. One-line description: how you do it, no secrets. 4. One or two buttons: low-friction, tied to the headline, never "Next" or "Sign up". 5. Check marks, icons or emoji for validation; logos below the fold. 6. Only then the key visual that reinforces that copy.
- Their example: layouts are centered content with an overlapping visual, visual on the right, or visual below the copy (usually a bad idea).
- Sources: [YT:nWbBZPjev_0, YT:9JnNtm4imqA, YT:lpqTva43Bxo, YT:q8yUIbRiNRc] (2025-10, 2026-07)

#### Key-visual plot
- When they use it: choosing or auditing a hero visual.
- Steps: 1. Axes: x abstract to literal, y irrelevant to relevant to the site. 2. Make 10 versions of one hero from nonsense to literal claim. 3. Place each on the chart. 4. Push the creative direction upward into the relevant zone (literal or abstract).
- Their example: for a notification filter, the invisible wall blocking hundreds while exactly five pass ranked top; a relaxed reader with a phone showing five notifications is the best literal one; an isometric scene says "everything is connected", the opposite of the app.
- Sources: [YT:lpqTva43Bxo] (2026-07)

#### Leading-lines audit
- When they use it: judging any hero or header.
- Steps: 1. Trace the eye path. 2. Each text line shorter than the one above, button the only colorful element. 3. Check that photo gaze, edges, curves and perspective point at the button. 4. Blur test. 5. Remove competing diagonals.
- Their example: Apple Watch hero (big headline, shorter line, shorter button in another colour; watch curve bends the path back up); a dark laptop is skipped, the eye lands on glowing edges and a glare pointing at the button, and he says Apple skipped the silver laptop "on purpose because of the leading lines principle". [quote src: YT:wrj4GDcLqJY]
- Sources: [YT:wrj4GDcLqJY] (2025-10)

#### UI audit order (rescue a bad UI)
- When they use it: critiquing a weak screen or a student portfolio; measure before judging taste.
- Steps: 1. Left margin equals right margin, draw guides. 2. About four spacing values on an 8 base (outer 20, inner 16, cards 24, bigger gaps between groups). 3. One typeface, 2–3 sizes (12/16/20). 4. One icon pack, stroke matched to text weight. 5. Lighter things closer. 6. One effect in one place. Fix order: alignment, fonts, hierarchy, icons, colour. Rescue in greyscale first, desaturate photos.
- Their example: the whole improvement came from unifying fonts and spacing, not colour.
- Sources: [YT:Kqg3vN1ms2E] (2026-08)

#### UI sandwich (case study and portfolio piece)
- When they use it: recruiter-facing case studies; visuals win attention, then people scroll.
- Steps: 1. Open with a big version of the thumbnail visual (many screens, often slightly diagonal, the mockup with the most screens). 2. Title, then plain TL;DR body with research, coloured section bands and photo breaks. 3. Project summary row (what, where, why, role, category, when). 4. Close with a similar but different hi-fi mockup, never repeating earlier ones.
- Their example: 600-wide white frame, 12 columns, margin 24, gutter 16, 24 from sides and 48 between sections.
- Sources: [YT:N-34Q9mhwvs, YT:Kqg3vN1ms2E] (2025-11, 2026-08)

#### Agency-site structure with breathers (his own site)
- When they use it: a services site that must convert.
- Steps: 1. Header: framing + selling point + what you do. 2. Two projects above the fold, alternating plain screens and people using the product, with outcome stats. 3. Bento of four "what we do" boxes (industries list, realistic conversion graphs, growth-strategy differentiator, chat bubbles explaining design decisions). 4. Project grid with alternating tile sizes. 5. Breather: dark background, lots of white space, only low-friction asks (a 15-minute call or email). 6. Two pricing tiers with direct buy buttons. 7. FAQ merged with about-us.
- Their example: test even the obvious; latest version performs 3x better than the previous one.
- Sources: [YT:QaJyYl0SvyY, YT:DEs40FcLWUI] (2025-10, 2026-03)

### Recipe library (exact, reusable)
- **Glow and shadow colours that match the element** — Make three swatches (element, dark inner shadow, light glow) and compare HSB. Element 200/93/95: hue of shadow and glow moves only 3–5; shadow keeps saturation, brightness about 15 lower; glow keeps brightness, drops saturation. [YT:FBDVzr0peO4] (2025-10)
- **Tinted palette from one main colour** — Pick the main colour from the safe list (green for organic); background = same hue, HSL lightness 80–90, saturation about 80, bottom of the layer list, locked; second accent = shift hue slightly (toward yellow), tune lightness; description text lightness −20 and saturation 15 to 10; hours and address labels saturation 10, lightness 60. [YT:ThmHV38Ecqk] (2025-10)
- **Aurora background** — Circle in a very light colour (secondary button fill), layer blur about 100, bottom of the layer list; duplicate in blue, green and a third near the centre; raise saturation and brightness, lower opacity; circles must overlap or the gradients don't blend. For mockups export at about 5x as PNG, low opacity. [YT:FBDVzr0peO4, YT:_PJLefMZwko] (2025-10, 2026-03)
- **AA-safe button** — Paste the button and label hex into a contrast plugin (Microsoft's) or a web checker; edit the colour until AA barely passes, then paste that hex onto the other buttons. Quick fixes: darken the green by 10 brightness (79 to 69); lower HSL lightness about 10 under white text; copy the dark title hex onto a pale button's label. [YT:FBDVzr0peO4, YT:ThmHV38Ecqk, YT:w2mNSOWiZbU] (2025-10, 2026-09)
- **Diagonal background gradient** — Same colour on both stops, switch one stop to HSB and cut brightness by about half, drag the line diagonal with handles inside the box. Button gradient: diagonal, darker toward the bottom, brightness gap 10–20. [YT:ySbVqIMV5Ng, YT:FBDVzr0peO4] (2021-10, 2025-10)
- **12-column web canvas** — Frame about 1120 × 750 (the fold), radius 32; layout grid Columns, 12, align Center, gutter 32, column 64, blue grid. Dashboard: menu 2 columns, orders 6, map 4; logo 32 from the top, first menu item 64 below it, 40 between items. [YT:w2mNSOWiZbU] (2026-09)
- **Mobile guides** — 390 wide: two cards = 390 − 32 − 32 − 24 = 302, so 151 each; tab bar = five 78 px low-opacity rectangles, icon and label centered in each, all labels 12 in one colour. [YT:ThmHV38Ecqk, YT:Kqg3vN1ms2E] (2025-10, 2026-08)
- **Case study kit** — Frame 600, export 2x; Plus Jakarta Sans, title extra bold 32, subtitle regular 16, section titles about 20 bold. Colour cards: swatch on top with only top corners rounded (radius 8), hex with # in 14 medium, role label small #999, white swatch with a light-gray inside stroke, text #2C2C2C. [YT:N-34Q9mhwvs] (2025-11)
- **Rotated mockup showcase** — Rotate the group 12–15° (usually 13°), leave one corner open, equalize gaps; shadow X = Y 24, blur at least 2x offset, tinted dark brand colour, barely visible; phone gap about 56–58 (over 50 with shadows); one large screen (width 500) on top, smaller ones lower opacity 25 to about 15. [YT:_PJLefMZwko] (2026-03)
- **Legible text on a photo** — Black linear gradient overlay, top 80–90% opacity fading to 0% at the bottom, white text; for a login visual make the bottom opaque, then lower it slightly so the photo stays visible; or duplicate the photo, cut out the subject, enlarge about 5%, blur the photo beneath and add a dark fill. [YT:ThmHV38Ecqk, YT:w2mNSOWiZbU, YT:N-34Q9mhwvs] (2025-10–2026-09)

### Fix library (problem → fix → why)
- **Problem:** form labels sit as close to the field above as to their own → **Fix:** 8 label-to-field, 24 between field groups, 32 screen margins → **Why:** proximity violation. [YT:ThmHV38Ecqk] (2025-10)
- **Problem:** cards 20 apart glue to the list below → **Fix:** 24–32 between groups, then one consistent 32 between sections → **Why:** same value inside a group, bigger between groups. [YT:Kqg3vN1ms2E] (2026-08)
- **Problem:** green text on a pale green secondary button → **Fix:** copy the dark title hex onto the label (a slightly more saturated gray also works) → **Why:** contrast rises. [YT:ThmHV38Ecqk] (2025-10)
- **Problem:** resume header with ribbon, stacked backgrounds, misaligned icons, name the size of body text → **Fix:** simplest header with a clear size step name > description > contact → **Why:** no size progression means no hierarchy. [YT:Kqg3vN1ms2E] (2026-08)
- **Problem:** heading too thick at extra bold 40 → **Fix:** bold 32, break after "free", text lightness 40 to 30, left edge on the 32 margin → **Why:** headings darker than labels add gradation. [YT:ThmHV38Ecqk] (2025-10)
- **Problem:** full-screen low-opacity photo behind a form → **Fix:** mask the photo into a large oval (about half the screen) at the top, form below → **Why:** at any opacity, contrast with fields and text is too low. [YT:FBDVzr0peO4] (2025-10)
- **Problem:** header CTA copies the main CTA → **Fix:** make it much less prominent, light it up after the hero scrolls away → **Why:** it competes for attention. [YT:9JnNtm4imqA] (2025-10)
- **Problem:** hero with several dancing people ("vertical chaos") → **Fix:** one person looking at the button or slogan, rating replaces the kicker, line before the CTA states the main incentive, show more product not more people → **Why:** vertical movement pulls focus off the button. [YT:aGTqFp7GAKE] (2024-10)
- **Problem:** AI landing template (pill kickers, very thick headline, CTA in the nav colour, tinted icon in a rounded square, mismatched SVG icons) → **Fix:** contrasting CTA colour, thinner headline weight, emotion-adapted photo, structure from the audience's questions by importance → **Why:** otherwise a generic template. [YT:3BvDDjmzYY8] (2026-07)
- **Problem:** desktop stacked into a long mobile scroll → **Fix:** redesign: bigger touch targets and graphs; 4K version hamburger top left, centered content, no underlined links; 10K version adds an app-store arrow for verification, collapses text links, drops the button glow, person looks at the button → **Why:** significant retention boost, less overload. [YT:cS1J77CLqmA, YT:Hzr3Q1CJPbI, YT:q8yUIbRiNRc] (2025-10–2025-11)
- **Problem:** brand colours flagged as sad by Web Review (green plus near-black purple), logo mark too thin → **Fix:** recolor, skip typical medical blue, thicker mark → **Why:** "instantly the website came to life". [YT:Qb7FVefKH3I] (2025-10)
- **Problem:** small product lost on a dark background → **Fix:** lighten the gradient behind it, add a heavily blurred white oval under it as glow, overlay blend → **Why:** makes it pop. [YT:ySbVqIMV5Ng] (2021-10)

### Coaching and critique moves
- Measure before judging taste: draw guides, compare left and right margins, count spacing values (about four). [YT:Kqg3vN1ms2E]
- Build a detector for bad design and ask "How can I test it?" of every proposal. [YT:Kqg3vN1ms2E]
- Interrogate each element ("Why are these shoes here?"); delete pretty elements with no job. [YT:Kqg3vN1ms2E]
- Check that numbers add up across flow steps, mutually exclusive buttons get unequal weight, no navigation destination appears twice. [YT:Kqg3vN1ms2E]
- Plot the hero visual on abstract-to-literal against irrelevant-to-relevant, then make ten versions. [YT:lpqTva43Bxo]
- Trace the eye path from headline to button; run a blur test. [YT:wrj4GDcLqJY]
- Strip the page to text only and ask whether it sells alone; try switching fear to desire. [YT:nq07DZq3Og8, YT:GzDBkU0irpA]
- List the audience's main questions and answer them in order of importance. [YT:3BvDDjmzYY8]
- Decoration test: switch the object off; if no function or readability is lost, low contrast is fine. [YT:FBDVzr0peO4]
- Zoom out to judge consistency; pure black text stands out when zoomed out. [YT:w2mNSOWiZbU, YT:ThmHV38Ecqk]
- Annotate why a page works (reading order, white space, objection removal, curiosity). [YT:Ni34Y2uSIF4]
- Test even the obvious and watch recordings of what people click and ignore. [YT:QaJyYl0SvyY, YT:Kqg3vN1ms2E]
