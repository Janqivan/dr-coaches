## PART 4. Video: generation, direction and editing

### Rules (ranked by how often they say it)
- **Bake motion into the starting still so the video "starts halfway".** Put `spinning wheels`, `motion blur` or `bokeh motion`, streaked road lines and a sharp subject into the image step; pick mid-stride, mid-turn, hands-up, flawed-looking stills (motion is "already halfway there"), not standing subjects facing the lens. Static rims on a static background make cars slide or roll backwards; a flat-heeled boot vibrates or walks backwards. [YT:swamNhl1pIs, YT:_nT6fav4qFA, YT:k8rciHNdlvs, YT:AOuXCRzjsS0] (2024-10 to 2026-02) · mentions 17
- **Code and 3D beat raw generation for control: block the scene, then hand the render to the video model.** Code-generated video and 3D is "way more controllable"; he blocks in three.js (fast previs, low-fi poly) and feeds the render to Seedance as reference video. Code-built motion graphics stay editable (change one letter in one frame) where a generated clip must be regenerated. Blender takes longer than everyone says. [YT:tlqnW1sp5X4, YT:nROQ4LJ4GcQ, YT:jJ0x0q9EEy8] (2026-09 to 2026-10) · mentions 10
- **Test cheap, spend on the dialed-in version.** Veo 2 quality before Veo 3 credits, 480p Midjourney before HD, a fast model for 20 to 30 tries (Fast Hours). Veo 3 is "not a playground like MidJourney"; knowing the tools means "you'll just generate less crap". He no longer runs video "willy-nilly" and has moved back toward images. [YT:5tjzTF6MR5A, YT:hAnYA2wJaZ4, YT:AOuXCRzjsS0, YT:YD5V0hKlRew] (2025-10 to 2026-02) · mentions 9
- **Say direction and mechanics literally.** Add the heading (`driving towards us`; Fast Hours: `walking right`, video is "so literal"), think mechanically for physics (fly-cast arm snapping at 10:00 and 2:00, line tension bending the rod), and script head and eye moves instead of asking for "natural" (Fast Hours: head down left, chuckle, look up, exhale). Otherwise cars drive backwards and doors open on the wrong side. [YT:k8rciHNdlvs, YT:vGy9dKPbtGc, YT:nJI3MR9LZXQ, YT:qhxUbwnJ-9g] (2024-10 to 2025-10) · mentions 8
- **Keyframes are the easy way to control video: set start and end, prompt only what happens between.** A start frame alone gives no context for the unseen side of a car, so wheels and paint change; an end keyframe fixes the last frame and style and keeps a person consistent. "If I can control the images I can control the video" (logos survive a camera move). Kling is the start-plus-end pick (Fast Hours). [YT:SbDupYyGXac, YT:AOuXCRzjsS0, YT:jBcL-XrHqzY, YT:2sY91XsiRuw] (n.d. to 2026-02) · mentions 8
- **Make keyframe pairs related, not identical.** A mirror in a different place, another spoiler shape, a beard at two sizes or different wall bricks all show as morphing; some variance, not too much. Keep the subject off-center and alternate sides (left, right, left) so it can travel instead of parking mid-frame; shrink the last frame to imply distance; use the edited destination image itself as the last keyframe. [YT:2sY91XsiRuw, YT:swamNhl1pIs, YT:J5U49z2_EKw] (2025-10) · mentions 7
- **With sound in the model, direct voice and sound in the prompt.** Veo voices sound alike unless you describe tone per character; Veo 3 collapsed the old six-to-seven-step route (image, video, ElevenLabs voice, lip-sync, sound library) into one prompt. Layer two or three sounds and match depth (Fast Hours). Keep a voice with a roughly 7-second clip as a Seedance ingredient. [YT:J5U49z2_EKw, YT:hAnYA2wJaZ4, YT:Rzr8TDN_0y8, YT:aqdZAjHVOmQ] (2025-10 to 2026-06) · mentions 7
- **Image references lock the look but cost control.** They lock character, setting and style, yet the model has done about half the work and a low-res or grainy base gives a bad video; keep prompts even shorter and focus on motion. With a start frame, lighting and color are baked in: describe camera motion and shot type, subject action and direction, environment interaction; in crowds add background motion. [YT:YD5V0hKlRew, YT:AOuXCRzjsS0] (n.d. to 2026-02) · mentions 6
- **Mix and match tools; each model needs its own prompting habits.** Do not be brand loyal: Luma for keyframes, Runway for quality; image references carry the aesthetic across tools. Veo 2/3 are prompted totally differently from Kling 2.0; Kling prompts carry across versions, Midjourney's change each version. Run one prompt through several models. [YT:YD5V0hKlRew, YT:5tjzTF6MR5A, YT:26bjN6CwwJs, YT:PJ347ccVJBs] (n.d. to 2025-10) · mentions 6
- **"70% of AI video is understanding how to troubleshoot it": many small hacks, hunt the one word.** Hide drift with a filmmaking cut; find the single word that broke a take (`faded` gave chipped paint); run it a couple of times, adjust the problem area, and if you do not need control let it ride (Fast Hours). [YT:hAnYA2wJaZ4, YT:SbDupYyGXac, YT:gdPY4W72-N0, YT:nJI3MR9LZXQ] (2025-10 to 2025-11) · mentions 5
- **You direct in three-dimensional space: learn camera and cinema terms.** Video is the photo element list plus camera movement and subject movement. Camera motion is what matters (forward, back, up, down, diagonal; 360-degree orbit, upward tilt): have ChatGPT look terms up. Runway works in 3D, so arcs and tilts move the camera; text-to-video needs a different prompt set than image-to-video. [YT:6w6pKyCCQFg, YT:k8rciHNdlvs, YT:QLbjv3CbfIo, YT:swamNhl1pIs] (n.d. to 2025-10) · mentions 5
- **Do not chase perfect video.** Two crowds: perfect with heavy curation (square peg in a round hole) versus using morphing as the style; no middle ground, accept it or fight it. Small formats get less scrutiny: "marketing is a little bit of deception". (Co-host Drew Brucker: embracing the tool's artifacts is a style choice.) [YT:O9g2mwlwQcQ, YT:YD5V0hKlRew] (n.d. to 2025-10) · mentions 4
- **Work backwards and string short controlled segments.** Know the end result first; if a team changes one word you do not rerun a 30-second generation, so chain short first/last-frame segments, add checkpoints before spending Seedance credits, and expose few inputs. [YT:N-tmQ_Can_o, YT:AxV8TR3BdqI] (2026-09) · mentions 4
- **Midjourney first, then video; every still is a static plus a moving asset.** Text-to-video straight in Runway is inconsistent for characters and look; Midjourney gives the consistent base. Aug 2024 stack: Midjourney, Runway, Luma second. 2026: Midjourney environment and character, GPT sheet, Seedance ingredients. [YT:k8rciHNdlvs, YT:YD5V0hKlRew, YT:Rzr8TDN_0y8] (2024-10 to 2026-06) · mentions 4
- **Pin brand details with keyframes or last-frame generation.** Text and logos morph (a Coca-Cola logo cannot distort); a transparent-background PNG as Runway's last frame holds text, logos and colors. Start with short, punchy, visceral prompts and expand only when a generation fails. [YT:YD5V0hKlRew, YT:jBcL-XrHqzY] (n.d.) · mentions 4
- **AI in pro production is VFX-like, not raw output.** Lots of doctoring, editing and CGI behind the scenes. (Fast Hours: AI video is a gap filler and leverage multiplier, not one-for-one replacement.) [YT:oKQd-ZumqCA, YT:RNF9xZYHOiQ] (2026-03 to 2026-08) · mentions 3
- **Color-correct AI video.** Even the slightest adjustment makes a visible difference (Drew credits Rory with the idea, Ep. 74). Guest Salma: generate in 4K or 1080 and color correct instead of using video upscalers. [YT:haCwSYl99ks] (2026-08) · mentions 2

### Methods (named or ordered)
#### Block in three.js, then hand the render to the video model
- When they use it: a scene too hard to describe to a generator ("a relatively insane concept"), or dialogue where reverse angles scramble the background.
- Steps: 1. Describe the scene to Opus and have it render low-fi poly in three.js. 2. Ask for any angle (aerial etc.) or switch to orbit, side or free camera and pick angles "as a producer". 3. Use the render as the reference video for Seedance, with character references and environment plates. 4. Dialogue: block the whole scene, direct it, then use image references (put this person here, that one there).
- Their example: follow a guy, flip the camera, push him out of a helicopter, parachute, land on skis; three experiments took about 40 minutes of his week. Golf-course chain: render, highlight video, start-frame images as Seedance references, texture on the render.
- Sources: [YT:tlqnW1sp5X4, YT:jJ0x0q9EEy8] (2026-09)
#### Asset library, then code renders
- When they use it: a whole animated piece where only the assets need generating.
- Steps: 1. One Midjourney still in the look (2D paper-craft) plus a style reference. 2. Tell Opus (in Astra) to "create an asset library": characters, environments, rig pieces (detachable hands and legs), scenery, storyboard; images from ChatGPT's image model. 3. Claude animates the rigged assets in code. 4. Magnific for sound effects, ElevenLabs for voice-over (library search `a deep raspy country British accent`), Claude writes narration and mixes. 5. Make 10 to 15 variations, trim to conflict points, character intros and fun scenes.
- Their example: Lord of the Rings piece for his young daughter, about four minutes in one continuous scroll; only the assets are generated, the rest is code.
- Sources: [YT:nROQ4LJ4GcQ] (2026-10)
#### Listicle-video system
- When they use it: repeatable countdown videos (five to one) a team runs from one-word inputs.
- Steps: 1. Work backwards from the end result. 2. Character sheet first (angles, expressions). 3. Build the board first, add the effect (torn cardboard) after. 4. Inputs: list text, location, optional images. 5. A second system prompt writes the next images in order with the first image as input so handwriting stays identical: "first frame last frame but with image generation". 6. Script into a system prompt that writes Seedance first/last-frame prompts per beat, plus a voice reference; a compositor bakes it. 7. Checkpoints before spending credits. [quote src: YT:N-tmQ_Can_o]
- Their example: chrome apple, 360° rotation through Grok, Meshy V6 3D asset, effect node; about five minutes.
- Sources: [YT:N-tmQ_Can_o] (2026-09)
#### End-frame chain
- When they use it: longer shots from one image (Midjourney, Runway, Kling).
- Steps: 1. Use a clip's end frame as the next clip's start frame. 2. Repeat, alternating subject sides. 3. Use switch-zoom transitions between places. 4. (Fast Hours) remove the background and regenerate to keep the same subject in a new scene.
- Their example: one car image through jungle, mountains, desert, city, back to a house; about 15 minutes after the feature shipped.
- Sources: [YT:J5U49z2_EKw, YT:nJI3MR9LZXQ] (2025-10)
#### Midjourney video V1 workflow (Fast Hours)
- Steps: 1. Run Auto low and high two or three times to see how it reads the still. 2. If nothing close, go manual: action, camera motion, environment interaction; do not repeat style words (Rory). 3. Extend up to four times (about 25 s): delete the carried-over prompt, write a net-new specific one, trim long prompts (co-host Drew Brucker). 4. Upscale the 480p in Topaz Astra. 5. Follow the clip that feels right longest.
- Sources: [YT:6vMsfe-KgRI, YT:aqdZAjHVOmQ, YT:hAnYA2wJaZ4] (2025-10)
#### GPT-for-Veo prompt workflow
- When they use it: Veo 2 text-to-video that needs heavy detail (Feb 2025).
- Steps: 1. Catalog about 15 of Veo's suggested prompts. 2. Have a GPT deconstruct them. 3. It returns three options per idea: Google-formula standard, broken out the way he prompts, experimental. 4. Edit from experience (add static interference); about 20 minutes before the first run.
- Their example: McLaren in a studio reflecting vintage racing footage; about 30 generations, all good, 52 seconds; narrative, emotion-driven wording beat technical.
- Sources: [YT:5v9vcLfTFag] (2025-10)

- **Seedance ingredients stack** — environment and character built in Midjourney, character sheet made with GPT, all dropped in as ingredients; no first frame needed, a sketch of the look can be added. [YT:YD5V0hKlRew] (n.d.)

### Fix library (problem → fix → why)
- **Problem:** cars slide, roll or drive backwards → **Fix:** `spinning wheels` and motion blur in the image prompt, `driving towards us` in the video text → **Why:** static rims and a clean static background give no motion cue. [YT:swamNhl1pIs, YT:_nT6fav4qFA, YT:k8rciHNdlvs] (2024-10 to 2025-10)
- **Problem:** fuzzy mid- or background face morphs → **Fix:** upscale the base image 4x in Magnific or Krea (even 2x for facial definition) → **Why:** the tool morphs what it cannot read. [YT:_nT6fav4qFA] (2024-10)
- **Problem:** turning car changes paint and rims → **Fix:** rewrite the prompt around the axle hitting the ground in slow motion so the body never shows, then jump to the tire keyframe → **Why:** a filmmaking cut hides the drift; he calls it standard troubleshooting, not a process, and expected it all to change within about 3 months. [YT:SbDupYyGXac] (2025-11)
- **Problem:** clean red-and-white Bronco came out chipped in Veo → **Fix:** find the one word (`faded` in a ~50-word prompt) and delete it → **Why:** single-word sensitivity. [YT:gdPY4W72-N0] (2025-10)
- **Problem:** reverse-angle dialogue scrambles the background; invented creatures look like "really bad illustrations" → **Fix:** block the scene in three.js, then use image references; give two reference images and let Opus build 3D models via the Magnific MCP → **Why:** image generators do not understand space. [YT:tlqnW1sp5X4] (2026-09)
- **Problem:** push-in needs pixels that do not exist → **Fix:** back the base image out in the Midjourney editor, then let the video tool zoom in → **Why:** zooming in is easier than out. [YT:swamNhl1pIs] (2025-10)
- **Problem:** static fashion pose gives a small head move → **Fix:** add dynamic motion in both Midjourney and Runway (hand flailing, candid half-finished pose, `cheering` as a weird token) → **Why:** stone-faced arms give the tool nothing to continue. [YT:_nT6fav4qFA] (2024-10)
- **Problem:** one element will not hold still (a traffic light kept moving despite `perfectly still`, motion low) → **Fix:** unsolved for him; co-host Drew Brucker states its locked position (`the traffic light is locked on the right side of the image during a static shot`). [YT:hAnYA2wJaZ4] (2025-10)
- **Problem:** video upscalers over-sharpen skin and edges → **Fix:** generate in 4K or 1080 and color correct (guest Salma) → **Why:** upscaling makes it artificial. [YT:haCwSYl99ks] (2026-08)
- **Problem:** Midjourney video extend will not reverse camera direction (Fast Hours) → **Fix:** keep the direction going or reverse a push-in with a pull-out; change pace low to high → **Why:** the clip already has momentum. [YT:6vMsfe-KgRI] (2025-10)

### Coaching and critique moves
- Study how domain experts direct before directing yourself — "I don't even know how to direct. But this guy obviously does." [YT:nROQ4LJ4GcQ]
- Steer at plan level and stay hands off while it runs — asks only "are we building assets for an entire recreation or just the brief?" [YT:nROQ4LJ4GcQ]
- Inversion for new angles — who is behind her, what is she looking at in front of her, how high do the buildings go? [YT:J5U49z2_EKw]
- When one take goes wrong, hunt the one word in the prompt before rewriting everything. [YT:gdPY4W72-N0]
- Reverse-engineer a tool's roadmap from its long-term vision (immersive 3D or 4D worlds) and list what that needs: consistency, dialogue, sound, camera control, editing. [YT:SbDupYyGXac]
- His stress test for image-to-video: human motion, camera movement, several moving background subjects, tattoos, mixed natural and artificial light. [YT:k8rciHNdlvs]
