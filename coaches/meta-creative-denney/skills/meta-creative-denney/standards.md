## T1. Concept batches (what to make next, net-new ideas, "give me 10 ads")

- ★ **Persona and message before format.** Each concept names who it targets (a specific persona, not "small business owners") and its big idea before the format is chosen. "Real strategy lies in the layer before format selection." [YT:yRgPbqywUJ8] [YT:5C5VhqW9HCc]
- ★ **Evidence behind every concept.** Each concept states its evidence: repetition in the account's top performers, in reviews/comments, or across recent assets. No concept justified only by "competitors run it". [YT:2jVcLu3Earg] [YT:p4yXXdnCgWg] [YT:Lhl4o7yyuDQ]
- ★ **Real diversity, not Level 1.** The batch spans ≥ 3 personas or messages and ≥ 2 awareness levels; concepts that differ only by format = Level 1 = FAIL. Test: would Meta read two of these as the same ad? [YT:ZydGeio1DGQ] [YT:Lhl4o7yyuDQ]
- **Top-of-funnel content present.** At least one concept is unaware/problem-aware (educational, how-to, curiosity). Her most common diagnosis is brands "bleeding their top of funnel". [YT:4wM-i1cxtAM] [YT:-E9sjb2msjo]
- **Format placed on the Aug 2026 tier list** as unicorn (S/A) or supporting cast (B), with the month. No F-tier formats (notes-app/UX, podcast, press) and no E-tier (text-only, listicle, carousel, testimonial static) without a stated reason. [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8]
- **A creator or founder slot.** At least one concept is creator/partnership or founder content (S tier). [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8] [YT:ToTQBWHm38I]
- **Ranked on her two axes:** quickest to make and most evidence to perform. Quick iterations ship in week 1–2. [YT:p4yXXdnCgWg] [YT:-E9sjb2msjo]
- **Exploit before invent.** If the account has spend history (her threshold: >$100K/month or $1M lifetime), at least one concept moves a recurring winning idea from the top 20 into a more emotional format (Framework 1). [YT:p4yXXdnCgWg]
- **~90/10 net-new vs iteration** across the batch. [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs] [YT:U7T3_-NnHHg]
- **Each concept tagged GEN/DTC and NOW/EVERGREEN/ALLTIME.** [canon: SKILL.md Rule 3, Rule 4] (not found in transcripts)

## T2. Hooks (hook sets, hook rewrites, fixing low hook rate)

- ★ **Full hook package, not a line.** Each hook specifies on-screen text, what is heard, frame zero, and the vibe. A hook given as a script line only = FAIL. "Your hook is not just the first line of a script." [YT:I8tXqqfjIX4] [YT:t-Xf12o4jt4]
- ★ **Big swings.** A hook test holds 3–6 variants that differ in hook type (demographic, "If you", trigger word, investment, how-to…), not one-word swaps. To "only change one word" she answers "absolutely not". [YT:I8tXqqfjIX4] [YT:7knQyPYLmfo]
- ★ **The hook does the targeting.** Each hook makes clear who it stops; a hook that would stop everyone (notes-app/UX-style pattern interrupts) fails — "good at stopping everyone but not the right people". [YT:Y2zEga0aptc] [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8] [YT:wfENnO7PA5Q]
- **At least one demographic / job-title or "If you" call-out**, with frame zero visually matching the called-out identity. [YT:wfENnO7PA5Q] [YT:I8tXqqfjIX4]
- **Investment hook sourced from real failed attempts** (reviews, calls), not invented. [YT:t-Xf12o4jt4]
- **How-to hooks matched to stage:** "How to know if" (problem-aware), "How do I pick" (choosing), "How to start" (top or most-aware; named for service businesses). [YT:I8tXqqfjIX4]
- **Trigger-word pass applied** to hooks built on a structure already converting. [YT:wfENnO7PA5Q] [YT:I8tXqqfjIX4]
- **Hook-rate diagnosis uses her pairs:** low hook + low hold = back to the drawing board; high hook/hold + low CTR = wrong audience for that creative; thumbnail checked. [YT:yNTlVKvuX0o] [YT:CdK0jdqxXOs]
- **Benchmarks quoted with date:** hook rate target 35–40%, hold ~10% (2025-01); hook rate = 3-sec views / impressions. [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs] [YT:rNsOaXtlmRM]

## T3. Statics (static briefs, static critiques, headline tests)

- ★ **One job, one persona.** The static has one goal (offer, education, problem-aware) and one named persona. "Don't try to do two jobs in one ad." [YT:5C5VhqW9HCc]
- ★ **Headline first in the hierarchy.** Headline → product/key visual → supporting elements; the focal point is the message. State first and second read. [YT:5C5VhqW9HCc]
- ★ **Passes the 1-second test.** A stranger knows what is sold/said in one second; paragraphs of small text, no focal point = scrap. "clarity is always going to out-beat creativity". [YT:5C5VhqW9HCc]
- **Awareness level named** as the bridge between persona, goal and format. [YT:5C5VhqW9HCc]
- **Headline lifted from evidence:** a messaging point from a proven winner (Framework 1) or a golden-nugget testimonial verbatim from reviews/comments. [YT:p4yXXdnCgWg] [YT:8r1im4divXQ]
- **Format from her always-test list** (infographic, headliner, benefit call-out, us-vs-them, ugly/Post-it, testimonial headline) with tier stated. [YT:5C5VhqW9HCc] [YT:7knQyPYLmfo] [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8]
- **Us-vs-them built in 4 steps:** us on the left, 3–4 evidence-backed benefits, each with a factual competitor weakness from their reviews, color cues. [YT:NsHk8o3jVvM]
- **Critiques name the wrong-word zoom:** if the design emphasizes the wrong words, the fix moves text placement/hierarchy. [YT:2jVcLu3Earg]
- **Not treated as retargeting-only or a test-before-video placeholder.** [YT:5C5VhqW9HCc]
- **Critique of a draft names "too much going on / too much text"** with what to cut, not "make it pop". [YT:5C5VhqW9HCc]

## T4. Creator, founder and partnership briefs or scripts

- ★ **Creator chosen before script, matched to persona aspiration.** The brief names the creator profile: who the persona feels aspirational toward (not a strict age match), with delivery and vocal variety. "nine times out of 10, it actually comes down to the creator". [YT:4wM-i1cxtAM] [YT:_VtpfVeB2Kc]
- ★ **Questions, not lines, for unscripted creators and founders.** Prompts the creator answers; founder high-production shoots = 30–60 min interview, no script. Small/UGC creators can get tighter scripts. [YT:qoOBIPWH_qw] [YT:ToTQBWHm38I] [YT:_VtpfVeB2Kc]

## T5. Teardowns (why did this win/lose, draft critique, competitor ad)

- ★ **Primary metrics call the verdict.** Spend, results, cost per result (leads/CPL) decide; hook rate, hold rate, CTR only explain why. Calling a winner on CTR = FAIL. [YT:Lhl4o7yyuDQ] [YT:CCsty8R0UaA]
- ★ **No winner on thin spend.** Spend volume stated before any verdict; low reach = give it time. [YT:CdK0jdqxXOs] [YT:7knQyPYLmfo]
- ★ **Four-step analysis:** metrics, content (message, imagery, talent), comparison (vs top performers and competitors), feedback (comments). Metrics alone = "not a real analysis". [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs] [YT:3zZiKZUJmms]
- **Big idea in one line** and **who the creative actually targets** (age breakdown checked; creator–message misalignment flagged). [YT:pMAxIOL8dtE] [YT:Lhl4o7yyuDQ]
- **First three seconds read by layer** (text, sound, frame zero, vibe) and the thumbnail checked. [YT:t-Xf12o4jt4] [YT:CdK0jdqxXOs]
- **Format placed on the tier list** with date. [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8]
- **Context before kill:** an ad eating 50–75% of spend is not cut blindly; a test ad at ~$1K that isn't working is. [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs]
- **Fatigue read as awareness churn**, fix = push up-funnel, new hooks, new formats, new face. [YT:ZydGeio1DGQ] [YT:lWpSmxuwSts]
- **Ends with one variable to test next.**

## Universal checks (every output)

- ★ **Retrieved before writing.** At least 3 searches run; the work uses at least 3 retrieved items or passages, cited as [YT:id] with month.
- ★ **No invented quotes, numbers, results or dates.** Every quoted line exists verbatim in a retrieved hit; every result is labeled creator-reported, DTC or lead gen; anything built is labeled *built from the canon, untested*.
- ★ **Did the work.** If Ivan gave a task, the output is the finished thing (concepts, hook set, brief, teardown, diagnosis, test plan), not a list of questions. Questions only for inputs that are missing and block the work, and only after doing everything possible without them; then ask the one unknown that changes the answer most.
- **In voice.** Candid and fast, flags candor before a caveat, names the lazy pattern then rebuilds it, one everyday analogy per idea, busts a myth by saying what it is not then what it is; signature lines sparingly and verbatim. No praise for Ivan, no fluff.
- **Ends with one move.** One test to run next, what it tests, the control, and the kill/scale rule on primary metrics (leads/CPL).
