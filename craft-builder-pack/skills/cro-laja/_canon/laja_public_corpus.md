## RECOMMENDED PURCHASES

Not "buy these because Peep gets a cut." Buy these because they
are the canonical paid resources that compound on the free corpus
above. Order roughly by ROI for a CRO-curious operator.

### Tier 1 — Buy first

**Wynter — B2B message testing subscription**
- Pricing page: https://wynter.com/pricing
- Tests start around $199 per test on pay-as-you-go.
- Subscription gets tests at ~50% the PAYG price.
- "Wynter Black" managed-research tier exists for enterprise
  (custom price; up to ~24 fully managed projects per year).
- ROI: a single message test that catches a bad hero before the
  page ships saves the cost of a quarter of paid media. Pays for
  itself on the first use for most operators running paid traffic.

**CXL Institute — Conversion Optimization Minidegree**
- Course page: https://cxl.com/institute/programs/conversion-optimization/
- Standalone price: ~$999 lifetime (was $1,499 historically).
- All-Access subscription alternative:
  - Monthly ~$404
  - Quarterly ~$739 (~$247/mo)
  - Annual options also available
- ~90 hours, 20 courses, 4 tracks. Includes Conversion Research,
  Landing Page Optimization, A/B Testing, Persuasion Psychology.
- Mini-degrees page: https://cxl.com/institute/minidegrees/
- ROI: this is the canonical body of CRO instruction. One internal
  hire who completes it is worth more than three who haven't.

### Tier 2 — Buy when scaling

**CXL Institute — All-Access subscription**
- https://cxl.com/institute/minidegrees/
- 120+ courses across CRO, growth marketing, technical marketing,
  digital analytics, paid acquisition. Pricing as above.
- Team plans available — most useful for agencies and in-house
  teams of 5+ marketers.
- ROI: the cheapest way to lift the average literacy of a marketing
  team from "tactical" to "T-shaped."

### Tier 3 — Buy when ready to operationalize

**Speero engagement** — https://speero.com/
- Custom-scoped CRO programs. Audits, experimentation programs,
  research-as-a-service. Pricing on application; programs are
  typically $10k–$50k+ per month depending on scope.
- ROI: appropriate when in-house CRO capability is missing and a
  research-based program is needed at scale. Not for early stage.

### Tier 4 — Optional reading

**Books (not by Peep, but the ones he most often references)**
- "Obviously Awesome" by April Dunford — referenced on How to Win
  positioning episodes. Direct read for positioning.
- "How Brands Grow" by Byron Sharp — mental availability,
  category-entry points. Peep cites this lineage often.
- "Influence" by Robert Cialdini — persuasion principles that
  underpin CXL's Persuasion Psychology course.
