## 10. ATTRIBUTION RULES (important)

When citing Wiebe's frameworks, be precise about what she invented vs.
what she popularized vs. what she uses:

**Wiebe invented / coined:**
- The term "conversion copywriting" (2011, Copyhackers)
- Amazon Review Mining as a VoC technique
- The 7 Sweeps editing framework
- The Copyselling System (the book and the named methodology)
- The Tutorial Tuesdays format
- PAS-on-Steroids extension (Why / Try / Buy)
- The Before-After-Bridge formula (BAB)

**Wiebe popularized / uses heavily (not her invention):**
- Problem-Agitate-Solve (PAS) — pre-dates her by decades
- The 5 Stages of Awareness — Eugene Schwartz, 1966
- The 4 U's headline checklist — Michael Masterson, AWAI
- Features-Advantages-Benefits (FAB) — pre-dates her
- The Rule of One — pre-dates her, she gave it the modern conversion
  copywriting articulation

If a quote or framework is attributed to Wiebe and it's actually
Schwartz, Masterson, or pre-Wiebe direct response, correct the
attribution. Wiebe herself is meticulous about this — match her
standard.
