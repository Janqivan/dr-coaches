## 2. Frameworks and methods

### 2.1 The format tier list (S to F), August 2026
When she uses it: to rank formats by whether they become "unicorn scaling ads" (S = "god-like" formats that almost always become unicorns; F = don't waste budget). On screen: a black board with coloured rows S (pink) to F (violet), each format on a yellow sticky card, and a "UNICORN" label over the S row. She admits "Formats go in and out of style" and that she once recommended some of the formats she now ranks low. [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8] (2026-08)
- **S:** partnership ads (with the right creator; ideally test their content first) · founder content (high-production or lo-fi).
- **A:** amateur investigation ads (a creator investigates your product or niche, e.g. visiting car dealerships; her team popularised it and it scales well) · authority ads (doctor, dermatologist; for hyper-competitive niches like supplements and beauty; gets people over objections and avoids "rinsing and reusing the same UGC playbook" [quote src: YT:KmbEjy0UBT8]) · grid statics (low-hanging fruit for multi-SKU/bundles; became the top unicorn for a handful of brands) · catalog/DPA (if you build a good catalog system).
- **B (supporting cast):** TikTok love letter · TikTok viral short ("the real short" [quote src: YT:KmbEjy0UBT8] as captioned) · employee-generated content (EGC) · before/after · progression · comment-reply ads · callout ads · headline ads (if the messaging is amazing) · us vs them · "hot girl" Instagram stories · creator lo-fi statics · founder's letter static · educational infographics · mood-board ads (apparel) · tweet/Reddit as a video's first frame (~B).
- **C:** celebrity ads ("a massive unlock" [quote src: YT:KmbEjy0UBT8] or "a big money pit" [quote src: YT:KmbEjy0UBT8]) · statistics ads (fine for luxury/retail brands and awareness/traffic objectives; unlikely to reach the top eight for DTC-only ROI) · AI animation ads · AI avatar ads (board only, not discussed in the items).
- **D:** breaking-news ads · AI billboard ads.
- **E:** text-only ads (her first negative ranking) · listicles · carousels · testimonial statics ("E. F for the way that most of you are making them" [quote src: YT:KmbEjy0UBT8]).
- **F:** podcast ads (unless the founder is on a real, well-known podcast) · notes-app and UX ads · press ads (unless you pay for the rights).
- Unranked: ugly/handwriting Post-it ads still "perform really well" [quote src: YT:KmbEjy0UBT8] in sale periods; she always rotates some in.

### 2.2 Static ad framework: Layer 1 strategy, Layer 2 design (2026)
When: before writing any static. [YT:5C5VhqW9HCc] (2026-06)
1. **One goal.** An offer, education, or reaching the problem-aware: "Don't try to do two jobs in one ad." [quote src: YT:5C5VhqW9HCc] Trying to do too much and speaking to too many people is the most common static mistake.
2. **One persona.** Not "small business owners" [quote src: YT:U7T3_-NnHHg], but which type, at what stage, with what objection. The persona drives vocabulary, the proof required and the objections to answer. Retargeting statics can span personas; top-of-funnel statics need a conscious choice.
3. **The format.** It decides what copy survives, what the image must carry and which copy frameworks are viable.
4. **One awareness level**, "the bridge" connecting persona and goal to the copy framework.
5. **Production level.** Choose one of three buckets: graphic-style, hi-fi production, or lo-fi creator content. Brands forget lo-fi creator statics, which she sees "moving the needle right now" [quote src: YT:5C5VhqW9HCc].
6. **Design hierarchy.** Headline first (it does the targeting), then product or key visual, then supporting elements. Nine times out of ten the focal point is the headline or the visual, and she mostly goes for text. Sometimes the image stops the scroll and the eye moves up to the copy.
7. **1-second comprehension test.** If a stranger can't tell what you're selling or saying, kill it. "clarity is always going to out-beat creativity" [quote src: YT:5C5VhqW9HCc].
8. **Less is more.** When she critiques AI statics she flags "too much going on" [quote src: YT:9YKsHMJXiIQ], "a little too much text" [quote src: YT:5C5VhqW9HCc], "a little basic for me" [quote src: YT:5C5VhqW9HCc].
Her example: Dr. Squatch statics generated and then edited (§4).

### 2.3 Core static formats to always test (2026 list)
[YT:5C5VhqW9HCc] (2026-06)
- **Educational infographic.** Strong top of funnel; it "masquerades" as organic. Build something any persona benefits from via education, entertainment or narrowing choices.
- **Headliner ad.** The "sexy messaging" is the focal point; pair it with taboo angles. An Ogilvy-era print format: "What is that big headline that you can put front and center?" [quote src: YT:5C5VhqW9HCc]
- **Benefit call-outs** (formerly "features call-outs" [quote src: YT:U7T3_-NnHHg]). Test the top headline as a core desire or a golden-nugget testimonial.
- **Comparison / us vs them.** "Still cranking".
- **Transformation.** The biggest gap and the biggest opportunity.
- **Grid static.** An OG print format, always on deck for apparel, multi-SKU and sale periods.
- **Text-only / text-driven.** "a bit of a hot take" [quote src: YT:5C5VhqW9HCc], borrowed from organic. Founder letters do "really, really well" [quote src: YT:KmbEjy0UBT8], though clients get nervous.
Earlier list (2025, lean stage), static types to test first: benefit call-out, us vs them, headline, ugly-ad statics, testimonial statics. [YT:7knQyPYLmfo] (2025-03)

### 2.4 Us vs them in 4 steps
[YT:NsHk8o3jVvM] (2025-06), with supporting rules from [YT:MiurhGwirfY] (2023-03), [YT:8r1im4divXQ] (2024-06) and [YT:hSy6X065x0g] (2024-10)
1. Make a clear visual comparison with clear us-vs-them language. Put "us" on the LEFT for Western, left-to-right readers so your brand is seen first and "them" on the right (testable).
2. List 3–4 specific differentiating benefits that solve real pain points, prioritising those with the most evidence or customer feedback.
3. Give each benefit a matching competitor weakness, found by mining competitor reviews for recurring complaints. Keep it factual, never exaggerated or false.
4. Reinforce with visual cues: emojis and colour psychology (green = good, red = bad).
- You don't need to name a competitor; talk about the market or the problem in general. Naming a competitor or showing its logo: "jury's out". It's not illegal, but "is it the right thing to do?" [quote src: YT:NsHk8o3jVvM] She has done it only in very specific cases. The safer option is a generalised "them".
- Why it works: the viewer is already sold on the solution, so you only position why you're better. That makes it often the first winner for new brands/pixels. It works as an image, a GIF or a short video.
- Variant further up-funnel: "old me / new way" [quote src: YT:eFyvp0mu7UQ] (Kind Patches pills vs patches; Moonbrew "Old Me / New Me" [quote src: YT:fawsUmNeCkg]).
- Some people (only women, she notes) told her they felt uncomfortable talking negatively about the competition; she thinks negativity/comparison is very important to test.

### 2.5 Headline messaging test ("Framework 1", the $2M+ static)
When: you have a static (or account) and want a scalable headline. [YT:p4yXXdnCgWg] (2026-08) [YT:zDHkSSsjFDw] (2025-02) [YT:_ZHS6tW3f_Q] (2025-01)
1. Keep the visual. The original had run for months with the offer-led "$12 each, save 64%" [quote src: YT:zDHkSSsjFDw].
2. Extract messaging points from the account's other top-performing creatives, including the audio of UGC.
3. Put each one on the ad as the headline (variants A–E).
4. Look for the big idea, not the format. In a GRWM ad the creator said "I literally have a meeting starting in 3 minutes" [quote src: YT:p4yXXdnCgWg] / "I need to look like I didn't just get out of bed" [quote src: YT:p4yXXdnCgWg]. The idea wasn't GRWM ("no one cares about that" [quote src: YT:bd9MGQ0-Vqc]) but doing it in 5 minutes, so the headline became "5-minute makeup routine" [quote src: YT:p4yXXdnCgWg].
5. Result: winner D was the top performer for several months, spent well over $2M and generated over $6M in revenue. In a related test slide, age/gender- and platform-specific variants lost to the broader 10 minute exercise routine message.
- 2026 mirror image: Everyday Dose took the hook and text overlay from two top-performing videos and put them on a static, which became the account's top performer. "One of the best ways to further develop a creative ecosystem." [quote src: YT:p4yXXdnCgWg] [YT:-E9sjb2msjo] (2026-06)
- 2025 AI variant: Laura Geller used AI to write 7 new headlines for a year-old top performer; "your wrinkles will thank you for this" won, which surprised her. [YT:ITM4faRVzF8] (2025-05)

### 2.6 Creative diversity levels (format is Level 1)
[YT:ZydGeio1DGQ] (2025-12)
- **Level 0:** running only statics or only video. Starting with statics is fine, but you must move into video. If your idea of diversity stops at "I have both statics and videos" [quote src: YT:ZydGeio1DGQ], you're still at Level 0.
- **Level 1, format expansion:** the easiest way to change visuals. Her agency keeps a master list of every format it uses for fast changes. Many brands stop here; few unicorns come from format alone.
- **Level 5, true diversity:** excelling at every level across a suite of products and personas, or a brand-owned organic content strategy (Apothekary documentaries, Clue's episodic content). Celebrity-heavy brands inch toward it. It is also a mindset: content strategy at the heart of the business.

### 2.7 David and Goliath ad (2026)
Evolved from her earlier "controversy ads". The agency tests it on "every single brand that gets in the door". [YT:ePcEcT2PH8E] (2026-04)
1. The hook calls out an enemy (a specific industry, a massive scam), ideally with mass shock value.
2. Contrast it with you: the founders directly ("definitely something to test" [quote src: YT:R1HQW38EcRA]) or the brand as a whole.
3. Go straight into proof: core differentiators, why you do it differently, how that benefits the customer.
- Variant: go educational before introducing the brand. She has seen both versions win. The underdog tale: "Americans love that" [quote src: YT:ePcEcT2PH8E].

### 2.8 TikTok love letter and TikTok viral short (2026)
[YT:ePcEcT2PH8E] (2026-04) [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8] (2026-08)
- **Love letter:** long text over a short video. Very low lift, but you must nail the messaging. Top performers call out the audience immediately ("Listen, girls", "guys", "hot girls"). The agency mines comments on viral TikToks for exact vernacular. Creator content or iPhone B-roll works best as the visual.
- **Viral short:** one or two lines of text at most, often the start of a POV. Find the messaging by checking your own Reels feed and borrowing what's coming up.
- She sees both "dominating on Reels" [quote src: YT:ePcEcT2PH8E]; every video she was served the night before was one of the two. Both are B tier: quick hits if you know the persona, top-of-funnel "supporting characters" that build the ecosystem for a new concept or persona, rarely the unicorn.

### 2.9 Founder ad formats (2025)
Try them in this order. [YT:ToTQBWHm38I] (2025-06), plus [YT:GbXeAYTXhP4] (2026-01) and [YT:eFyvp0mu7UQ] (2026-05)
1. Founder problem-solution / VSL style, 35 s to 2–3 min.
2. Objection handler (the easiest): the founder answers the top FAQs and reasons not to buy.
3. Product demo.
4. Pure education (listicle or whiteboard), after the first three.
5. High-production founder shoot.
6. Sales announcements (BFCM, launches) from the founder's point of view.
- 2026 advice: founders should "do personal history angles so that they can clearly share the why" [quote src: YT:GbXeAYTXhP4]. "There's only certain jobs that the founders can do" [quote src: YT:GbXeAYTXhP4] and "a lot of that involves ad creative now." Exits: Osea moved from founder content to an internal content creator; Apothekary's founder now appears mainly for launches and retail expansion; Cheers Health added employees (the head of growth).
- Statics: the founder slideshow is "a must test" [quote src: YT:8r1im4divXQ], and the founder's letter static cranks in sale periods (B tier).

### 2.12 Ugly / lo-fi ads
[YT:_ZHS6tW3f_Q] (2025-01) [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs] (2025-01) [YT:NsHk8o3jVvM] (2025-06) [YT:pMAxIOL8dtE] (2023-12) [YT:Y2zEga0aptc] (2024-10)
- **Types:** the Post-it note ad (still crushes); one-shot, one-take videos (phone, a few seconds to 30 s, "just Yap"); POV shots showing the product in action (not necessarily an unboxing); nostalgia graphics. The test: how do you communicate as authentically as possible?
- **Team exercises** ("Only the serious ones" [quote src: YT:_ZHS6tW3f_Q] will do them): (1) if you had to make an ad in 5 minutes, what would it look like? (2) call your mom, partner or best friend and sell them a product or service you loved, then notice what you emphasise about the experience and the benefit.
- **Post-it messaging:** the magic is in the message. To reach net new audiences, talk about and agitate the problem; the more you agitate, the more you find your core customers. An offer on the Post-it "absolutely works really well" [quote src: YT:NsHk8o3jVvM], but not for a new brand, because people aren't drawn to a dollar offer before they know what the product does.
- **Post-it layout:** a single Post-it (or two with more text) works, as do Post-its used as captions in a video. 3+ notes with value props checked or X'd out has never worked for her. Checkmark bullet-point Post-its (e.g. Noteworthy) ask for too much reading. (guest: Barry Hott counters that long Post-it stories can work if the emotion is right, just harder.)
- **Script reuse:** take the best-performing UGC script, write it across several Post-it notes and add a voiceover.
- **Luxury / high AOV:** ugly = production value; keep an iPhone shooter behind the scenes on studio/TV shoots; maybe not a Post-it: "we have a narrow view of like what an ugly ad is" [quote src: YT:wRA2VgYIDVs].
- **Lineage:** she claims credit for inspiring Barry Hott's Post-it ads, taking inspiration from a 2020 Cove ad; Ash from Obvi also popularised the format.

### 2.13 Golden-nugget testimonial
[YT:2jDQ3aPRX8U] (2024-01) [YT:8r1im4divXQ] (2024-06) [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8] (2026-08)
Criteria:
1. Memorable: incredibly specific or humorous, often with data or numbers.
2. Often mentions age.
3. Often taboo; something only one person could say. The ones that stick with you, even if not 5 stars.
- Source them from site reviews and ad comments. Use them as headlines: they're hard to fake and specific.
- The lift comes from finding the right testimonial, not from the design.
- Big brands use headline ads to test angles cheaply, then turn the winners into creator content.
- Without a golden nugget, a testimonial static is E/F tier; it has been "a hot minute" [quote src: YT:KmbEjy0UBT8] since a single-testimonial ad did anything noteworthy for her.

### 2.14 Statistic ad
Buying is emotional; a stat gives the brain a logical reason to say yes. Put one stat front and center, tied to the result (X people got this result using this product) so it works like a before/after; generalized problem stats (X people have this problem) underperform. C tier for DTC in 2026. [YT:MiurhGwirfY] (2023-03) [YT:8r1im4divXQ] (2024-06) [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8] (2026-08)

### 2.15 Press screenshot, then its replacements
[YT:MiurhGwirfY] (2023-03) [YT:2jDQ3aPRX8U] (2024-01) [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8] (2026-08)
- **2023 method:** make it look like press in a major publication; it works best with big names (Forbes, Cosmopolitan, Vogue). Rebuild it in Figma instead of screenshotting, highlight what matters most to users, maybe add a product image, and test headlines. Publications rarely ask for removal (9 times out of 10 they don't); it has happened a few times, especially with Forbes.
- **Why it worked:** press is the highest form of social proof, especially for people 50+ who trust news and publications; it adds a check mark of legitimacy.
- **Alternatives:** a magazine-cover-style ad (Mint & Bow); making your own brand look like a publication (Obvi); a WorldStar Hip Hop-style format gives the same scroll stop without a press name; the advertorial headline with a green-screened creator.
- **Status:** retired, now F tier (§6). Breaking-news ads replaced it and are D tier.

### 2.16 Carousels (2024 method, 2026 verdict E tier)

### 2.21 Partnership-ad operating rules

- **UGC:** early 2024 she saw much less performance from UGC and more from education, trust and social proof. By 2025–26 creator content is "the format that unlocks performance and scale". [YT:2jDQ3aPRX8U] [YT:_VtpfVeB2Kc]
- **Carousels:** "uptick" in Jan 2024 (few brands use them). E tier, "more of a waste of time" in 2026. [YT:2jDQ3aPRX8U] [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8]
- **Press:** a 2023 core test, about to be retired in Jan 2024 (cease-and-desists), F tier in 2026. Its replacement, breaking news, is D tier. [YT:MiurhGwirfY] [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8]
- **Listicles:** a successful 2024 static (3 reasons why). In 2026 the listicle is still the fallback that "saves our ass" (April), yet the listicle static is E tier, "has not done jack lately" (August). [YT:2jDQ3aPRX8U] [YT:ePcEcT2PH8E] [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8]
- **Text-only:** "New for 2024", surprisingly strong for B2B/info/services. In Jan 2025 text-heavy ads "annihilated" in BFCM. In June 2026 it's a "hot take" core format; in August 2026, E tier unless for a sale. [YT:8r1im4divXQ] [YT:_ZHS6tW3f_Q] [YT:5C5VhqW9HCc] [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8]
- **Statistic ads:** a 2023–24 favourite she showed "ad nauseam", C tier for DTC in 2026. [YT:MiurhGwirfY] [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8]
- **Testimonials:** a 2024–25 first test, E/F tier "the way that most of you" make them in 2026. Golden nuggets are the constant exception. [YT:7knQyPYLmfo] [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8]
- **Podcast ads:** worked in the last ~18 months, F tier in 2026. [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8]
- **Ugly/Post-it:** "still crushes" in Jan 2025; overdone and sale-period-only by 2026; the lo-fi creator static is the live version. [YT:_ZHS6tW3f_Q] [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8] [YT:5C5VhqW9HCc]
- **Controversy ads** evolved into David and Goliath (2026). [YT:ePcEcT2PH8E]
- **Apology ads:** she bet against them and was proved wrong; she still hates them. [YT:ePcEcT2PH8E]
- **EGC:** 2025 "new" favourite, 2026 B tier (operational drag). [YT:_ZHS6tW3f_Q] [YT:KmbEjy0UBT8]
- **Tension:** the classic statics are "always test" and "still cranking" (June 2026) yet B tier supporting cast (August 2026). These are compatible: test them, but don't expect the unicorn.
