## 2. Converts in general — 12 principles (in 2025/older evidence AND restated in 2026)
1. **Meta is a creative game.** With targeting handed to Meta's ML, offer + creative are the edge (2023); "Facebook ads is now a creative game." (2025); bottleneck = enough good ideas to feed the machine (2026). [YT:KgO_J6pwv9I] (2023-09), [YT:M2VReAjHiN8] (2025-10), [YT:TpZrPY6jTE8] (2026-07) → §1.1
2. **Offer first.** "Single most important thing" (2019) → still first in the 2026 testing order. [YT:WeQrKeAwZDg] (2019-07), [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M] (2026-03) → §1.2
3. **Proof proof proof.** Proof in nearly every ad since at least 2024; 2026 marketing for his agency is ROAS-proof-led. [YT:z3LiJ0PbY-0] (2024-10), [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M] (2026-03) → §1.8
4. **The hook is the ad for most people.** 90–95%+ never pass ~3 seconds; better hook rate usually tracks better ROAS/CPA. [YT:GPp8Y-lKzJw] (2025-02), [YT:bAo6SICFyIE] (2026-03) → §1.4
5. **Run a format mix.** "Image people" vs "video people"; multiple formats per ad set = more conversions (~16% per FB rep, up to 50% his tests) and slower fatigue. [YT:HzDJpKu7vew] (2020-07), [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M] (2026-03) → §1.7
6. **One angle per ad.** Mixing angles hides which reason got the response. [YT:E0lbvr71wR8] (2020-07), [YT:KgO_J6pwv9I] (2023-09), [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M] (2026-03) → §1.14
7. **Judge on cost per result over conversion volume; don't panic.** "It's not time, it's conversion volume that you need to look out for." Don't pause winners to test; never edit live ads, duplicate. [YT:dwUxmlgWfxQ] (2021-03), [YT:D6Sxk8Mbkq4] (2025-05), [YT:wwdvrZBjock] (2026-07) → §1.11
8. **Testing is venture capital.** Most bets fail and drag blended ROAS; one UGC winner at 5.7 pays for failures "100 times over"; 6–12 months of persistence beats "90 plus percent" of competitors. [YT:E0lbvr71wR8] (2020-07), [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M] (2026-03) → §1.12
9. **Video has to be good to count.** [YT:GPp8Y-lKzJw] (2025-02), [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M] (2026-03) → §1.9
10. **Real people beat polish.** Creators/UGC feel like a recommendation from someone like you; niche faces stop the scroll. [YT:D6Sxk8Mbkq4] (2025-05), [YT:IRyR9PzSnM8] (2025-09), [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M] (2026-03) → §1.5, §5
11. **Iterate from winners.** 2021: duplicate a winner, ask why it worked, build lookalike ads (3–6 months cut cost per purchase 80%); 2025–26: AI variations of past winners are the low-hanging fruit. [YT:dwUxmlgWfxQ] (2021-03), [YT:_MIWfyD91yc] (2025-11), [YT:TpZrPY6jTE8] (2026-07) → F6, §1.16
12. **Let automation run, but be the filter.** "You always need to be the filter" — enhancements mostly on since 2023, AI variants filtered in 2026. [YT:KgO_J6pwv9I] (2023-09), [YT:M2VReAjHiN8] (2025-10), [YT:mMKLLRTk-7c] (2026-03) → §1.15

Strong but *not* restated in 2026 (treat as EVERGREEN/ALLTIME, not confirmed current): "the complexity of the ad format should match the size of the ask" [quote src: YT:GPp8Y-lKzJw] (latest 2025-02); headlines state the offer plainly, no clever curiosity (2021–2023); call-out openers (2019–2023). → §1.6, §1.13, F13

## 4. Hooks and watch time
**What the evidence backs most**
- **Hook = opening scene + opening line, first ~3 seconds — "by far the most important part".** 90–95%+ drop before it, so a new hook is effectively a new ad to most people. Make 4–5+, ideally ~10, per body. [YT:GPp8Y-lKzJw] (2025-02), [YT:IRyR9PzSnM8] (2025-09) → §1.4
- **Visual change is the variant.** Location, background, clothing, camera, lighting, colour vs B&W. 2025-10 he still counted "one camera angle is one hook"; 2026-02 Andromeda sometimes merges near-identical ads, so hooks must look different and go through the testing tool. [YT:M2VReAjHiN8] (2025-10), [YT:onFwSud9C2Y] (2026-02) → §7
- **Measure it.** Hook rate = 3-second plays ÷ impressions; read against account average and against CPA. Benchmarks from his booked-call account: best just under 27%, worst 7–9%. One hook swap took a client from 8% to 33% (80 vs 330 three-second views per 1,000 impressions). [YT:bAo6SICFyIE] (2026-03), [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M] (2026-03) → §6
- **Watch time is secondary to cost per result.** Physio compilation: high hook rate, low average watch time, great ROAS — "who cares about average watch time". [YT:z3LiJ0PbY-0] (2024-10)

**Hook types with his wording**
- **Call-out question** — names the situation so the right scroller thinks "that's me": "Moving home or redecorating?", "Ever used a personal trainer?" (modified call-out aimed at people who have NOT), "Want to monetize your skills and expertise without having to go anywhere?". Location: "New Yorkers...", "Texans, check this out". - **Pattern-break visual** — "Stop looking at your ad account upside down": video starts upside down and flips while talking about transforming results (he has run one). [YT:M2VReAjHiN8] (2025-10)
- **Shock-value differentiator** — lead with what competitors don't offer: What no other mentorship offers: daily live access to $600M+ ad experts. [YT:M2VReAjHiN8] (2025-10)

## 5. What stopped working or changed (dated)
- **Video share (2019 → 2025):** 2019 "just use video all the time" [quote src: YT:WeQrKeAwZDg] called wrong; 2020 "i'm probably an image person" [quote src: YT:HzDJpKu7vew]; 2025 marked shift to video (~60% of polled advertisers' best format), predicted to hold, not reach 100%. Poll inconsistency: Feb-2023 poll read 53% video; the 2025 video cites 44% two years earlier. → canon §7
- **Structure:** 2020 multi-step funnels (lead magnet → webinar) and 2021 1 campaign × 4 targeting ad sets × 4 ads → 2025 testing + scaling, one broad ad set each → 2026 all formats in one ad set, Meta runs the funnel. [YT:E0lbvr71wR8], [YT:dwUxmlgWfxQ], [YT:IRyR9PzSnM8], [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M]
- **Automation trust:** 2021 dynamic creative showed no gain → 2023 enhancements mostly on → 2025 flexible ads "nearly always" win budget → 2026 most toggles on, filter AI variants. [YT:sxQwH4kjGW4], [YT:KgO_J6pwv9I], [YT:M36tVQD26TY], [YT:mMKLLRTk-7c]
- **Small variables demoted:** 2019–2023 weekly headline/primary-text rounds, five headlines → 2026-03 "I don't think it matters" [quote src: YT:8dJxOaZrK1M]. Unresolved: 2026-02 still says B&W vs colour gaps of 10–15% and text overlays matter. [YT:sxQwH4kjGW4], [YT:onFwSud9C2Y], [YT:8dJxOaZrK1M]
- **Hook variants:** a new line or camera angle counted as a hook (2025-10) → must look visibly different, Andromeda merges near-identical ads (2026-02).
- **Volume:** 2026-02 against 20+ completely different creatives per ad set vs 2026-07 (Arcads-sponsored) volume matters post-Andromeda. Unresolved.

### 7b. Proven in lead gen — transfer as-is
1. **Free, named, low-risk entry offer, stated in the headline.** Service providers should offer a free quote/consultation/strategy session; if you can't afford the time, "raise your price". Name the thing: "Free interior design consultation", not "free consultation" ("free consultation for what?"). He uses this to test hires. In a me-too market a strong free offer that demonstrates the service is the differentiation. → §2a, [YT:WeQrKeAwZDg] (2019-07), [YT:1EQrp8MJRek] (2021-01) ALLTIME
2. **Size the ask, then pick the format.** In-person meeting at premises (even free) = big ask → video; free Zoom call = smaller → simpler formats OK. His done-for-you agency: longer videos with proof — simple image maybe for warm retargeting but "to cold audiences no chance". His free webinar: mostly image plus some carousels, no long video. → §2a, F1, [YT:N3X8CuD_VQk] (2023-02), [YT:GPp8Y-lKzJw] (2025-02) EVERGREEN

### 7e. Lead-gen copy rules from the canon
- Headline states the offer plainly; curiosity belongs in email subjects and YouTube titles. → §1.13
- Low-risk intro offers need little copy — sign up, no obligation. → §2a
- Offer in the first ~3 lines above "See more"; CTA at end of primary text; description echoes CTA. → §4
- No testimonials in primary text (feels scammy) — they work inside the video. No fake urgency. → §4, §7
- Primary text pairs result + no-hassle: "We'll design a beautiful interior for your home and manage the project for you" [quote src: YT:1EQrp8MJRek]. → §4
- Build order: headline (states offer) → primary text (supports headline) → creative last, though users see them in reverse. → F11
