## ACCURACY NOTES (read before quoting)

1. **"7 Silent Killers of Success" is NOT canonical Rohn.** No trace on jimrohn.com or success.com; circulates via AI-era YouTube channels. The verified framework is the **Diseases of Attitude** — indifference, indecision, doubt, worry, over-caution, pessimism, complaining — confirmed verbatim in seminar transcript [YT:0sG31mTbHJU] and the essay "Facing the Enemies Within" [LAIL]. Teach the diseases, not the "silent killers." (SKILL.md framework #9 corrected accordingly, Aug 28 2026.)
2. **"Pain of discipline vs pain of regret"** — official canonical wording is "We must all suffer one of two **things**: the pain of discipline or the pain of regret." [S60]. The "ounces/tons" addendum is popular but unverified in any primary source this pull. See pain_discipline_regret.md.
3. Several signature lines are **Shoaff-origin, Rohn-carried** ("Don't wish it were easier…", "Work harder on yourself…"). Both attributions are official; cite Rohn with a Shoaff nod. See verbatim_quotes.md.
4. Bio: born Sept 17, 1930, Yakima, WA; raised on the family farm in Caldwell, **Idaho** ("Farm boy from Idaho, raised in obscurity" — his own words [YT:tSn5sEWEv_8]). Met Shoaff at 25 (1955, AbundaVita); millionaire by 31; broke at 33 by his own telling; first public seminar 1963; died Dec 5, 2009 (pulmonary fibrosis). [JR: /about, /about/legacy; Wikipedia corroboration]
