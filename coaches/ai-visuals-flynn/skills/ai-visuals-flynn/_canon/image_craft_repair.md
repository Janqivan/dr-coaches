## PART 3. Image craft and repair: getting stills that don't look AI-made

Labels: unlabeled = Rory Flynn; (Fast Hours) = podcast, speaker unclear; co-host Drew Brucker; guests named.

### Rules (ranked by how often they say it)
- **Chaos, weird and exp are for exploring and mining, not control.** Chaos default about 5, weird at 1-10 for specific jobs; go high only to find looks you would never prompt, personalization off ("mining SREFs"), then save the winner as sref, image reference or mood board. Exp: for abstract, short, open prompts. "if you're looking for control, these aren't the parameters". [YT:u7EI0Z-0ULE, YT:Mw5iwBFp9EE, YT:Ye6DOhTgsDc] (2024-10, 2025-10) · mentions 16
- **Keep stylize modest for photoreal people.** Rory lives below the default: 90-100 "is a great one for me", camera or film looks toward 0, flat/vector 20-50; illustrated styles at 700-800 start to look 3D. (Fast Hours) photoreal sweet spot about 50-250; above 250-300 skin loses detail, gains gloss, pushes into the face. [YT:EBSPo2XCml8, YT:u7EI0Z-0ULE, YT:_nT6fav4qFA, YT:26bjN6CwwJs] (2024-10, 2025-10) · mentions 15
- **Aspect ratio is the first decision and steers the whole result.** Pick it by what the shot must emphasize (subject scale vs wider environment); a 2:1 or 3:1 face gets only eyes and nose; 1:1 for true symmetry ("a square is inherently symmetrical"), then outpaint; his default moved from 5:4 to 3:4 and 2:3. (Fast Hours) run the idea in two or three ratios. Co-host Drew Brucker: 3:2 beats 16:9 for faces. [YT:t0dgrFHEcwM, YT:u7EI0Z-0ULE, YT:GzrYcMt62gA, YT:_nT6fav4qFA] (2024-10, 2025-10) · mentions 15
- **If text will not add or change a small element, build the element elsewhere and bring it back as a reference.** Neon lips, smoke color, rug, end table, Walkman, a furnished room: make or find the element, load it in the editor as image reference (plus style reference), highlight only that zone. Adding is the weak case; changing, replacing, deleting are easy. [YT:kR05Q2WxhCA, YT:k7UtYDkFGQs, YT:26bjN6CwwJs, YT:swamNhl1pIs] (2024-10, 2025-10) · mentions 14
- **Build outward from a tight shot, in steps.** Generate the close-up (3:2) for face sharpness, expand in the editor and edit the prompt each step; (Fast Hours) step 4:5 to 4:3 or 5:6, not straight to 16:9. Endorses Allar's point that starting close reveals the environment, but expansion adds randomness, usually "not a good random". [YT:u7EI0Z-0ULE, YT:_nT6fav4qFA, YT:kR05Q2WxhCA, YT:GzrYcMt62gA] (2024-10, 2025-10) · mentions 12
- **Repaint is the last step, at 80-90% done (Fast Hours).** Creative pass first (prompt, vary, pan, zoom out), then Repaint as cleanup. Select only the broken part (top half of wonky fingers; the whole hand if the shape is bad; eyes only, rerun the identical repaint two or more times); never leave a sliver unselected inside a fill; delete a removed item from the prompt. Rory: repaint only the face, not a box round the head, or it balloons. [YT:rLPZaPKupYM, YT:QLbjv3CbfIo, YT:wlsmAeMQNEM] (2024-10) · mentions 12
- **Realism is built from specific imperfection.** Push named flaws (scar by the eye, a pimple, "no makeup"), grain, uneven texture. His AI-spotting checklist (fix what it finds): symmetry (fold it in half), odd-shaped features, too-smooth skin, off eyes, jewelry physics, stray hair wisps, no noise. Own iPhone photos as a mood board: output "looks like a goddamn iPhone photo". Photoreal is where audiences notice AI most; other styles or deliberate imperfection go unnoticed. [YT:NmIcjfPNcsE, YT:zPIBzQRJVT8, YT:A4ZvuMXGQvY, YT:oKQd-ZumqCA] (2024-10 to 2026-08) · mentions 11
- **Test a new model by rerunning old prompts and probing parameter ranges first.** "I don't want to think. I just want to test." Day one: "Can I use stylize 800?"; each model needs new prompting; wait 3-4 weeks before judging; copy the seed to rerun a prompt on the new version; fan one prompt out to four or five models to see which reads the ask best. He judges models by dynamic range first (house style: high contrast). [YT:F6P2EAfAwjw, YT:Siclp1Y75Mc, YT:wlsmAeMQNEM, YT:N-tmQ_Can_o] (2024-10 to 2026-09) · mentions 9
- **Route by job.** Midjourney first for look, color and presentation imagery ("doesn't look like stock ever"), (Fast Hours) also environments and textures; Nano Banana or GPT for specific, on-brand work and edits. He comes back to Midjourney for weird, slightly uncomfortable, abstract output with realism baked in, and vibes there (random prompts, 17 srefs, smashed boards) before taking the best character into Nano Banana to make it specific. His best Midjourney images cannot be carried into other tools; on V8 prompt text overrides boards, so one stray "blue" turns the image blue. By 2025-10 Rory does not do text in Midjourney; 2026-02 theory: small text needs 4K. [YT:wlsmAeMQNEM, YT:X-Nm3NGvrTE, YT:5v9vcLfTFag, YT:AOuXCRzjsS0] (2024-10 to 2026-02) · mentions 8
- **Name the model's polished default and reject it with rules.** ChatGPT and Nano Banana default to a centered, symmetrical, camera-facing medium shot. Counter with "never looking at the camera", subject off-balance, cropped framing, controlled negative space; break polish with tilt, motion blur, chromatic aberration. Client images too perfect: have a tool give the prompt behind them, then add contrast, saturation, analog film look, harsh flash. [YT:AxV8TR3BdqI, YT:EaENLPTAt2E, YT:AOuXCRzjsS0] (n.d., 2025-12, 2026-02) · mentions 8
- **Photoshop is the scalpel, the Midjourney editor the Swiss Army knife; destination decides.** Editor for broad fixes (black bars on a 16:9); its edits keep Midjourney's aesthetic, which generative fill does not. Photoshop for pixel-level fixes (an earring floating off the ear). An image for himself can stay in the editor; client work or artwork goes to Photoshop, and client detail drift is retouched by hand. [YT:X-Nm3NGvrTE, YT:Mw5iwBFp9EE, YT:k8rciHNdlvs, YT:oKQd-ZumqCA] (2024-10 to 2026-08) · mentions 7

Midjourney's upscalers are his "biggest gripe" (soften, over-polish). Allar: over-upscaling wrecks detail. Co-host Drew Brucker: for UGC he prefers 1K-2K, 4K only for heavy text. - **Run Subtle and Creative upscale on the same image and pick.** Rory runs both on every image like a slot machine, leaning Creative. (Fast Hours) Subtle when a face is visible; Creative when there is no face or writing it can fix. Co-host Drew Brucker: Creative fixes mushy faces and words but rolls dice on details you liked. [YT:_nT6fav4qFA, YT:wlsmAeMQNEM, YT:A4ZvuMXGQvY, YT:t0dgrFHEcwM] (2024-10, 2025-10) · mentions 7

Nano Banana Pro erase/replace blurs and loses resolution; ChatGPT inpainting changes outside the highlight, "drives me nuts"; (Fast Hours) chained edits turn to pixelated noise. Rory: decision-tree mindset, if not this then this; lens fix (8mm too wide, try 85mm). Remix Strong with nothing changed beats a reroll; Remix Subtle "doesn't really do anything" now. [YT:t0dgrFHEcwM, YT:26bjN6CwwJs, YT:wlsmAeMQNEM, YT:Siclp1Y75Mc] (2024-10, 2025-10) · mentions 7

### Methods (named or ordered)
#### Keep / change edit brief
- When they use it: any edit-capable model (Nano Banana Pro, GPT, Seedream); one image or a thousand.
- Steps: 1. Write the keep/change formula (Recipes). 2. Get about 75% right, then change single variables instead of re-prompting (Fast Hours). 3. If a composite fails, swap which image is which. 4. Micro-edits: reference plus palette swatches plus one annotation list (Fast Hours). 5. Batch angle variants in one box separated by a star character.
- Their example: `keep this exact car, preserve the fine details in the environment, change to a front view`, then `perfect rear view`, `perfect 3/4 angle view`.
- Sources: [YT:_az4W2z5Fqw, YT:AxV8TR3BdqI, YT:zUe96tRUpDg, YT:RjGYZlVywd8] (n.d., 2025-12, 2026-02)
#### Build outward from a close-up (editor)
- When they use it: full body, wide scenes, banners, reel covers, any far shot that smooshes faces.
- Steps: 1. Generate a tight 3:2 close-up on a plain background. 2. In the editor scale the image inside the frame, drag it into place, change aspect ratio. 3. Edit the prompt each step: medium close-up (canvas to mid-chest), medium-full (waist or mid-thigh), full. 4. Small jumps only (custom zoom 1.2; 10% scale in five or six steps, since one jump gave a stray box and distorted hands). 5. Fix small faces with Magnific fractality 1-2 or Krea. 6. Banners: leave room for text (3:1); reel cover: 3:2 to 3:4, erase the bottom, re-prompt.
- Their example: `spiritual hero healer wearing a red dress` after erasing the bottom added dress and smoke.
- Sources: [YT:u7EI0Z-0ULE, YT:_nT6fav4qFA, YT:kR05Q2WxhCA] (2024-10)
#### Element-as-reference repair
- When they use it: a text edit will not land (small object, smoke color, neon lips, filling a room).
- Steps: 1. Highlight only the target zone (about half the image adds reliably; small zones fail more). 2. Make the element alone: generate it, find a realistic photo, or train a Krea model on about five images; screenshot just the relevant corner. 3. Load it as image reference plus style reference; image weight 1.5-2 (Rory), 0.5-1 (co-host Drew Brucker), lower if pasted-on; one weight per job. 4. Repeat zone by zone. 5. Still failing: restate it, run each edit twice, or (Fast Hours) build a reference from the same sref plus only the missing texture.
- Their example: orange smoke would not take from text; colored-smoke image plus style reference on the zone, then silky, puffy, wispy smoke, then neon lips.
- Sources: [YT:kR05Q2WxhCA, YT:26bjN6CwwJs, YT:k7UtYDkFGQs, YT:t0dgrFHEcwM] (2024-10, 2025-10)
#### Parameter ladder (his threshold test)
- When they use it: a new model or parameter; mining weird looks.
- Steps: 1. One prompt (broad and ambiguous, or specific); lock one or two components (composition, lighting, angle) if mining. 2. Step one parameter up a ladder (chaos 5 vs 90; weird 0/50/100/500/800/1500). 3. Personalization off. 4. Find where output leaves the prompt; save the exciting one as sref, image reference or mood board seed.
- Their example: `award-winning photo of an ancient warrior`, `--ar 4:5 --s 500`: close to the prompt at low weird, varies from about 100; chaos 5 + weird 10 gave a painted-face texture he liked.
- Sources: [YT:Mw5iwBFp9EE, YT:u7EI0Z-0ULE] (2024-10, 2025-10)

### Recipe library (exact, reusable)
- **Edit-model formula** — `keep; things I want to keep; change; things I want to change`. Example: `keep this exact product visible, preserve the fine details, change the background to whatever`; simplest `keep the bottle, change the background`. Nano Banana Pro: `the person in the exact environment, in the exact position, with the exact lighting`; `keep this product exactly as is and change whatever`. [YT:_az4W2z5Fqw, YT:zUe96tRUpDg] (n.d., 2026-02)
- **Re-angle without rebuilding** — Shark retouch of unused shoots: `drop the hero angle 30 degrees, push in closer, make the lens wider, remove the fence at the back`, then change the lighting. Nano Banana: `keep the same exact image`, then 18 mm wide angle and push in closer (field of view held); an exact overhead of an eye-level room kept correct dimensions. [YT:N-tmQ_Can_o, YT:2sY91XsiRuw] (2025-10, 2026-09)
- **Expand with an empty side** — 4:5 to 16:9, subject on the right: add `left side of the image is empty`; without it random things appear, with it "extremely reliable"; same keep-empty logic for video. Nano Banana told to switch to 16:9 does poorly: add white space either side, or upload an empty 16:9 frame and tell it to fill it (co-host Drew Brucker). [YT:hAnYA2wJaZ4, YT:2sY91XsiRuw] (2025-10)
- **Odd banner sizes** — type the exact pixel size as the ratio (600 by 200 becomes 3:1); you get the ratio, not the pixels; upscale after. [YT:u7EI0Z-0ULE] (2024-10)
- **Style reference built in layers in the editor** — load the style-weight-zero image, set 3:2, slide it over, expand, erase part of the black; add an sref at about 450 so the new area fills with color; erase more, scale out, paste a second sref at about 490, submit. [YT:34FQ8Ewa9ek] (2024-10)
- **Retexture over a composition (Fast Hours)** — upscale, open the full editor, remove any competing sref, add sref code plus style weight; one weight per run (100, 200, 500 with a neon-red sref: 100 enough, 200 past neon). Always pair with an sref or mood board. [YT:t0dgrFHEcwM, YT:Mw5iwBFp9EE] (2025-10)
- **Real skin in an edit model (Fast Hours)** — weak: `increase micro contrast skin texture and realism` (drifted warm and green); better: name what makes skin real (visible pores and the rest) plus `no airbrush skin no beauty retouching`; fix stacked-edit color drift with `decrease saturation and warmth by 50 percent`. [YT:qVWfoe0voIk] (2026-09)
- **Anti-model-face** — tokens: everyday plus a role, natural look, natural skin texture, blemishes; negatives `no makeup`, `no airbrushing`; stack a mood board of iPhone photos of non-model friends. Blank AI face: `visceral` plus a mechanical description (screaming with mouth open). [YT:26bjN6CwwJs, YT:A4ZvuMXGQvY, YT:JskVAZqt34w] (2025-10)
- **Unpolished snapshot / period look** — `Facebook photo from 2012` plus `shot on iPhone` plus `--weird 15`; period looks: documentary or amateur photography (not editorial or cinematic), grain, light leaks, decade and year, stylize about 70-75; promote the winner to sref, then board. [YT:u7EI0Z-0ULE, YT:26bjN6CwwJs] (2024-10, 2025-10)
- **Q2 for texture** — `--q 2` on texture-heavy prompts, every time for interiors (rugs, couches, throws) and outdoor nature: it separates fabrics and counters the 3D-rendered look; less on face close-ups, where it overdoes it. [YT:k7UtYDkFGQs, YT:u7EI0Z-0ULE] (2024-10, 2025-10)
- **Cooler grade for warm AI output** — temperature cooler, hue from green toward purple, plus texture, noise, a little darker, a vignette; he wonders if it is overcorrection from fatigue with "perfect". [YT:oKQd-ZumqCA] (2026-08)
- **Upscale settings** — lo-fi look: precision upscale with ultra detail, a sharpener, Smart Grain about 16. (Fast Hours) Precision V2 Sublime, creativity 0, `sublime sharpened 40%, smart grain 10%`; feed Magnific the non-upscaled Midjourney image, straight to 4x. [YT:SQIGjY_Rs2s, YT:_nT6fav4qFA, YT:qv9_8mJT7h4] (2024-10 to 2025-12)
- **Upscale and cleanup tricks** — dot edit: one dot on an image in the editor, resubmit, then upscale (V8 images now upscale directly, Fast Hours 2026-07). Blurred words after 4K: `crisp the letters` (Fast Hours). [YT:MJZjmv6HMKA, YT:FGJCBhAiQJk, YT:RjGYZlVywd8] (2025-12 to 2026-07)
- **Composite and brush fixes** — relight a stacked composite: flatten it so lighting becomes congruent, then plain text such as `make the light come from the left` (Weavy; speaker inferred). Text-only editors (Fast Hours): paint a white stroke and prompt `remove the white paint stroke`, or draw a box and say add it exactly there. ChatGPT removal by words alone mostly failed: mark the unwanted items in red in Figma and tell it to just remove those things. [YT:Xh5lD1LolTk, YT:SQIGjY_Rs2s, YT:ZY5GqIXkET4] (2025-10, 2025-12)
- **Composite by 3D positioning guide** — screenshot the real product, matte it to a grayscale 3D-render look, build a 3D asset in Trellis or Rodin, place it as the guide; Seedream and Nano Banana pasted the lid exactly there first shot, versus about 80 failed tries. [YT:PJ347ccVJBs] (n.d.)
- **Sketch as structure (Weavy, then Mystic)** — same prefix on every sketch prompt, scene appended: `storyboard sketch, cinematic composition, Stranger Things aesthetic, 90s nostalgic film tone, soft graphite shading with selective color wash, dramatic lighting, film grain`. Restyle in Mystic with the sketch as the structure image (settings he told viewers to screenshot: control image strength, adherence, HDR, resolution, realism model, creative detailing about 33) and `keep the image construction and characters, use a specific camera, color grade the contrast, the color scheme, the lighting`. Mystic nailed structure and changed the lighting; Reve too desaturated, Seedream not photoreal, Midjourney oversaturated. [YT:EaENLPTAt2E] (2025-12)
- **Imperfection as parameterized shaders** — the Figma agent builds custom shaders for what he did in Photoshop ("you can basically build Camera Raw in here"): Matrix overlay, VCR (compression artifacts, interlacing, pixelation, tracking bar), each an adjustable parameter; more for fun than function. Gen Effect node: describe the effect (prompt or image reference), get sliders such as block size, exposure, shadows (Game Boy Camera pixel look), on images and video; color-correct first, then template it with the references locked. [YT:oKQd-ZumqCA, YT:FGJCBhAiQJk, YT:N-tmQ_Can_o] (2026-07 to 2026-09)

### Fix library (problem → fix → why)

- **Problem:** smooshed face or hand in a far shot (16:9) → **Fix:** Creative upscaler (fixes minor errors, will not save a complex image); small face in a good image: Magnific fractality 1-2 (3 gets crazy) → **Why:** gives the face structure. [YT:wlsmAeMQNEM, YT:_nT6fav4qFA] (2024-10)

### Coaching and critique moves
