### 2a. Ben Heath (lead-gen lens)

### Heath — Call-out method and primary-text techniques
- When he uses it: targeting can't reach the audience (no "redecorating" or recently-moved targeting), or to filter location.
- Steps (five primary-text techniques): 1. call-out; 2. benefit-rich; 3. right tone; 4. CTA at the end; 5. shorter usually better.
- Call-out types: life moment when in-market ("Moving home or redecorating?" [quote src: YT:KgO_J6pwv9I]); location ("New Yorkers...", "Texans...", "West Londoners", "Bristolians") because Meta's default location now includes "people recently in" [quote src: YT:KgO_J6pwv9I], so non-residents ignore it and Meta learns from engagement; inverted call-out ("Ever used a personal trainer?" [quote src: YT:1EQrp8MJRek] aimed at people who have NOT, since past users are taken or burned).
- Sources: Heath [YT:KgO_J6pwv9I] (2023-09), [YT:1EQrp8MJRek] (2021-01), [YT:WeQrKeAwZDg] (2019-07)

### Heath — "Frankenstein" ad
- When he uses it: one ad has a strong body (low cost per booked call) but a weak hook; another has a strong hook.
- Steps: 1. add a custom Hook Rate column (3-second plays ÷ impressions) and sort; 2. find the body with the best cost per booked call (15.55% hook, £11.02/booked call over 33 schedules); 3. splice a top hook (~26%) onto it; 4. judge hook rate vs your own account average.
- Result: "we've done that so many times" [quote src: YT:bAo6SICFyIE] — often the best performer. Best hook ad: just under 27%, ~£30/schedule over 64; ~25% hooks with weak bodies cost ~£86/schedule; worst hooks 7–9%.
- On screen: founder-on-camera verticals, one centered white italic caption; hooks vary location/lighting (sunny swing vs dark stage, colour vs B&W) while the body talk stays the same. (VISUAL)
- Sources: Heath [YT:bAo6SICFyIE] (2026-03) · NOW

### Heath — service/coaching script structure (from his AI-ideation run)
- Prompt inputs: who you are + what you sell and what's included; the next step in the sales process ("book a call with us using the calendar on our website" [quote src: YT:M2VReAjHiN8]) so CTAs fit; the audience (warm + cold; owners/marketers who know Meta ads but aren't experts); then ask for 10 creative ideas with multiple hooks each.
- Script: 1. hook direct to camera, confident, slight lean forward; 2. problem agitation in a frustrated tone ("maybe you get a response in three to five business days" [quote src: YT:M2VReAjHiN8]); 3. revelation with proof ("after generating over 600 million for our clients" [quote src: YT:M2VReAjHiN8]); 4. specific pain moment ("When your campaign breaks at 2 p.m. on a Tuesday" [quote src: YT:M2VReAjHiN8]); 5. the offer.
- CTA variants for book-a-call: "Book your strategy call" [quote src: YT:M2VReAjHiN8], Apply for mentorship, "Get your free consultation" [quote src: YT:M2VReAjHiN8], "Claim your spot" [quote src: YT:M2VReAjHiN8], "Schedule your call" [quote src: YT:M2VReAjHiN8].
- Visual elements he rates for coaching/service ads: screen recordings of real ad accounts, live-call footage, student results, behind-the-scenes, real-time win notifications, social-proof numbers ($600M+, 1,000+ students).
- Sources: Heath [YT:M2VReAjHiN8] (2025-10) · EVERGREEN

### 2b. Other speakers — positions

Levinger: research for B2B/services is almost the same because you sell to humans; only the output differs. Zorsky (guest): with product-led growth the user is the buyer and B2B ads are consumer ads.
