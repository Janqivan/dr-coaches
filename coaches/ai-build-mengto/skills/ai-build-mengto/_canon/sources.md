# Sources, corpus and accuracy notes (ai-build-mengto)

## Tag legend
- [YT:<id>] → https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<id>
- (YYYY-MM) = upload month (approximate for channel uploads; derived from YouTube's relative dates). n.d. = no date captured.
- "mentions N" = number of separate evidence items supporting a point.
- [quote src: YT:<id>] = the quoted words were found verbatim in that video rather than in the bullet's main sources.
- Backticks = prompts, settings or code exactly as given in the source.

## What the corpus is (pulled Oct 5, 2026)
- Meng To: 66 videos, 44 hours, 442K words of auto-captions. 60 from the Design+Code channel, Oct 2024 to Sept 2026, and 6 guest appearances (Dive Club ×2, Peter Yang, SQ Collective, Config APAC 2024, Basit). 13 Design+Code videos are presented by other instructors and were excluded. 1,059 sourced items from the 53 videos where Meng speaks.
- Sergei Chyrkov (second source, cited as Sergei): all 71 videos on his channel, Oct 2023 to Sept 2026, 21 hours, 187K words, 497 sourced items.
- Pre-2024 Design+Code videos (mostly SwiftUI and Figma tutorials) were not read.
- Not in the corpus: Meng's X posts, Aura documentation, the paid Design+Code courses, Sergei's paid mentoring.

## Accuracy notes
- Transcripts are YouTube auto-captions without speaker labels; attribution was judged from context.
- Every quoted fragment of 8 or more characters was machine-checked against the transcripts and found verbatim. Quotes are capped at 15 words.
- Tool and model behavior changes month to month. Give the date with every tool claim, and say when a newer source may contradict it.
