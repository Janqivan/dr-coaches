## 2. Converts in general — principles in older evidence and repeated in 2026

1. **The creative does the targeting.** Under broad/Advantage+ delivery, the first 3 seconds (imagery, words, overlay) tell Meta who to deliver to; interests, lookalikes and prospecting/retargeting splits are "pretty much useless". 2021: broad-only tests → 2025-12 Andromeda explainer → 2026 "more of a persona-based algorithm" [quote src: YT:-E9sjb2msjo]. GEN. [YT:KFFcno59vQ8] (2021-09) [YT:wfENnO7PA5Q] (2025-02) [YT:x1b4h42HTG8] (2025-12) [YT:I8tXqqfjIX4] (2026-06) → VH §1.3 · TA §1.2, §1.9
2. **The hook decides most of the outcome, and a hook is a package.** On-screen text + sound + frame-zero visual + vibe. "Hooks are literally the 80/20 of ad creative." [quote src: YT:eFyvp0mu7UQ] Visual hook changes tend to move performance more than text changes: "the best hooks show, they don't tell." [quote src: YT:t-Xf12o4jt4] 2022 → 2025-08 → 2026. GEN. → VH §1.2, §1.6, §2.2
3. **Never bet on one hook.** 3–6 hooks filmed per ad (2022), 2–4 per test (2024), 4 per video (2025), 3–6 big swings (2026). "we can't place all of our hope in just one hook" [quote src: YT:wfENnO7PA5Q]. GEN. → VH §1.4
4. **New hooks on proven winners are the cheapest lift.** Hook swaps, "POV" prefixes, virality overlays, stacked hook reels, re-recorded VO, from 2022 through 2026. GEN. [YT:CdK0jdqxXOs] (2022-07) [YT:2jVcLu3Earg] (2025-05) [YT:I8tXqqfjIX4] (2026-06) → VH §1.5, §2.7, §2.10
5. **Messaging wins, format carries it.** A winner is "generally never" winning on format alone; if a variation wins, it's likely the message, so put it into drastically different formats. 2024-11 Arrae → 2026 "Real strategy lies in the layer before format selection." [quote src: YT:yRgPbqywUJ8]. GEN. → FS §1.6 · MS §1.1 · TA §1.8
6. **Exploit what already works.** Only ~5% of her creatives are truly new (2024); 2026 Framework 1 says your next top performer already likely exists in your account: top 20 ads → extract the big idea → find recurrences → move it into a more emotional format. GEN. [YT:ZQAjWwxk2C0] (2024-05) [YT:p4yXXdnCgWg] (2026-08) → MS §1.8, §2.5
7. **Specific beats generic.** Exact numbers for time, effort and cost, named demographics, concrete outcomes: 5 minutes, under $5, debloat in under 1 hour, 10-minute exercise routine. 2024-01 golden nuggets → 2026-06 static copy layer. GEN. → MS §1.9, §2.11
8. **Golden-nugget testimonials beat generic praise.** Memorable, specific, often with numbers or age, often taboo; mined from reviews and ad comments; used as headlines. Without one a testimonial static is E/F tier. 2024-01 → 2026-08. GEN. → FS §2.13 · MS §2.12
9. **Negative marketing and trigger words.** Regret, hate, scam, embarrassed, 0–1-star reviews, calling out who it's not for. Strongest in skeptical categories. 2024-06 → 2026-07. GEN/DTC. → VH §1.11 · MS §1.10
10. **Look native, still sell.** 2020: "people can tell what's an ad and what's not" [quote src: YT:RTUae3Tctgs]; 2024: native fonts only; 2026: creator content that sells without feeling like an ad. Counterweight every year: good light, clean backgrounds, presentable creators. GEN/DTC. → VH §1.7, §2.13 · FS §1.8
11. **Founder content wins at any production level.** Favourite format 2023, #1 to take hi-fi 2024, S tier 2026; a hi-fi founder ad still ran after 4+ years. GEN/DTC. → VH §1.9 · FS §1.2
12. **Optimise for the most scalable message, not the fastest converter.** Offer/value/numbers variants convert first; the broadest message (10-minute routine, the "hate" question, 5-minute routine) wins longest. 2025-01 → 2026 top-of-funnel diagnosis. DTC/GEN. → MS §1.4–1.5
13. **Education converts cold traffic.** Tutorial/how-to UGC never mentioning the brand became top performers (2023); a pure educational ad took ~75% of spend (2025); how-to hooks and VSLs S tier (2026). GEN/DTC. → VH §1.12
14. **Objections are creative briefs.** TikTok response bubbles from real FAQs (2022), founder objection handlers (2025), objection ads in the top 15 (2026). GEN. → VH §3.2 · MS §1.11
15. **Comparison/us vs them is a reliable first winner, not a scaler.** Often the first ad that crushes for new brands (2023–2025); B-tier supporting cast in 2026; brands that stay on it get stuck at $1–6K/day. DTC. → FS §1.11, §2.4 · MS §5
16. **Keep testing separate from scaling; take big swings.** Dedicated testing campaign since 2021, restated for a $2M/month account in 2026; one-variable split testing "totally dead" (2024), "You do not need to split test every single little thing." ## 4. Hooks and watch time

**How she measures it**
- Hook rate = 3-second views ÷ impressions (custom column). Her 2025 target 35–40% (old adage 20–25%); 2022 Thesis average ~24%, best 40–50%, up to 60% on scaled Reels-only ads. Hold rate = % watching the first 15 s; her 2025 target ~10% (used to be ~5%). Images have only CTR. [YT:CdK0jdqxXOs] (2022-07) [YT:wRA2VgYIDVs] (2025-01) [YT:CCsty8R0UaA] (2025-01) → VH §2.1, §5 · TA §5
- Read them as "storytelling" metrics: they explain why, never call the winner (spend, results, cost per result, ROAS/CPL do). Low hook + low hold = back to the drawing board; high hook/hold + low CTR = wrong audience for that creative; high-hook winner = make more hooks like it. Check the thumbnail (a blacked-out frame inflated one ad's hook rate). Her weighting of these metrics fell 2025–26 as Meta automates. → TA §1.7, §2.7, §6 · VH §2.1

**Hook structure** — four layers in the first 3 seconds: (1) text overlay, (2) sound (spoken line, music, whisper), (3) frame-zero visual, (4) the vibe (light, font, feel). "Your hook is not just the first line of a script." [quote src: YT:I8tXqqfjIX4] (2025-08 → 2026) → VH §2.2

**Hook types her evidence backs most (2024–2026)**
1. Demographic / identity / "If you" — top type in 2024 and 2025, still on the 2026 list; job titles work (*Freelancers, here's how to land high-paying clients*); 50+ call-outs "incredibly necessary". → VH §1.10

**Retention moves**
- Hook reel: stack 2–3 hooks at the start (winning combos: demographic + controversy; demographic + anti-traditional); explosion visuals work slightly better as a second hook. → VH §2.7
- After the hook, agitate the problem before the solution so viewers know it isn't clickbait; "Frontload your problems" [quote src: YT:qoOBIPWH_qw]. Test module order with hold rate. → VH §2.8 · TA §2.7
- Pacing much faster than other editing (~0.5 s clips on mundane footage); J-cuts/L-cuts; punch-in or slow zooms. → VH §2.13
- Sound on (70% of Reels viewers have sound on); add sound effects; whisper re-record of a winning VO; vocal variety in creators. → VH §1.14, §2.13, §6
- Native fonts only in creator-style ads; B-roll is "liquid gold"; stock beats iPhone footage behind text for 55+; fewer captions performed as well. → VH §2.13
- Creator over script: magnetism, vocal variety, energy; give questions not lines. → VH §2.11
- Tension she leaves open: hooks are the 80/20 and the creator decides it "nine times out of 10" [quote src: YT:RVRRGf746Bw]; the canon reconciles it as the hook package including the creator's look, voice and vibe. → VH §6
