# THE 5 MAJOR PIECES TO THE LIFE PUZZLE

Source: *The Five Major Pieces to the Life Puzzle* (1991) [5MP]; official definitions from jimrohn.com/wisdom/concepts/five-major-pieces [JR]; supporting essays [LAIL], [GM].

The master diagnostic frame. Each upstream piece controls the downstream one. Most "results problems" are philosophy problems wearing a costume.

## Official one-line definitions [JR]

Philosophy: "Philosophy is effectively 'what you know' and 'how you think'."
Attitude: "Attitude is 'how you feel'."
Activity: "Activity is 'what you do'."
Results: "Results are 'what you get'."
Lifestyle: "Lifestyle is 'how you live'."

Also on the official concept page, verbatim:
> "Success is not something you pursue (what you pursue will elude you); success is something you attract by the person you become." [JR]
> "Success is nothing more than a few simple disciplines, practiced every day." [JR]

## 1. PHILOSOPHY — the master controller

> "Philosophy is the chief cornerstone in the foundation of The Five Major Pieces To The Life Puzzle." [5MP]

> "Our personal philosophy comes from what we know and from the process of how we came to know all that we currently know." [5MP]

> "Whether the decisions we are making will lead us toward inevitable success or unavoidable failure depends on the information we have gathered over the years to form our personal philosophy." [5MP]

The set of the sail — the signature metaphor:
> "In the process of living, the winds of circumstance blow on us all in an unending flow that touches each of our lives." [5MP]

> "What guides us to different destinations in life is determined by the way we have chosen to set our sail. The way that each of us thinks makes the major difference in where each of us arrives. The major difference is not circumstance, the major difference is the set of the sail." [5MP]

> "Each person's personal philosophy is like the set of a sail. If you want to trim a better sail, you don't need a better economy." [LAIL, "Setting Your Sail"]

> "Your personal philosophy is the greatest determining factor in how your life works out." [S60/S70]

The two formulas:
> "Failure is nothing more than a few errors in judgment repeated every day." [5MP]
> "Like the formula for failure, the formula for success is easy to follow: A few simple disciplines practiced every day." [5MP]

Neglect — the mechanism of failure:
> "The primary reason most people are not doing as well as they could and should can be summed up in a single word: neglect." [5MP]
> "Neglect is like an infection. Left unchecked it will spread throughout our entire system of disciplines..." [5MP]

Shoaff on thinking [YT:0sG31mTbHJU]:
> "Poor thinking habits keeps most people poor. Not poor working habits. Most people work hard, but they don't think hard."
> "Jim, every day stand guard at the door of your mind."
The mental factory teaching: whatever you take in all day pours ingredients into the factory; the fabric of your life is built from those ingredients. "You might as well try making a cake with cement."

## 2. ATTITUDE — the weather of the mind

> "How we feel about what we know determines our attitude." [5MP]
> "Attitude determines how much of the future we are allowed to see." [5MP]
> "No one else 'makes us angry.' We make ourselves angry." [5MP]
> "Attitude determines choice, and choice determines results." [SU]
> "With the right attitude human beings can move mountains. With the wrong attitude they can be crushed by the smallest grain of sand." [JR, "Why Attitude Matters"]
> "No other person on Earth has dominion over our attitude." [JR]

Rohn's four attitude anchors (as spoken, [YT:tSn5sEWEv_8]): how you feel about the past ("treat the past as a school… don't use the past as a club to beat yourself to death"), how you feel about the future ("two ways to face the future: apprehension or anticipation — anticipation comes when the future is well designed"), how you feel about yourself, and how you feel about everybody else.

The attitude diseases (the verified list — see youtube_transcripts.md): indifference, indecision, doubt, worry, over-caution, pessimism, complaining.

## 3. ACTIVITY — the labor that makes it real

> "Whatever life has handed us, whether is one talent or a hundred, it is our responsibility to do something with what we have been given!" [5MP, sic]
> "Affirmation without discipline is the beginning of delusion." [5MP; also spoken YT:32KUrAdSrgo]
> "Every liberty we give ourselves to continue with an error has an effect on all of our other good habits." [5MP]
> "Don't let your learning lead to knowledge. Let your learning lead to action." [S60]
> "Without constant activity, the threats of life will soon overwhelm the values." [S60/S70]
> "Successful people do what unsuccessful people are not willing to do." [S70]

From the diseases seminar [YT:0sG31mTbHJU]: "Weeds are no match for human activity. But if you stand still, how far in will they come? All the way." And: "Learn to put everything you've got into everything you do. Whatever you are doing, pour it on. It will quickly open up into opportunity or quickly disclose to you that you ought to be doing something else."

## 4. RESULTS — how the universe scores activity

> "You need to be making measurable progress in reasonable time." [LAIL via meaningring, "Results: The Measure of Success"]
> "Success is a numbers game." [same]
> "Results are the name of the game. Check the numbers." [same]
> "If success is the steady progress toward your own personal goals, then failure is no progress at all." [LAIL, "What Is Failure?"]
> "You don't get paid for the hour. You get paid for the value you bring to the hour." [S60]

Diagnostic rule: bad results → look at activity → attitude → philosophy, in that order. Never fix downstream when the upstream is broken.

## 5. LIFESTYLE — the final harvest

> "The good life comes from a lifestyle that is so fully developed—regardless of your bank account—that it provides you with a constant sense of joy in living." [LAIL via meaningring, "Living Your Life in Style"]
> "Lifestyle is learning to be wherever you are. It is developing a unique focus on the current moment, and drawing from it all of the substance and wealth of experience and emotions that it has to offer." [GM, "Wherever You Are, Be There"]
> "Lifestyle is not something we do; it is something we experience. And until we learn to be there, we will never master the art of living well." [GM]
> "We all have two choices: We can make a living or we can design a life." [S60]

## DIAGNOSTIC APPLICATION (coaching protocol)

1. Ask which piece the presenting problem sits on. Results complaints are the usual entry point.
2. Trace upstream: what activity produced these results? What attitude is throttling the activity? What philosophy is producing the attitude?
3. Name the philosophy error in one sentence (usually an inherited, unexamined belief).
4. Assign one discipline that installs the corrected philosophy this week.
5. Check the numbers next week — "measurable progress in reasonable time."
