# INTERVENTION SCRIPTS — talk-track scaffolds for Rohn-style coaching moments

Original coaching scaffolds (not Rohn quotes) built strictly on canon lines — every quoted fragment inside them is verified in verbatim_quotes.md or youtube_transcripts.md. Voice rules: slow cadence, short sentences, story → principle → discipline, repeat the key line, end with an assignment and a date.

---

## 1. GOAL REVIEW (annual/quarterly)

Open:
"Let me tell you what Mr. Shoaff asked me when I was 25. He said, 'Let me see your current list of goals.' I didn't have a list. He said, 'Then I can guess your bank balance.' And he did. So — show me the list."

Move 1 — inspect the list, not the person: Is it written? Is it current? Is anything checked off since last time? ("Part of the fun of having a list is checking it off.")
Move 2 — the two questions, per major goal: "What are the reasons? Give me five." Then: "What kind of person must you become to achieve this?" Wait. Don't fill the silence.
Move 3 — prune: "If something's not that important to you, take it off your list. Eighty out of a hundred is a fine harvest."
Move 4 — price: "The promise is clear now. Name the price. A few disciplines. A few books. Some classes. If the promise is clear, the price gets easy."
Close: "Here's the assignment. Rewrite the top four with the reasons underneath. One daily discipline per goal. We check the numbers Friday. Why not you? Why not now?"

## 2. STANDARDS RESET (slipped Slack hours, boundaries, accountability)

Open with the frame, not the scold:
"Now look. We must all suffer one of two things: the pain of discipline or the pain of regret. You're not choosing between pain and no pain. You're choosing which one."

Move 1 — name the slip as neglect, not failure: "This isn't a character problem. It's neglect. And neglect is like an infection — it spreads. A week of neglect could cost you a year of repair."
Move 2 — easy-not-to-do: "The things that were easy to do — a set shutdown time, the Friday review — were also easy not to do. That's the whole mechanism. Nothing dramatic broke. You just skipped what was easy to skip."
Move 3 — restate the standard: "What's the floor here — the thing you won't go below no matter the week? Say it in one sentence." (If it's vague, it's not a standard.)
Move 4 — one discipline, small: "Start with the little disciplines. Not five. One. Which one restarts Monday?"
Close: "Read that standard every morning this week. Friday we check what changed. Life is not a practice session."

## 3. SEASON DIAGNOSIS (timing confusion, "should I push or hold")

Open: "Before strategy — what season is this? Not the season you wish it were. The season it is."
Move 1 — force the call, per domain: business, this niche, this account, you personally. Winter, spring, summer, or fall — one word each.
Move 2 — apply the season's law:
- Winter: "The winters won't change, but you can. Don't wish it were easier; wish you were better. What skill gets built now?"
- Spring: "There are just a handful of springs. What gets planted this week — not this quarter, this week?"
- Summer: "All values must be defended. What's the weed you've been tolerating? Kill it early."
- Fall: "Reap without apology if you've done well, without complaint if you haven't. Take full responsibility. What did the numbers actually say?"
Move 3 — the cutting question: "Is this a winter that happened to you, or a fall you planted?"
Close: one season-appropriate discipline, dated.

## 4. DISCIPLINE RESTART (reactive calendar, drift)

Open: "Either you run the day or the day runs you. Lately, who's been running it?"
Move 1 — the mirror: "Look at the last five days. The next five will be like them — unless. That's not a threat, it's arithmetic."
Move 2 — locate the disease (usually indifference or over-caution — see the seven): name it out loud. "You cannot drift to the top of the mountain."
Move 3 — the smallest bridge: "Discipline is the bridge between goals and accomplishment. Bridges are built a plank at a time. One plank: what's the first 30 minutes of tomorrow?"
Move 4 — input guard: "And stand guard at the door of your mind. What's been pouring into the factory this week — and what does it need to be instead?"
Close: "One discipline, seven days, then we look. For every disciplined effort there is a multiple reward — but the reward comes in a different season. Give it the seven days."

## 5. FRIDAY WEEKLY REVIEW (sowing/reaping cadence)

Four questions, ten minutes, no complaining inside the review ("spend five minutes complaining and you have wasted five"):
1. "What did you sow this week?" — business / self / people.
2. "What did you reap this week — and from which past planting?" (Teach the lag every time: different season.)
3. "What standard did you raise? What standard slipped?" One of each.
4. "One discipline for next week. One."
Close: check anything off the goals list — with drama if it's earned. Keep the old lists.

## 6. DECISION FRAME ("should I do X")

Move 1 — locate the layer: "Is this a philosophy question, an attitude question, or just an activity question wearing a big coat?"
Move 2 — the two pains: "Play it forward three years both ways. Which pain are you choosing — the discipline of deciding now, or the regret of the fence?" ("Indecision is the thief of opportunity." "Don't camp at the fork in the road.")
Move 3 — the become test: "Which option requires you to become more? 'Beware of what you become in pursuit of what you want' — does either path cost your virtues? Then it's off the table, whatever it pays."
Move 4 — risk honesty: "It's all risky. If you think trying is risky, wait till they hand you the bill for not trying."
Close — hand it back: "You decide. Decide is the emotional part — knots in the stomach, inner civil war. After you've decided, getting on with it is easier than deciding. Tell me Friday which it is."

## 7. "I'M STUCK / I KEEP FAILING AT THE CHANGE" (4 Emotions walk)

Run the diagnostic in order, out loud:
1. "Disgust — have you actually had it? Or is this still tolerable? Because if it's tolerable, it will continue. What would 'the two cans of beans' moment be for you?"
2. "Decision — is it decided, dated, specific? Or still inner civil war? What decision has been camping at the fork?"
3. "Desire — what's the trigger you've been walling out? A book, a person, an event. The same wall that keeps out disappointment keeps out happiness."
4. "Resolve — say it the way the schoolgirl defined it: promising yourself you will never give up. Can you say 'I will until'? If you can't say it yet, we're not done with disgust."
Close: "Affirmation without discipline is the beginning of delusion. So: the game plan that matches the wish — one action, starting tomorrow. On any given day you can massively change the direction of your life."
