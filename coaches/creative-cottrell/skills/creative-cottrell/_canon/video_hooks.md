## 2. Frameworks and methods

### 2.1 Hook anatomy (visual / audible / text)
- **When:** writing any hook; how Fraggle briefs hooks. **Steps:** (1) visual hook = what is happening on screen (incl. on-screen captions in one version); (2) audible hook = what is said by creator/VO + sound effects; (3) text hook = on-screen text that either captions the line or adds something different (meme text above heads, scene-setting). Add ingredients: lead with a problem tied to the product; open a loop closed later; "mirror hooks" that mirror who the viewer is and their problems. **Example:** Aloha "This is what goes into most protein bars" + pouring white liquid (pairing two hook types = one of his favourites, used all the time in VSLs). — [YT:opWKMc6iavo] (2025-10) · [YT:93gJ8_qvOQg] (2026-03) · [YT:Mqj05tYuDI0] (2026-09) · [YT:RExPkxyRB6o] (2026-10)
- **Hook writing process:** write the line first, then find a visual people don't often see (not just face/product-in-hand) to enhance the audio. — [YT:0K7WXqYPFCE] (2024-11)

### 2.2 Top 10 Meta hooks (2025-10 list)
(1) exposing the truth · (2) wild visual · (3) the test · (4) blur · (5) product hook · (6) reaction hook · (7) found footage · (8) demographic hook · (9) TikTok comment response · (10) stacking multiple hooks. Rules per type:
- Exposing the truth: tell them you're about to expose a secret; "people love gossip" [quote src: YT:opWKMc6iavo]; dangling a carrot; hook-only version of his expose VSLs.
- Wild visual: something people don't often see; AI is perfect because it can make the unreal and viewers are still entertained.
- Test: put something to the test (best skincare product; robot mower vs me) — opens a loop held to the end.
- Blur: blur the item so they must watch/click to see.
- Product hook: product doing something attention-grabbing — "I love doing hooks like this because they always win" [quote src: YT:opWKMc6iavo]; "the easiest way to improve your hook rates" [quote src: YT:opWKMc6iavo].
- Reaction: best for CPG or a "mindblowing" demo; stronger out of context.
- Found footage: shot as if by someone across the street + iPhone zoom; pair with Snapchat-style captions (low-opacity black bar) — strong per TikTok Shop data.
- Demographic: call out who it's for (caption or spoken); lowers CPM, qualified viewers, maybe lower hook rate.
- Comment response: fake TikTok reply-to-comment graphic; feels organic, tells scrollers what they're in for.
- Stacking: combine types; "A good hook is just whatever works for you" [quote src: YT:opWKMc6iavo] — B2B differs from B2C, only testing finds it.
— [YT:opWKMc6iavo] (2025-10, EVERGREEN)

### 2.5 Villain-vs-hero script (the million dollar villain and hero script framework)
- **When:** "always gives me consistent winning results" [quote src: YT:KKp3rA0QK9A]; recyclable across every video; works in short ads too (Underbrush: villain built in first ~10s). Villain = industry, competitor product ("more importantly probably your competitor" [quote src: YT:4o46eGI6XwQ]), a situation, or a habit (urge to bite nails).
- **Steps (2025-04, 7-step):** (1) hook; (2) set up villain; (3) say how bad it is, "twist the knife" [quote src: YT:k533N5RxXPY]; (4) reveal hero, show how it fixes villain-made problems, list USPs; (5) handle an objection; (6) social proof; (7) CTA.
- **Steps (2026-01, 5-part):** hook → villain and why it's the worst → product as hero → demo/proof → CTA (where, maybe price, offer); optional benefits.
- **Rules:** list of reasons to hate the villain "as long as your arm" [quote src: YT:4o46eGI6XwQ]; show hero in the most epic way, cut against unappealing villain footage; payoff must be big — most common mistake "the payoff just isn't worth it" [quote src: YT:4o46eGI6XwQ]. Layered villains ("it's like Shrek" [quote src: YT:4o46eGI6XwQ]), e.g. chai: sleep hacks + "bonus villain" sugary coffee-shop chai.
- **Examples:** Leaf Razor (product at 40s), The Black Stuff, Kolkata Chai, Leaf Shave clog test, Underbrush gum.

### 2.6 Problem-solution ("the simplest and probably most effective ad script" [quote src: YT:505rIrOKhWI])
- **Steps:** (1) hook with the problem or tease the solution (crazy shot, relatable caption or VO); (2) break the problem down; (3) agitate it ("twist the knife" [quote src: YT:k533N5RxXPY]) because viewers may not feel it's that bad; (4) present solution (paired with a benefit), show how it solves it; (5) proof/social proof; (6) CTA / where to buy.
- **Rules:** "has made millions" [quote src: YT:tVXjXJSEjeA] for clients; problem must be common and real — research reviews; it's "very script heavy" [quote src: YT:cUHJkQzisDQ]; it is "an umbrella format" [quote src: YT:505rIrOKhWI], "not a script format" [quote src: YT:505rIrOKhWI] — weave proof, social proof and objection handling throughout, order is flexible as long as problem and solution are there; once the solution lands, get into small details (knit, band, strap) that "are now becoming part of the solution" [quote src: YT:505rIrOKhWI]. Scenario-listing variant: several scenarios, each hitting a sub-persona (Aloha Minis).

### 2.8 Fraggle script structure: Hook / Body / Close
(1) Hook — visual + audible, built to target the right person; (2) Body — emotional journey that builds rapport, opens loops, resolves the objection; body varies by format: UGC testimonial = USPs/benefits, storytelling UGC = yapper conversational story, educational VSL = facts and figures; (3) Close/CTA — what to do, where to go, what they get (benchmark: TikTok Shop "Click the little yellow cart…"). — [YT:IX-2-uSJDk8] (2026-06)

### 2.9 Mini VSL / VSL

### 2.10 Founder ad
- **Script:** founder story as problem-solution — "They had this problem, they couldn't find a solution, so they made a solution" [quote src: YT:y7TFcfgwhP8] ("the ultimate founder ad script format" [quote src: YT:y7TFcfgwhP8] that "works every single time" [quote src: YT:y7TFcfgwhP8]). ## 3. Templates and exact wording

**Hook templates (his)**
- "I swapped this for this" — familiar/well-known item (McDonald's breakfast bag; granola + yogurt) thrown out, your product rolled in. TOF. Familiar thing must be universally known. — [YT:93gJ8_qvOQg] (2026-03) · [YT:Mqj05tYuDI0] (2026-09)
- "This is what goes into most" — pair with an educational "truth" video. TOF. — [YT:93gJ8_qvOQg]

## 5. Numbers and benchmarks

## 6. What changed over time and where they contradict themselves
- **Sound-off → sound-on** (2024-11): ~3 years earlier everyone optimised sound-off; TikTok made sound-on normal; add SFX/soundscapes. — [YT:VCnx3v_gZQI]
- **Glued-on hook → hook that sets up the video** (2026-09): less "glue this hook onto the front" and more "use the hook to set up the rest"; reasons hooks work "in this age of advertising" differ from his earlier hook video. Then (2026-10) hook "isn't as important as it used to be"; hold rate now matters more. Earlier (2024-11 to 2026-01) the hook was "arguably one of the most important factors". — [YT:Mqj05tYuDI0] · [YT:RExPkxyRB6o] · [YT:KKp3rA0QK9A]
- **Traditional UGC → story / yapper / raw UGC**: 2025-05 "Traditional UGC is kind of dead"; 2025-10 polished UGC from 2–3 years ago no longer works, AI fake UGC erodes trust; 2026-10 talking head + B-roll cut-ins "doesn't really work anymore". Yet 2025-05 also: traditional UGC still works in some accounts (Govee); 2024-12 he recommended blending UGC with studio shots. — [YT:2cT3ccIzLTU] · [YT:bNMeFhW6YmA] · [YT:RExPkxyRB6o] · [YT:jv5ECAtsrHc]
- **Listicle hooks**: 2025-10 he says he deliberately avoids list hooks like "top three reasons why" because everyone uses them; 2026-03 and 2026-09 he teaches "four truths" listicle hooks as working (completionists, low commitment). — [YT:opWKMc6iavo] · [YT:93gJ8_qvOQg] · [YT:Mqj05tYuDI0]
- **Product placement timing**: listicle/story UGC → product as reason #1 (2025-05); villain ads and VSLs → hold product to 40 s / near the end (2025-04, 2026-03, 2026-10). Reconcile: short UGC front-loads; long story/VSL back-loads with payoff.
- **AI voiceover**: 2023-10 AI VO builds a montage in minutes; 2025-02 AI VO acceptable for founder ads; 2025-09/2025-11 avoids AI VO in founder/VSL/heavy-talk ads (emotion) and hires humans; yet the foot-file winner (2025-12) and the Kolkata founder story (2025-07) used AI VO.

- **Hook-swap testing** (2026-02): swapping only the overlay hook text no longer counts as a new test post-Andromeda; storytelling is what separates brands under Andromeda. — [YT:_BuCO3Jf-K0]
